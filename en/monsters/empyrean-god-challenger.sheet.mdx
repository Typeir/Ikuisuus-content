## Traits

<Trait>

#### Tombsteel Armor

The God-Challenger wears custom heavy armor forged from tombsteel, The armor has the following properties:

- Increases base Deflect by +1
- Increases the weight of the armor by +[= 8 burden =]
- Causes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks
- Grants advantage on saving throws made to resist being **displaced** or **knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]**
- Grants the following features:
  ** Grave Chill**
  Tombsteel armor holds up to **10 charges** of reactive cold stored in its rime-slicked seams. When a creature hits the God-Challenger with a **melee weapon attack**, the attacker immediately takes **10 frost damage** as frozen tendrils erupt from the armor. This effect requires no action or reaction.
  - Each activation consumes **1 charge**
  - Charges **regenerate at midnight**, recovering **[% 1d4 +4 %] charges**

  ** Cold Eternal**
  If a creature wearing Tombsteel Armor is **not resistant or immune to frost damage**, they save Constitution against DC 12 at the end of each hour while wearing it. On a failed save, the creature gains **1 level of exhaustion**.
  This effect occurs even while sleeping or [# kw:condition:unconscious #]. The God-Challenger is resistant to frost damage and thus ignores this effect.

  **Fraternity** (Integrated)
  The armor carries a permanent [_Fraternity_](/en/library/spells/fraternity) binding the God-Challenger to their mount; its +1 Deflect is already counted.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Target of the Hunt

The God-Challenger has sworn to hunt specific prey. The God-Challenger's chosen prey are Aberrations and Monstrosities.
The God-Challenger gains the following benefits against the chosen prey:

- Gains **advantage on Constitution (Survival) checks** to track them
- Gains **advantage on Wisdom checks** to recall lore or information about them
- Gains **double tier bonus** on Charisma (Intimidation) checks made against them
- Speaks **one additional language** used by the selected prey type (Hidden, in this case)

</Trait>

<Trait>

- The aura is always active while the God-Challenger is conscious

</Trait>
