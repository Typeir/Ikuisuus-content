Soul of the Brume’s tombsteel armor holds up to 12 charges.  
When a creature hits Soul of the Brume with a melee weapon attack, the attacker immediately takes 15 frost damage as frozen rime shards erupt from the armor. This effect requires no action or reaction.

- Each activation consumes 1 charge
- Charges regenerate at midnight, recovering [% 1d6 +6 %] charges

</Trait>
