### Actions

##### Rusting bite

Accuracy +5, reach [= 1 stride =], one creature.  
On a hit, 8 ([% 1d8 +3 slashing %]).  
The target’s Dexterity score is reduced by **2** (minimum 1) as its joints harden and corrode.  
Each hit also applies **one stack of [# kw:Erosion #]**.  
A creature reduced to **Dexterity 0** by this effect becomes **[# kw:condition:petrified #]**, its body turning into a crusted mineral shell.  
The Dexterity reduction lasts until the creature finishes a [# kw:Recovery #] or receives [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration), cast as its greater working.

**Petrifying bile**
