## Traits

<Trait>

#### Amorphous Mass

The Silent One can move through a space as narrow as **[= 1 stride =]** wide without squeezing. It cannot be knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Many-Armed Horror

The Silent One has twelve arms and makes six **Slam** attacks on its turn.  
If it begins its turn **grappling no creatures**, each Slam instead deals **double damage**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Digestive Maw (Concentration)

A creature [# kw:condition:grappled #] by **three or more arms** saves Strength against DC 17 or is **swallowed**.  
While swallowed, the creature is **[# kw:condition:blinded #]** and **[# kw:condition:restrained #]**, has **total cover** against effects from outside, and takes **28 ([% 8d6 chemical %])** at the start of each of the Silent One’s turns.

The Silent One must maintain this swallow as if **concentrating on a spell**. Each time it takes damage, it saves Constitution against DC 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher.  
On a failure, it immediately **regurgitates all swallowed creatures**, which fall **[# kw:condition:prone #]** in spaces within [= 2 stride =] of it.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Innate Spellcasting (Psionics)

**Spell Save DC**: 15  
The Silent One can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components:

- **At will**: [_Arcane Sense_](/en/library/spells/arcane-sense), [_Detect Thoughts_](/en/library/spells/detect-thoughts), _Eldritch Blast_ (5 beams, accuracy +9, [% 1d10 force %] each; **pulls the target [= 2 stride =] toward the Silent One on a hit**), [_Misty Step_](/en/library/spells/misty-step)
- **3/day each**: [_Command_](/en/library/spells/command), [_Suggestion_](/en/library/spells/suggestion), [_Zone of Truth_](/en/library/spells/zone-of-truth), [_Knock_](/en/library/spells/knock), [_Arcane Lock_](/en/library/spells/arcane-lock), [_Dimension Door_](/en/library/spells/dimension-door)
- **1/day each**: [_Divination_](/en/library/spells/divination), [_Magic Missile_](/en/library/spells/magic-missile) (7th level)

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Silent Presence

The Silent One’s body produces no natural sound, and its voice is heard only as faint murmurs.  
While this aura is active, creatures within **[= 6 stride =]** have **disadvantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing, and spellcasters within this range must succeed on a **DC 15 Concentration check** to cast a spell with a **verbal component**.  
The Silent One can suppress or reactivate this aura at the start of its turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### All-Around Eyes

The Silent One has **advantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks and **cannot be surprised**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Whirl of Limbs

As an [# kw:delayed #] effect, each creature of the Silent One’s choice within **[= 2 stride =]** saves Strength against DC 18, halving **35 ([% 10d6 bludgeoning %])**.  
**[# kw:condition:Grappled #] creatures automatically fail**, and take an additional **10 chemical damage**.

</Trait>
