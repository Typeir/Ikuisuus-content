## Traits

<Trait>

#### Legendary Deeds

Albedo has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of her turn.

</Trait>

<Trait charges="3/Repose">

#### Legendary Deed: Resist

When Albedo fails a saving throw, she can expend a legendary deed to succeed instead. This feature can be used **3 times per [# kw:Repose #]**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Titanic Godmass

The listed statistics represent **Albedo’s control nucleus** (roughly horse-sized), extruded from a miles-wide quasi-liquid body. Only the nucleus can be targeted. The rest of Albedo functions as terrain and sources of spawns created by her abilities.

- The nucleus can’t be [# kw:condition:grappled #] or knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].
- If the nucleus would be pushed or pulled, Albedo may instead shift the ground beneath her and remain in place.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Cancerous Proliferation

Albedo **spawns** when anything bleeds or breaks near her.

- **When Hit**: Whenever Albedo takes **20 or more damage** from a single source, **spawn 1 Petal** in an empty space within [= 3 stride =] of the attacker (**2 Petals** on a critical hit).
- **On Hit**: Whenever Albedo hits a creature with a melee attack, the target is **seeded** with **1 Parasite** (see _Reflected Parasite_).
- **End of Each Turn**: If Albedo damaged **two or more** different creatures since her last turn, **spawn 1 Petal** adjacent to each damaged creature.
- **Brood Cap (Soft 20)**: At the end of each round, for each spawn beyond **20**, **merge** the two lowest-HP Petals into **1 Bloom**.
- **At the start of each turn**: if three **Blooms** are present within [= 2 stride =] of one another, they automatically **merge into 1 Flower** at the end of the round.
- **At the start of each turn**: if three **Flowers** are present within [= 2 stride =] of one another, they automatically **merge into 1 Bouquet** at the end of the round.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Self-Fertilization

_As a Minor Action_, Albedo **consumes** up to **3** allied spawns within [= 6 stride =]. For each creature consumed, permanently **increase the damage die size of all her melee and area attacks by one step** on this scale (no maximum):

> d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → **[% 2d8 %]** → **[% 2d10 %]** → **[% 2d12 %]** → **[% 4d8 %]** → **[% 4d10 %]** → **[% 4d12 %]** → **[% 8d8 %]** → **[% 8d10 %]** → **[% 8d12 %]** → … (continue increasing die count in the same pattern)

Additionally, each spawn gains a **+1 accuracy and damage bonus**, also stacking infinitely.

Albedo loses all such bonuses if she is reduced to 0 HP, or if she consumes no spawns during her turn.

Additionally, whenever Albedo rests for longer than 1 hour, she loses all such bonuses.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Endless Reef

Ground Albedo has affected this combat becomes **semi-living reef**:

- Counts as **difficult terrain** for creatures other than Albedo.
- A creature that walks across Endless Reef takes **7 ([% 2d4 chemical %])** per [= 1 stride =], unless it willingly walks at half its speed.
- Certain effects convert areas into Endless Reef as noted.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### White Life

Albedo floods the surrounding area with its **endless reef**, a mass of pale coral-like growths that spread and pulse with vile intent.  
When a creature touches the reef, is caught within **[= 6 stride =]** of Albedo as an [# kw:delayed #] effect, or is struck by one of its attacks, it saves Constitution against DC 21 or rolls on the [# kw:White Life #] table.  
Creatures **immune to disease and curses** that would roll in the table ignore this effect and instead take **[% 2d4 chemical %]** as the coral burns against their skin.

</Trait>

### Reactions

### Shedding Wake

When a creature **ends its movement within [= 2 stride =]** of Albedo's Godmass, **spawn 1 Petal** adjacent to that creature (once per creature per round).

## Deeds

Albedo has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of her turn.

Albedo can expend legendary deeds to use the options below, each taken with different timing, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).

#### Lair

At the start of the creature with the highest initiative's turn, Albedo can expend a legendary deed to use one of the following lair deeds. She cannot use the same lair deed two rounds in a row.

### Womb Weeps

**Spawn [% 1d4 %] Petals** adjacent to each hostile creature that **moved** since the previous round.

### Crawlspace

Choose up to **three [= 2 stride;ADJ =] squares** within [= 24 stride =]; they become **Endless Reef** and immediately **spawn 1 Petal** each.

---


### Bloodied (50%) — Knots of Flesh

At the end of each of Albedo’s turns, **merge automatically**: for every **3 Petals** within [= 2 stride =] of each other, create **1 Bloom** (consuming those Petals).

### Doomed (25%) — Critical Mass

The nucleus destabilizes; replication overruns structure.

- At the start of each round, **spawn Petals until there are exactly 10 active**.
- Then, **merge every three Petals** within [= 6 stride =] into **one Bloom**.
- Each time a **Bloom forms**, Albedo **uses Self-Fertilization** as a **free action**, consuming **1 Petal** if any remain.
- All ground within **[= 24 stride =]** becomes **Endless Reef**.

---

#### Act

Act deeds are taken at the end of another creature’s turn, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).

- **Spawn Petal.** (Costs 1 Deed) Create **1 Petal** in an empty space within [= 3 stride =]
- **Detonate Parasites.** (Costs 2 Deeds) Each **Parasite** on creatures within [= 12 stride =] **detonates** for **10 ([% 4d4 chemical %])** + **10 ([% 4d4 psychic %])**.
- **Reef Stride.** (Costs 2 Deeds) Albedo **teleports up to [= 12 stride =]** to a square of **Endless Reef** she can sense.
- **Merge Swarm.** (Costs 3 Deeds) Choose up to **3 Petals** within [= 4 stride =]; they merge into **1 Bloom** at full HP.
