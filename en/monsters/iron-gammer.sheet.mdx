<Action cost="1 Major Action">

#### Throw
**Major Action**

The Iron Gammer throws **one creature or object** it is holding, grappling, or that is within reach and unsecured, of **Large size or smaller**, up to **[= 12 stride =]** in a straight line to an unoccupied space it can see.

If a **creature** is thrown, it takes **[% 1d10 bludgeoning %]** per [= 2 stride =] traveled on landing. 
The thrown movement counts as the creature’s movement for the current round. 
Immediately after the Iron Gammer’s turn ends the creature takes its turn, even if it has already acted this round. 
A creature can only benefit from **one gifted turn per round**.

If an **object** is thrown, each creature in the object’s landing space saves Dexterity against DC 18 or takes **[% 3d10 bludgeoning %]**, or half as much on a success. 
The object then comes to rest in the landing space.

If a creature is unwilling, it saves Strength against DC 20 or is thrown. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not moved.

</Action>
