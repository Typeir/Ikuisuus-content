_Large Aberration, Chaotic Evil_

Discarded flesh born from great Silent Ones often develops an animalistic, brutal nature. This leads to them being routinely mistaken by
great wyrms or dragons of legend, and subsequently hunted down by the locals of their areas.

> With a Hail of Harpoons... With every last drop of my being! — Last words of Sire Ahav of Irémia.

## Traits

#### Amphibious

The abominable avian can breathe air and water.

#### Gold Glimmer

The abominable avian emits a faint, mesmerizing glow. As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, creatures within [= 2 stride =] of it saves Wisdom against DC 16 or is **[# kw:condition:charmed #]** [# kw:briefly #].

The abominable avian’s body is swollen with volatile electrical organs.  
It has advantage on Constitution saving throws against being [# kw:condition:stunned #] or [# kw:condition:paralyzed #].

#### Multiattack

The abominable avian makes **two attacks**: one with its bite and one with its talons.

- **Bite.**  
  Accuracy +8, reach [= 1 stride =], one creature.  
  On a hit, 13 ([% 2d8 +4 piercing %]).

- **Talons.**  
  Accuracy +8, reach [= 1 stride =], one creature.  
  On a hit, 11 ([% 2d6 +4 slashing %]).

#### Quacke (Recharge 5–6)

The abominable avian releases a catastrophic blast of compressed air in a **[= 12 stride;ADJ =] cone**.  
Each creature in the area saves Constitution against DC 16, halving **50 ([% 10d8 bludgeoning %])**.

Creatures that fail the save are also **[# kw:condition:deafened #] and knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]** [# kw:briefly #].

#### Lightning Dive

The abominable avian flies up to **[= 12 stride =]** in a straight line. Each creature whose space it passes through saves Dexterity against DC 15 or takes **22 ([% 4d10 lightning %])** and is knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].
The abominable avian does not provoke opportunity attacks when it flies out of an enemy’s reach.
