# Monsters

This bestiary contains information about the creatures from the _Vile and Beautiful lands of Damocles_. 
All manners of beasts, from the lowliest vermin to the mightiest monstrosities, can be found within.

A monster's lethality determines the expected level of a party of four adventurers to be able to take it down without casualties.
This value assumes the characters are archetypal, unoptimized and carry no magical items.
As such, well equipped or specialized parties will often punch well above their weight, even up to double their expected lethality.

---

## Building an Encounter

One monster whose lethality equals the party's level is an **easy** encounter for four adventurers, and that monster's written Experience is the party's easy budget. 
For parties with mixed levels, each takes up a quarter of the Experience a monster of their **Level** as **Lethality** would ential.

The rest of the scale is a multiple of that figure.

| Difficulty | Budget |
| :--------- | :----: |
| Easy       |   ×1   |
| Medium     |   ×2   |
| Hard       |   ×5   |
| Deadly     |  ×10   |

Add the written Experience of every monster in an encounter and read the total against the scale. 
A [# kw:Repose #] holds two medium encounters, or one easy, one medium and one hard. 
The Experience an encounter is worth is divided among the characters who earned it, and counts towards the totals in [Character Progression](/en/library/rules/the-measure-of-the-self/character-progression).
The experience budget for an encounter is additionally doubled per creature if there is more than a creature of their CR.
The experience budget is doubled further if the Party is outnumbered.

If the encounter entails objectives, the difficulty of said encounter is increased depending on the standing difficulty of the tests required to accomplish said objective, at the Game Director's behest.
Objectives in pre-built encounters will list what they add to the Experience budget.
