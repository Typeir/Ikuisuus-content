## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chosen Sight

The Väki is perceived only by those it chooses. To every other creature it is **[# kw:condition:invisible #]** and makes no sound. Choosing, or unchoosing, costs it nothing.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Splinter Is the Whole

The Väki is Rot, and Rot remembers. A creature that reduces it to 0 hit points is known to every Väki of Rot, and has disadvantage on every check to parlay with Rot until the Idea tires of the grudge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Felid Guise

As a **Minor Action**, the Väki takes the shape of an ordinary house feline, or sheds it. Its statistics are unchanged save its size, which becomes Tiny.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Ripening

Creatures within [= 2 stride =] of the Väki regain only half the hit points they would from any source. Food and drink in the same area spoils within the hour.

</Trait>
