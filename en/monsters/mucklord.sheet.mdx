## Traits

<Trait>

#### Legendary Deeds

The Mucklord has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Breathless, Inertity

The Mucklord does not need to breathe or eat. Although it may do either of the two just for the sake of it.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Aura of Stillness ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] radius)

As an [# kw:delayed #] effect, creatures in the aura save Strength against DC 24 or their speed becomes **0** [# kw:briefly #]. Creatures immune to paralysis instead have their speed **halved**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Garbage Communion

The Mucklord is aware of refuse and corpses within **[= 1 league =]**. As a **Minor Action**, it calls debris in an **[= 16 stride =] radius** to itself, **5 temporary hit points** per corpse CR or Character Level, **consuming said corpses**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Belly of the Muck

A swallowed creature is **banished** to the **Muck Dimension** (see [_Nullbrand_](/en/library/items/heirlooms/nullbrand)). While there, the creature is **[# kw:condition:incapacitated #]** and takes [% 6d10 force %] at the start of each of its turns until it escapes or the effect ends.
If a creature that is not a **construct** falls below 0HP while in the Muck Dimension, they are **instantly** absorbed into the muck, along with all they are carrying, and dissolve into sludge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Cannon Tongue

The Mucklord’s tongue is a siege cannon. It can target any point it can see within **[= 120 stride =]** It has **disadvantage** on attack rolls against creatures within **[= 2 stride =]**

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Sad Resilience

Once per round, when the Mucklord takes damage, it gains **temporary hit points** equal to **half the damage taken** until the start of its next turn. The source creature saves Wisdom against DC 24 or is **[# kw:condition:terrified #]** of the Mucklord [# kw:briefly #].
If the mucklord then takes damage that does not exceed the temporary hit points, the source of the damage is cursed with a **Curse of indolence**, applying a stack of [# kw:Erosion #] **as if the d4 had landed on a "4"**,
and forcing them to use their Minor Action to harmlessly sigh when in combat. This effect stacks indefinitely.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Gag Reflex

When attacked, the Mucklord saves Concentration or spews out the contents of it's belly, including those transported to the **Muck Dimension**

</Trait>

## Features

#### Multiattack

The Mucklord makes **one Cannon Tongue** attack and **two Crushing Claw** attacks.

#### Swallow

The Mucklord makes one **Crushing Claw** attack against a **[# kw:condition:grappled #]** Medium or smaller target. On a hit, the target is **swallowed** and transported to the **Muck Dimension** (see _Belly of the Muck_).

</Action>

<Attack accuracy="+18" reach="[= 4 stride =]">

</Attack>

##### Cannon Tongue

</Attack>

## Deeds

The Mucklord has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn.

The Mucklord can expend legendary deeds to use the options below, each taken with different timing, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).

#### Lair

<Action deed="lair">

#### Forlorn Summons

The Mucklord convulses, belching up fragments of the **Muck Dimension**.  
From the brine emerge malformed Forlorn — remnants of abandoned machines and creatures forgotten by time.

Roll [% 1d4 %], summoning the corresponding Forlorn in unoccupied spaces within **[= 12 stride =]** of the Mucklord.  
They act immediately after the Mucklord in the initiative order and remain until destroyed or until the Mucklord uses this action again.

| d4  | Summoned Creature                                                                 |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **1 + [% 1d6 %] [Forlorn Sludgelings](/en/library/monsters/forlorn-sludgeling)**  |
| 2   | **2 [Brine Sea Worm](/en/library/monsters/brine-sea-worm)**                       |
| 3   | **1 [Toxic Avenger](/en/library/monsters/toxic-avenger)**                         |
| 4   | **1 [Abandoned Old War Machine](/en/library/monsters/abandoned-old-war-machine)** |

</Action>

<Action deed="lair">

#### Forlorn Detonation

The Mucklord emits a shuddering groan that resonates through the brine, forcing every active Forlorn within **[= 60 stride =]** to rupture violently.

Each Forlorn detonates in a **[= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius**, forcing all creatures in range to save Dexterity against DC 24.  
On a failure, the creature takes **chemical and force damage equal to as many d6 as the Forlorn's CR** and is **knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]**.  
On a success, it takes half damage and is not knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].

If **three or more** Forlorn are destroyed this way, the Mucklord regains **50 hit points** and gains **temporary hit points equal to 10 × the forlorn's CR** for every forlorn destroyed.

</Action>

<Action deed="lair">

#### Mass Rusting

The brine surrounding the Mucklord rises and begins to seethe with Nullbrand’s resonance.  
All creatures **standing on brine** or within **[= 12 stride =]** of the Mucklord that are touching the ground gain **one stack of [# kw:Erosion #]** .

If a creature already has **[# kw:Erosion #]**, regardless of whether it is on brine or not it saves Constitution against DC 24 or gains **another stack** instead.  
Objects and weapons made of metal in contact with the ground take [% 10d10 force %], ignoring resistance.

> Everything fades, eventually.

</Action>

<Action deed="lair">

#### Temper Tantrum

When wounded or ignored, the Mucklord lets out a miserable, childlike wail that shakes the entire field.  
Shutes around the chamber release offerings to appease it — the **rotting corpses** of failed warriors and discarded equipment.

Roll [% 1d20 %] to determine the tantrum’s intensity.

- The number rolled is the number of **corpses ([% 1d20 %])** that **fall into the arena**, landing in random unoccupied spaces within the lair of the Mucklord.
- The **DC** for the resulting shockwave is **35 – the number rolled** (minimum 15, maximum 34).
- The Mucklord then emits a thunderous cry, or a joyful screech. All creatures of its choice within **[= 60 stride =]** save Constitution against DC result.
  - **Fail**: The creature is **[# kw:condition:deafened #] and [# kw:condition:blinded #]** [# kw:briefly #] and takes **(DC)d4 bludgeoning damage**.
  - **Success**: No damage or condition.

The corpses remain where they fall and can be consumed by the Mucklord as part of its **Garbage Communion** feature.  
They are inert but reanimate if struck by brine or acid during subsequent rounds.

> The tantrum passes quickly. The corpses stay forever.

</Action>

#### Stratagem

<Action deed="stratagem" cost="1 Deed">

#### The Pit Remembers

_Declared at the end of the Mucklord’s turn; resolves at the start of its next turn. Recharges after combat._

The mucklord attempts to consume a **[= 24 stride;ADJ =] cone** in front of it. Creatures in the area save Strength against DC 25 or **sink [= 2 stride =]** and become **[# kw:condition:restrained #]**.  
At resolution, all **[# kw:condition:restrained #]** creatures are devoured and **banished** to the **Muck Dimension** (see [_Nullbrand_](/en/library/items/heirlooms/nullbrand)) for **1 minute**, taking [% 6d10 force %] at the start of each of their turns there.  
If the Mucklord dies while this effect is active, the breach collapses, dealing 150 ([% 20d12 force %]) to all creatures within **[= 24 stride =]**

</Action>

<Action deed="stratagem" cost="1 Deed">

#### Gluttony and Hatred

_Declared at the end of the Mucklord’s turn; resolves at the start of its next turn. Recharges after combat._
At declaration, the Mucklord **devours itself**, consuming its own mass. Its body vanishes entirely at the **end of its turn**.
Until resolution, a **spectral afterimage** of the Mucklord is visible, trudging in a **straight line** along the path it will take through the **Muck Dimension**. This path is obvious, slow, and uninterrupted; it cannot turn, stop, or change direction once declared.
The afterimage ignores terrain, obstacles, and creatures.
At the start of its next turn, the Mucklord **re-emerges violently** at any point along that line, detonating outward in a rupture of brine and compressed mass.

- **Re-emergence Area**: [= 8 stride;ADJ =] radius sphere centered on the emergence point.
- **Saving Throw**: DC **+25 Strength**.

On a **failure**, a creature takes 88 ([% 16d10 bludgeoning %]) and 44 ([% 8d10 chemical %]), is **pushed [= 6 stride =]** directly away from the center, and is **knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]**.  
On a **success**, the creature takes half damage, is pushed **[= 2 stride =]**, and is not knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].

If the Mucklord emerges in a space occupied by one or more creatures, those creatures **automatically fail** the saving throw, take the full damage, and are forcibly displaced to the nearest unoccupied spaces of the Mucklord’s choice.
Creatures reduced to **0 hit points** by this detonation are **absorbed** along with everything they were carrying, the Mucklord gains **20 temporary hit points per creature** absorbed this way.
The Mucklord is considered **absent from the battlefield** between disappearance and re-emergence. It cannot be targeted, damaged, or affected during this time.

</Action>

- **Brine Rattle.** (Costs 3 Deeds) The Mucklord releases a mist of poisonous brine. Creatures within [= 8 stride =] of the Mucklord save Dexterity against DC 24 or take 54 ([% 12d8 poison %]) and gain a level of exhaustion. Creatures take half as much damage on a success and do not gain exhaustion.
