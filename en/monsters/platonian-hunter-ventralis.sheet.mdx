## Infection Mechanic

**Ventralis Infection.**  
Any creature hit by a melee weapon attack saves Constitution against DC 15 or becomes **infected** with a latent fungal curse.
If the creature is already infected by any of the [# kw:Black Rot #] effects, they instantly roll on the Table again.

While infected:

- The creature has **disadvantage on death saving throws**.
- If the creature dies, it **rises as a fungal zombie** in 1 minute (use Ghoul or Tombsteel Husk stat block).
- Magical healing restores HP normally, but does **not** remove the infection.
- [_Remove Affliction_](/en/library/spells/remove-affliction), [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration), or [_Wish_](/en/library/spells/wish) can cure it.
- \*After a full day infected, the character must roll on the [# kw:Black Rot #] Table.

> The infection is magical and demonic in origin. It cannot be cured by mundane means.
