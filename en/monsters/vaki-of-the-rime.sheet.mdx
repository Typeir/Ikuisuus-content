## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chosen Sight

The Väki is perceived only by those it chooses. To every other creature it is **[# kw:condition:invisible #]** and makes no sound. Choosing, or revoking costs it nothing.
The Väki may not interact with creatures that cannot perceive it beyond the natural effects of it's existence, cold is still cold whether or not the anima within it can be seen.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Splinter Is the Whole

A creature that reduces it to 0 hit points is known to every Väki of Cold, and has disadvantage on every check to parlay with Cold until the Idea tires of the grudge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Felid Guise

As a **Minor Action**, the Väki takes the shape of an ordinary house feline, or sheds it. Its statistics are unchanged save its size, which becomes Tiny.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Where Warmth Leaves

Mundane flames within [= 3 stride =] of the Väki gutter out. Each creature that starts its turn within [= 3 stride =] takes 7 ([% 2d6 frost %]) and saves CON against DC 16 or is **[# kw:condition:slowed #]** [# kw:briefly #].

</Trait>
