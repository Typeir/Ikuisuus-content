## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chosen Sight

The Väki is perceived only by those it chooses. To every other creature it is **[# kw:condition:invisible #]** and makes no sound. Choosing, or unchoosing, costs it nothing.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Splinter Is the Whole

The Väki is Stone, and Stone remembers. A creature that reduces it to 0 hit points is known to every Väki of Stone, and has disadvantage on every check to parlay with Stone until the Idea tires of the grudge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Felid Guise

As a **Minor Action**, the Väki takes the shape of an ordinary house feline, or sheds it. Its statistics are unchanged save its size, which becomes Tiny.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Turning

A creature **[# kw:condition:grappled #]** by the Väki has its speed reduced to 0. At the start of each of its turns it takes 3 ([% 1d6 bludgeoning %]), and the damage grows by [% 1d6 %] for each consecutive turn it stays [# kw:condition:grappled #], to a maximum of [% 4d6 %].

</Trait>
