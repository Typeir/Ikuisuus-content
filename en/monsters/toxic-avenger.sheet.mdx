## Traits

<Trait>

#### Legendary Deeds

The Avenger has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn.

**Pipe Network**.

Instead of walking, the Avenger inhabits and manipulates its pipe system.
**Once per round**, it may instantly **shift** to any pipe outlet or _burrow_ it has placed within **[= 60 stride =]**.
This does not provoke opportunity attacks.

> - **Vulnerabilities**: **Force**
> - **Immunities**: **Poison**, **Psychic**, **Dark**, **Holy**

**Burrow** (Pipe Placement).

_As a Major Action_, the Avenger extrudes a new pipe segment up to **[= 12 stride =]** long through stone, earth, or metal, creating a new outlet for **Pipe Network**.
Outside a sewer/lair, the pipe remains as a slime-filled tunnel large enough for a Medium creature to crawl through (difficult terrain).

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Reforming body

The avenger regenerates 40 hit points at the start of it's round.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Corrosive Bulk

Creatures that hit the Avenger with a melee attack while within [= 3 stride =] take **9 ([% 2d8 chemical %])**.
Weapons that strike it break after **three** hits, gaining a **cummulative -1 modifier to attack and damage rolls** each hit until breakage.  
If struck by equipment, the attacker gains **one stack of [# kw:Erosion #]**.

**Toxic Domain** ([= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius around pipes).

The ground in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius around any active pipe outlet is slick and is difficult terrain.
As an [# kw:delayed #] effect, creatures there save Dexterity against DC 18 or **fall [# kw:condition:prone #]**.

</Trait>

## Actions

<Action>

#### Multiattack

The Avenger makes **two** attacks with its **Spine Sickle** or **Acid Tendrils**.

<Attack accuracy="+10" reach="[= 3 stride =]">

##### Spine Sickle

On a hit, 26 ([% 4d10 +4 slashing %]) and **10 ([% 3d6 chemical %])**. The target is **knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]** and is affected by the effects of the [_Slow_](/en/library/spells/slow) spell [# kw:briefly #].  
If this attack **reduces a creature to 0 hit points**, the Spine Sickle draws and consumes that creature's spine, killing them instantly.  
The creature **dies instantly**.  
After consuming a spine this way, the Spine Sickle's reach **permanently increases by [= 1 stride =]** This effect has no upper limit.

</Attack>

<Attack accuracy="+8" reach="[= 6 stride =]">

##### Acid Tendrils

On a hit, 21 ([% 3d10 +4 chemical %]).  
The target saves Strength against DC 18 or is [# kw:condition:grappled #], forcing its movement within the Avenger's range.  
If a creature is already [# kw:condition:grappled #], the Avenger restrains them instead. Only one creature at a time may be [# kw:condition:restrained #] this way.

</Attack>

</Action>

<Action>

#### Deathcall (Recharge 6).

The Avenger raises its own severed head as it lets out a pressurized scream of acid vapor and psychic noise.  
Each creature within **[= 12 stride =]** that can hear it saves Wisdom against DC 18.  
On a failed save, the creature takes **27 ([% 6d8 psychic %])** and is **[# kw:condition:terrified #]** [# kw:briefly #].  
If the creature was already [# kw:condition:terrified #], it additionally takes **48** chemical damage.  
On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and is not [# kw:condition:terrified #].  
If **Toxic Surge** is available, it immediately activates.

</Action>

## Toxic Surge (Recharge 3–6).##

A pressure blast erupts through all connected outlets.  
Each creature within **[= 3 stride =]** of any active pipe saves Constitution against DC 18, taking **31 ([% 7d8 chemical %])** and becoming **[# kw:condition:blinded #]** until the start of the Avenger's next turn on a failure, or half as much on a success.

## Reactions

- **Pipe Escape.**  
  When hit by an attack or targeted by a spell it can perceive, the Avenger uses **Pipe Network** to shift to another outlet within **[= 60 stride =]**.
  If used after the attack roll is made, the triggering attack is made at **disadvantage**.

---

## Legendary Deed: Lair

At the start of the creature with the highest initiative's turn, the Avenger can expend a legendary deed to use one of the following lair deeds. It cannot use the same lair deed two rounds in a row.

**Rising Pressure**

A random outlet bursts, creating a **[= 2 stride;ADJ =]** radius geyser of corrosive sludge.
Each creature in the area saves Dexterity against DC 18 or takes **31 ([% 7d8 bludgeoning %])** and is **pushed [= 2 stride =]**.

**Backflow**

As part of this action, the Avenger reverses sewage currents and immediately **relocates to a pipe within [= 24 stride =]**, even if it has already shifted using **Pipe Network** this round.
