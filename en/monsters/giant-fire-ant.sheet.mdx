## Actions

#### Multiattack

Wildfire makes three attacks: either three **Scorching Ray** attacks or three **Scorching Fangs** attacks.

- **Scorching Ray.** Accuracy +9, range [= 24 stride =], one creature. On a hit, 22 ([% 4d10 fire %]).
- **Scorching Fangs.** Accuracy +9, reach [= 1 stride =], one creature. On a hit, 11 ([% 2d10 fire %]) plus 11 ([% 2d10 chemical %]). The target saves Constitution against DC 15 or is **[# kw:condition:paralyzed #] [# kw:briefly #]**.
- **Oviposition (Recharge 6).** If a creature is **[# kw:condition:paralyzed #]**, Wildfire may implant a **burning egg**. The creature gains the **Incubating Inferno** condition (see below).
- **Pyroblast (1/[# kw:Recovery #]).** Wildfire casts [Pyroblast](/en/library/spells/pyroblast) in a [= 4 stride =] radius sphere. Each creature in the area saves Dexterity against DC 15, halving **28 ([% 8d6 fire %])**.

## Condition: Incubating Inferno

A creature affected by _Incubating Inferno_ suffers the following:

- Cannot reduce levels of **exhaustion** by any means
- Gains **vulnerability to fire damage**
- Gains **resistance to frost damage**
- The condition does **not deal damage over time**
- At the end of the creature’s next [# kw:Repose #], the egg **hatches**:
  - The creature takes **22 ([% 4d10 fire %])**
  - Then saves Constitution against DC 15
    - On failure: the creature is **[# kw:condition:Stunned #] for 1 minute** (save at end of each turn to end)  
      Only **Restoration**, **Heal**, or **Wish** can remove the condition before hatching.
