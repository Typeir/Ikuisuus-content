## Traits

<Trait>

#### Puppet Master

The Duke can use his action to manipulate a creature that he can see within [= 12 stride =], forcing it to make a **Wisdom saving throw (DC 19)**. On a failed save, the creature must use its reaction to move up to half its speed and make one melee attack against a target of the Duke's choice.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Regeneration

The Duke regains 15 hit points at the start of his turn. If they take holy damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of his next turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Master Manipulator

The Duke has advantage on Charisma checks and saving throws.

</Trait>
