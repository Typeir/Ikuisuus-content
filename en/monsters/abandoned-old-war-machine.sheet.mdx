## Traits

#### Legendary Deeds

The Abandoned Old War Machine has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn.

#### Null Core

When reduced to 0 hit points, the War Machine erupts in a [= 60 stride;ADJ =]-radius nullwave (blocked by solid terrain).  
All ongoing spells or magical effects in the area end instantly.  
After a three turn delay, the war machine explodes.
Each creature within range saves Dexterity against DC 23, taking **180 ([% 40d8 force %])** and becoming **[# kw:condition:blinded #]** [# kw:briefly #] on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.

#### Acid Overflow

When the War Machine takes damage, chemical leaks from its seams.  
All creatures within [= 1 stride =] of the machine take **8 ([% 2d8 chemical %])** and each saves Dexterity against DC 23 or gains **1 stack of [# kw:Erosion #]**.

#### Rust Breaker

The War Machine scores a critical hit on a roll of 20; this threshold is lowered by 1 for each stack of **[# kw:Erosion #]** on the target.

#### Construct Nature

The War Machine does not require air, food, drink, or sleep.

## Legendary Deed: Act

The Abandoned Old War Machine can expend legendary deeds to use the options below. Only one legendary deed can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

- **Pseudopod Lash.** (Costs 1 Deed) The War Machine makes one **Pseudopod Slam** attack.
- **Null Spark.** (Costs 2 Deeds) The War Machine emits a brief nullwave; all ongoing magical effects on creatures within [= 3 stride =] are suppressed until the start of its next turn.
