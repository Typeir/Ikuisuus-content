## Deeds

The Golden Pig has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn.

The Golden Pig can expend legendary deeds to use the options below, each taken with different timing, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).

#### Lair

At the start of the creature with the highest initiative's turn, the Golden Pig can expend a legendary deed to use one of the following lair deeds. It cannot use the same lair deed two rounds in a row.

<Action deed="lair">

#### Burrowed Angels

Angelic eggs sprout from the ground in a [= 4 stride;ADJ =] radius centered on a point within the pig's lair. Each creature in that area saves Dexterity against DC 18 or takes 14 ([% 4d6 holy %]) and becomes [# kw:condition:blinded #] [# kw:briefly #]. The eggs remain for 1 minute, providing difficult terrain.

</Action>

<Action deed="lair">

#### Winged Mockery

The pig sprouts ethereal wings for 1 round, granting it a flying speed of [= 12 stride =]. During this time, it can make one attack with its beak, dealing 21 ([% 6d6 psychic %]) on a hit. Additionally, each creature within [= 6 stride =] of the pig saves Wisdom against DC 18 or is [# kw:condition:terrified #] [# kw:briefly #].

</Action>

#### Act

Act deeds are taken at the end of another creature’s turn, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).
- **Cast a Cantrip.** (Costs 1 Deed) The pig casts a cantrip.
- **Mocking Laughter.** (Costs 1 Deed) The pig emits a mocking laugh. Each creature of its choice within [= 4 stride =] saves Wisdom against DC 18 or takes 10 ([% 3d6 psychic %]) and have disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.
- **Crown of Thorns (Costs 2 Deeds).** _Range: [= 24 stride =], Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute._ One humanoid of the pig's choice that the pig can see within range saves Wisdom or becomes [# kw:condition:charmed #] by the pig for the duration. While the target is [# kw:condition:charmed #] in this way, a twisted crown of jagged iron appears on its head, and a madness glows in its eyes. The [# kw:condition:charmed #] target must use a Major Action before moving on each of its turns to make a melee attack against a creature other than itself that the pig mentally chose. If there are no creatures, the target takes 8 ([% 2d6 psychic %]) and can act normally.
- **Degrading Whispers (Costs 3 Deeds).** The pig speaks degrading words that penetrate the mind of a creature. A creature of the pig's choice within [= 6 stride =] saves Wisdom against DC 18. On a failed save, the creature is [# kw:condition:stunned #] and [# kw:condition:prone #] [# kw:briefly #].
