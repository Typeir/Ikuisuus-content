## Traits

<Trait>

#### Legendary Deeds

Heart of the Brume has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of his turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Entropic Sarcophagus

Heart of the Brume is encased in an extremely heavy armor.

- Heart of the Brume has disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks
- Heart of the Brume has advantage on saving throws made to resist being pushed, pulled, or knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]
- Heart of the Brume cannot benefit from bonuses to movement speed granted by mundane effects

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Thorns of penitence

Heart of the Brume’s thorny armor holds up to 12 charges.  
When a creature hits Heart of the Brume with a melee weapon attack, the attacker immediately takes 15 piercing damage as metallic shards erupt from the armor. This effect requires no action or reaction.

- Each activation consumes 1 charge
- Charges regenerate at midnight, recovering [% 1d6 +6 %] charges

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Ferocious Rider

The Heart of the Brume's mount, a [_Lunar Manticore_](/en/library/monsters/lunar-manticore), can take the attack action even when used as a mount by Heart of the Brume.
While mounted, the Heart of the Brume's mount gains a +7 accuracy and damage bonus, and resistance to all damage.

While mounted, Heart of the Brume gains the following benefits:

- Cannot be critically hit
- Automatically succeeds on the first saving throw it makes each round

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Doctrine of the Brume

Heart of the Brume projects a disciplined doctrinal aura in a [= 4 stride;ADJ =] radius while conscious.

For each other rider of the Brume within range, all riders gain the following benefits (bonuses stack):

- +3 bonus to all saving throws
- +2 bonus to Armor Class

**Formation Thresholds**

- Three active auras:
  - Resistance to all damage
  - Immunity to forced movement
  - Cannot be knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]
  - Riders and Mounts automatically Disengage at the start of their turns.

- Two active auras:
  - Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, fire, frost, lightning, and dark damage
  - Advantage on saving throws against being pushed, pulled, or knocked [# kw:condition:prone #]

- One or fewer active auras:
  - No resistances or movement immunities from this feature

A rider’s aura is suppressed only while that rider is [# kw:condition:incapacitated #].  
[# kw:condition:Paralyzed #], [# kw:condition:stunned #], or [# kw:condition:unconscious #] creatures do not contribute to the doctrine.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Central Dogma

At the start of each of Heart of the Brume’s turns, each allied rider of the Brume within Doctrine range regains 30 hit points.

- This effect does not stack with itself
- This is the only passive healing effect available to the trio

</Trait>

## Deeds

Heart of the Brume has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of his turn. Heart of the Brume can expend legendary deeds to use the options below, each taken with different timing, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).

#### Stratagem

<Action deed="stratagem" cost="1 Deed">

#### Empire Breaker

The Heart of the Brume slams its sword-spear into the ground, creating a shockwave that ripples through the battlefield.
All creatures within a [= 10 stride;ADJ =] radius centered on Heart of the Brume save Strength against DC 23.
On a failure, a creature takes 27 ([% 6d8 bludgeoning %]) and is knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].
On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].
On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not pushed.
At the start of its next turn, Heart of the Brume pulls the spear out of the ground, creating a secondary shockwave.
All creatures within a [= 10 stride;ADJ =] radius centered on Heart of the Brume save Strength against DC 23.
On a failure, a creature takes 27 ([% 6d8 bludgeoning %]) and is swallowed by the earth, becoming [# kw:condition:incapacitated #] until freed.
In order to free itself, the creature must use a Major Action to make a DC 36 Athletics check, or another creature must do it for them.
While [# kw:condition:incapacitated #] this way, the creatures is [# kw:condition:Suffocating #] and takes 27 ([% 6d8 bludgeoning %]) at the start of each of its turns.
On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not swallowed.
[# kw:condition:Prone #] creatures automatically fail the saving throw against being swallowed.

</Action>

#### Act

Act deeds are taken at the end of another creature’s turn, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).
- **Coordinated Strike.** (Costs 1 Deed)
  Heart of the Brume makes one melee weapon attack with its Grand Empyrean Sword-Spear.
- **Relentless Advance.** (Costs 1 Deed)  
  Heart of the Brume moves up to **[= 4 stride =]** without provoking opportunity attacks.  
  This movement cannot benefit from bonuses to speed.
- **Structural Reinforcement (Costs 2 Deeds).**  
  Heart of the Brume or one allied rider of the Brume within **[= 4 stride =]** gains **20 temporary hit points** and advantage on the next saving throw it makes before the end of its next turn.
- **Mount.** (Costs 1 Deed)  
  Heart of the Brume mounts its creature if it is within [= 1 stride =] and willing.
- **Dismount.** (Costs 1 Deed)  
  Heart of the Brume dismounts its creature and may move up to **[= 2 stride =]** immediately after.
