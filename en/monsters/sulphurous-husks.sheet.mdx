# Husks of Xanthosis

These creatures are old empyrean and human inhabitants of the original city of the brume, buried in sulphur, fire and xanthosis when their god-patron Xanthous destroyed his own empire in a mad fit of rage and self sabotage.

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Diseased Body

Any creature hit by the husk's sword saves Constitution against DC 10 or is infected with a minor disease. After 1 hour, the infected creature gains disadvantage on Constitution saving throws and skill checks for 24 hours. The disease can be cured with _Restoration_ or after 24 hours without a failed saving throw.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Undead Fortitude

If damage reduces the husk to 0 hit points, it saves Constitution with a DC equal to the damage taken, unless the damage is holy or from a critical hit. On a success, the husk drops to 1 hit point instead.

</Trait>
