## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chosen Sight

The Väki is perceived only by those it chooses. To every other creature it is **[# kw:condition:invisible #]** and makes no sound. Choosing, or unchoosing, costs it nothing.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Splinter Is the Whole

The Väki is Flame, and Flame remembers. A creature that reduces it to 0 hit points is known to every Väki of Flame, and has disadvantage on every check to parlay with Flame until the Idea tires of the grudge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Felid Guise

As a **Minor Action**, the Väki takes the shape of an ordinary house feline, or sheds it. Its statistics are unchanged save its size, which becomes Tiny.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Catching

The Väki can move through the spaces of other creatures. The first time it enters a creature's space on a turn, that creature saves DEX against DC 12 or becomes **[# kw:condition:burning #]**. Flammable objects the Väki touches ignite.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Fed

Whenever a creature within [= 6 stride =] of the Väki becomes [# kw:condition:burning #], the Väki regains 5 hit points.

</Trait>
