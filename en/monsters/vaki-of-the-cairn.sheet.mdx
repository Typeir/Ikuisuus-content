## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chosen Sight

The Väki is perceived only by those it chooses. To every other creature it is **[# kw:condition:invisible #]** and makes no sound. Choosing, or revoking costs it nothing. 
The Väki may not interact with creatures that cannot perceive it beyond the natural effects of it's existence, a rock is still a rock whether or not the anima within it can be seen.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Splinter Is the Whole

A creature that reduces it to 0 hit points is known to every Väki of Stone, and has disadvantage on every check to parlay with Stone until the Idea tires of the grudge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Felid Guise

As a **Minor Action**, the Väki takes the shape of an ordinary house feline, or sheds it. It becomes Tiny, but is otherwise unchanged.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Unmoved

The Väki cannot be [# kw:displace #]d.

</Trait>
