## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chosen Sight

The Väki is perceived only by those it chooses. To every other creature it is **[# kw:condition:invisible #]** and makes no sound. Choosing, or revoking costs it nothing.
The Väki may not interact with creatures that cannot perceive it beyond the natural effects of it's existence, a flame is still a flame whether or not the anima within it can be seen.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Splinter Is the Whole

A creature that reduces it to 0 hit points is known to every Väki of Flame, and has disadvantage on every check to parlay with Flame until the Idea tires of the grudge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Felid Guise

As a **Minor Action**, the Väki takes the shape of an ordinary house feline, or sheds it. Its statistics are unchanged save its size, which becomes Tiny.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Kept Warmth

Creatures of the Väki's choice within [= 2 stride =] of it have resistance to frost damage, and advantage on saves against [# kw:condition:exhaustion #] from cold.

</Trait>
