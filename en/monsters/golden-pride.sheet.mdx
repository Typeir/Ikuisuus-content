## Traits

#### Aura of Splendor

The Golden Pride emanates an aura of radiant authority in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius. Creatures of the pride's choice within the aura gain a **+1 bonus to saving throws**. This bonus stacks with the auras of other Golden Prides (maximum +5).

#### Unshakable

The first time the Golden Pride fails a saving throw, it succeeds instead. Once per [# kw:Recovery #].

_Range: [= 24 stride =], Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute._ One humanoid of the pride's choice that the pride can see within range saves Charisma against DC 17 or becomes **[# kw:condition:charmed #]** by the pride for the duration. While the target is [# kw:condition:charmed #] in this way, a burning halo of golden light appears above its head. The [# kw:condition:charmed #] target must use a Major Action before moving on each of its turns to make a melee attack against a creature other than itself that the pride mentally chose. If there are no creatures, the target takes 8 ([% 2d6 holy %]) and can act normally. The target can [# kw:resist #] at the end of each of its turns.

#### Overwhelming Presence (1/recovery)
