## Traits

<Trait>

#### Legendary Deeds

The Steel Guard has **1 legendary deed per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Iron Carapace

When attacked from behind (Game Director’s discretion, flanking, or any attack that would target its back), the Steel Guard’s AC becomes **35**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Superiority Dice

The Steel Guard has **5 superiority dice (d10s)**. It regains all expended dice when it rolls initiative.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Martial Maneuvers

The Steel Guard knows the following maneuvers:

- **Grappling Strike.** After it hits a creature with a melee attack, it can expend one superiority die and attempt to grapple as a Minor Action, adding the die to the Athletics check.
- **Lunging Attack.** It can expend one superiority die to extend its reach by [= 1 stride =]. On hit, the attack gains a damage bonus equal to the die.
- **Trip Attack.** When it hits with a melee attack, it can expend one superiority die to force a Strength save against DC 18. On failure, the target is knocked [# kw:condition:prone #] and takes the superiority die’s damage.
- **[# kw:brace #].** When a creature enters its reach, it can spend a superiority die to make one weapon attack against that creature as a reaction. On a hit, add the superiority die to damage.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Crushing Frame

The Steel Guard counts as one size larger for grappling and shoving.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Siege Weaponry

Its weapons deal double damage to objects and structures.

</Trait>

##### Giant Stake

Creatures hit by the stake save Strength against DC 16 or are **pinned** to a surface and knocked [# kw:condition:prone #] (the Steel Guard can only have one pinned creature at a time). A pinned creature can use a Major Action to make a DC 16 Strength check, freeing itself on a success. The Steel Guard can use its Minor Action to free the pinned creature, reclaiming its stake in the process.

</Attack>

The Steel Guard has **1 legendary deed per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn. It can expend deeds to use the options below, each taken with different timing, as described in [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds).

<Feature deed="act" cost="1 Deed">

#### Bloodmoon Drill

The Steel Guard makes a single weapon attack, or moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

</Feature>
