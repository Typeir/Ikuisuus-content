## Traits

<Trait>

#### Iron Carapace

When attacked from behind (Game Director’s discretion, flanking, or any attack that would target its back), the Steel Guard’s AC becomes **35**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Superiority Dice

The Steel Guard has **5 superiority dice (d10s)**. It regains all expended dice when it rolls initiative.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Martial Maneuvers

The Steel Guard knows the following maneuvers:

- **Grappling Strike.** After it hits a creature with a melee attack, it can expend one superiority die and attempt to grapple as a Minor Action, adding the die to the Athletics check.
- **Lunging Attack.** It can expend one superiority die to extend its reach by [= 1 stride =]. On hit, the attack gains a damage bonus equal to the die.
- **Trip Attack.** When it hits with a melee attack, it can expend one superiority die to force a Strength save against DC 18. On failure, the target is knocked [# kw:condition:prone #] and takes the superiority die’s damage.
- **Brace.** When a creature enters its reach, it can spend a superiority die to make one weapon attack against that creature as a reaction. On a hit, add the superiority die to damage.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Crushing Frame

The Steel Guard counts as one size larger for grappling and shoving.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Siege Weaponry

Its weapons deal double damage to objects and structures.

</Trait>

## Actions

<Action>

#### Multiattack

The Steel Guard makes two attacks with its weapon.

<Attack accuracy="+10" reach="[= 2 stride =]">

##### Giant Stake

On a hit, 20 ([% 4d6 +6 piercing %]). Creatures hit by the stake save Strength against DC 16 or are **pinned** to a surface and knocked [# kw:condition:prone #] (the Steel Guard can only have one pinned creature at a time). A pinned creature can use a Major Action to make a DC 16 Strength check, freeing itself on a success. The Steel Guard can use its Minor Action to free the pinned creature, reclaiming its stake in the process.

</Attack>

<Attack accuracy="+10" reach="[= 1 stride =]">

##### Pummel

On a hit, 14 ([% 3d4 +6 bludgeoning %]).

</Attack>

</Action>

<Action>

#### Giant Hammer

<Attack accuracy="+10" reach="[= 2 stride =]">

On a hit, 32 ([% 4d12 +6 bludgeoning %]). When using the hammer, the Steel Guard cannot Multiattack. The hammer's attacks are cleaving arcs, dealing half damage to all creatures within [= 1 stride =] of the target. All creatures that take damage from the attack are pushed [= 2 stride =] away from the Steel Guard.

</Attack>

</Action>

## Reactions

<Action cost="1 Reaction">

#### Shielded Stance

When hit by an attack from the front, the Steel Guard may roll a d10. On an 8–10, it gains damage reduction against that attack equal to the number rolled + 6.

</Action>

## Legendary Deed (Shared with Ludwig)

If Ludwig is present, he may spend **1 legendary deed** at the end of another creature's turn to command one Steel Guard to make a single weapon attack or move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

</Monster>
