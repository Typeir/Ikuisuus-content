### Actions

<Action cost="1 Major Action">

#### Multiattack

The Iron Snail Warrior makes two attacks: one with the Giant Stake and one with the pommel of the Giant Stake. If using a hammer it cannot use multiattack.
- **Giant Stake**: [% 3d6 +4 piercing %], [% 1d4 +4 bludgeoning %] with pommel, accuracy +2
- **Giant Hammer**: [% 2d12 +4 bludgeoning %], cannot use pommel, accuracy +2

</Action>

<Action cost="1 Major Action">

#### Grappling Strike

Immediately after the Iron Snail Warrior hits a creature with a melee attack on its turn, it can expend one superiority die and then try to grapple the target as a Minor Action (see [Grapple](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/attack-alterations#grapple)). Add the superiority die to its Strength (Athletics) check.

</Action>

<Action cost="1 Major Action">

#### Lunging Attack

When the Iron Snail Warrior makes a melee weapon attack on its turn, it can expend one superiority die to increase its reach for that attack by [= 1 stride =]. If it hits, it adds the superiority die to the attack's damage roll.

</Action>
