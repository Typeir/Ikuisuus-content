## Traits

<Trait>

#### Curved-Blade Discipline
The militiaman treats curved weapons (shotel, khopesh, hooked halberd) or falchions, bows and daggers as **finesse weapons** and may **reroll 1s** on their damage rolls, **once per turn**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Shield Catch
When the militiaman hits a creature wielding a shield with a curved weapon, they may **immediately attempt to shove or disarm** the target as a **Minor Action**. The contest is **Athletics vs. Athletics**, and the target has **disadvantage** if the attempt targets their shield.

</Trait>

<Trait>
