## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chosen Sight

The Väki is perceived only by those it chooses. To every other creature it is **[# kw:condition:invisible #]** and makes no sound. Choosing, or revoking costs it nothing.
The Väki may not interact with creatures that cannot perceive it beyond the natural effects of it's existence, rot is still rot whether or not the anima within it can be seen.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Splinter Is the Whole

A creature that reduces it to 0 hit points is known to every Väki of Rot, and has disadvantage on every check to parlay with Rot until the Idea tires of the grudge.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Felid Guise

As a **Minor Action**, the Väki takes the shape of an ordinary house feline, or sheds it. Its statistics are unchanged save its size, which becomes Tiny.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### From Within

The Väki deals double damage to objects and structures of wood, bone, or leather.

</Trait>
