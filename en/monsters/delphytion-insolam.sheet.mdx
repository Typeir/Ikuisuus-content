# Delphytion Insolam

_Huge Monstrosity, Unaligned_

<BlendedImage
  src='/library/images/monsters/delphytion.webp'
  alt='Delphytion Insolam'
/>

A **Delphytion Insolam** is a chitinous solar monstrosity born from the festering corpse-light of the slain sun god **Päivätär**.
Its body—an anchor-headed insult to life itself, a mixture of fishlike, serpentine and horribly insectoid features, crowned by a great translucent orb filled with pale, sickening amber amniotic fluid.
Within this sun-womb writhe hundreds of larval **Sunspawn**: serpentine, shrimp-like offspring awaiting release.
The Delphytion’s needle-limbs float a full foot from the orb, held in place by an unseen repulsive field, and its long dolphin-tail sports spout-like vents that hiss superheated vapor.
When threatened, the orb’s glow intensifies to blinding brilliance as the creature lowers its core and lets its limbs whirl in a screaming cyclone of chitin and light.

## Traits

#### Legendary Deeds

The Delphytion Insolam has **1 legendary deed per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of its turn.

#### Solar Vessel

While not prone and not attacked from above [= 4 stride =], the Delphytion Insolam has **resistance to all damage** and cannot be knocked prone. It emits **bright light in a [= 12 stride;ADJ =] radius** and dim light for an additional [= 12 stride =].

#### Twilit Weakness

The Delphytion's core sits within the top [= 2 stride =] of its [= 6 stride;ADJ =]-tall frame. As an **excessively tall creature**, it always holds the [high ground](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/contextual-conflict#high-ground) against attackers of size Large or smaller — only a creature positioned above its upper boundary can overcome this (see **High Ground**).

It has the following vulnerabilities:

- While [_prone_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#prone), it loses resistance to all damage and becomes **vulnerable to all damage**.
- Attacks made against it from a position **[= 4 stride =] or more above ground level** bypass its resistance to all damage and deal damage as if it were [_prone_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#prone). This represents striking down into its exposed core — the one angle its towering frame cannot defend.

Whenever the Delphytion takes damage to which it is vulnerable, it reflexively births **[% 1d4 %] Sunspawn** (see **Sunspawn**) into unoccupied spaces within [= 2 stride =] of it. Sunspawn created this way act immediately after the Delphytion in initiative.

#### Spout Vents

The Delphytion has no conventional anatomy. Vents near its tail and anchor-head expel superheated vapor. At the start of each of its turns, each creature within [= 2 stride =] of it must succeed on a **DC 20 Constitution saving throw** or take **7 ([% 2d6 fire %])** and be [_blinded_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#blinded) until the start of its next turn.

## Minor Actions

#### Dusk Star

The Delphytion falls **prone** and enters its **dusk form**. While in dusk form:

- Its walking speed is **doubled** ([= 18 stride =]).
- Its limbs rotate in a screaming vortex with a radius of **[= 3 stride =]**; As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures must make a **DC 20 Dexterity saving throw**, being knocked [_prone_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#prone) and taking **22 ([% 4d10 piercing %])** on a failure, or half as much on a success.
- Its core is lowered to ground level and the **Solar Vessel** trait is suppressed (no damage resistances, no light emission).

The Delphytion can end dusk form as a Minor Action (see **Dawning Glory**).

## Actions

#### Multiattack

The Delphytion Insolam makes three attacks: two with its **Needle Limbs** and one with either its **Barbed Proboscis** or **Tail Sweep**.

- **Needle Limb.**  
  _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +12 to hit, reach [= 4 stride =], one target.  
  **Hit**: 18 ([% 2d10 +7 piercing %]). If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a **DC 20 Strength saving throw** or be [_grappled_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#grappled). The Delphytion can grapple up to two creatures this way.

- **Barbed Proboscis (Recharge 5–6).**  
  _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +12 to hit, reach [= 3 stride =], one creature **grappled** by the Delphytion.  
  **Hit**: 27 ([% 4d10 +7 piercing %]).
  The target must succeed on a **DC 20 Constitution saving throw** or be implanted with a **Sunspawn embryo**.
  At the start of each of the implanted creature’s turns, it takes **11 ([% 2d10 poison %])** as the embryo feeds.
  The embryo dies if the host receives **20 or more points of holy damage** or if any effect cures disease.
  After **3 rounds**, the embryo erupts: the host takes **33 ([% 6d10 piercing %])** and a fully formed **Sunspawn** appears in an unoccupied space within [= 1 stride =] of it.

- **Tail Sweep.**  
  _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +12 to hit, reach [= 3 stride =], cone (all creatures in a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone).  
  **Hit**: 16 ([% 2d8 +7 bludgeoning %]). Each Large or smaller creature hit must succeed on a **DC 20 Strength saving throw** or be pushed **[= 3 stride =]** away and knocked [_prone_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#prone).

## Reactions

**Vent Discharge.**  
_When the Delphytion takes damage from a melee attack,_  
The Delphytion releases a burst of superheated vapor from its spout vents. The attacker must succeed on a **DC 20 Dexterity saving throw** or take **14 ([% 4d6 fire %])** and be [_blinded_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#blinded) until the end of its next turn.

## Legendary Deed: Act

The Delphytion Insolam can expend legendary deeds to use the options below. Only one legendary deed can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature’s turn.

#### Engender Sunspawn (Costs 1 Deed)

The Delphytion regurgitates a larval Sunspawn and hurls it as a projectile at a creature within **[= 12 stride =]** that it can sense.

_Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +10 to hit, one target.  
**Hit**: 11 ([% 2d6 +4 chemical %]), and a **Sunspawn** appears in an unoccupied space adjacent to the target. The Sunspawn acts immediately after the Delphytion in initiative.

#### Dawning Glory (Costs 1 Deed)

The Delphytion ends its **dusk form**, stands up (removing the [_prone_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#prone) condition), and its **Solar Vessel** trait reactivates. When it rises, amniotic fluid splashes from its orb in a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] radius. Each creature in that area must make a **DC 20 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **18 ([% 4d8 holy %])** on a failure, or half as much on a success.
The fluid lingers as a **radiant pool** until the end of the Delphytion’s next turn; As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), it deals **9 ([% 2d8 holy %])**.
