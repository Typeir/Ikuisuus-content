## Traits

<Trait>

#### Bloodgift of the Moon

At the start of each of its turns, the warrior gains **10 temporary hit points**. If it begins its turn with no temporary hit points, it may teleport up to [= 2 stride =] to an unoccupied space it can see.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Moon Step Technique

If the Moonblessed Warrior forgoes all movement on its turn, it regains **10 hit points** and gains **+1 to AC, saving throws, and skill checks** until the start of its next turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Innate Spellcasting

The Moonblessed Warrior can use its Alfanjón as a spellcasting focus and casts the following spells innately (save DC 15):

- At will: [_Lesser Mooncleave_](/en/library/spells/lesser-mooncleave)- 1/Repose: Mooncleave (weapon feature, [= 6 stride =] cone, DC 16)

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Lunar Dissolution

As a Minor Action, the Alfanjón can vanish or reappear in its hand in a shimmer of silver mist.

</Trait>
