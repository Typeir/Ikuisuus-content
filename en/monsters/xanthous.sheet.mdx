## Traits

<Trait>

#### Legendary Deeds

Xanthous has **3 legendary deeds per round**, and regains all expended deeds at the start of his turn.

</Trait>

<Trait charges="3/Repose">

#### Legendary Deed: Resist

When Xanthous fails a saving throw, he can expend a legendary deed to succeed instead. This feature can be used **3 times per [# kw:Repose #]**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Poison of the Mind

Xanthous's very presence causes the mind to unravel and disassemble itself. If a creature interacts with Xanthous in any way, such as speaking, touching, or being struck by him in any way, they save Wisdom against DC 23 or are affected by [# kw:Poison of the Mind #]. Characters immune to sickness and curses ignore this feature, and take [% 6d6 psychic %] instead.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Twin Reactions

Xanthous can take up to **2 reactions per round**, but no more than one per turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Angels

Xanthous can summon and control up to four angels, each taking their turn directly after his own.
Each angel shares Xanthous's hit point pool, and when an angel takes damage, Xanthous takes equally as much "indirect" damage.
When Xanthous is reduced to 0 hit points, all angels immediately perish.
All angels are tethered to Xanthous by a [= 12 stride =] long, sulphurous umbilical cord.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Dual Spellcasting

Xanthous is a 20th-level spellcaster. He has the following spells slots 1st (4), 2nd (3), 3rd (3), 4th (3), 5th (3), 6th (2), 7th (2), 8th (1), 9th (1).

Xanthous always casts spells as though he were under the effect of the [_Twinned Spell_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/scion/spell-sculpt) sculpting technique, targeting two creatures instead of one. if the spell imposes an area, Xanthous instead uses [_Heightened Spell_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/scion/spell-sculpt) on all affected creatures instead of one.
His spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 27, accuracy +19 with spell attacks). He has the following spells prepared:

- **Cantrips**: [_Envenom_](/en/library/spells/envenom), [_Minor Illusion_](/en/library/spells/minor-illusion), [_Ashen Anointment_](/en/library/spells/ashen-anointment)

- **1st level**: [_Argent Strands_](/en/library/spells/argent-strands), [_Shield_](/en/library/spells/shield)
- **2nd level**: [_Cystbolt_](/en/library/spells/cystbolt), [_Hold_](/en/library/spells/hold), [_Misty Step_](/en/library/spells/misty-step)
- **3rd level**: [_Fear_](/en/library/spells/fear), [_Stinking Cloud_](/en/library/spells/stinking-cloud), [_Sulfurous Sphere_](/en/library/spells/sulfurous-sphere)
- **4th level**: [_Shadow Of The Hunt_](/en/library/spells/shadow-of-the-hunt), [_Invisibility (overcast)_](/en/library/spells/invisibility), [_Phantasmal Force_](/en/library/spells/phantasmal-force)
- **5th level**: [_Cloudkill_](/en/library/spells/cloudkill), [_Dominate_](/en/library/spells/dominate), [_Enervation_](/en/library/spells/enervation), [_Forbidden Sun_](/en/library/spells/forbidden-sun)
- **6th level**: [_Psychic Crush_](/en/library/spells/psychic-crush), [_Sunbeam_](/en/library/spells/sunbeam), [_Eyebite_](/en/library/spells/eyebite), [_Disintegrate_](/en/library/spells/disintegrate)
- **7th level**: [_Invoke Agony_](/en/library/spells/invoke-agony)
- **8th level**: [_Mind Blank_](/en/library/spells/mind-blank)
- **9th level**: [_Foresight_](/en/library/spells/foresight), [_Bolide Bombardment_](/en/library/spells/bolide-bombardment)

- **Angelical Link**: Xanthous can summon angels with which he shares a health pool. Anytime an angel takes damage, Xanthous takes half as much "indirect" damage.

- **Unbreakable Mind**: If Xanthous isn't dealt at least 100 direct damage in a round, he regenerates all the damage he took, up to half his maximum hit points, including angelical link damage.

- **Weakened Form.** Xanthous is currently diminished.
  - He is vulnerable to all damage he is not resistant or immune to.
  - He cannot cast spells of **6th level or higher**.
  - His **Multiattack** is reduced to **three strikes** instead of five.
  - He suffers a **–7 penalty** to all saving throws, attack rolls, and skill checks.
  - His **AC is reduced by 7**.
  - His attacks deal damage only and do not impose additional effects (no saving throws for exhaustion, restraint, fear, etc.).
  - Xanthous begins combat with **no angels under his command**.
  - This condition lasts until the next day.

- **Scourge of the Mind**: Xanthous immediately knows when one is withholding information, as it understands when something has not reached the cusp of its being, even words, phrases, or truths, even if he does not know the truth itself. Additionally, Xanthous always knows each creature's vulnerabilities or least developed areas.

- **Aura of Devotion**: As an [# kw:delayed #] effect, a creature within [= 3 stride =] of Xanthous saves Wisdom against DC 25 or is affected by the [_Charm_](/en/library/spells/charm) spell [# kw:briefly #]. A creature can only be affected by this once every minute.

</Trait>

## Minor Actions

<Action cost="1 Minor Action">

#### Mind Wraps

As a Minor Action, Xanthous can grab (Athletics contest) a creature and grapple them. Xanthous can grab up to 4 creatures this way. Xanthous must succeed on Concentration checks to keep the grapples.

</Action>

## Legendary Deed: Lair

At the start of the creature with the highest initiative's turn, Xanthous can expend a legendary deed to use one of the following lair deeds. He cannot use the same lair deed two rounds in a row.

<Action deed="lair">

#### First Cross / Second Cross

Xanthous projects a 45-degree cross that's [= 3 stride =] wide and [= 40 stride =] long. The cross is made of egg-shaped **chemical** or **frost** bombs. These count as difficult terrain, and explode as a [# kw:persistent #] effect, forcing the intersecting creature to save Dexterity against DC 25 or take 21 ([% 6d6 %] damage) of the corresponding type and fall [# kw:condition:prone #].
Each cross counts as a separate deed.

</Action>

## Legendary Deed: Act

Xanthous can expend legendary deeds to use the options below. Only one legendary deed can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

<Action cost="1 Deed" deed="act">

#### Quick Reflexes

Xanthous regains two reactions.

</Action>

<Action cost="1 Deed" deed="act">

#### Awaken Angel

One of Xanthous's four angel-appendages drops from their shell, tethered to it by a [= 12 stride =] long cord. Angels count as invocations and take turns after Xanthous.

</Action>

<Action cost="1 Deed" deed="act">

#### Enact Angelical Will

As a legendary deed, an awakened angel takes a turn. During this turn, the angel has a +10 accuracy and damage bonus, and a +10 bonus to every saving throw and skill check.

</Action>

<Action cost="1 Deed" deed="act">

#### Loving Embrace of the Miracle

If a creature is directly below Xanthous, it saves Wisdom against DC 20, regardless if Xanthous is currently weakened or not. If the creature fails, Xanthous assumes total control of it [# kw:briefly #].

</Action>

<Action cost="2 Deeds" recharge="5-6" deed="act">

#### Pure Radiance

_Xanthous emits a pulse of psychic energy in a [= 12 stride;ADJ =] cone._

Each creature in that area saves Wisdom against DC 27.
On a failed save, a creature takes [% 4d10 psychic %], and [% 4d10 holy %] and is [# kw:condition:charmed #] by Xanthous for 1 minute.
While [# kw:condition:charmed #] in this way, the creature regards Xanthous as a trusted friend to be heeded and protected.
This effect does not end early if Xanthous or his allies harm the creature or their allies, as the creature believes they must have a reason for it.
The [# kw:condition:charmed #] target can [# kw:resist #] at the end of each of its turns.

On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and is not [# kw:condition:charmed #].

</Action>

</Monster>
