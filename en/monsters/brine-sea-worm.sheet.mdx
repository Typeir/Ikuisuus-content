## Traits

#### Brine Dweller
The worm can breathe both air and water.  
While fully submerged in saltwater or industrial brine, it regains **10 hit points** at the start of its turn.

#### Suffocating Coils
A creature **[# kw:condition:grappled #]** by the Brine Sea Worm begins **[# kw:condition:suffocating #]** at the start of its next turn as its lungs fill with pressurized brine.  
While [# kw:condition:suffocating #], the creature can’t speak or cast spells with verbal components.  
This effect ends if the grapple ends or the worm releases the target.  
A [# kw:condition:suffocating #] creature saves Constitution at the start of each of its turns, suffering **one failed death saving throw** on a failure.

#### Burrower’s Ambush
While underground or submerged, the worm has advantage on **Stealth** checks and can emerge up to [= 6 stride =] as part of a **Bite** attack.
