# Want of Frenzy

_Channel Abandon into Violent Fury._

Berserkers who walk the Want of Frenzy pour for violence and nothing else. Their path is one of untrammeled fury, and they thrill in the chaos of battle as they let their Abandon seize and empower them.

<Collapsible>
## 3rd Level – Frenzy
When you use **Reckless Attack** while **Reckless Abandon** is active, you can use your a **Minor Action** to make one **Weapon Attack**

If you use this feature, once your **Reckless Abandon** ends, you gain one level of **Exhaustion**, as your body recoils from the overexertion.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 6th Level – Mindless Abandon
While **Reckless Abandon** is active, you have **Immunity** to the [_Charmed_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#charmed) and [_Frightened_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#frightened) conditions.

- If you are [_Charmed_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#charmed) or [_Frightened_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#frightened) when you pour, the condition ends on you, and you gain **temporary hit** points equal to your **Pour** for every condition ended this way.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 10th Level – Endure Pain

While you are **Bloodied**, you gain an additional **action** each turn.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 14th Level – Intimidating Presence
As a **Minor Action**, you can strike terror into others with your menacing presence.

- Each creature of your choice in a **[= 6 stride;ADJ =] Aura** originating from you must make a **Wisdom saving throw** (DC = **8 + your Strength modifier + your Tier Bonus**).
- On a **failed save**, a creature gains the **Terrified condition** for **1 minute**, and you ignore the creature's **resistances** to damage.
- At the end of each of its turns, the creature may repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a **Recovery**, unless you spend **6 Abandon points** (no action required) to restore its use.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

## 17th level – Unfettered Brutality

Whenever you use your **_Brutal Strike_** feature, you may use **two effects** instead of one, and the extra damage dealt by them increases to [% 2d10 %].

</Collapsible>
