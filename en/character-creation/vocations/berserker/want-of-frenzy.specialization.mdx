# Want of Frenzy

<Specialization vocation="berserker">

_Channel Abandon into Violent Fury._

Berserkers who walk the Want of Frenzy pour for violence and nothing else. Their path is one of untrammeled fury, and they thrill in the chaos of battle as they let their Abandon seize and empower them.

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Frenzy
When you use **Reckless Attack** while **Reckless Abandon** is active, you can use your a **Minor Action** to make one **Weapon Attack**

If you use this feature, once your **Reckless Abandon** ends, you gain one level of **Exhaustion**, as your body recoils from the overexertion.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="6">

## Mindless Abandon
While **Reckless Abandon** is active, you have **Immunity** to the [# kw:condition:Charmed #] and [# kw:condition:Frightened #] conditions.

- If you are [# kw:condition:Charmed #] or [# kw:condition:Frightened #] when you pour, the condition ends on you, and you gain **temporary hit** points equal to your **Pour** for every condition ended this way.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="10">

## Endure Pain

While you are **Bloodied**, you gain an additional **action** each turn.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="14">

## Intimidating Presence
As a **Minor Action**, you can strike terror into others with your menacing presence.

- Each creature of your choice in a **[= 6 stride;ADJ =] Aura** originating from you saves Wisdom against DC **10 +your Strength modifier + your Tier Bonus**.
- On a **failed save**, a creature gains the **[# kw:condition:Terrified #] condition** for **1 minute**, and you ignore the creature's **resistances** to damage.
- At the end of each of its turns, the creature may [# kw:resist #].

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #], unless you spend **6 Abandon points** to restore its use.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="17">

## Unfettered Brutality

Whenever you use your **_Brutal Strike_** feature, you may use **two effects** instead of one, and the extra damage dealt by them increases to [% 2d10 %].

</Feature>

</Specialization>
