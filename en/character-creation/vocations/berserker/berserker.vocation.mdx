# Berserker

Berserkers want something the world has no standing to refuse. That want has a name — **Abandon** — and it is the oldest power there is: the blind striving by which the first Wills proved they existed, by moving. In a mortal it narrows to one body and one lifetime, and it is still enough to make flesh do what flesh was never built to do.

A Berserker turns that assertion into an edge impossibly sharp. Abandon floods muscle and marrow and the body obeys past every limit it owns; torn tendons pull through misery, pierced lungs keep hauling breath. It is poured out of the guard they were keeping, and the guard does not come back while it burns.

A Berserker who keeps something in reserve has already lost, because Abandon answers to appetite and ambition, and graveyards are littered with the hesitant. So they spend all of it, every time — for a killed brother, a stolen name, a hatred nursed for thirty years, a person they have decided will not die today. Those who reach old age reach it crooked, limping, and impossibly proud — they will tell you the trade was fair.

## 1st Level – Reckless Abandon

You hold a reserve of ruinous want called **Abandon**. The size of your pool is shown in the **Abandon** column of the Berserker Features table.

As a **Minor Action**, you can spend any number of points from your pool. The number spent is your **Pour** — What you let go of in order to achieve your wants —, and **Reckless Abandon** lasts for **1 minute**.

---

### While Reckless Abandon Is Active:

- You gain a **damage bonus** equal to your **Pour**. Weapon, spell, any source, any damage type. This damage is affected by critical strikes.
- You have **damage reduction** equal to your **Pour** against **Physical damage**. It cannot reduce damage below 1.
- Your **Armor Class is reduced** by your **Pour**.
- You add your **Pour** to **Strength Checks and Saving Throws**

---

### The Ceiling

A single **Pour** can never exceed your **Armor Class minus 10**, nor **twice your _Berserker Level_**. Your Armor Class therefore never falls below **10** from Reckless Abandon.

You can **Pour** again as a **Minor Action** while Reckless Abandon is active. Each **Pour** is capped by your **current** Armor Class minus 10, adds to your **Pour**, and refreshes the duration to 1 minute.

---

### Borrowed Guard

Your **Pour** can never exceed the Armor Class funding it. If the Armor Class you poured against drops — a spell ends, a shield breaks, armor is sundered — your **Pour** drops with it, until it is once again no greater than that Armor Class minus 10. The points stay spent.

**Example.** Your Armor Class is 15. You cast **[Shield](/en/library/spells/shield)** to raise it to 18 and **Pour** 8, leaving you at Armor Class 10 with a +8 **Pour**. When Shield ends, the Armor Class funding the **Pour** is 15 again, so your **Pour** falls to 5 and your Armor Class stands at 10. The other 3 points are gone.

---

### Spellcasting

While Reckless Abandon is active, if you can cast spells through a spellcasting feature:

- Your spell attacks take an **accuracy penalty** equal to your abandon **Pour**.
- **Spell save DCs** you impose are reduced by your abandon **Pour**.
- **Spell damage** gains the bonus like everything else.
- **Concentration** rolls have a penalty equal to your abandon **Pour** — The raw surge of power hinders control over the arcane.

---

### Ending

Reckless Abandon ends after 1 minute, when you become **Incapacitated**, or when you end it as a **Minor Action**. Spent points are never refunded.

You regain Abandon points equal to your **Constitution modifier** when you finish a **Repose**, and your **entire pool** when you finish a **Recovery**.

## 1st Level – Weapon Mastery

You can use the **Mastery properties** of **two melee weapons** of your choice.

- Whenever you finish a **Recovery**, you can change one of these choices.
- You gain additional Mastery options at higher levels (see table).

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 2nd Level – Reckless Attack

When you make your first attack roll on your turn, you may choose to forego all intent of protecting yourself from retaliation.

- You gain **Advantage** on all **Strength-based attack rolls** this turn.
- All attack rolls against you have **Advantage** until your next turn.

</Collapsible>

## 3rd Level – Primal Knowledge

- Gain proficiency in **one additional skill** from the Berserker skill list.
- While **Reckless Abandon** is active, you may add half of it to **Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival** rolls.

</Collapsible>

## 4th Level – Ability Score Improvement

You gain the **[Ability Score Improvement feat](/en/library/character-creation/feats/ability-score-improvement)** or another **[feat of your choice](/en/library/character-creation/feats)**.

You also gain this feature at levels **8th, 12th, and 16th level**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 5th Level – Extra Attack

When you take the **Attack action**, you can attack **twice** instead of once.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 5th Level – Feral Instinct

You have **Advantage on Initiative rolls**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 7th Level – Crushing Pounce

As part of the **Minor Action** you take to Pour into **Reckless Abandon**, you can move up to **half your Speed**, through a leap, ignoring difficult terrain and non-solid ground.
Any creature within [= 1 stride =] of your landing point has to make a **Dexterity Saving throw** Equal to **10 + your Pour** or be knocked prone.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 9th Level – Brutal Strike

When using **Reckless Attack**, you may choose to forgo Advantage on one **Strength-based attack** that has no Disadvantage.

- On a hit, deal **+[% 1d10 %] extra** of the same type as the attack.
- Apply one of the following effects:

#### Forceful Blow

Push the target **[= 3 stride =] away**, then move **up to half your Speed** toward it without provoking **Opportunity Attacks**.

#### Hamstring Blow

Reduce the target’s **Speed by [= 3 stride =]** until the start of your next turn.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 11th Level – True Wrath

While you are in **Reckless Abandon**, the **Damage Reduction** applies to **Any Source**, and is doubled while you are **Doomed**.

Additionally, your **Reckless Abandon** now lasts **10 minutes** instead of 1 minute. It still ends early if you fall **Unconscious**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 11th Level – Retaliatory Blow

When you get hit by an attack, you may spend your reaction to temporarily gain **resistance to that attacks damage**.

If you are in **Reckless Abandon** when you use this feature, you may deal **bludgeoning** damage equal to your **Pour** to the source of the attack.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 13th Level – Improved Brutal Strike

Gain new Brutal Strike options:

#### Staggering Blow

The target has **Disadvantage on its next saving throw** and cannot make **Opportunity Attacks** until the start of your next turn.

#### Sundering Blow

Gain a **+10 accuracy bonus** for that attack. Only one Sundering Blow bonus can apply to a creature at a time.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 15th Level – Trancing Steps

While in **Reckless Abandon**, your movement speed increases by **[= 3 stride =]**, and you gain a **swimming and climbing speed** equal to your walking speed.

Additionally, Your trance allows you to ignore barriers you would otherwise have to skirt. You gain a **burrowing speed** of half your walking speed while in **Reckless Abandon**

You may burrow through **stone, dirt, wood, and similar materials**, but not through **metal or supernatural barriers**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 18th Level – Indomitable Might

If the total of your **Strength check or saving throw** is less than your **Strength score**, you may use your **Strength score** instead, and you gain proficiency in **Dexterity Saving throws**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 19th Level – Epic Boon

Gain an **[Epic Boon feat](/en/library/character-creation/feats)** or another feat of your choice.

**[Boon of Irresistible Offense](/en/library/character-creation/feats/boon-of-irresistible-offense)** is recommended.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 20th Level – Primal Champion

Increase your **Strength** and **Constitution** by **+4**, to a maximum of **25**.

</Collapsible>
