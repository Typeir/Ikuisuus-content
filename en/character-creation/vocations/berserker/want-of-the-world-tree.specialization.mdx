# Path of the World Tree

_Trace the Roots and Branches of What Comes After._

Berserkers who follow the Path of the World Tree reach, through **Abandon**, for the **Tree of Fate** — the thing that grows where a life is spent and bears what comes after it. Its roots do not respect distance, and neither, briefly, do these Berserkers. They draw on it for vitality, and for the habit of coming up somewhere that was not adjacent.

<Collapsible>
## 3rd Level – Vitality of the Tree
What you pour out taps into the life force of the World Tree.

### Vitality Surge

When you **pour into Reckless Abandon**, you gain:

- **Temporary Hit Points equal to your Berserker level**

---

### Life-Giving Force

At the **start of each of your turns** while **Reckless Abandon** is active, you may choose one creature within **10 feet** of you. That creature gains **Temporary Hit Points** equal to:

- **d6 × your abandon value**

Any remaining Temporary Hit Points from this feature **vanish when Reckless Abandon ends**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 6th Level – Branches of the Tree
Whenever a creature you can see **starts its turn within 30 feet** of you while **Reckless Abandon** is active, you may take a **Reaction** to summon spectral branches of the World Tree around it.

The target must succeed on a **Strength saving throw**:

- **DC = 8 + your Strength modifier + Tier Bonus**

---

### On Failure:

- Teleport the target to an **unoccupied space within 5 feet of you** or the **nearest unoccupied space you can see**.
- After teleporting, the target’s **Speed is reduced to 0** until the end of the current turn.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 10th Level – Battering Roots
Your **Melee weapon reach increases by 10 feet** when wielding a weapon with the **Heavy** or **Versatile** property.

When you **hit with such a weapon** on your turn, you may activate the **Push** or **Topple** mastery property **in addition to** any other mastery property you’re using with that weapon.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 14th Level – Travel along the Tree
When you **pour into Reckless Abandon**, and as a **Minor Action** while it is active, you can teleport:

- **Up to 60 feet** to an unoccupied space you can see.

---

### Greater Teleportation (Once per Abandon)

Once per **Reckless Abandon**, you may increase the teleport distance to **150 feet**. When you do so, you may bring **up to six willing creatures** within **10 feet** of you.

Each creature teleports to an **unoccupied space of your choice** within **10 feet** of your destination.

</Collapsible>
