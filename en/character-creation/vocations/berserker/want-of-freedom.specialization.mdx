# Want of Freedom

_Take what your heart desires and be free of worldly chains._

Berserkers who follow the Want of Freedom are **Raiders**: They take and never give back, they see the flow of resources and time as theirs to shape and decide upon.
This is done not out of malice, but by a fundamental truth; The edict of motion and will is warranted through strength and strength alone, and only the truly strong — Those whose will dwarfs even the most powerful men — get to wear that crown, the crown of true freedom.

<Collapsible>

## 3rd Level – Resilience and Guts

You harbour a resilience hardened in long journeys and harsh environments, Your skin hardened, scarred and calloused to a sculptured perfection.

While wearing no armor, you add your **Constitution** modifier to your **AC**. This feature works even if you are wearing a shield.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

## 3rd Level – Arms of the Raid

While using a **Handaxe**, **Battleaxe**, **Greataxe**, **Halberd**, **Reaper** or **Glaive**, whenever you use a [_Masterful Blow_](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-properties) against a creature, the effect deals additional damage equal to your **Constitution** modifier, and you grant your allies advantage on their next attack against them.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

## 6th Level – Sound the Horn

You gain proficiency with the [_Music_](/en/library/items/tools/music) trade, and specialize in **Folk** music.

Beginning at 6th level, you learn to craft a **War Horn**. the horn is a mundane item you craft using the [_Music_](/en/library/items/tools/music), or any horn that qualifies as a musical instrument.

When you use your **Reckless Abandon** you may sound the **War Horn** as a **Minor Action**, and give you and every creature of your choice within 120 feet of you that can **Hear** the horn the following benefits:

- Immediately gain the [_Steady_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/actions-in-combat) state, without movement penalties.
- Roll initiative again, and add your **Pour** to it. If a creature rolls **higher** than **your current initiative** and have **not taken a turn** yet, they **immediately take a turn**.
- Move up to **half their** speed as a **reaction**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

## 10th Level – Trophied Scars

Whenever you become **Wounded**, **Bloodied** or **Doomed** for the first time during a **Recovery** you gain a **[Deed](/en/rules/steel-and-strife/deeds-grit-and-misdeeds)** that you can only use for yourself, and sits outside the **Deed Pool**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

## 14th Level – Pour One Out

Whenever you **Slay** a creature, you can give yourself or any ally within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your **Constitution Score**.

If you **Slay a second creature** and **The temporary hit points you previously granted remain**, you cannot use this feature.

If an ally you can see within 30ft of you, or you are **Slain**, you can use this feature, granting double the normal bonus.

</Collapsible>
