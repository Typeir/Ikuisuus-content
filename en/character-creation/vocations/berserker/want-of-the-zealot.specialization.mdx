# Want of the Zealot

<Specialization vocation="berserker">

_Abandon in Ecstatic Union with a God._

Berserkers who walk the Want of the Zealot receive boons from a god or pantheon. These Berserkers experience the pour as an ecstatic episode of divine union that infuses them with power. They are often allies to priests and other followers of their deity.

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Divine Fury
On each of your turns while **Reckless Abandon** is active, the **first creature you hit** with a **weapon or Unarmed Strike** takes extra damage equal to:

**[% 1d6 %] + half your Berserker level (rounded down).**

You choose whether this damage is **Dark or Holy** each time you deal it.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible open level="3">

## Warrior of the Gods
You gain a **pool of four d12s** that you can spend to heal yourself.

- As a **Minor Action**, expend any number of dice from the pool.
- Roll the dice and regain Hit Points equal to the total.

You regain all expended dice when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

At higher levels, your pool increases:

| Level | Dice in Pool |
| ----- | ------------ |
| 3     | 4            |
| 6     | 5            |
| 12    | 6            |
| 17    | 7            |

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="6">

## Fanatical Focus
Once per **Reckless Abandon**, when you fail a **saving throw**, you may reroll it.

- Add a bonus to the new roll equal to your **abandon value**.
- You must use the new roll.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="10">

## Zealous Presence
As a **Minor Action**, unleash a battle cry infused with divine energy.

- Up to **10 other creatures** of your choice within **[= 12 stride =]** gain **Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws** until the start of your next turn.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #], unless you spend **6 Abandon points** to restore its use.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="14">

## Abandon of the Gods
When you pour into **Reckless Abandon**, you may assume the form of a **divine warrior**. This form lasts for **1 minute** or until you drop to **0 Hit Points**.

Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

While in this form, you gain the following benefits:

### Flight

You gain a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed**, and you can **hover**.

---

### Resistance

You gain **Resistance to Dark, Psychic, and Holy damage**.

---

### Revivification

When a creature within **[= 6 stride =]** of you would drop to **0 Hit Points**, you may use your **Reaction** to spend **6 Abandon points**.

- Instead of dropping to zero, the creature’s **Hit Points become equal to your Berserker level**.

</Feature>

</Specialization>
