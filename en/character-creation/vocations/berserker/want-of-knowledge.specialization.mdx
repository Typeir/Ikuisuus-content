# Want of Knowledge

_Channel Abandon into stolen Truths._

Berserkers who walk the Want of Knowledge want the one thing strength alone has never taken: to understand. They take the scholar's road at a run — the Fold yields to eons of patient study, and patience can be plundered like anything else. What a wizard earns in a tower across a lifetime, these Berserkers rip loose in campaigns: from looted grimoires, captured lecturers, and the marginalia of dead empires.

Most who follow this Want end crowned, mad, or both. They are impossibly articulate for people who settle arguments with an axe, and they will explain — carefully, correctly, at length — why the world was always going to kneel.

## 3rd Level – Savage Spellcasting

You have forced open the door wizards spend eons unlocking. See [The Crux of the Cast](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/the-crux-of-the-cast) for the rules on spellcasting.

**Cantrips.** You know two cantrips of your choice from the **Want of Knowledge spell list** below. You learn a third at 10th level, as shown in the Cantrips column of the Savage Spellcasting table.

**Prepared Spells.** You prepare spells from the **Want of Knowledge spell list** below, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Savage Spellcasting table. The spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. Whenever you gain a Berserker level, you can replace one prepared spell with another spell from the list of a level for which you have spell slots.

**Spell Slots.** The Savage Spellcasting table shows how many spell slots you have. You regain all expended slots when you finish a **Recovery**. When determining spell slots for a mixed character, each Berserker level you have counts as **one-third of a level**, per [Mixing Spellcasting](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/mixing-spellcasting).

**Spellcasting Ability.** Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Spellcasting Focus.** Your regalia serve as your spellcasting focus: a crown, a standard, or a weapon you are holding.

## 3rd Level – Vainglory

Your magic is not requested from the Fold; it is conquered from it through sheer force of **Will**.

- When you cast an **enchantment** spell, or produce any spell or effect that would inflict the [_Frightened_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#frightened) or [_Terrified_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#terrified) condition, its saving throw DC is **8 + your Strength modifier + your Tier Bonus**.
- While **Reckless Abandon** is active, these DCs are **not reduced** by your **Pour** — the want and the word are the same thing.
- While **Reckless Abandon** is active, you have **resistance** to **psychic** damage.

</Collapsible>

## 6th Level – The Unspeakable

You have internalized the unspeakable nature of the world, and you can impart that knowledge upon others, however mind-scarring it is.

As a **Minor Action**, you speak a true thing to one creature within **[= 6 stride =]** that can hear you.

- The target must make an **Intelligence saving throw** (DC = **8 + your Intelligence modifier + your Tier Bonus**).
- On a **failed save**, it takes **psychic damage** equal to [% 2d10 %] + your **Intelligence Score**, and is [_Staggered_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#staggered) until the end of its next turn.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a **Repose**, unless you spend **4 Abandon points** (no action required) to restore its use.

Additionally, when an effect would inflict [short-term or long-term madness](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/illnesses/madness) on you, you can spend **2 Abandon points** (no action required) to refuse it. When you do, the GM tells you one true thing about the effect's source, and you gain advantage on your next Intelligence check or saving throw.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 10th Level – Kneel

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a **Brutal Strike**, you can spend **1 Abandon point** bellow a one-word command as part of the blow.

- The target must make a **Wisdom saving throw** (DC = **8 + your Strength modifier + your Tier Bonus**).
- On a **failed save**, it obeys, as the [_Command_](/en/library/spells/command) spell.
- The target need only **hear** you; it does not need to understand your language. The body obeys before the mind can object.

</Collapsible>

## 14th level  – Deathly Insight

You have ingested the maddening speech of the universe countless times, and have learnt to weaponize it.

Whenever you inflict the [_Frightened_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#frightened) or [_Terrified_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#terrified) condition, or [_Staggered_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#staggered), through the **_The Unspeakable_** feature,
you may deal an additional [% 4d10 psychic %] damage, and cause the creature to suffer [short-term madness](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/illnesses/madness), as it learns of a mind-shattering reality.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 17th Level – Mad King's Coronation

When you pour into **Reckless Abandon**, you may crown yourself as the Insight bound Heir to the Will of the world. The coronation lasts until your **Reckless Abandon** ends.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a **Recovery**.

While crowned, you gain the following benefits:

### Heavy is the crown

Your **Armor Class** goes down an additional maximum of **10 points**, up to **zero** and it is added to your **Pour**.

**Borrowed Guard** applies to any reduction that would leave you under **zero**. The points stay spent.

---

### To turn the wheel

When you **Slay** a creature, you regain one expended **spell slot** of **2nd level or lower**.

---

### Unbound Cognition

Your become **Immune** to psychic damage.

---

### One True Answer

The moment you crown yourself, you may put one question, no more than five words, to the world's machinery, and the world answers truly, as far as it knows. The answer arrives inside the pour; no other creature hears it.

</Collapsible>
