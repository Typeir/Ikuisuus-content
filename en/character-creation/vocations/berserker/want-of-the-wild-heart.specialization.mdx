# Path of the Wild Heart

_Walk in Community with the Animal World._

Berserkers who follow the Path of the Wild Heart view themselves as kin to animals. These Berserkers learn magical means to communicate with animals, and pouring heightens their connection to the animal world as it fills them with supernatural might.

<Collapsible>
## 3rd Level – Animal Speaker
You can cast **[Beast Bond](/en/library/spells/beast-bond)** and **[beastly communion](/en/library/spells/communion)** as **Rituals**.  
Your **spellcasting ability** for these spells is **Wisdom**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 3rd Level – Abandon of the Wilds
When you pour into **Reckless Abandon**, choose one of the following options:

### Bear

While Reckless Abandon is active, you have **Resistance** to all damage types **except Force, Dark, Psychic, and Holy**.

---

### Eagle

When you pour, you can take the **Disengage** and **Dash** actions as part of the same **Minor Action**.

While Reckless Abandon is active, you can take a **Minor Action** on each of your turns to use both **Disengage** and **Dash**.

---

### Wolf

While Reckless Abandon is active, **allies have Advantage on attack rolls** against enemies that are **within 5 feet of you**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 6th Level – Aspect of the Wilds
You gain one of the following features.  
You may change your choice whenever you finish a **Recovery**.

### Owl

You gain **Darkvision with a range of 60 feet**.  
If you already have Darkvision, its range increases by **60 feet**.

---

### Panther

You gain a **Climb Speed equal to your Speed**.

---

### Salmon

You gain a **Swim Speed equal to your Speed**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 10th Level – Nature Speaker
You can cast **[Commune with the Land](/en/library/spells/commune-with-the-land)** as a **Ritual**.  
Your **spellcasting ability** for this spell is **Wisdom**.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 14th Level – Power of the Wilds
When you pour into **Reckless Abandon**, choose one of the following options:

### Falcon

While Reckless Abandon is active, you gain a **Fly Speed equal to your Speed** if you are **not wearing armor**.

---

### Lion

While Reckless Abandon is active, **enemies within 5 feet of you have Disadvantage on attack rolls** against targets other than you or another Berserker using this feature.

---

### Ram

While Reckless Abandon is active, when you **hit a Large or smaller creature** with a melee attack, you may cause it to become **Prone**.

</Collapsible>
