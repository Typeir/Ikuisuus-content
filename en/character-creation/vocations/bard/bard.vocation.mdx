# Bard

Invoking magic through music, dance, and verse, Bards are experts at inspiring others, soothing hurts, disheartening foes, and creating illusions. Bards believe the world was spoken into existence, and that remnants of those first Words still resound in the Canvas and shimmer through every Fold of it. Bardic magic attempts to harness these words, transcending any language.

Bards are fascinated by almost everything. They become masters of performance, magic, lore, and storytelling. A Bard’s life is spent traveling, gathering knowledge, telling stories, and living on the gratitude of audiences. Their depth of knowledge and magical expertise set them apart.

## Becoming a Bard

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all the traits in **Core Bard Traits**.
- Gain the Bard’s level 1 features.

---

### As a Mixed Character

- Gain the Bard’s **Hit Point Die**.
- Gain proficiency in **one skill** and **one additional instrument** from your [Music](/en/library/items/tools/music) trade.
- Gain **Light Armor Training**.
- Gain the Bard’s level 1 features.
- Use the **[Mixing Spellcasting](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/mixing-spellcasting)** rules to determine your spell slots.

## Bardic Inspiration

As a **Minor Action**, inspire a creature within **[= 12 stride =]** who can see or hear you. That creature gains a **Bardic Inspiration die (d6)**.

- The creature may use it within the next **hour** when they fail a **d20 Test**.
- Roll the Bardic Inspiration die and **add it to the roll**, potentially turning failure into success.
- The die is **expended when it is rolled**.
- A creature can only have **one Bardic Inspiration die at a time**.

---

### Number of Uses

You can use Bardic Inspiration a number of times equal to your **Charisma modifier (minimum of 1)**.  
You regain all uses when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

## Spellcasting

You have learned to cast spells using bardic arts.

- **Spell list**: [_Bard spell list_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/bard/spells)
- **Spellcasting Ability**: Charisma
- **Spellcasting Focus**: An instrument from your [Music](/en/library/items/tools/music) trade

---

### Cantrips

- Know **2 cantrips** at level 1.
- Learn additional cantrips at **4th** and **10th level**.
- Cantrips may be replaced on level up.

---

### Spell Slots

Use the **Bard Features Table** for your spell slots.  
You regain all expended slots after a [# kw:Recovery #].

---

### Prepared Spells

- You prepare a number of Bard spells equal to the **Prepared Spells column** in the Bard Features table.
- Prepared spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots.
- Spells from other Bard features do not count against this number.

You may change your prepared spells when you gain a **Bard level**.

</Feature>

## Expertise

Choose **two skill proficiencies**. Your **Tier Bonus is doubled** for these.

At **9th level**, choose **two additional skills** to gain Expertise.

</Feature>

## Jack of All Trades

You have **Familiarity** with ability checks that:

- Use a skill in which you are **not proficient**, and
- Do not otherwise use your Tier Bonus.

## Font of Inspiration

- You regain **all uses of Bardic Inspiration on a [# kw:Repose #] or [# kw:Recovery #]**.
- You can expend a **spell slot** to regain one use of Bardic Inspiration.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="7">

## Countercharm

When you or a creature within **[= 6 stride =]** fails a saving throw against being **[# kw:condition:Charmed #] or [# kw:condition:Frightened #]**, you can use your **Reaction** to:

- **Reroll the saving throw**,
- The new roll has **Advantage**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="10">

## Magical Secrets

When you gain a level where your **Prepared Spells** increase, you may choose spells from the:

- **Bard, Pilgrim, Druid, and Wizard spell lists.**
- These count as **Bard spells** for you.

You may replace spells from these lists when you change your prepared spells.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="18">

## Superior Inspiration

When you **roll Initiative**, if you have fewer than **two uses** of Bardic Inspiration remaining, you regain uses until you have two.

</Feature>
