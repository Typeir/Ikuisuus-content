# Light Domain

<Specialization vocation="pilgrim">

_Bring Light to Banish Darkness._

The Light Domain emphasizes the divine power to bring about blazing fire and revelation. Pilgrims who wield this power are enlightened souls infused with radiance and the discerning vision of their deities, charged with chasing away lies and burning away darkness.

The Light Domain is associated with gods of truth, vigilance, beauty, insight, and renewal. Some of these gods are identified with the sun or as charioteers who guide the sun across the sky. Others are sentinels who pierce deception, or deities of artistry who teach that beauty improves the soul.

## Warding Flare
When a creature you can see within **[= 6 stride =]** makes an **attack roll**, you can use your **Reaction** to impose **Disadvantage** on the attack roll, causing a flare of light to dazzle the attacker.

- You can use this feature **a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier** (minimum of once).
- You regain all expended uses when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

## Radiance of the Dawn
As a **Major Action**, present your **Holy Symbol** and expend a use of your **Channel Divinity** to unleash a flash of light in a **[= 6 stride;ADJ =] Aura** originating from you.

- **Magical Darkness** in that area is **dispelled**.
- Each creature of your choice in the area saves Constitution.
- On a **failed save**, the creature takes **Holy damage equal to [% 2d10 %] + your Pilgrim level**.
- On a **successful save**, the creature takes **half damage**.

</Feature>
