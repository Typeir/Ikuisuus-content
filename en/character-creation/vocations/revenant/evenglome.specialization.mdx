# Evenglome

A knight of Evenglome holds dominion over nothing at all. The blade holds on to that which it bites, be it the grace of the ruined, the rot of the hale — and carries it out unasked, to give to whatever they meet next.
What the Evenglome means to one was write in grooves ancient as wills and dark as nulls.

## How one becomes a Revenant

You died in the gloaming, in the hour that is neither, and the world refused to move on without you, or let you move on without it.
Taiva is a will wrought paradise which held refuge for the nulls out of its own grace; a rejection of the first thought, and an acknowledgment of the second. That which is, can become. The ebb and flow of the world is undeniable, and inalienable.
Whatever remained of that refusal had you arise in the dichotomy to make is to unmake, and only that which is forgotten has once existing. You now walk holding both truths. An aspect of them irrigates the very core of all you touch.

## 1st Level – Tarnish
Your blade cannot pass through a living thing without keeping some of it.

### The Stain

Whenever you hit a creature with a melee weapon with the [**two handed**](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-properties) property, compare your [**threshold band**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands) to that creature's. Your blade takes the corresponding **stain**, replacing any stain it already carried.

| The creature you struck    | Your blade takes                   |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| A **worse** band than you  | **Holy**                           |
| A **better** band than you | **Dark**                           |
| The **same** band as you   | Nothing — the blade is wiped clean |

This costs nothing, happens on every hit, and cannot be declined.
A creature with no [**threshold band**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands) leaves your blade unchanged.

---

### The Strike

As a **Major Action** costing **both actions from your pool**, make one melee attack with a weapon with the two handed property.

- On a hit, **Execution's** extra damage becomes the type of the stain your blade **already carries**, and applies that stain's effect.
- The blade then takes its new stain from the creature you struck, as above.
- If your blade carries no stain, the attack resolves normally.

---

### Dark

**Execution's** extra damage becomes **dark damage**.
The target **cannot regain hit points** until the start of your next turn.

### Holy

**Execution's** extra damage becomes **holy damage**.
The ally in the lowest [**threshold band**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands) within **[= 6 stride =]** of you gains **temporary hit points** equal to **your tier bonus + your Intelligence modifier**. If several are tied, you choose.
You can never be the recipient of this effect.

## 3rd Level – Lykewake
Something walks beside you that should have gone on ahead. It does not follow you — it follows the ones who put it down, and you happen to be going the same way.

Your **Retinue** carries the **opposite stain** to your own at all times, changing whenever your blade's stain changes.

- **Grave Swipe's** damage becomes the opposite stain's type and applies that stain's effect.
- Temporary hit points granted by the Retinue's stain are **half** the amount you would grant, rounded down.
- If your blade loses its stain, the Retinue's blade loses its stain with it.

---

### Release

Its service is what frees it. When your Retinue is reduced to **0 hit points**, it is **released** rather than destroyed, and goes out as the stain it was carrying.

Each creature within **[= 2 stride =]** of it must make a **Dexterity saving throw** against your spell save DC.

- On a failed save, the creature takes **[% 4d6 %]** damage of the Retinue's stain type.
- On a success, it takes half as much.
- A creature in a lower [**threshold band**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands) than you is not affected, and instead gains **temporary hit points** equal to **your tier bonus + your Intelligence modifier**.

</Collapsible>

## 6th Level – Temper

You have learned to read the grooves upon which your blade is writ, and to steady it against what it touches.

- **Tarnish** costs **one action** from your pool instead of two.

---

### Held Temper

As a **Minor Action**, you steady the blade against what it touches.

Until the start of your next turn:

- Your blade does not take new stains.
- You gain **no benefit from Tarnish** on any attack you tomake.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 14th Level – Sufferance
Your **Abundance and Decay** stops making exceptions. It strips the seam bare and what surfaces depends on what is already in each creature standing there, including you.

- The **Armor Class reduction** applies to **every** creature inside the area, including **you and your summons**.

---

### The Pulse

At the start of each of your turns, every creature inside your Abundance and Decay is affected according to its [**threshold band**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands) compared to yours:

| The creature's band       | Effect                                                        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Below** your band       | Regains hit points equal to your **Intelligence modifier**    |
| **Above** your band       | Takes **dark damage** equal to your **Intelligence modifier** |
| The **same** as your band | Nothing                                                       |

---

### Shared Rot

Whenever **any** creature deals damage to another creature inside the area, it regains hit points equal to your **Intelligence modifier**.
A creature can regain hit points this way only **once per turn**.

This replaces **Abundance and Decay's** usual restriction to you and your Retinue.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
## 18th Level – The Cross Within

The proof of existance is in the act of obliteration. To be forgotten one must have been remembered.

While you are [**Slain**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands), your blade holds **both stains at once**. It cannot be wiped clean and cannot be replaced.

Every **Tarnish** you make applies both:

- **Execution's** extra damage becomes **true damage** and applies both stains' effects.
- The target **cannot regain hit points** until the start of your next turn.
- The ally in the lowest [**threshold band**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands) within **[= 6 stride =]** gains **temporary hit points** as normal.

---

### Last Cross

When you die while your **Abundance and Decay** is active, the area empties itself before it dissipates. Each creature inside it is affected according to its own [**threshold band**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands):

- A creature that is [**Doomed**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/damage#thresholds-and-bands) or worse gains **temporary hit points** equal to **your Revenant level + your Intelligence modifier**.
- Any other creature must make a **Constitution saving throw** against your spell save DC, taking **[% 12d10 dark %]** on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

This applies to allies and enemies alike. It does not ask.

</Collapsible>
