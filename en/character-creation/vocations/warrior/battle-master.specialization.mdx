# Battle Master

<Specialization vocation="warrior">

_Master Sophisticated Battle Maneuvers._

Battle Masters are students of the art of battle, learning martial techniques passed down through generations. The most accomplished Battle Masters are well-rounded figures who combine carefully honed combat skills with academic study in history, theory, and the arts.

<Collapsible>

<Feature level="3">

## Combat Superiority
Your battlefield experience grants you refined fighting techniques fueled by **Superiority Dice**.

### Maneuvers

- You learn **three [_maneuvers_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/warrior/maneuvers)** of your choice from the **Maneuver Options** section.
- Many [_maneuvers_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/warrior/maneuvers) enhance attacks or offer tactical advantages.
- You may only use **one maneuver per attack**.
- You learn **two additional [_maneuvers_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/warrior/maneuvers)** at **7th, 10th, and 15th level**.
- Each time you gain new [_maneuvers_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/warrior/maneuvers), you may replace one you know.

---

### Superiority Dice

- You begin with **four Superiority Dice**, which are **d8s**.
- You regain all expended dice when you finish a **[# kw:Repose #] or [# kw:Recovery #]**.
- At **7th level**, you gain a **fifth die**.
- At **15th level**, you gain a **sixth die**.

---

### Saving Throws

If a maneuver requires a saving throw, the DC is:

**10 +your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice) + your Tier Bonus**

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

<Feature level="3">

## Student of War
- You gain **proficiency with one type of Artisan’s Tools** of your choice.
- You gain **proficiency in one additional skill** from the Warrior skill list.

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

<Feature level="7">

## Know Your Enemy
As a **Minor Action**, you may study a creature within **[= 6 stride =]** and learn if it has **Immunities, Resistances, or Vulnerabilities**. If it has any, you learn what they are.

- You may use this feature **once per [# kw:Recovery #]**, or expend **one Superiority Die** to restore a use.

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

<Feature level="10">

## Improved Combat Superiority
Your **Superiority Dice** become **d10s**.

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

<Feature level="15">

## Relentless
Once per turn, when you use a maneuver, you may roll **[% 1d8 %]** and use the result instead of expending a Superiority Die.

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>

<Feature level="18">

## Ultimate Combat Superiority
Your **Superiority Dice** become **d12s**.

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

</Specialization>
