# Champion

<Specialization vocation="warrior">

_Pursue Physical Excellence in Combat._

A Champion focuses on the development of martial prowess in a relentless pursuit of victory. Champions combine rigorous training with physical excellence to deal devastating blows, withstand peril, and garner glory. Whether in athletic contests or bloody battle, Champions strive for the crown of the victor.

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Improved Critical
Your attack rolls with **weapons** and **Unarmed Strikes** score a **Critical Hit** on a roll of **19 or 20**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Remarkable Athlete
- You have **Advantage** on **Initiative rolls** and **Strength (Athletics) checks**.
- Additionally, when you score a **Critical Hit**, you may move up to **half your Speed** without provoking **Opportunity Attacks**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="7">

## Additional Fighting Style
You gain an additional **[Fighting Style feat](/en/library/character-creation/feats/fighting-styles)** of your choice. When you gain a Fighting Style this way, you do not increase an ability score.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="10">

## Heroic Warrior
The thrill of battle drives you toward victory.  
At the **start of your turn**, if you do not have **Heroic Inspiration**, you may grant it to yourself.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="15">

## Superior Critical
Your attack rolls with **weapons** and **Unarmed Strikes** score a **Critical Hit** on a roll of **18–20**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="18">

## Survivor
You attain the pinnacle of resilience in battle, gaining the following benefits:

### Defy Death

- You have **Advantage** on **Death Saving Throws**.
- When you roll **18–20** on a Death Saving Throw, you gain the benefits of rolling a **20**.

---

### Heroic Rally

At the **start of each of your turns**, if you are **Bloodied** and have at least **1 Hit Point**, you regain **5 + your Constitution modifier** hit points.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="21">

## Legendary Dreadnought
You gain one additional use of Indomitable.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="21">

## Unstoppable
As a reaction, when you make an attack roll or a Strength check to forcibly strike or break an object, structure, restraint, or magical barrier (including wall of force, forcecage, or similar effects), you may expend one use of Indomitable.
When you do so, you gain an **accuracy bonus** on the attack (or an equal bonus to the Strength check) and a **damage bonus**, each equal to your Warrior level.

If the effect normally states that it cannot be broken or allows no roll, you may still attempt it; the DC is 35.
If the roll succeeds, the object or barrier is automatically broken.

Additionally, you can use Unstoppable on an attack roll against any creature or object. When you do so, you gain an **accuracy and damage bonus** on that attack equal to your Warrior level.

You can do this after the outcome of the original roll is determined, but before any effects of the success or failure occur.

</Feature>

</Specialization>
