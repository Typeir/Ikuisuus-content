# Monk

Monks use rigorous combat training and mental discipline to bring themselves into alignment with the world as it actually runs, and to focus their internal reservoirs of power. Whether channeled into combat or as supernatural defense, this power infuses all that a Monk does.

## Vocation Features

<Feature collapsible level="1">

## Martial Arts

When unarmed or wielding Monk weapons, and not wearing armor or using a shield:

- **Bonus Unarmed Strike**: Make an Unarmed Strike as a **Minor Action**.
- **Martial Arts Die**: Use your Martial Arts die instead of normal damage.
- **Dexterity Attacks**: Use Dexterity for attacks, damage, Grapple DC, and Shove DC.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="1">

## Unarmored Defense

While unarmored and not using a shield, your **AC = 10 + Dex mod + Wis mod**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="2" accuracy="your tier bonus + your Wisdom modifier">

## Monk's Focus

You gain **Focus Points**, used for special abilities.

- **Flurry of Blows**: 1 Focus Point to make **two Unarmed Strikes** as a **Minor Action**.
- **Patient Defense**: Take [# kw:bail #] as a **Minor Action**, or spend 1 Focus Point to also take [# kw:brace #].
- **Step of the Wind**: Take [# kw:bolt #] as a **Minor Action**, or spend 1 Focus Point to also [# kw:bail #] and double your jump distance.

Focus Points recover on a **[# kw:Repose #] or [# kw:Recovery #]**.

Your Focus [# kw:accuracy #] and [# kw:damage #] are keyed to Wisdom.
A save against your Focus abilities is 10 plus that accuracy.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="2">

## Unarmored Movement

While unarmored and not using a shield, your **speed increases** as shown in the progression table.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="2">

## Uncanny Metabolism

When you roll **Initiative**, you may:

- Regain all expended **Focus Points**.
- Roll your **Martial Arts die** and regain **HP equal to Monk level + the roll**.

You may use this feature once per [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Deflect Attacks

As a **Reaction** when hit by an attack that deals **Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage**, gain **damage reduction** against it equal to:

**[% 1d10 %] + Dexterity modifier + Monk level**

If reduced to 0, you may spend **1 Focus Point** to redirect the attack:

- **Melee**: Choose a creature within **[= 1 stride =]**.
- **Ranged**: Choose a creature within **[= 12 stride =]** (no Total Cover).

The target saves Dexterity or takes **2 × Martial Arts die + Dex mod** damage of the same type.

</Feature>

## Feat

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="4">

## Slow Fall

As a **Reaction** when falling, reduce fall damage by **5 × Monk level**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="5">

## Extended Assault

You may attack **twice** instead of once when you take [# kw:assault #].

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="5">

## Stunning Strike

Once per turn when you hit with a Monk weapon or Unarmed Strike:

- Spend **1 Focus Point** to force a **Constitution saving throw**.
- **Fail**: Target is **[# kw:condition:Stunned #]** until your next turn.
- **Success**: Target’s **Speed is halved** and next attack against it has **Advantage**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="6">

## Empowered Strikes

Your **Unarmed Strikes** deal either:

- **Force damage**, or
- Their **normal damage type**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="7">

## Evasion

When a Dexterity save would halve damage:

- **Success**: Take **no damage**.
- **Fail**: Take **half damage**.

You don’t benefit if **[# kw:condition:Incapacitated #]**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="9">

## Acrobatic Movement

While unarmored and unshielded, you can:

- Move along **vertical surfaces** and across **liquids** without falling during the movement.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="10">

## Heightened Focus

Your Focus abilities improve:

- **Flurry of Blows**: Spend 1 Focus Point to make **three Unarmed Strikes** instead of two.
- **Patient Defense**: Gain **Temporary HP = 2 × Martial Arts die**.
- **Step of the Wind**: Move a **willing creature within [= 1 stride =]** with you without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="10">

## Self-Restoration

At the end of each turn, remove one of the following conditions from yourself:

- **[# kw:condition:Terrified #], or [# kw:condition:Poisoned #]**

You also don’t suffer **Exhaustion from forgoing food or drink**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="13">

## Deflect Energy

You can now use **Deflect Attacks** against attacks that deal **any damage type**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="13">

## Tongue of the Sun and Moon

You learn to touch the ki of other minds so that you understand **all spoken languages**. Moreover, any creature that can understand a language can understand what you say.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="14">

## Disciplined Survivor

- Gain **proficiency in all saving throws**.
- When you fail a saving throw, you may spend **1 Focus Point** to reroll.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="15">

## Perfect Focus

When you roll **Initiative** and don’t use **Uncanny Metabolism**, regain expended Focus Points until you have **4 Focus Points** if you have fewer than that.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="18">

## Superior Defense

At the start of your turn, you may spend **3 Focus Points** to gain **Resistance to all damage except Force** for **1 minute** or until **[# kw:condition:Incapacitated #]**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="19">

## Epic Boon

Gain an **[Epic Boon feat](/en/library/character-creation/feats)**.  
**[Boon of Irresistible Offense](/en/library/character-creation/feats/boon-of-irresistible-offense)** is recommended.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="20">

## Body and Mind

Your **Dexterity and Wisdom scores each increase by 4**, to a maximum of **25**.

</Feature>

</Vocation>
