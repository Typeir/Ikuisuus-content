# Warrior of Shadow

<Specialization vocation="monk">

_Harness Shadow Power for Stealth and Subterfuge._

Warriors of Shadow practice stealth and subterfuge, harnessing the power of the Everdark. They draw gloom around themselves to hide, leap from shadow to shadow, and take on a wraithlike form.

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Shadow Arts
You learn to draw on the power of the **Everdark**, gaining the following features:

### Darkness

- Spend **1 Focus Point** to cast **[Empty Skies](/en/library/spells/empty-skies)** without components.
- You can **see within the Darkness** you create.
- At the **start of each of your turns**, you may **move the area of Darkness** to a space within **[= 12 stride =]** of you.

---

### Darkvision

You gain **Darkvision** with a range of **[= 12 stride =]**.  
If you already have Darkvision, increase its range by **[= 12 stride =]**.

---

### Shadowy Figments

You know **[Minor Illusion](/en/library/spells/minor-illusion)**.  
**Wisdom** is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="6">

## Shadow Step
While you are entirely in **Dim Light or Darkness**, you may use a **Minor Action** to:

- **Teleport up to [= 12 stride =]** to an unoccupied space you can see that is also in **Dim Light or Darkness**.
- Gain **Advantage** on the next **melee attack** you make before the end of the turn.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="11">

## Improved Shadow Step
When you use **Shadow Step**, you may spend **1 Focus Point** to:

- **Ignore the Dim Light or Darkness restriction** for that teleportation.
- Immediately make an **Unarmed Strike** as part of the same **Minor Action** after you teleport.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="17">

## Cloak of Shadows
As a **Major Action** while entirely within **Dim Light or Darkness**, you may spend **3 Focus Points** to shroud yourself in shadows for **1 minute**, until you:

- Become **[# kw:condition:Incapacitated #]**, or
- End your turn in **Bright Light**.

---

### Cloak of Shadows Benefits

- **Invisibility**: You have the [# kw:condition:Invisible #].
- **Partially Incorporeal**:
  - You may **move through occupied spaces** as if they were **Difficult Terrain**.
  - If you end your turn in an occupied space, you are **shunted to the last unoccupied space** you were in.
- **Shadow Flurry**: You may use **Flurry of Blows** without expending any **Focus Points**.

</Feature>

</Specialization>
