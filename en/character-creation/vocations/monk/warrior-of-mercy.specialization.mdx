# Warrior of Mercy

<Specialization vocation="monk">

_Manipulate Forces of Life and Death._

Warriors of Mercy manipulate the life force of others. These Monks are wandering physicians, yet they bring a swift end to their enemies. They often wear masks, presenting themselves as faceless bringers of life and death.

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Hand of Harm
Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an **Unarmed Strike** and deal damage, you may expend **1 Focus Point** to deal additional **Dark damage** equal to:

**1 × Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier**

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Hand of Healing
As a **Major Action**, you may expend **1 Focus Point** to touch a creature and restore:

**Hit Points equal to 1 × Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier**

---

### Flurry of Blows Synergy

When you use **Flurry of Blows**, you can **replace one of the Unarmed Strikes** with **Hand of Healing**, without expending a Focus Point for the healing.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Implements of Mercy
You gain proficiency in:

- **Insight**
- **Medicine**
- [**Herbalism**](/en/library/items/tools/herbalism)

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="6">

## Physician’s Touch
Your healing and harming abilities improve:

### Hand of Harm

When you use **Hand of Harm**, you may also impose the **[# kw:condition:Poisoned #] condition** on the target until the **end of your next turn**.

---

### Hand of Healing

When you use **Hand of Healing**, you may also end **one of the following conditions** on the target:

- **[# kw:condition:Blinded #]**
- **[# kw:condition:Deafened #]**
- **[# kw:condition:Paralyzed #]**
- **[# kw:condition:Poisoned #]**
- **[# kw:condition:Stunned #]**

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="11">

## Flurry of Healing and Harm
When you use **Flurry of Blows**, you may:

- **Replace each Unarmed Strike with Hand of Healing** (no Focus Point cost for healing).
- When you deal damage with a Flurry of Blows **Unarmed Strike**, you may also use **Hand of Harm** with that strike **without expending a Focus Point**.

You may still use **Hand of Harm only once per turn**.

---

### Uses

Use this feature a number of times equal to your **Wisdom modifier** (minimum of once).  
Regain all uses after a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="17">

## Hand of Ultimate Mercy
As a **Major Action**, touch the corpse of a creature that died **within the past 24 hours** and expend **5 Focus Points**.  
The creature returns to life with:

**[% 4d10 %] + Wisdom modifier Hit Points**

---

### Conditions Removed

If the creature died while **[# kw:condition:Blinded #], [# kw:condition:Deafened #], [# kw:condition:Paralyzed #], [# kw:condition:Poisoned #], or [# kw:condition:Stunned #]**, those conditions are removed upon revival.

---

### Uses

Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

</Specialization>
