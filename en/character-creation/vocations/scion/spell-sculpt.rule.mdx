# Sculpting Techniques

Sculpting Techniques are arcane manipulation methods that allow a spellcaster to reshape spells at the moment of casting.

**Sculpting Techniques are optional spell alterations.**  
They are declared **when you cast a spell**, , and modify that spell’s parameters or resolution.

Unless stated otherwise, **only one Sculpting Technique can be applied to a spell**.

--- 

### Overpowering Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell.
> **Cost:** A number of [# kw:grievous wounds#] equal to the sum of the cost of the total **Overcast levels**, in addition to the base Spell Sculpt [# kw:grievous wounds #] cost
> **Effect:** The spell is **Overcast** to the desired level.
> **Example:** A Scion casts the **Shield** spell, but they know the hit landed 6 armor points away from their current AC. So they are short **3** points. They use **Overpowering spell** to raise the spell's level by 3, taking **1** Grievous wounds for the **Spell Sculpt** feature, then an additional **2** and **3** for the Overcast cost.

---

> ### Vicious Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell with an attack roll or on that forces creatures to make a saving throw   
> **Effect:** Choose affected targets up to your **Charisma Modifier**.
> The critical hit range for the spell increases by your **Charisma Modifier**, and creatures whose save roll landed on a side equal to or lower than 1 + your **Charisma Modifier** take **Maximized Damage**

---

> ### Careful Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell that forces creatures to make a saving throw   
> **Effect:** Choose a number of affected creatures up to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).  
> Chosen creatures **automatically succeed** on their saving throw against the spell, and **take no damage** if the spell would normally deal half damage on a successful save.

---

> ### Distant Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell   
> **Effect:**  
> - If the spell has a range of **[= 1 stride =] or greater**, **double its range**.  
> - If the spell has a range of **Touch**, its range becomes **[= 6 stride =]**.

---

> ### Savage Spell
> **When:** You roll damage for a spell   
> **Effect:** Reroll a number of the spell’s damage dice up to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).  
> You **must use the new rolls**.  
>  
> You may use this technique **even if another Sculpting Technique was already applied** to the spell.

---

> ### Extended Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell with a duration of **1 minute or longer**   
> **Effect:** Double the spell’s duration, up to a maximum of **24 hours**.  
>  
> If the spell requires **Concentration**, you have **Advantage on saving throws** made to maintain it.

---

> ### Heightened Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell that forces a creature to make a saving throw  
> **Effect:** One target of the spell has **Disadvantage on saving throws** against the spell.

---

> ### Quickened Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell with a casting time of **1 action**  
> **Effect:** The spell’s casting time becomes **1 Minor Action** for this casting.  
>  
> **Restriction:** You cannot modify a spell this way if you have already cast a **1st-level or higher spell** on this turn, nor can you cast another **1st-level or higher spell** after using this technique.

---

> ### Seeking Spell
> **When:** You miss with a spell attack roll   
> **Effect:** Reroll the d20 and **use the new result**.  
>  
> You may use this technique **even if another Sculpting Technique was already applied** to the spell.

---

> ### Subtle Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell   
> **Effect:** The spell is cast **without verbal, somatic, or material components**, except for materials that are **consumed** by the spell or that have a **listed cost**.

---

> ### Transmuted Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell that deals chemical, frost, fire, lightning, poison, or bludgeoning damage   
> **Effect:** Change the spell’s damage type to **another type from the same list**.

---

> ### Twinned Spell
> **When:** You cast a spell that targets only one creature and does not have a range of **Self**  
> **Cost:** A number of [# kw:grievous wounds #] equal to the spell’s level (None if the spell is a cantrip), in addition to the **Spell Sculpt** cost.
> **Effect:** Target a **second creature in range** with the same spell.  
>  
> **Restriction:** To be eligible, the spell must be **incapable of targeting more than one creature** at its current level.
