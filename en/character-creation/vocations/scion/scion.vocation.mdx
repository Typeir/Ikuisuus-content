# Scion

Scions wield innate magic that is stamped into their being. Some Scions can’t name the origin of their power, while others trace it to strange events in their personal or family history. The blessing of a väki at a baby’s birth, or the strike of lightning from a clear sky, might spark a Scion’s gift. So too might the attention of a god, exposure to something that came up out of the Folds, or a single unlucky glimpse of how the Canvas is actually put together.

Scions don’t learn magic; the raw, roiling power of magic is part of them. The essential art of a Scion is learning to harness and channel that innate magic, allowing the Scion to discover new and staggering ways to unleash their power.

## Spellcasting

You know four Scion cantrips of your choice. When you gain a Scion level, you can replace one cantrip with another from the Scion spell list.
You prepare Scion spells as shown in the Features table. Your spellcasting ability is Charisma, and you use an Arcane Focus.

##### Spell Points

Instead of spell slots, you have a pool of Spell Points. Your Scion level determines your maximum Spell Points and your maximum Spell Level, as shown in the Features table (Spell Points column). You regain all spent Spell Points when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

##### Casting a Spell

To cast a spell of 1st level or higher, you expend a number of Spell Points equal to the spell’s level cost from the table below. Cantrips cost nothing.

| Spell Level | Point Cost |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| 1st         | 1          |
| 2nd         | 2          |
| 3rd         | 3          |
| 4th         | 5          |
| 5th         | 8          |
| 6th         | 13         |
| 7th         | 21         |
| 8th         | 34         |
| 9th         | 55         |

Each spell costs as much as the sum of the two previous levels, rounded down. For example, a 4th-level spell costs 5 Spell Points (2 + 3 = 5), and a 9th-level spell costs 55 Spell Points (21 + 34 = 55).

## Font of Magic

Your body is a reservoir of arcane prowess.

As part of **casting** any spell, you can fuel your cast with additional energies.

By taking [# kw:grievous wounds #] damage equal to the **Point Cost** of the spell, as a **Reflex**, you can infuse it and sculpt it (cantrips cost 1).

Conversely, by taking the same damage as a **Reflex**, you can recover a single **Spell Point** per **two** [# kw:grievous wounds #] you take.

</Feature>

## Innate Sorcery

As a **Minor Action**, unleash your inner magic for **1 minute**:

- Your **spell save DC** increases by **+1**.
- You have **Advantage** on attack rolls of Scion spells.

You can use this feature **twice per [# kw:Recovery #]**.

</Feature>
