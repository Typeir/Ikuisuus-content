# Druid

Druids belong to ancient orders that call on the forces of nature. Harnessing the magic of animals, plants, and the four elements, Druids heal, transform into animals, and wield elemental destruction.

Revering nature above all, Druids unite to mark the passage of the seasons and safeguard the delicate balance of life.

## Becoming a Druid

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all **Core Druid Traits**.
- Gain the Druid’s level 1 features.

---

### As a Mixed Druid

- Gain the Druid’s **Hit Point Die** and training with **Light Armor** and **Shields**.
- Gain the Druid’s level 1 features.
- See [Mixing Spellcasting](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/mixing-spellcasting) for spell slot progression.

## Shillelagh

_Stub — ported from the retired Shillelagh spell; the full design lands with the Druid rework._

The wood knows you. As a **Minor Action**, you imbue a club or quarterstaff you are holding with nature's power. The imbuement is **permanent**; imbuing a new weapon returns the previous one to plain wood.

- You can use your **spellcasting ability** instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls of melee attacks with that weapon.
- The weapon's damage dice improve **one step** along the dice ladder (d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → 2d8 → 2d10 → 2d12 → 4d8…). The improvement grows by **one additional step** at Druid levels **5, 11, 17, 23, and 30**.

Shillelagh is a **vocation feature, not a spell or cantrip**: it cannot be learned, copied, or granted by anything that grants spells — but weapon buffs, imbuements, and attack effects stack with it freely.

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

## Wild Shape

As a **Minor Action**, assume a **Beast form** you know. You remain in this form for hours equal to half your Druid level, until you **dismiss it**, become **[# kw:condition:Incapacitated #]**, or die.

- Uses: 2 (see table for progression).
- You may replace 1 form per [# kw:Recovery #].

| Druid Level | Known Forms | Max CR | Fly Speed |
| ----------- | ----------- | ------ | --------- |
| 2           | 4           | 1/4    | No        |
| 4           | 6           | 1/2    | No        |
| 8           | 8           | 1      | Yes       |

---

### Wild Shape Rules

- Gain **Temporary Hit Points** equal to your Druid level.
- Use Beast’s physical stats, but keep your **Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, saving throws, and skills** if higher.
- No spellcasting while in Beast form.
- Equipment merges, falls off, or is worn (Game Director's discretion).

</Feature>

## Archdruid

You are a living extension of nature itself.

### Evergreen Wild Shape

- When you roll Initiative and have no uses of Wild Shape left, regain **1 use**.

---

### Nature Magician

- Convert Wild Shape uses into spell slots.
- Each use contributes **2 spell levels** (e.g., 2 uses = level 4 slot).
- This feature refreshes after a [# kw:Recovery #].

---

### Longevity

- For every **10 years** that pass, you age only **1 year**.

</Feature>
