# Villein

Villeins quest for knowledge hidden in the weave of the Canvas itself. They often begin by delving into tomes of forbidden lore, dabbling in invocations meant to attract something's attention across the Folds, or seeking out places where that attention has already been felt and left a mark. In no time, each Villein is drawn into a binding pact with a powerful patron.

Drawing on the ancient knowledge of celestials, Hiisi, the greater väki, Nulls that held together long enough to bargain, and things out of the Vastness that have no interest in being named, Villeins piece together arcane secrets to bolster their own power. Villeins view their patrons as resources—means to an end. Some respect or even love their patrons, while others serve grudgingly or seek to undermine them.

Once a pact is made, a Villein’s thirst for power and knowledge often leads to adventure.

## Becoming a Villein

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all the traits in the Core Villein Traits table.
- Gain the Villein’s level 1 features, which are listed in the Villein Features table.

### As a mixed Character

- Gain the Hit Point Die from the Core Villein Traits table.
- See [Mixing Spellcasting](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/mixing-spellcasting) for spell slot progression.
- Gain the Villein's level 1 features.

### 1st Level – Eldritch Blast

_Stub — ported from the retired Eldritch Blast spell._

Your patronage arms you directly. As a **Major Action**, you can use the **Cast** action to unleash a beam of crackling energy at a creature within **[= 24 stride =]**: make a ranged spell attack; on a hit, the target takes [% 1d10 force %].

- You fire additional beams as you gain Villein levels: **two at level 5, three at level 11, four at level 17, six at level 23, and seven at level 30**. Direct them at one target or several; make a separate attack roll for each.
- The beams are **spell attacks**; effects that modify your spell attacks and your Eldritch Invocations interact with them as normal.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
### 1st Level – Pact Magic

Through occult ceremony, you've formed a pact with a mysterious entity. This gives you access to **Pact Magic**—a unique form of spellcasting. You choose your spells from the [_Warlock spell list_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/villein/spells).

- **Cantrips**: You know one Villein cantrip. [Prestidigitation](/en/library/spells/prestidigitation) is recommended.
- **Spell Slots**: You have a small number of spell slots, which recharge on a [# kw:Repose #] or [# kw:Recovery #]. All slots are the same level.
- **Prepared Spells**: You prepare Villein spells of a level no higher than your current slot level. For example, at level 5, you prepare spells of levels 1–3, casting them as 3rd-level spells.
- **Spellcasting Ability**: Charisma.
- **Spellcasting Focus**: An Arcane Focus.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
### 2nd Level – Magical Cunning

Once per [# kw:Recovery #], you can perform a 1-minute ritual to regain half your Pact Magic spell slots (round up).

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible>
### 3rd Level –  Eldritch Invocations

At 3rd level, you learn one [Eldritch Invocation](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/villein/eldritch-invocation), such as **Pact of the Tome** or **Pact of the Blade**. You gain additional invocations as you level, as shown in the table.

Whenever you gain a Villein level, you can replace one invocation you know with another for which you qualify.

</Collapsible>

<Feature collapsible level="9">

## Contact Patron

You can cast Contact Other Plane to communicate directly with your patron without risk of psychic damage. Once per [# kw:Recovery #], you can cast it without a spell slot.

</Feature>

<Collapsible>
### 11th Level – Mystic Arcanum

At level 11, you gain a **Mystic Arcanum**—a powerful spell you can cast once per day without using a spell slot. You gain additional Arcanum spells at levels 13, 15, and 17 (7th, 8th, and 9th-level spells).

</Collapsible>

<Feature collapsible level="20">

## Eldritch Master

At level 20, using **Magical Cunning** now restores **all** Pact Magic spell slots.

</Feature>
