# Tinker Gadgets

This page lists the **gadgets** you can learn through **Replicate Magic Item**.

Each gadget corresponds to a specific magic item entry.
Unless otherwise stated, a gadget’s effects are identical to the referenced magic item.

---

## Gadgets (Level 2+)

| Gadget                                                                                                | Attunement | Description                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Alchemy Jug**                                                                                       | No         | A ceramic jug that can produce specific liquids on command.                                                         |
| **Bag of Holding**                         | No         | An extradimensional bag that can store far more than its physical size suggests.                                    |
| **Cap of Water Breathing**         | No         | Creates an air bubble around your head underwater so you can breathe normally.                                      |
| **Common magic item (not a Potion, Scroll, or cursed)**                                               | Varies     | Choose a single Common magic item (excluding potions, scrolls, and cursed items). Each choice is a separate gadget. |
| **Goggles of Night**                     | No         | Grants **Darkvision [= 12 stride =]** (or extends existing Darkvision).                                                      |
| **Manifold Tool**                           | Yes        | A tool that can transform into different artisan’s tools, granting broad utility for crafting and checks.           |
| **Repeating Shot**                         | Yes        | A ranged weapon enhancement: **+1** accuracy and damage bonus, ignores **Loading**, and can generate its own ammunition.     |
| **Returning Weapon**                     | No         | A thrown weapon enhancement: **+1** accuracy and damage bonus, and it returns to your hand immediately after the attack.     |
| **Rope of Climbing**                     | No         | A rope you can command to move, fasten itself, and support heavy loads.                                             |
| **Sending Stones**                         | No         | A paired set that allows casting **_Sending_** between the two stones.                                              |
| **Shield +1**                             | No         | A shield that grants a **+1** bonus to AC while held (in addition to a normal shield’s AC).                         |
| **Wand of Magic Detection**       | No         | Has charges to cast [**_Arcane Sense_**](/en/library/spells/arcane-sense).                                                                             |
| **Wand of Secrets**                       | No         | Has charges to detect nearby secret doors and traps.                                                                |
| **Wand of the War Mage +1** | Yes        | Grants **+1** accuracy bonus on spell attacks and lets you ignore **Half Cover** on spell attacks.                            |
| **Weapon +1**                             | No         | A weapon that grants **+1** accuracy and damage bonus.                                                             |
| **Wraps of Unarmed Power +1**      | No         | Worn wraps that grant **+1** accuracy and damage bonus with unarmed strikes (and similar eligible attacks).        |

## Gadgets (Level 6+)

| Gadget                                                                                              | Attunement | Description                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Armor +1**                             | No         | Armor that grants **+1** to AC while worn.                                                                          |
| **Boots of Elvenkind**               | No         | Your steps make no sound; you gain Advantage on Dexterity (**Stealth**) checks.                                     |
| **Boots of the Winding Path** | Yes        | Lets you teleport a short distance to a space you previously occupied recently.                                     |
| **Cloak of Elvenkind**               | Yes        | Grants Advantage on Dexterity (**Stealth**) checks; Wisdom (**Perception**) checks to notice you have Disadvantage. |
| **Cloak of the Manta Ray**       | No         | Grants underwater breathing and a strong swim capability while worn.                                                |
| **Dazzling Weapon**                     | Yes        | A **+1** weapon that can shed bright light; can be used to hinder foes via its dazzling flare.                      |
| **Eyes of Charming**                   | Yes        | Lenses with charges to cast [**_Charm_**](/en/library/spells/charm) (fixed save DC).                                                     |
| **Eyes of Minute Seeing**         | No         | Enhances close vision; grants strong bonuses to examining tiny details and fine work.                               |
| **Gloves of Thievery**               | No         | Invisible gloves that grant **+5** to Dexterity (**Sleight of Hand**) checks.                                       |
| **Helm of Awareness**                 | No         | Grants Advantage on initiative rolls.                                                                               |
| **Lantern of Revealing**           | No         | A hooded lantern that reveals Invisible creatures and objects while lit.                                            |
| **Mind Sharpener**                       | Yes        | Has charges to let you succeed on a failed Concentration save (Reaction).                                           |
| **Necklace of Adaptation**       | Yes        | Lets you breathe normally in any environment; improves resilience against poison effects.                           |
| **Pipes of Haunting**                 | No         | Charged pipes that create an eerie melody to frighten nearby creatures.                                             |
| **Repulsion Shield**                   | No         | A **+1** shield with charges to repel a nearby attacker, pushing it away on a hit.                                  |
| **Ring of Swimming**                   | No         | Grants a **Swim Speed [= 8 stride =]**                                                                                      |
| **Ring of Water Walking**         | No         | Lets you cast [**_Water Walk_**](/en/library/spells/water-walk) from the ring, targeting only yourself.                                              |
| **Sentinel Shield**                     | No         | A shield that heightens alertness, improving awareness and reaction readiness.                                      |
| **Spell-Refueling Ring**           | Yes        | As a Minor Action, recover one expended spell slot (level 3 or lower), once per day.                                |
| **Wand of Magic Missiles**       | No         | Has charges to cast [**_Magic Missile_**](/en/library/spells/magic-missile), spending more charges for higher-level casts.                              |
| **Wand of Web**                             | Yes        | Has charges to cast [**_Web_**](/en/library/spells/entangle).                                                                                      |
| **Weapon of Warning**                 | Yes        | Prevents you from being surprised and magically wakes allies when combat starts.                                    |

## Gadgets (Level 10+)

| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Armor of Resistance**               | Yes        | Grants resistance to one damage type (determined by the GM or randomly).                                     |
| **Dagger of Venom**                       | No         | A **+1** dagger that can be coated with magical poison as a Minor Action.                                    |
| **Elven Chain**                               | No         | Chain shirt with **+1 AC** that you can wear even without medium armor training.                             |
| **Ring of Feather Falling**       | Yes        | Prevents fall damage and slows your descent when you fall.                                                   |
| **Ring of Jumping**                       | Yes        | Lets you cast [**_Jump_**](/en/library/spells/jump) from the ring, targeting only yourself.                                             |
| **Ring of Mind Shielding**         | Yes        | Blocks mind-reading and similar detection; allows telepathy only if you permit it.                           |
| **Shield +2**                             | No         | A shield that grants a **+2** bonus to AC while held (in addition to a normal shield’s AC).                  |
| **Uncommon wondrous item (not cursed)**                                                               | Varies     | Choose a single Uncommon wondrous item that isn’t cursed. Each choice is a separate gadget.                  |
| **Wand of the War Mage +2** | Yes        | Grants **+2** accuracy bonus on spell attacks and lets you ignore **Half Cover** on spell attacks.                     |
| **Weapon +2**                             | No         | A weapon that grants **+2** accuracy and damage bonus.                                                      |
| **Wraps of Unarmed Power +2**      | No         | Worn wraps that grant **+2** accuracy and damage bonus with unarmed strikes (and similar eligible attacks). |
