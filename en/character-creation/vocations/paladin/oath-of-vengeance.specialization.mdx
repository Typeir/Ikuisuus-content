# Oath of Vengeance

<Specialization vocation="paladin">

_Punish Evildoers at Any Cost._

The Oath of Vengeance is a solemn commitment to punish those who commit grievous acts of evil. When tyrants oppress the innocent, when dragons rampage, or when injustice festers unchecked, Paladins of this oath arise to set things right, no matter the personal cost.

**Tenets of Vengeance**:

- Show the wicked no mercy.
- Fight injustice and its causes.
- Aid those harmed by injustice.

## Vow of Enmity
When you take the **Attack action**, you can expend one use of **Channel Divinity** to swear a **Vow of Enmity** against a creature within **[= 6 stride =]** of yourself.

- You have **Advantage on attack rolls** against the creature for **1 minute** or until you use this feature again.
- If the creature drops to **0 Hit Points** before the vow ends, you can transfer the vow to a different creature within **[= 6 stride =]** of yourself.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="7">

## Relentless Avenger
When you hit a creature with an **Opportunity Attack**, you can:

- Reduce that creature's **Speed to 0** until the end of the current turn.
- Move up to **half your Speed** as part of the same Reaction. This movement does **not provoke Opportunity Attacks**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="15">

## Soul of Vengeance
Immediately after a creature under the effect of your **Vow of Enmity** hits or misses with an attack roll, you can take a **Reaction** to make a **melee attack** against that creature if it is within range.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="20">

## Avenging Angel
As a **Minor Action**, you gain the benefits below for **10 minutes** or until you end the effect.  
Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #], unless you expend a **5th-level spell slot** to restore it.

### Benefits:

**Flight.**  
Spectral wings sprout from your back. You gain a **Fly Speed of [= 12 stride =]**, and you can hover.

**Frightful Aura.**  
You emanate an aura of a range of [= 1 stride =] times your **Charisma** modifier. As an [# kw:delayed #] effect of the aura, an enemy saves Wisdom or has the **[# kw:condition:Frightened #]** condition for **1 minute** or until it takes any damage.  
Attack rolls against the [# kw:condition:Frightened #] creature have **Advantage**.

</Feature>

</Specialization>
