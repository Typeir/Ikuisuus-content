# Paladin

Paladins are united by their oaths to stand against annihilation and corruption. Whether sworn before a god’s altar, in a sacred grove, or in solitude with the dead as witness, a Paladin’s oath is a powerful bond—turning a devout warrior into a blessed champion.

Paladins master weapons and armor but rely even more on divine magic to heal the injured, smite foes, and protect the helpless. Their life is one of adventure and cosmic struggle, lived on the front lines against corruption and ruin.

## Becoming a Paladin

### As a Level 1 Character

- Gain all Core Paladin Traits.
- Gain the Paladin’s 1st-level vocation features, listed in the Paladin Features table.

### As a Mixed Character

- Gain the Hit Point Die.
- Gain proficiency with Martial weapons.
- Gain training with Light and Medium armor and Shields.
- Gain the Paladin’s 1st-level vocation features.
- See [Mixing Spellcasting](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/mixing-spellcasting) for spell slot progression.

---

## Breaking Your Oath

If you unrepentantly violate your oath, speak with your Game Director about appropriate consequences, such as changing your specialization or retraining into another vocation.

## Vocation Features

<Feature collapsible level="1">

## Lay On Hands

Your blessed touch can heal wounds. You have a pool of healing power that replenishes when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #]. With that pool, you can restore a total number of Hit Points equal to **five times your Paladin level**.

As a **Minor Action**, you can touch a creature (which could be yourself) and draw power from the pool to restore a number of Hit Points to that creature, up to the amount remaining in the pool.

You can also expend **5 Hit Points** from the pool to remove the **[# kw:condition:Poisoned #]** condition from the creature. Those points don’t also restore Hit Points.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="1">

## Spellcasting

You have learned to cast spells through prayer and meditation. You choose your spells from the [_Paladin spell list_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/paladin/spells).

- **Spell Slots.** The Paladin Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level or higher. You regain all expended slots when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].
- **Prepared Spells.** You prepare Paladin spells of 1st level or higher. The number you can prepare is shown in the Prepared Spells column of the table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots.
- **Changing Spells.** Whenever you finish a [# kw:Recovery #], you can replace one prepared spell with another Paladin spell.
- **Spellcasting Ability.** Charisma is your spellcasting ability for Paladin spells.
- **Spellcasting Focus.** You can use a Holy Symbol as a spellcasting focus for Paladin spells.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="1">

## Weapon Mastery

Your training allows you to use the mastery properties of **two weapons of your choice** with which you have proficiency.

Whenever you finish a [# kw:Recovery #], you can change your chosen weapons.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible open level="2">

## Fighting Style

You gain a **[Fighting Style feat](/en/library/character-creation/feats/fighting-styles)** of your choice. When you gain a Fighting Style this way, you do not increase an ability score.

Instead of choosing a feat, you can choose the option below:

**Blessed Warrior.**  
You learn two Pilgrim cantrips of your choice. They count as Paladin spells for you, and Charisma is your spellcasting ability for them. Whenever you gain a Paladin level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another Pilgrim cantrip.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="3">

## Channel Divinity

You can channel divine power through your oath to fuel magical effects.

- You have **two uses** of Channel Divinity.
- You regain **one use on a [# kw:Repose #]** and **all uses on a [# kw:Recovery #]**.
- You gain an additional use at **11th level**.

If an effect requires a saving throw, it is against 10 plus your Paladin [# kw:accuracy #].

**Divine Sense.**  
As a **Minor Action**, you detect Celestials, Aberrations, and Undead within **[= 12 stride =]** for **10 minutes**, unless you become [# kw:condition:Incapacitated #]. You also detect consecrated or desecrated places or objects in that area.

</Feature>

## Extra Attack

You can attack **twice**, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="5">

## Faithful Steed

You can call on the aid of an otherworldly steed.

You always have **Find Steed** prepared. You can cast it once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="6">

## Well of Perseverance

Your Oath gives you a near endless well of will and determination, which you can extend to those you share a cause with.

**Perseverance**
 - You always add your **Charisma** to the **Accuracy** of your saving throws. This is your **Perseverance**.

**Comradery**
- At the end of a recovery, you can designate up to **TB** willing creatures to become **Determined**. These are your **Comrades**.
Regardless of distance, as long as you are alive, these creatures can add your **Perseverance** to their [# kw:grit #] rolls, and they gain a single, personal [# kw:deed #] at the end of every [# kw:repose #]

If a creature would be affecteed by another **Comradery** effect, they lose the effects of the prior one.
If a creature would turn **Hostile or Neutral** to you on [# kw:disposition #] track, **Comradery** vanishes too.
Only those who share your cause will receive the boons from it.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="9">

## Abjure Foes

As a **Major Action**, you can expend one use of Channel Divinity to overwhelm foes with awe.

Choose a number of creatures you can see within **[= 12 stride =]** equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum one). Each target saves Wisdom or is **[# kw:condition:Frightened #]** for 1 minute or until it takes damage. While [# kw:condition:Frightened #] this way, a creature can take only one of the following on its turn: move, take a Major Action, or take a Minor Action.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="10">

## Well of Courage

Whenever you or one of your **Comrades** rolls a **1** on a **Grit** effect, you can reroll it.
Whenever you or one of your **Comrades** rolls a **20** on a **Grit** effect, you immediately succeed, regardless of the final result.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="11">

## Radiant Strikes

Paladin's smite extra damage after casting a spell becomes [% 2d8 %] plus your Charisma modifier.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="14">

## Restoring Touch

When you use Lay On Hands on a creature, you can also remove one or more of the following conditions: **[# kw:condition:Blinded #], [# kw:condition:Deafened #], [# kw:condition:Frightened #], [# kw:condition:Paralyzed #], or [# kw:condition:Stunned #]**.

You must expend **5 Hit Points** from the Lay On Hands pool for each condition removed.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="18">

## Obliterating Smite

Attacks that carry a **Paladin's Smite** or extra holy damage from the **Radiant Strikes** or **Paladin's smite** features are enhanced.
These attacks have their critical strike range reduced by 1.

You can spend a [# kw:deed #] to further reduce the range by your charisma modifier, before you make the attack.
Once you've spen a [# kw:deed #] this way and successfully landed a critical hit, you cannot use it in this way again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="19">

## Epic Boon

You gain an **Epic Boon feat** or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="21">

## Divine Will

When you or a **Comrade** fail on a **Deed** roll, you can use your **Channel Divinity** as a **Reaction** to have them reroll it.

Your charisma score increases by **4**.

</Feature>
