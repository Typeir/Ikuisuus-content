# Oath of Devotion

<Specialization vocation="paladin">

_Uphold the Ideals of Justice and Order._

The Oath of Devotion binds Paladins to Justice and Order. These Paladins embody the archetype of the knight in shining armor, holding themselves—and sometimes the world—to the highest standards of conduct.

Many swear this oath before gods of law and goodness, while others revere angelic ideals, weaving symbols of wings into their armor and heraldry.

**Tenets of Devotion**:

- Let your word be your promise.
- Protect the weak and never fear to act.
- Let your honorable deeds be an example.

## Sacred Weapon
When you take the **Attack action**, you can expend one use of **Channel Divinity** to imbue a **Melee weapon** you’re holding with positive energy.

For **10 minutes**, or until you use this feature again:

- You gain an **accuracy bonus** equal to your **Charisma modifier** (minimum +1) on **attack rolls** made with the weapon.
- Each time you hit, the weapon deals either its **normal damage type** or **Holy damage** (your choice).
- The weapon emits **Bright Light** in a **[= 4 stride;ADJ =] radius** and **Dim Light** for an additional [= 4 stride =].

You can end this effect early. It ends if you’re no longer carrying the weapon.

</Feature>

## Aura of Devotion

- You and your allies within the aura are **immune to the [# kw:condition:Charmed #] condition**.
- If a [# kw:condition:Charmed #] ally enters the aura, the condition has no effect while they remain inside.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="15">

## Smite of Protection
Whenever you use **Paladin’s Smite**, you and your allies gain **Half Cover** while in your aura.

This benefit lasts **until the start of your next turn**.

</Feature>

<Feature collapsible level="20">

## Holy Nimbus
As a **Minor Action**, you can imbue your aura with radiant power. This lasts for **10 minutes** or until you end it. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #], unless you expend a **5th-level spell slot** to restore it.

### Benefits:

**Holy Ward.**  
You have **Advantage on saving throws** forced by Aberrations or Undead.

**Holy Damage.**  
As an [# kw:delayed #] effect of your aura, an enemy takes **Holy damage equal to your Charisma modifier + Tier Bonus**.

**Sunlight.**  
Your aura emits **Bright Light** that counts as **sunlight**.

</Feature>
