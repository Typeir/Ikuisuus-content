# Oath of the Ancients

<Specialization vocation="paladin">

_Preserve Life and Light in the World._

The Oath of the Ancients is as old as the first elves.  
Paladins who swear this oath cherish life and light, valuing the beauty of the natural world more than rigid principles of honor or justice. They often adorn themselves with images of growing things—leaves, antlers, or flowers—to symbolize their connection to the living world.

**Tenets of the Ancients**:

- Kindle the light of hope.
- Shelter life.
- Delight in art and laughter.

## Nature's Wrath
As a **Major Action**, you can expend one use of **Channel Divinity** to conjure spectral vines.

Each creature of your choice that you can see within **[= 3 stride =]** saves Strength or becomes **[# kw:condition:Restrained #]** for **1 minute**.

A [# kw:condition:restrained #] creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

## Aura of Warding
Ancient magic surrounds you, forming a mystical ward.  
You emanate an aura of a range of [= 1 stride =] times your **Charisma** modifier that wards against the unseen:

- You and your allies within the aura gain **Resistance** to **Dark, Psychic, and Holy damage**.

</Feature>

<Feature level="15">

## Undying Sentinel
When you are reduced to **0 Hit Points** and not killed outright, you can choose to drop to **1 Hit Point instead**, then immediately regain additional Hit Points equal to **three times your Paladin level**.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

Additionally, you:

- Cannot be aged magically.
- Cease visibly aging.

</Feature>

</Collapsible>

<Feature level="20">

## Elder Champion
As a **Minor Action**, you can empower your aura with primal energy for **1 minute** or until you end the effect.

Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a [# kw:Recovery #], unless you expend a **5th-level spell slot** to restore it.

### Benefits:

**Diminish Defiance.**  
Enemies in the aura have **Disadvantage on saving throws** against your **spells** and **Channel Divinity** options.

**Regeneration.**  
At the start of each of your turns, you regain **10 Hit Points**.

**Swift Spells.**  
When you cast a spell that has a casting time of **1 action**, you can cast it using a **Minor Action instead**.

</Feature>
