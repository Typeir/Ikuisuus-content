# Ironeye

<Feat ability="Strength or Dexterity">

_Prerequisite: **Strength** or **Dexterity** 17, Familiar in a Flexed, Slung or Hammered art, the [_Sharpshooter_](/en/library/character-creation/feats/sharpshooter) feat.

No target is too distant for you, your aim is true and your vision is keen.

---

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

Your long range is doubled for any ranged weapon your arts cover.

When using a weapon that reloads, you can choose to perform an aimed shot, which has the following benefits:

- Your critical hit range with that weapon increases by 2.
- Your attacks deal extra damage equal to your tier bonus.


</Feat>
