# Ability Score Improvement

_No prerequisite._

Relentless drilling, hard-won experience, or a quiet unlocking of what already slept within; you sharpen the raw stuff of yourself.

---

Increase one ability score by **2**, or two ability scores by **1** each.

You can take this feat more than once.
