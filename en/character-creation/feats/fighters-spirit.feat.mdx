# Fighter's Spirit

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Strength 13** or **Dexterity 13**, proficiency with at least one martial weapon."
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

You are one with the blade, your mind focused entirely on the art of combat.

---

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

Once per [# kw:Repose #], as a Minor Action, you can enter a state of heightened combat awareness until the start of your next turn. While in this state, you gain the following benefits:

- You have Advantage on all attack rolls.

- You gain a +2 bonus to AC.

- You gain advantage on dexterity saving throws.

- You immediately end one effect on yourself that is causing you to be [# kw:condition:charmed #] or [# kw:condition:frightened #].

- You gain temporary hit points equal to your level.

</Feat>
