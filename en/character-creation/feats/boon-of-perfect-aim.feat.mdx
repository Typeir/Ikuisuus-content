# Perfect Aim

<Feat
  category="epic boon"
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

The martial world was shaped for war before it was shaped for anything else.
Those who train long enough stop swinging at where a foe stands and begin swinging at where it is fated to fall.

Your body no longer waits for the opening. It makes one.

> “The blade did not miss. The world had merely not yet caught up.”

---

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefit:

<Feature trigger="when you miss with an attack roll on your turn" charges="1/turn">

#### Unerring Strike

You can treat the roll as a hit instead.

</Feature>

</Feat>
