# Shield Master

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Interception** or **Protection** Fighting Style"
  ability="Strength or Constitution">

You have trained to exploit the full tactical potential of your shield — using it not just to deflect blows but to throw enemies off balance.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action" trigger="if you take [# kw:assault #] on your turn" targets="a creature within [= 1 stride =] of you">

#### Shield Shove

You can try to **shove** the creature with your shield.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" trigger="if you are subjected to an effect that allows a **Dexterity saving throw** to take only half damage">

#### Block

You take **no damage** on a successful save, interposing your shield between you and the source.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Fortified Stance

While you are holding a shield, you gain a **+1 bonus to saving throws**.

</Feature>

</Feat>
