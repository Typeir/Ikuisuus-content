# Lunar Fleshcraft

<Feat
  prerequisite="[Lunar Curse](/en/library/character-creation/feats/lunar-curse)"
  ability="Constitution">

The flesh is clay, and mother moon whispers the secret paths of its shaping. You have learned to hear that distant voice and mold your body through sheer will.

---

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- When you **enter lunar shifting**, its duration becomes **10 minutes × your tier bonus**, instead of 1 minute.

- Each time you enter lunar shifting, you may choose **one additional [Lunar Adaptation](/en/library/character-creation/feats/lunar-curse#lunar-adaptations)** from the Lunar Adaptations list granted by **[Lunar Curse](/en/library/character-creation/feats/lunar-curse)**. You gain that benefit for the duration of the shift, in addition to the adaptation you normally choose.

  You can choose a different additional adaptation from the [_Lunar Adaptations_](/en/library/character-creation/feats/lunar-curse#lunar-adaptations) each time you shift.

</Feat>
