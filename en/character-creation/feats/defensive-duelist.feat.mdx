# Defensive Duelist

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Dueling** style, **Dexterity Score 13**"
  ability="Dexterity">

Your training in one-on-one combat has taught you to read your opponent's body language and turn aside their strikes with a flick of your blade.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" trigger="when another creature hits you with a melee attack">

#### Parry

When you are wielding a **finesse weapon** with which you are proficient, you can add your **DEX** to your Dodge until the end of your round — potentially causing the attack to miss.

</Feature>

</Feat>
