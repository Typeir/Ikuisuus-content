# Defensive Duelist

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Dueling** style, **Dexterity 13**"
  ability="Dexterity">

Your training in one-on-one combat has taught you to read your opponent's body language and turn aside their strikes with a flick of your blade.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- **Parry.** When you are wielding a **finesse weapon** with which you are proficient and another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your **reaction** to add your **Dexterity Bonus** to your AC until the start of your next turn — potentially causing the attack to miss.

</Feat>
