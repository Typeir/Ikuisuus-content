# Lucky

Some are simply spared. The arrow finds the gap in the crowd, the floor holds where it had no reason to, the wrong word lands as the right one.
It is not a blessing and it is not a spell — only a crook in the world’s arithmetic that happens to bend your way.

> “The math of the world does not balance. Stand where it spills.”

---

Increase your **Any ability score** by 1, up to a maximum of **20**.

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- **Fortune’s Reserve.**  
  You have a number of **Luck Points** equal to your **tier bonus**. You regain all expended Luck Points when you finish a **Recovery**.

- **Fortune’s Favor.**  
  When you make an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw, you can spend one Luck Point to roll an additional **d20**. You choose which of the d20s to use for the roll.

- **Fortune’s Bane.**  
  When a creature makes an attack roll against you, you can spend one Luck Point to roll a **d20**, then choose whether the attack uses its roll or yours.

This fortune is **not magical**, and so it may be used even on a **Raw Roll [RR]**.
