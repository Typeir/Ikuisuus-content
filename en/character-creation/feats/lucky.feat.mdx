# Lucky

<Feat>

Some are simply spared. The arrow finds the gap in the crowd, the floor holds where it had no reason to, the wrong word lands as the right one.
It is not a blessing and it is not a spell — only a crook in the world’s arithmetic that happens to bend your way.

> “The math of the world does not balance. Stand where it spills.”

---

Increase your **Any ability score** by 1, up to a maximum of **20**.

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Pool max="tier bonus" recharge="[# kw:Recovery #]">

#### Fortune’s Reserve

You have a number of **Luck Points** equal to your **tier bonus**. You regain all expended Luck Points when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Pool>

<Feature cost="1 Luck Point" trigger="when you make an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw">

#### Fortune’s Favor

You can roll an additional **d20**. You choose which of the d20s to use for the roll.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Luck Point" trigger="when a creature makes an attack roll against you">

#### Fortune’s Bane

You can roll a **d20**, then choose whether the attack uses its roll or yours.

</Feature>

This fortune is **not magical**, and so it may be used even on a **Raw Roll [# kw:raw roll;RR #]**.

</Feat>
