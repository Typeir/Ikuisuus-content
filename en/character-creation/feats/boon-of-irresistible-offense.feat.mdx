# Irresistible Offense

<Feat
  category="epic boon"
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

An old truth carried down from the Age of Motion holds that which moves with enough purpose cannot be refused.
Ward, hide, and scale are only delays.

Your blows carry that purpose. Nothing in the world is truly braced against them.

> “One cannot stop the unstoppable, nor evade the inevitable.”

---

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature>

#### Sundering Force

Your weapon strikes and unarmed strikes ignore **Resistance** to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

</Feature>

<Feature trigger="when you critically strike">

#### Overwhelming Blow

You deal extra damage equal to the ability **score** increased by this feat.

</Feature>

</Feat>
