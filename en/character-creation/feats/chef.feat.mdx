# Chef

<Feat
  category="general"
  prerequisite="proficiency with [Cooking](/en/library/items/tools/cooking)"
  ability="Constitution or Wisdom">

A company marches on what it last ate. You know the difference between filling a
stomach and mending a body, and you can tell which one the day calls for.

---

<Feature charges="1/[# kw:Repose #]">

#### The Long Table

As part of [# kw:sustenance #] during a [# kw:Respite #] or a [# kw:Repose #],
you prepare a **Lavish Meal** in portions equal to your **tier bonus**.

A portion left unstored spoils after **1 hour**. Stored properly, it keeps its
worth for **8 hours** and is a **Standard Meal** thereafter. No storage holds it
past **10 days**, after which it is spoiled however it was kept.

</Feature>

<Feature trigger="with an hour of work, or as you finish a [# kw:Repose #]">

#### Bolstering Treats

You make treats equal to your **tier bonus**. They keep for **8 hours**.

A creature may spend **1 Minor Action** to eat one, gaining **temporary hit
points equal to your tier bonus**. This effect applies once per
[# kw:Repose #] per creature.

</Feature>

</Feat>
