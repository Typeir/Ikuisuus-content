# Dual Wielder

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Two-Weapon Fighting** style"
  ability="Dexterity">

You have mastered the fluid art of the twin blades, removing the clumsiness that limits other dual wielders.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature>

#### Unrestricted Off-Hand

You can use two-weapon fighting even when the one-handed melee weapons you are wielding aren't light.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Quick Draw

You can draw or stow two one-handed weapons when you would normally be able to draw or stow only one.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Defensive Rotation

While you are wielding a melee weapon in each hand, you gain a **+1 bonus to AC**.

</Feature>

</Feat>
