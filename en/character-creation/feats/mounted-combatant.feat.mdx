# Mounted Combatant

<Feat ability="Constitution, Dexterity, Strength, or Wisdom">

You have forged a battle-bond with your mount through countless skirmishes across the lands of Damocles. When mounted, you and your steed move as one — a single instrument of war.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature targets="any unmounted creature that is smaller than your mount">

#### Mounted Superiority

You have advantage on melee attack rolls against the creature.

</Feature>

<Feature trigger="when your mount is targeted by an attack">

#### Rider's Ward

You can force the attack to target you instead.

</Feature>

<Feature trigger="if your mount is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage">

#### Mounted Evasion

It instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Steady Bond

You can command your mount to use the [Steady](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/movement-terrain-and-traversal#interactions) action. When you do so, you both use it and both count as if steady.

</Feature>

</Feat>
