# Fold Stepping

<Feat
  category="epic boon"
  ability="Wisdom or Constitution">

The world is folded. Every place lies a breath away from every other, if one knows where the seam runs.
You have walked the wild long enough to feel those creases underfoot.

Where others take a step, you take the shortest one there is.

> “Distance is a courtesy the Fold extends. It may be declined.”

---

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefit:

<Feature trigger="immediately after you use a **Major Action** on your turn">

#### Foldstep

You can teleport up to **[= 2 stride =]** to an unoccupied space you can see.

</Feature>

</Feat>
