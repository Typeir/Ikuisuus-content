# Ire of the Revenant

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Constitution**, **Strength** or **Dexterity** 19."
  ability="Constitution, Strength, or Dexterity">

You have fallen in combat countless time, allowing you to muster strength before losing consciousness.

---

When you select this feat you gain the following benefits:

- Once per [# kw:Repose #], when you drop below 1 hit point but are not killed outright, you immediately take a full turn.

- During this turn, you have advantage on every roll.

- After this turn ends, you fall [# kw:condition:unconscious #] as normal unless you have more than 0 hit points.

- You can use this feature once per [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feat>
