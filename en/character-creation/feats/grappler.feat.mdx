# Grappler

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Strength 13 (or higher)**"
  ability="Strength">

You learned close-quarters grappling from the Graspbrood of the Black Cradle, mastering holds and locks taught in its iron yards.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature>

#### Advantage on [# kw:condition:grappled #] attacks

You have advantage on attack rolls against a creature you are grappling.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Major Action" targets="a creature [# kw:condition:grappled #] by you">

#### Pin

You can try to pin the creature. To do so, make another grapple check. If you succeed, you and the creature are both [# kw:condition:restrained #] until the grapple ends.

</Feature>

</Feat>
