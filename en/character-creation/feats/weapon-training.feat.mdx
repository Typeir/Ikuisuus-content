# Weapon Training

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Weapon Familiarity**"
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

You have drilled an art past the point your vocation took it.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain **Weapon Proficiency**: you gain one more Familiar art, and one of your Familiar arts becomes Proficient.

</Feat>
