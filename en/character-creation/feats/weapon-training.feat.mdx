# Weapon Training

<Feat
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

You have studied the blade and its principles.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain **Weapon Proficiency**: gain familiarity in one art you do not already hold, and proficiency in one you are familiar with. If you held none, you instead gain familiarity in two arts you did not hold.

</Feat>
