# Weapon Training

<Feat
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

You have studied the blade and its principles.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain **Weapon Proficiency**: one more Familiar art, one you do not already hold, and one of the Familiar arts you held before taking this feat becomes Proficient. If you held none, you instead gain two Familiar arts you do not already hold.

</Feat>
