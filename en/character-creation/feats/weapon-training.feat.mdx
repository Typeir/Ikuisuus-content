# Weapon Training

<Feat
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

You have studied the blade and its principles.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain **Weapon Proficiency**: gain familiarity in one art you are not already Familiar with, and proficiency in one you are Familiar with. If you are Familiar with none, you instead gain familiarity in two arts.

</Feat>
