# Anima Warrior

<Feat>

A splinter of the primordial forces of the world is lodged within your will, granting you the ability to briefly call upon the mantle of existence to bolster your attack.

---

Increase **any** of your scores by 1.

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature
  trigger="whenever you deal damage from any source"
  targets="a target within [= 20 stride =] of you"
  charges="tier bonus/[# kw:Recovery #]">

#### Anima Assault

You may choose to expend one use of this feature to bolster the damage one of the following effects:

  - **Scorching Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d6 %] **fire damage**, and saves DEX or is affected by the [# kw:condition:Burning #] condition.
  - **Sleet Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d6 %] **frost damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves CON, or its speed is reduced to 0 until the end of your round.
  - **Storm Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d6 %] **lightning damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves CON, or it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of your round, and is [# kw:condition:Staggered #].
  - **Stone Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d8 %] **bludgeoning damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves STR or is displaced [= 2 stride =] away from you from you in a straight line.
  - **Severing Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d10 %] **slashing damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves CON or is affected by the [# kw:condition:Bleeding #] condition.
  - **Skewering Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d10 %] **piercing damage**. If you can effectively grapple the target, it saves DEX or is [# kw:condition:Grappled #] by you.
  - **Sulfuric Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d8 %] **chemical damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves CON or gains the [# kw:condition:Sundered #] condition [# kw:briefly #].
  - **Serpentine Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d12 %] **poison damage**, and saves CON or gains the [# kw:condition:Poisoned #] condition until the end of your round.
  - **Synaptic Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d8 %] **psychic damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves WIS or falls [# kw:condition:Prone #].
  - **Sonic Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d10 %] **force damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves WIS, or becomes [# kw:condition:Deafened #] until the end of your round.
  - **Seraphic Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d6 %] **holy damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves CHA or is [# kw:condition:Blinded #] until the end of your round.
  - **Shadow Strike**. The target takes an extra [% 1d8 %] **dark damage**. If the target is a creature, it saves WIS or is [# kw:condition:Frightened #] until the end of your round.
  - **Supreme Strike**. All the damage from your attack becomes **true damage**, and it deals extra damage equal to **half your tier bonus**, **rounded down**, to a minimum of 1.

You choose which effect to apply when you take this feat, and you may change your choice whenever you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

These effects are keyed to the ability you increased when you took this feat.
