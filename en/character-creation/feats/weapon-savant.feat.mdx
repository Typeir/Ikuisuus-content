# Weapon Savant

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Weapon Expert**, level 11"
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

There is nothing left in your art that you have not made your own.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain **Weapon Savant**: you gain one more art you are not already Familiar with at each rung below Savant, and one of your Expert arts becomes Savant. With a weapon of the Savant art you apply both of its masteries when you hit.

</Feat>
