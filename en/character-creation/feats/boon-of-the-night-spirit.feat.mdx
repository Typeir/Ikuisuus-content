# Epic Boon: Night Spirit

Beneath the mangled moon, the dark is not an absence — it is a tenant, patient and old.
Steal into it often enough and it learns your shape, then lends that shape back to no one.

You do not hide in shadow. Shadow hides in you.

> “The dark keeps its own. Ask it, and it will keep you.”

---

Increase your Dexterity or Wisdom score by 1.

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- **Shadowmeld.**  
  While you are in **Dim Light** or **Darkness**, as a _minor action_, you can take the **Magic** action to become **Invisible**, along with anything you are wearing or carrying.  
  You remain Invisible until you move, take an action, or take a Reaction.

- **Umbral Ambush.**  
  The first time you hit a creature while Invisible in this way, it takes an extra [% 2d10 %] **Psychic** damage.
