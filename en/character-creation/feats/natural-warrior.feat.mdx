# Natural Warrior

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Strength Score 13** or **Dexterity Score 13**, a set of natural weapons or the Unarmed Fighting feat."
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

Your body is a weapon, honed to perfection.

---

- When you take this feat you gain the following benefits:

- Your natural weapons are Fisted weapons, and you are **Expert** in [_Pummeling_](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-arts#the-twelve-arts) or [_Knifing_](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-arts#the-twelve-arts), your choice.
- Your natural weapons gain an increase in their dice level, as shown in the rules for [_Weapons And Dice Levels_](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapons-and-dice-levels).

- Your natural weapons have the following mastery and Masterful Blow, depending on their damage type:

| Damage Type | Mastery | Masterful Blow |
| ----------- | ------- | -------------- |
| Bludgeoning | Push    | Tackling       |
| Piercing    | Vex     | Hemorrhaging   |
| Slashing    | Sap     | Hamstringing   |
| Other       | Graze   | Sundering      |

</Feat>
