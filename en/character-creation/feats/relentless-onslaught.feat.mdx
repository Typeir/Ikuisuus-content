# Relentless Onslaught

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Extra Attack** feature"
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

You have learned to press your attacks relentlessly when focused on a single target, never letting up until the fight is decided.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature>

#### Press the Target

Whenever you land subsequent attacks against the same target, each deals additional damage equal to half your **tier bonus** (rounded down).
This bonus damage is applied to each attack after the first, and it stacks with itself for each additional attack you make against the same target on your turn.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" trigger="if you only attack a single target on your turn" charges="tier bonus/[# kw:Recovery #]">

#### Endless Barrage

You may make one additional attack against that target.

</Feature>

</Feat>
