# Heavy Armor Master

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Defense** Fighting Style, **Proficiency with heavy armor**"
  ability="Strength or Constitution">

Your discipline in wearing heavy armor is total. You have trained yourself to turn aside punishment that would fell lesser warriors.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature>

#### Damage Reduction

While wearing **heavy armor**, you have **damage reduction** equal to your **tier bonus** against **physical** damage.

</Feature>

</Feat>
