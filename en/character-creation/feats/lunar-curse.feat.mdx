# Lunar Curse

<Feat category="origin">

Through forbidden rites of rebirth, lunar fleshcraft, a failed moonblessing—or something worse—you have learned to briefly reshape your body at will.

---

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action" charges="tier bonus/[# kw:Recovery #]">

#### Lunar Shifting

You can enter a **lunar shifting** state. This transformation lasts for **1 minute**, until you die, or until you end it early as a Minor Action.

- When you enter lunar shifting, you gain **temporary hit points equal to 2 × your tier bonus**.

- While shifted, you gain **one Lunar Adaptation** of your choice from the list below. You choose the adaption when you take the feat.

- Additionally, while sifted, you gan darkvision out to a range of **[= 12 stride =]**, and, if you already have darkvision, its range increases by **[= 6 stride =]**.

</Feature>

## Lunar Adaptations

<Feature>

#### Beasthide

You gain **[% 1d6 %] additional temporary hit points**, and while shifted, you gain a **+1 bonus to Armor Class**.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action">

#### Longtooth

While shifted, you can make an unarmed strike with elongated fangs. On a hit, the target takes **[% 1d6 %] + your Strength modifier piercing damage**.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Swiftstride

While shifted, your walking speed increases by **[= 2 stride =]**. In addition, as an [# kw:delayed #] effect, when a creature is within [= 1 stride =] of you, you can use your reaction to move up to **[= 2 stride =]** without provoking opportunity attacks.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Wildhunt

While shifted, you have **advantage on Wisdom checks**, and no creature within **[= 6 stride =]** of you can make attack rolls with advantage against you unless you are [# kw:condition:incapacitated #].

</Feature>

</Feat>
