# Colossal Weaponmaster

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Strength Score 17**"
  ability="Strength">

You excel in wielding weapons that were never meant for you. You do so not cleanly, but decisively.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- You may choose to ignore any source of advantage on an attack roll you make with a weapon of a size larger than you. Make this choice before rolling. If you do, you gain a **+2 accuracy and damage bonus** on that attack. This choice applies even if that advantage would otherwise be canceled by disadvantage.

<Feature charges="1/turn" trigger="when you make an attack with a weapon of a size larger than you">

#### Colossal Extremes

Apply the first matching result below.

- **Perfect Contact.** When you roll at **disadvantage** and **both dice would hit**, if one of them is a **critical strike**, the attack is treated as a **critical hit**.

- **Shockwave.** When you roll at **disadvantage**, **neither die would hit**, and your arts cover the weapon, you immediately unleash a shockwave at the point of impact.
  Creatures in a **[= 3 stride;ADJ =] cube centered on the target** save STR or take **[% 2d8 bludgeoning %]** and are displaced [= 2 stride =] away from you.

</Feature>

</Feat>
