# Swift and Steady

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Extra Attack** feature, **Dexterity 15**"
  ability="Dexterity">

You have trained to combine disciplined stillness with explosive efficiency — when you plant your feet, you attack faster.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- **Steady Strikes.** While you are in the [# kw:condition:steady #] state, you may make **one additional melee weapon attack** as part of the **Attack** action on your turn.

</Feat>
