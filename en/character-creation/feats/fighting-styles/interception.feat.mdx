# Interception

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Proficiency with shields** or **a finesse weapon**"
  ability="Constitution">

You intercept blows aimed at your allies, putting your own body or blade between them and harm.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefit:

- When a creature you can see **hits a target other than you** that is within [= 1 stride =] of you with an attack, you can use your **reaction** to grant the target **damage reduction** equal to **1d10 + your Tier Bonus + your level** against that attack.

When you use Steady, you gain access to the following technique:

- Your reaction range extends to **[= 3 stride =]**. Additionally, you can move yourself to any point within **[= 1 stride =] of the target** as part of using this technique.

</Feat>
