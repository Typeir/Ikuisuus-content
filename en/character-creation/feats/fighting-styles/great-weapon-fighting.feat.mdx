# Great Weapon Fighting

<Feat
  category="fighting style"
  prerequisite="**Strength Score 15**"
  ability="Strength">

You train to channel your full weight and power into sweeping, devastating blows.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefit:

- A damage roll for a **Two-handed weapon, or a weapon gripped in two hands,** is floored at 3.

When you use Steady, you gain access to the following technique:

- Your melee attack range with Two-handed weapons and weapons gripped in two hands extends by **[= 1 stride =]**.

</Feat>
