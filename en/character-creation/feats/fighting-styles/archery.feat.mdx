# Archery

<Feat
  category="fighting style"
  prerequisite="**Proficiency with any ranged martial weapon**"
  ability="Dexterity">

You have trained extensively in the discipline of ranged combat.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefit:

- You gain a **+2 accuracy bonus** to attacks you make with ranged weapons.

When you use Steady, you gain access to the following technique:

- Designate a **[= 1 stride =] sphere** that you can see within your range. Enemies that move through it without starting their turn there provoke opportunity attacks from you.

</Feat>
