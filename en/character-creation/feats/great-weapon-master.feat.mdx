# Great Weapon Master

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Great Weapon Fighting** style"
  ability="Strength">

You have learned to put the momentum of a heavy weapon to devastating use — every swing carries the full weight of your strength and will.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- **Cleave Through.** When you score a **critical hit** with a melee weapon or reduce a creature to 0 hit points with one, you can make one melee weapon attack **as a Minor Action**.

- **Grand Blows** When you deal damage to a creature using a heavy weapon, you deal extra damage equal to your **tier bonus**.

</Feat>
