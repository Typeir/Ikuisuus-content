# Great Weapon Master

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Great Weapon Fighting** style"
  ability="Strength">

You have learned to put the momentum of a heavy weapon to devastating use — every swing carries the full weight of your strength and will.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action" trigger="when you score a **critical hit** with a melee weapon or reduce a creature to 0 hit points with one">

#### Cleave Through

You can make one melee weapon attack.

</Feature>

<Feature trigger="when you deal damage to a creature using a unwieldy weapon">

#### Grand Blows

You deal extra damage equal to your **tier bonus**.

</Feature>

</Feat>
