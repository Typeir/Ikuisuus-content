# Epic Boon: Spell Recall

A spell, once truly learned, is not spent when it is cast — only set down for a moment.
Walk long enough beside the Engine and you learn what the First Pilgrim learned: some things refuse to end.

The words return to you as though they had never left your tongue.

> “What was cast is not lost. It is only remembered.”

---

Increase your Intelligence or Charisma score by 1.

When you select this feat, you gain the following benefit:

- **Recalled Casting.**  
   When you can cast a spell you have prepared of level 4 or lower, you roll 1d4. if it lands on a 4, you cast the spell without expending a spell slot.
