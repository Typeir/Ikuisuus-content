# Weapon Expert

<Feat
  prerequisite="**Weapon Training**, level 5"
  ability="Strength or Dexterity">

You fight the way the masters of your art fight.

---

When you take this feat, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain **Weapon Expertise**: you gain one more art at each rung below Expert, and one of your Proficient arts becomes Expert. With a weapon of the Expert art you apply one of its two masteries when you hit.

</Feat>
