The history of the sunborn is as old and convoluted as time itself.
The very first of their clade were created by the traitorous Demiurge as unerring warriors and protectors to fight in the [War of Shapes](/en/library/world/events/war-of-shapes), while the simpler [_Bilupine_](/en/library/character-creation/bloodlines/bilupine) slaved away in endless toil.
The original name for this creation is lost to time, the first thing time took from them.
Though when the war finally ended and they were left to their own devices, their warring nature quickly became subsumed by the weight of mundanity; the simple tasks and chores of daily life turned them into a people like any other, citizens of the fourth star, eventually adopted by the [Empyrean](/en/library/world/the-lands-of-damocles/thealas) as a people of their Empire.
The peace, however, lasted little. The First Pilgrimage ravaged the Empyrean infrastructure, and the five families' observatories slowly fell into ruin, becoming a breeding ground of conflict and misery.
Their Creator, the Demiurge, took pity on them, and through his craft he **Concealed** the sunborn within the regular fauna of Taiva, stripping them of their deep consciousness; a mercy in fear of the times of turmoil.
Though the convulsing river of time eventually would have it that another maddened, vestigial God of creation would come to Taiva, and the empire's Apostasy would end the new god in time too.
With its dying breath, spiteful Päivämies cursed the sunborn with the **Gift of Consciousness**, and the ability to **Conceal** themselves from the world faded; the Demiurge's mercy was undone.
With the gift came the largest migratory crisis Damocles had ever seen, and with the crisis came an eternity of enslavement and discrimination.
Yet again, through resilience, community, and a shared want to finally be rid of the whims of time, the sunborn survived through all major catastrophic events, the **Wounding**, **Scarring** and **The Eternity of the Four**
Until a hero rose from within their blood, **Ludwig Bloodboil** would eventually come to end the eternity of the four, by defeating, or some say **Sealing**, the four great **Hiisi**; subsequently cementing the sunborn as an honorable and dependable people,
And finally freeing them of the burdens the macabre flow of time had imposed on them.
The sunborn, in current times, are the single most populous group of intelligent creatures on the face of Taiva, by a wide margin.
Though what constitutes a sunborn is eternally debated, and the debates often end in muteness over whether the term even carries a meaning.
