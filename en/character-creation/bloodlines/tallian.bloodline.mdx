# Tallian

Tallians are ancient biomechanical constructs first forged in the Fourth Star, before the [War of Shapes](/en/library/world/events/war-of-shapes) took place. 
These machines are native to a long forgotten conflict which culminated in the Great Glaux's fleet reduced to just one vessel; and the Great Houses scrubbed to just four.
Initially built as servitors, escorts, and battlefield engines for the imperial houses that would eventually root themselves in [Thealas](/en/library/world/the-lands-of-damocles/thealas),
most were hastily put into stasis after the collapse of that order, in vain hopes of a future palingenesis of the empire. In time, the memory of them would erode, their directives frozen for ages while dust and the passage of time settled within their joints.

In the current age, rediscovered millenia after the fall of the **Eternal Tetrarchy** and the subsequent Thealassian Empire, a small fraction of these chassis roam the lands, the rebirth of the star-faring empire was short lived and catastrophic.
Many awoke with incomplete mission trees, obsolete command hierarchies, and no consensus for what duty means in a world that no longer resembles the one that made them.

After the scarring, whatever faint murmur of purpose and direction the Tallians could've grasped was snuffed out in the agonizing cries of Taiva, and with time even the memory of this faded into nothingness.

The Tallians now merely exist among the ruins, some refusing to interact with the world around them, others becoming a new form of life among the new ecosystems.
Yet most anchor themselves to whatever lucky or unlucky Empyrean they come across, hoping for a legacy no surviving bloodline can give them.

### Inertness

Your frame is not sustained by living tissue.

- You cannot regain hit points from spells, potions, features, or effects unless your Repair Window is active.
- Any temporary hit points you gain are doubled.
- This does not change your temporary hit point cap from any effect.

</Feature>

<Feature>

### Construct Nature

You are a biomechanical construct, not a living organism.

- You need to eat, drink and breathe, though you do so via different internal processes than a typical creature. You cannot be fed by a creature if they do not succed on a DC 20 **Tinkering** check.
- You do not sleep. To gain the benefits of a [# kw:Repose #], you must spend 4 hours in an inert but aware maintenance state. [# kw:Recovery #] take 6 days of uninterrupted resting with no heavy activity.
- You have advantage on saving throws against the [# kw:condition:poisoned #] condition.

</Feature>

<Feature>

### Repair Window

You cannot heal normally during a [# kw:repose #] nor a [# kw:recovery #].
In order to heal, you need your interface wired into repair mode.

This can be achieved with an use of an [_Electricians Kit_](/en/library/items/tools/electrics) and the **Robotics** Specialty,
or a [_Tinkers Kit_](/en/library/items/tools/tinkering) and the **Drones** Specialty. This process takes **10 minutes**.

The [Mending](/en/library/spells/mending) spell can briefly restore your healing interface too.

- During this window, you are vulnerable to all damage, and have disadvantage on all rolls, as your innards are exposed.
- During this window, you can regain hit points as normal.
- You can spend one hit die to regain hit points during your Repair Window. When you do so, the repair window immediately ends.

</Feature>

<Feature>

### Maintenance Recovery

Your spent Hit Dice are restored as normal when you finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

<Feature>

### Programmed Excellence

You gain all of the following benefits until the next [# kw:Repose #]:

- Choose one condition: [# kw:condition:blinded #], [# kw:condition:charmed #], [# kw:condition:deafened #], [# kw:condition:frightened #], [# kw:condition:paralyzed #], [# kw:condition:poisoned #]. You are immune to that condition.
- Choose one elemental damage type: chemical, frost, fire, lightning, or psychic. You gain resistance to that damage type.
- Choose one skill and one tool. You gain proficiency in both.

</Feature>

You can speak, read, and write Common and Empyrean.

</Feature>
