# Armour in Damocles

### Armour Categories and Shields

- Armour in the game is organized into three categories: light armour, medium armour, and heavy armour. Many warriors also use shields.
- The armour table collects the most commonly available types of armour and lists their cost, weight, and other properties. Armour types range from leather to chain mail to costly plate armour, with other variations between.

### Armour Proficiency

- Anyone can put on a suit of armour or strap a shield to an arm. Only those with proficiency in the armour's use know how to wear it effectively.
- Proficiency in armour is granted by features from your vocation, bloodline, feats or other sources. If you wear armour that you lack proficiency with, you suffer the following mechanical penalties:
  - You have **disadvantage** on any ability check, saving throw, or attack roll that involves Strength or Dexterity.
  - You **cannot cast spells** while wearing armour you are not proficient with.

### Defence

Armour sets your Deflect and a shield adds to your Dodge; both are read from the tables below.

You can dodge while in armour, but more restrictive armour will impose a cap into how much of your DEX contributes to your Dodge. Dodge from other sources is unaffected.

## Clothing

Clothing is worn, but does not carry any deviance from the base 10 + Dodge + Deflect calculations. It can, however, if it is of a quality above mundane, increase Deflect as the rungs describe.

## Proficiency

Armour Proficiency: Anyone can put on a suit of armour or strap a Shield to an arm. Only those proficient in the armour’s use know how to wear it effectively, however. Your vocation gives you proficiency with certain types of armour. If you wear armour that you lack proficiency with, you have disadvantage on any ability check, saving throw, or Attack roll that involves Strength or Dexterity, and you can’t cast Spells.

Strength Requirement: If the Armour table shows “Str 13” or “Str 15” in the Strength column for an armour type, the armour reduces the wearer’s speed by [= 2 stride =] unless the wearer has a Strength score equal to or higher than the listed score.
