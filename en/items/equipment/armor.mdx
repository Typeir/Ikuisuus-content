# Armor in Damocles

### Armor Categories and Shields

- Armor in the game is organized into three categories: light armor, medium armor, and heavy armor. Many warriors also use shields.
- The armor table collects the most commonly available types of armor and lists their cost, weight, and other properties. Armor types range from leather to chain mail to costly plate armor, with other variations between.

### Armor Proficiency

- Anyone can put on a suit of armor or strap a shield to an arm. Only those with proficiency in the armor's use know how to wear it effectively.
- Proficiency in armor is granted by features from your vocation, bloodline, feats or other sources. If you wear armor that you lack proficiency with, you suffer the following mechanical penalties:
  - You have **disadvantage** on any ability check, saving throw, or attack roll that involves Strength or Dexterity.
  - You **cannot cast spells** while wearing armor you are not proficient with.

### Armor Class (AC)

- Armor (and shields) determine your base Armor Class. The exact base AC value depends on the specific armor or shield being worn.

## Proficiency

Armor Proficiency: Anyone can put on a suit of armor or strap a Shield to an arm. Only those proficient in the armor’s use know how to wear it effectively, however. Your vocation gives you proficiency with certain types of armor. If you wear armor that you lack proficiency with, you have disadvantage on any ability check, saving throw, or Attack roll that involves Strength or Dexterity, and you can’t cast Spells.

Strength Requirement: If the Armor table shows “Str 13” or “Str 15” in the Strength column for an armor type, the armor reduces the wearer’s speed by [= 2 stride =] unless the wearer has a Strength score equal to or higher than the listed score.
