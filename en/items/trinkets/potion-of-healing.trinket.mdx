# Potion of Healing

Adventuring Gear

A stoppered vial of bright red fluid that smells faintly of iron and crushed herbs.
Drinking a potion takes an Action, administering it to another creature takes a Major action.
The amount each potion heals depends on it's quality, as described in [_Potions of Healing_](https://ikuisuus.com/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/potions-of-healing).

**Damage**: —  
**Properties**: Special (healing, consumable)  
**Range**: touch  
**Weight**: 0.[= 3 burden =].
