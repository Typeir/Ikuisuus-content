# Silver Ointment

Adventuring Gear

A pot of argent paste, worked into a blade's edge with a cloth and left to cure.
As a Major Action, you can treat one weapon or up to five pieces of ammunition.
The treatment lasts until the end of your next [# kw:Repose #], or until the weapon scores ten hits, whichever comes first.

While treated, the weapon counts as silvered: creatures that resist or ignore mundane weapon damage take full damage from it.
The treatment does not change the weapon's damage die, damage type, properties, or Mastery.
