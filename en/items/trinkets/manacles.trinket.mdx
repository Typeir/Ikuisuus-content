# Manacles

<Trinket
  category="Adventuring Gear"
  damage="—"
  range="melee"
  properties="Special (restraint)"
  burden="[= 3 burden =]">

Heavy iron shackles that bind a Small or Medium creature.
Escaping the manacles requires a successful DC 20 Dexterity check.
Breaking the manacles requires a successful DC 20 Strength check.
Each set comes with one key. Without the key, a creature proficient with [Thievery](/en/library/items/tools/thievery) can pick the lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity check.
Manacles have 15 hit points.

</Trinket>
