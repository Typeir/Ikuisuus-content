# Bola

<Trinket
  category="Adventuring Gear"
  damage="—"
  range="30/60"
  properties="Thrown, Special (restrain, trip)"
  burden="[= 1 burden =]">

A set of weighted cords designed to entangle.
As a Major Action, you can throw the bola up to [= 6 stride =], treating it as a thrown weapon.
Make a ranged attack against a large or smaller creature; on a hit, the target must succed on a  (escape DC 14 Strength) or have it's movement speed set to zero and falls [# kw:condition:prone #].
The target must use a Major Action to free itself, it can also be freed by an ally or will be freed automatically if the Bola breaks.
