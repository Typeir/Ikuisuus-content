# Smoke-Flask

<Trinket
  category="Adventuring Gear"
  range="40"
  properties="Thrown, Area ([= 3 stride;ADJ =] radius), Obscuring, Consumable"
  burden="[= 1 burden =]">

A glass flask containing oil and soot.
As a Major Action, you can throw it up to [= 8 stride =], shattering it on impact.
The flask releases dense black smoke that fills a [= 3 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere for 1 minute or until dispersed by moderate wind.
The area is heavily obscured.
Drawing and throwing the flask counts as an object interaction.

</Trinket>
