# Firecracker

  category="Adventuring Gear"
  damage="1 (if immune to fear)"
  range="10/30"
  properties="Thrown, Special (frighten)"
  burden="[= 1 burden =] per 5">

A small clay tube packed with powder and fuse.
As a Major Action, you can throw the firecracker up to [= 6 stride =].
When lit, it explodes with light and sound.
Creatures within [= 3 stride =] that can see or hear it save Wisdom against DC 13 or become [# kw:condition:frightened #] [# kw:briefly #].
If thrown directly at a creature, it saves Dexterity against DC 13, or take [% 3d4 fire %].
Creatures immune to fear take 1 bludgeoning damage instead.
