# Lock

<Trinket
  category="Adventuring Gear"
  damage="—"
  range="—"
  properties="Special (security)"
  burden="[= 1 burden =]">

An iron padlock supplied with a matching key.
Without the key, a creature proficient with [Thievery](/en/library/items/tools/thievery) can pick the lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity check.
The Game Director may rule that better locks exist at higher prices, with different DCs.

</Trinket>
