# Basic Poison

<Trinket
  category="Adventuring Gear"
  damage="[% 1d4 %]"
  range="—"
  properties="Special (coating, consumable)"
  burden="—">

A vial of extracted venom or alchemical toxin for coating weapons.
As a Major Action, you can coat one slashing or piercing weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition.
The poison retains potency for 1 minute before drying.
A creature hit by a poisoned weapon or ammunition saves Constitution against DC 10 or takes [% 1d4 poison %].

**Damage Type**: Poison  

</Trinket>
