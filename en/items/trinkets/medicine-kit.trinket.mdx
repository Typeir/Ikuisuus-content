# Healer's kit

<Trinket
  category="Adventuring Gear"
  damage="—"
  range="touch"
  properties="Special"
  burden="[= 1 burden =]">

A compact kit containing bandages, salves, and tools for treating wounds.
As a Major Action, you can use the healer's kit to stabilize a creature that is dying or to tend to wounds.
Using the kit to stabilize a dying creature instantly clears their death saving throws and stabilizes them.
Alternatively, you can use the kit during a [# kw:Repose #] to restore hit points equal to your **Medicine** bonus, provided you are proficient in the medicine skill.
In order for a creature to benefit from this healing, they must spend at least one hit dice during the [# kw:Repose #].
You may only use a healer's kit once per [# kw:Repose #], and a creature may only benefit from one healer's kit during a [# kw:Repose #].

</Trinket>
