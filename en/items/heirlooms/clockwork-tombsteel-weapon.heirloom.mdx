# Clockwork Tombsteel Weapon

<Heirloom
  rarity="very rare"
  attunement="required"
  base="Any Martial Weapon (chosen on attunement)"
  quality="Superior"
  enchantment="+2 accuracy and damage bonus"
  masterfulBlow="Swap-Strike">

Forged in the final years of the Empyrean Empire, these weapons combine tombsteel cores with intricate clockwork gearing, allowing the wielder to shift its form mid-battle.
The mechanisms vent bursts of white-hot steam when activated, searing foes and staggering them under its weight.

> “No matter what the Empire ever did to stall it, the death of this world was engineered long ago.” — The Archivist

#### Swap-Strike <span>Masterful Blow</span>

As part of an attack with this weapon, you may trigger the clockwork mechanism, venting white-hot steam. Targets save Constitution (DC = 10 + your tier bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier). Failing targets are stunned until the start of your next turn and take an additional 10 fire damage.
