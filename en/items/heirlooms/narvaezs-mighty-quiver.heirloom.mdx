## Traits

<Trait>

#### Graze

When you **miss** an attack, the target still takes damage equal to your **Strength modifier**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Mighty Armament

This quiver requires 20 Strength to wield; otherwise, attacks are at disadvantage. Each sword weighs [= 3 burden =]. The quiver itself weighs [= 5 burden =]. It holds up to 10 special greatswords. Drawn blades lose Heavy and Two-Handed, but gain Thrown ([= 6 stride =]/[= 12 stride =]) and Brittle (breaks on impact).

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Weapon Compatibility

Standard greatswords suffer -5 to hit unless modified (5 gp, 1 hour by smith). Enchanted greatswords are forcefully ejected if placed in the quiver.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Proficiency Override

While attuned, you gain proficiency with Narvaez’s quiver swords.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Fatebound Link

A creature attuned to Narvaez’s Mighty Quiver cannot attune to Carranza’s Great Épée. If both wielders are within [= 12 stride =] of each other, they ignore the Mighty Armament restriction (Strength 20 no longer required), and greatswords stored in the quiver weigh [= 0 burden =] for carrying capacity. If the thread of fate is severed, these effects become permanent.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Fated Demise

If the wielder of Carranza’s Great Épée is [# kw:condition:unconscious #] or slain, and you are still alive, Carranza’s épée flies toward you if within [= 120 stride =], traveling at [= 24 stride =] per round or [= 12 stride =] per round through solid terrain. You automatically draw both weapons (no action), treating them as light weapons.

You gain advantage on all attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks. You are immune to all conditions except [# kw:condition:prone #] and [# kw:condition:exhaustion #]. You gain 1 level of [# kw:condition:exhaustion #] per round until you fall [# kw:condition:unconscious #].

If the fallen ally is revived, these effects end immediately, though [# kw:condition:exhaustion #] remains. You can choose to unbind the weapons, which severs their enchantment, inflicts 1 level of [# kw:condition:exhaustion #] (unless already at 5), and forces both wielders to unattune.

If you are more than [= 120 stride =] apart, you gain 1 [# kw:condition:exhaustion #] per day at dawn until the bond is restored or severed.

</Trait>

#### Storm of Blades

While under Fated Demise, when you take [# kw:assault #], you can replace one of your attacks with a casting of Call Lightning, where thunderous, lightning blades fall from the sky. This version works regardless of sky visibility, as the blades manifest from your quiver. You cast Call Lightning (DC = 10 + Strength modifier + tier bonus) as though using a 3rd-level spell slot. The storm cloud appears instantly over the target (no 10-minute casting delay). The spell uses Strength as your casting ability. You can call the bolt immediately on the same turn. The spell requires no verbal, somatic, or material components when cast this way. While Call Lightning is active, drawn greatswords crackle, dealing +[% 1d6 lightning %] on hit.
