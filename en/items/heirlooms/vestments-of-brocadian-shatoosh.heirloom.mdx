# Vestments of Brocadian Shahtoosh

<Heirloom
  rarity="Legendary"
  attunement="required"
  base="Studded Leather Armor"
  enchantment="+2"
  armorClass="14 + Dexterity modifier"
  stealth="Advantage"
  burden="[= 5 burden =]">

A leather vestment made of **Seekersilk**, **demon leather**, and the heat-conductive Shahtoosh wool shorn from the manes of **lunar manticores**.  
The resulting garment can stop a spear yet folds like smoke between the fingers.  
Few robes of such quality exist, for each demands a great sacrifice, be it willing or not.

> “There is finery in punishment and beauty in suffering.”  
> — Master Seamstress Petunya

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Dressed to Kill

While you are wearing this armor, you gain a **+2 bonus to initiative rolls**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Shahtoosh Insulation

You are **immune to mundane frost damage** and ignore all penalties from extreme cold environments.  
You are **resistant to magical frost damage**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Threaded Silence

The fabric dampens movement and sound.  
You have **advantage** on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to avoid detection.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### High Maintenance

The Brocadian Shahtoosh cannot be repaired by mundane means, and requires a master robesmaker familiar with both **silk** and **leather** techniques.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" charges="3 per [# kw:Recovery #]">

#### Demonic Brocadian Lamination

The layered weave absorbs kinetic shock. When you are hit by a melee or ranged attack, you may use your **reaction** to gain resistance to attack’s damage.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="at will">

#### Feather Descent

While wearing this armor, you can cast [_Feather Fall_](/en/library/spells/feather-fall) targeting only yourself, without expending a spell slot or using components.

</Feature>

</Heirloom>
