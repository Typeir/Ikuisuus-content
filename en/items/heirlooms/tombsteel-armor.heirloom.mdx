# Tombsteel Armor

<Heirloom
  rarity="Rare"
  attunement="by a creature proficient in heavy armor"
  base="Heavy Armor (Any)"
  burden="+[= 8 burden =] over base"
  stealth="Disadvantage">

Tombsteel was the hallmark alloy of the final Empyrean era—its cold silence a grim echo of that civilization's decline. Forged for the **Demon-Hunting Knights** and **Godslaying Battalions**, this armor is feared not for what it does, but for what it represents: the slow death of a world once governed by mechanical perfection.

Though forged of mundane alloy, tombsteel is nullifying: Fold-born magic falters and frays against its surface, while the older animistic currents of the Väkis pass through unbroken. The metal is unnaturally dense and eternally cold to the touch. To wear it is to **entomb oneself in duty**.

> "To move in this armor is to refuse rest. The body aches. The world remembers."

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Cold Eternal

While wearing this armor, any creature without resistance or immunity to frost damage saves Constitution against DC 13 at the end of each hour. Failing creatures gain 1 level of exhaustion. This effect applies even while a creature is unconscious or asleep.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Unyielding Defense

While wearing this armor, you gain a **+1 bonus to AC** from the unnerving rigidity and unnatural construction of the armor.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Stalwart Frame

You have **advantage on saving throws** against **being pushed, pulled, or knocked prone**.

</Trait>
