# Tombsteel “Hand” Ballista

A massive tombsteel ballista used to slay Päivätär during the first god-slaying crusades of the Empyrean era. Now an unwieldy and hazardous relic of that time.
Impossible to wield by a single person, these siege engines were mounted on large warships and crewed by teams of soldiers.
Yet perhaps a creature of immense strength and skill could manage to operate one alone.

> “These machines return the cold death that their winds brought to my homeland.” — Apatheria, Thief of Winds

### Traits

<Trait>

#### (Special, Loading)

The ballista can only be fired once per round, regardless of other factors.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Nullifying

The "Hand" Ballista is forged of tombsteel, which nullifies the arcane. Fold-born enchantments falter against it, while the older animistic currents of the Väkis pass through unbroken. Its effects cannot be dispelled and ignore magic resistance.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Siege Impact

Damage from this weapon is doubled against constructs, objects and structures.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature>

#### Great Equalizer

When the "Hand" Ballista hits or misses a target, the projectile detonates in a burst of tombsteel shards. All creatures within a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius of the impact point, other than the main target, must make a saving throw at a DC of 11 plus your tier bonus plus your Dexterity modifier. On a failure, a creature takes [% 2d10 piercing %] plus [% 2d10 frost %]. Objects and structures automatically fail the save.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Misfire

On a natural 1, the firing mechanism locks. The wielder must spend a Major Action and succeed on a DC 15 Strength check to reset it.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Push

On a hit, the target of this weapon is pushed [= 2 stride =] away from you.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Major Action" targets="a creature or surface within range">

#### Hookshot

Fire a Godslaying Harpoon at a creature or surface within range. On a hit, the target is impaled and grappled. The grappled creature has its movement speed halved. If the target is smaller than you, you may pull it up to [= 6 stride =] directly toward you. If the target is larger than you, you may instead reel yourself up to [= 6 stride =] directly toward it. If the target is an object or surface, you move the same distance toward the point of impact. The movement from this effect does not provoke opportunity attacks. The chain does not retract until the creature escapes or you use a Minor Action to recall it.

</Feature>
