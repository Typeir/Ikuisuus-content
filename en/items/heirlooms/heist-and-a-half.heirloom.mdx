A whip of supple, ink-dark leather found within an ancient Silent One hoard of the [Hidden Kingdom](/en/library/world/the-lands-of-damocles/hidden-kingdom). 
Whenever it cracks and strikes true, it is said the hoard from whence it came is engorged, yet when it strikes false, that same hoard wanes.

> “No treasury can hold infinity, But mine will certainly be close.”  
> — Ozymandias, the Great Silent One, after the conquest and subsequent fall of Old Sauvan

#### Fifty-Two-Finger Discount <span>Masterful Blow</span>

When you hit a creature with this weapon, you may expend a use to make a **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check contested by the target's **Wisdom (Perception)** check. On a success, you steal one trinket the target carries and roll a **d100** on the **Stolen Trinkets** table below.
If the target carries no trinkets, you still come away with one — roll and use **half the result**, rounded up. No one is certain where these trinkets come from.

</Feature>
