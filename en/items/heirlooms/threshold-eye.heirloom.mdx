# Threshold Eye

<Heirloom
  rarity="uncommon"
  base="Monstrous Graft (Eye, Head)">

This magical eye replaces a real one that was lost or removed. While the Threshold Eye is embedded in your eye socket, you can see through the tiny orb as though it were your natural eye and any undead in your field of vision glows faintly unless they are protected against divination.

---

<Sheet>

## Grafting

Attaching this _minor monstrous graft_ to a humanoid requires an **8-hour surgical procedure** using **Alchemist’s Supplies**, **Surgical Tools**, and **Magical Healing**, with a healing spell required after each failed Medicine check, performed by a surgeon with **proficiency in Medicine** and the proper tools.

During the procedure, the subject saves Constitution against **DC 15** before **each Medicine check**, [# kw:chained #](8/1). Failing the chain permanently rejects the graft; if the graft is self-applied, they die instantly and violently instead.

Each hour requires the surgeon to check Wisdom (Medicine) **DC 15** or the subject takes **[% 6d6 poison %]** and the hour yields no progress. The procedure can be stopped at any moment. Stopping or interrupting it rejects the graft permanently.

The graft is **mundane** and **permanent** unless surgically removed, which destroys it.
