# Queenless Hive

_Very rare_  
_Musket (Martial, Two-Handed, Ranged 40/120, Mastery: Slow)_

The epochial tides of the corpse-god bring with them seasons most uncanny, a summer of boiling blood, and a winter of gored coagulation. Yet in the interstitial times between the extremes
The vermin that inhabit the dead moon set off to find a new nesting spot, leaving behind the hive that once nurtured them, along with the pupas and crysalis that would have become the next generation of the hive. 
In time, the walls of the hive harden into a stone-like caparace, and the hive becomes the tomb of a generation.
Some are lucky, and instead become subjects to a different queen altogether.

>  “The only Aerie that exists is that which you make your own” — Old Pieni proverb, coined during their Diaspora.

## Special Features

### Empty Hive  

The musket is a hollowed-out hive, and most of the vermin that once lived within it have left.
As a consequence, the barrel and the chamber can fit a plethora of ill-advised ammunition formats, such as [_Trinkets_](/en/library/items/trinkets) of no more than [= 2 burdens =] and one fourth of a [= 1 stride;ADJ =].

## Effects

### Sound the alarm

The Locusts that inhabit the hive warn against incoming danger, shrieking when alerted by hostile pressence.

When a creature whose [# kw:disposition #] towards the wielder is hostile comes within [= 6 stride;ADJ =] of the wielder, and the wielder does not perceive them or is [# kw:condition:incapacitated #], the weapon automatically fires a warning shot.
If the wielder would've been [# kw:surprised #], they are not, and instead gain advantage on their initiative roll.
Up to **TB** creatures of the wielder's choice within [= 6 stride;ADJ =] of the wielder are also not [# kw:surprised #] and gain advantage on their initiative rolls.
This feature consumes an use of the weapon's [# kw:masterful blow #].

### Emptying the Hive

When you take the Attack action with this weapon, if a [_Trinket_](/en/library/items/trinkets) is loaded, that trinket's range increases to that of this weapon, and the trinket's effects are applied as if it were a ranged attack with this weapon.
If the trinket would deal no damage on an attack roll, it instead deals [% 1d10 bludgeoning %].
If the trinket would affect something other than a Creature, like a [_lock_](/en/library/items/trinkets/lock) or a [_Tinderbox_](/en/library/items/trinkets/tinderbox) , it will apply its effects as normal on a hit, provided it is shot at a valid target.
Trinkets with several uses will **expend one use** on a hit, and be **destroyed** afterwards, **regardless of remaining** uses.
Attacks made this way are considered to be **made with the musket**, and the wielder adds their **bonuses as normal**.
Attacks made this way gain a** +1 bonus** to [# kw:accuracy #] and [# kw:damage #].
