# Astral Stonework

<Heirloom
  rarity="Rare"
  attunement="by a Druid of Stars">

A slab of dense astral shale resembling a worn grave marker, carved with drifting constellations that seem to rearrange themselves whenever the stone is held. Its glyphs are unmistakably written by Väkis, though whatever message they once carried is long lost.

> “☐☐☐☐ ☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐ ☐☐☐ ☐ ☐☐ ☐☐ ☐☐☐☐☐ ☐☐☐☐ ☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐” — Some Väki, forgotten in time.

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Starry Form Enhancements

While attuned to the Astral Stonework, whenever you activate Starry Form you immediately gain temporary hit points equal to your druid level + your Wisdom modifier.

</Trait>

<Trait cost="When the Chalice constellation triggers and you target a creature with a healing spell">

#### Chalice — Overflowing Chalice

The secondary healing from the constellation always restores the maximum possible amount.

</Trait>
