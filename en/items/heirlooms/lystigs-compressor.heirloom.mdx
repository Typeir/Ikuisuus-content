# Lystig's Compressor

<Heirloom
  rarity="very rare"
  attunement="required">

> “Believe me when I say Gunpowder, Alchemical Precursor, and a metal pot is one hell of a combination. Don't ask me how, just believe me.”
>
> - Lystig Curio, hobbit mercenary

---

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Crafting and Binding

Crafting and fitting this compressor into a gun requires proficiency with [Tinkering](/en/library/items/tools/tinkering) and [Alchemy](/en/library/items/tools/alchemy), and 100 gp worth of materials. A gun fitted with this compressor becomes a magical object.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Destruction on Removal

If the compressor is removed from the gun, both the compressor and the gun break.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature charges="[# kw:tier bonus #] per refuel">

#### Cantrip Channeling

When the compressor is fitted or when you forgo a [# kw:Repose #], you can fuel it with 10 gp worth of alchemical materials in order to cast a cantrip along with a gunshot. Melee spell attack components are treated as ranged. Only the gun attack is rolled; necessary attack rolls from the cantrip are not made. If the cantrip requires a saving throw, it is rolled as normal, but its range becomes that of the gun, provided they share targets or origin. Cantrips with a range of self are ineligible. Cantrips with a range of touch can be cast using the gun's range, provided they share targets. The gun grants +1 [# kw:accuracy #] and damage on attacks made with it when using this feature.

</Feature>

</Heirloom>
