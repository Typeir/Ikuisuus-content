# Inverted Kratteous Core

<Heirloom rarity="legendary" attunement="required" base="Monstrous Graft (Core, Chest)">

When the empyreans crossed the Everdark, Daedal mechanisms kept their husks alive beyond flesh. Against a creature as vital as the Dread Lord, this generator inverts its function, channeling overflowing energy into herculean feats.

Once implanted, the core fuses with your chest and becomes part of your body.

> “I am in control; even the beat of my heart is of my own volition. Can you say the same?” — Nekarion, the Dreadlord.

## Grafting

Attaching this major monstrous graft to a humanoid requires a 16-hour surgical procedure. The procedure uses Alchemist’s Supplies, Surgical Tools, and Magical Healing (one healing spell per failed check to stabilize the patient), and requires a surgeon with proficiency in Medicine and the necessary tools.

During the procedure, the subject saves Constitution against DC 20 before every Medicine check, [# kw:chained #](16/1). Failing the chain permanently rejects this graft. If self-applied, the subject dies instantly and violently as their body rots away.

Each hour of the procedure requires the surgeon to check Wisdom (Medicine) DC 40 or the subject takes [% 10d6 dark %] and the hour yields no progress. The procedure can be stopped at any moment. Stopping or interrupting it rejects the graft permanently.

This graft is permanent until surgically removed, which destroys it. If attachment fails, it cannot be healed or mended magically.

Alternatively, you may stab your heart with the core, taking [% 100d4 true %] in the process. The graft roots into your chest permanently. Removing it deals [% 100d4 true %].
