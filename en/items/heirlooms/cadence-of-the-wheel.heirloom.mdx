# Cadence of the Wheel

<Heirloom

Originally a weapon of entropy wielded by a foolish minion of Nigredo, fate and time transformed it into a beacon of perdurance and enmity towards the Lord of Flies and Rot.

On one end, a fossilized skeletal hand beckons death as a friend, crumbling the illusion of eternity. Wrapped along the shaft, thin layers of parchment hold the memories and legacy of a whole dynasty who danced with entropy and perdured, a testimony of their arcane wielders who studied for millennia the secrets that keep the wheel of change moving.

If someone unattuned holds this heirloom, an overwhelming dissonance creeps into their senses. It seems that only those who deeply comprehend the mysteries of entropy can aspire to keep up with the rhythm of the cycle.

> “To be forgotten is the only true death. I will bind our knowledge in the flesh of our fallen to make our struggles a lesson of beauty and perseverance. This will be our legacy.”  
> — Keyumar, the Scribe of Flesh

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Bizarre Spellbook

The staff functions as a spellbook for the wielder. It always contains all necromancy spells of the wizard spell list from level 1 to 9, as if they were always inscribed on its sheets of engraved parchment. The staff also rejects spells of 3rd level or lower that aren't from the necromancy school—the texts simply vanish as they are being copied onto its sheets.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Unsavory Tune

The staff vibrates with a dissonant pulse that unnerves unattuned holders. Any unattuned wielder loses the ability to use reactions until they drop the staff.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Adaptive Power

You gain an [# kw:accuracy #] bonus on spell attacks and a bonus to your spell save DC with necromancy school spells, each equal to half your tier bonus, rounded down.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Unfit for Brutality

Weapon damage dice are decreased by one step and the weapon lacks a mastery.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Dance Oblige

While attuned to the staff, you can't cast spells of 3rd level or lower that aren't from the necromancy school. In return, every spell you cast from the necromancy school is treated as being cast one level higher than the level you declare.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature>

#### Finale of Beauty

When a creature is reduced to 0 hit points by a necromancy spell you cast, it is decomposed and rapidly covered in moss, fungi, and small flora. The creature can't become undead.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Magic Action" charges="1/next dusk">

#### Unpredictable Sway

While concentrating on the spell **Danse Macabre**, you gain a bonus to your AC and saving throws equal to the number of undead summoned by the spell that are still under your control. You can cast the **Danse Macabre** spell without expending a spell slot.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### False Grace

While under the **False Life** spell, your Armor Class becomes **13 + your Dexterity modifier** and any critical hit against you counts as a normal hit instead.

</Feature>

</Heirloom>
