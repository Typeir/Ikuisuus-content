# The Kalymnos

<Heirloom
  rarity="Legendary"
  attunement="by a creature with Strength 24 or higher"
  base="Clothing (Hat)"
  burden="[= 300 burden =]">

An impossibly large, slanted hat with a vast wing and a conical top, made of layered hide and cloth.
Its craftsmanship seems unremarkable, yet for some reason, most who witness it cannot shake the feeling that this object is the very embodiment of **“hatness,”** if such a thing exists.
Perhaps it is yet another splinter from the idea of the thing it represents—the Hat.

> “It is unknown where the association between wizards and pointy hats comes from, yet it is proven right time and time again.” — Loitsunme, of the Hidden Society

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Arcane Conduit

While wearing **The Kalymnos**, your spells and your attacks made using the hat gain a **+4 [# kw:accuracy #] and [# kw:damage bonus #]**. That [# kw:accuracy #] raises the DCs of the saving throws those spells and attacks impose by the same amount.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Heavy Is the Hat

To wear **The Kalymnos**, your Strength score must be **24 or higher**. If it is lower, the hat collapses forward when you attempt to don it, and you are **knocked [# kw:condition:prone #] and [# kw:condition:restrained #]** beneath it until removed. Removing the hat from a [# kw:condition:restrained #] creature requires a **DC 25 Strength (Athletics)** check made as a **Major Action**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Massive Fit

**The Kalymnos** can only be worn by creatures **Large or larger**. Smaller creatures attempting to wear it automatically fail and are considered **crushed** ([# kw:condition:restrained #]) beneath its weight until freed.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Indestructible Fabric

The hat cannot be resized, cut, altered, or destroyed by any known means.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action" charges="1" recharge="after a [# kw:Repose #]">

#### Bouncing Hat

When you throw **The Kalymnos**, it transforms into a spinning hazard. You are **proficient** with the hat, and you may use your **Intelligence** or **Dexterity** modifier for the attack roll. Make a ranged attack with the hat against one target within [= 12 stride =] that deals 95 ([% 10d10 +40 slashing %]) on a hit.

After the first impact, the hat **ricochets** up to [= 12 stride =] in a straight line toward the nearest creature. Each [= 4 stride;ADJ =] stretch of the ricochet becomes **hazardous terrain** until the end of the next round, as sharp, glasslike blades erupt and swirl there. Entering that terrain deals 26 ([% 2d10 +15 slashing %]), and moving through it deals the same damage per [= 1 stride =]; the terrain counts as **difficult terrain**.

The hat may ricochet up to **3 times**. When it can no longer ricochet, **The Kalymnos** automatically returns to your head, dealing its full damage to any creatures it passes through on the way back.

</Feature>
