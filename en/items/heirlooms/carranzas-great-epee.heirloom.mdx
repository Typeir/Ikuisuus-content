### Traits

<Trait>

#### Mighty Armament

You must have Strength 20 or higher to wield this weapon effectively. If your Strength is less than 20, you make attack rolls with this weapon at disadvantage.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Upkeep Penalty

After [% 1d4 +1 %] encounters without maintenance, this weapon suffers -5 to attack rolls. Maintenance requires either 10 gp and 1 hour at a smithy, or 8 hours with a proficient alchemist or tinker.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Fatebound Link

A creature attuned to Carranza's Great Épée cannot attune to Narvaez's Mighty Quiver. If both wielders are within [= 12 stride =] of each other, they ignore the Mighty Armament restriction and the Épée's upkeep is suspended. If the thread of fate is severed, these effects become permanent.

</Trait>

#### Parry and Riposte <span>Masterful Blow</span>

When a creature makes a melee attack against you, you can force them to reroll the attack with disadvantage. If their attack misses, you can counterattack, dealing an extra [% 1d8 lightning %] damage.

When you are targeted by a ranged attack, you can attempt to redirect it. Roll a Dexterity save against DC = max(10, half damage). On a success, you redirect the attack at a target within [= 6 stride =]/[= 12 stride =]; you roll attack and damage as if the original attacker had aimed for them. On a failure, you take normal damage or ignore the miss.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Fated Demise

If the wielder of Narvaez's Mighty Quiver is unconscious or slain, and you survive, the quiver flies toward you if within [= 120 stride =], traveling at [= 24 stride =] per round, or [= 12 stride =] per round through solid terrain. You automatically draw both weapons, treating them as light.

While Fated Demise is active, you gain advantage on all attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks. You have immunity to all conditions except prone and exhaustion, and you suffer 1 level of exhaustion per round.

If the fallen wielder is revived, these effects end immediately, but exhaustion persists. You can choose to unbind the weapons: all magic in both items is suppressed, you suffer 1 exhaustion level, and both wielders lose attunement.

If you are separated from the other wielder by more than [= 120 stride =], you gain 1 exhaustion per day at dawn until the link is broken or restored.

While under Fated Demise, when you use a Parry and Riposte option (the redirect or counterattack), you can replace it with a shockwave riposte. Every creature within [= 1 stride =] of you must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 10 + Dexterity modifier + tier bonus). On failure, they take [% 2d8 slashing %]. If the attack you deflected was ranged, creatures make the save with advantage.

If the thread of fate is severed, the shockwave riposte option becomes a 1-charge feature, recharging daily at noon.

</Feature>
