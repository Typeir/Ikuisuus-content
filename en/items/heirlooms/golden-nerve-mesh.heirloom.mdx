# Golden Nerve Mesh

<Heirloom
  rarity="very rare"
  attunement="required"
  base="Monstrous Graft (Skin, Chest)">

When the Order of the Wheel located their predecessor's tomb, they were surprised by the baroque machinery that kept their ancestor in an unholy stasis for millennia. The contraption was destroyed to preserve the sanctity of the cycle, but its precious metals were used to reconstruct the castigated flesh of the necromancer within.

> “So, this is our creator? A desiccated wretch called Cream Puff? The Black One is quite an insidious joker.”

### Attachment

Attaching this monstrous graft to a humanoid requires an **8-hour surgical procedure** using:

- **[Alchemy](/en/library/items/tools/alchemy), Surgical Tools, and Magical Healing** (one healing spell per failed check to stabilize the patient)
- A surgeon with proficiency in Medicine and the necessary tools

During the procedure:

- The subject saves Constitution against **DC 15** before every Medicine check, [# kw:chained #](8/1). Failing the chain permanently reduces their maximum hit points by **[% 4d6 %]**. This reduction is irreversible, even if the graft is removed or destroyed.

Each hour of the procedure requires the surgeon to check Wisdom (Medicine) **DC 30** or the subject takes **[% 5d6 dark %]** and the hour yields no progress.

- The procedure can be stopped at any moment. Stopping or interrupting it rejects the graft permanently.

This graft is **permanent until surgically removed** (which destroys it). If botched, it cannot be healed or mended magically.
