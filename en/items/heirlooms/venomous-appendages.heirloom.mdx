**Range:** Melee ([= 1 stride =])

This graft replaces a subject’s limbs and spinal musculature with **venom-conductive tissues** and **gland-laced extensions**, including **three articulated tails** and **reinforced clawed limbs**. Originally harvested from a **lunar manticore**, the appendages remain reactive and hostile to intrusion.

## Grafting

Attaching this _major monstrous graft_ to a humanoid requires an **8-hour surgical procedure** using **Alchemist’s Supplies**, **Surgical Tools**, and **Magical Healing**, with a healing spell required after each failed Medicine check, performed by a surgeon with **proficiency in Medicine** and the proper tools.

During the procedure, the subject saves Constitution against **DC 10** before **each Medicine check**, [# kw:chained #](8/1). Failing the chain permanently rejects the graft; if the graft is self-applied, they die instantly and violently instead.

Each hour requires the surgeon to check Wisdom (Medicine) **DC 20** or the subject takes **[% 6d6 poison %]** and the hour yields no progress. The procedure can be stopped at any moment. Stopping or interrupting it rejects the graft permanently.

The graft is **mundane** and **permanent** unless surgically removed, which destroys it.
