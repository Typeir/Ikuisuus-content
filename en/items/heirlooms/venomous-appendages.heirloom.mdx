# Venomous Appendages

<Heirloom
  rarity="very rare"
  base="Natural Weapon (Finesse, Special)"
  quality="Mundane"
  damage="[% 1d6 poison %]"
  mastery="Slow">

**Range:** Melee ([= 1 stride =])

This graft replaces a subject’s limbs and spinal musculature with **venom-conductive tissues** and **gland-laced extensions**, including **three articulated tails** and **reinforced clawed limbs**. Originally harvested from a **lunar manticore**, the appendages remain reactive and hostile to intrusion.

> “Upon preliminary vivissection the subject demonstrates three autonomous appendages with stingers attached to a common gland. Nature knows that venom is an effective method, but giving three weapons to the same being is just inefficient depravity.” - Loterra, Praefectus Fabrum

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Attachment

Attaching this _major monstrous graft_ to a humanoid requires an **8-hour surgical procedure** using **Alchemist’s Supplies**, **Surgical Tools**, and **Magical Healing**, with a healing spell required after each failed Medicine check, performed by a surgeon with **proficiency in Medicine** and the proper tools.

During the procedure, the subject must succeed on a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw** before **each Medicine check**. On a failure the subject permanently rejects the graft; if the graft is self-applied, they die instantly and violently instead.

Each hour requires a **DC 20 Medicine check**. On a failure, the subject takes **[% 6d6 poison %]** and must repeat the check. If the procedure is interrupted, the graft is rejected permanently.

The graft is **mundane** and **permanent** unless surgically removed, which destroys it.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Venom Strike

The appendages' attacks deal **[% 1d6 poison %]**, replacing the normal damage of unarmed strikes or natural attacks.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Slow

When this weapon deals damage, the target's **movement speed is reduced by [= 2 stride =]** until the start of your next turn.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature>

#### Venom Pulse

When you take the Attack action, you may replace one of your attacks with a venom burst directed at a creature you can reach. That creature makes a Constitution saving throw (DC = 18 + your tier bonus). On a failure, it takes [% 6d6 poison %] and is poisoned until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and is not poisoned.

</Feature>
