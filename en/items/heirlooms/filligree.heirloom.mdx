**Filigree** was once the favored knife of **Master Seamstress Petunya**, used to skin beasts and stitch together the hides of her own making.  
Its edge was never reforged, only resharpened, until it learned what to cut by memory alone.  
The knife hums faintly when drawn, as if recalling the rhythm of thread pulled through flesh.

> “All that is torn can be mended. All that is mended can be torn again.”  
> — Petunya, Master Seamstress

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Dual Function

Filigree can be used as a set of artisan’s tools for skinning, tailoring, and leatherworking. When used this way, you have advantage on any relevant Dexterity or Wisdom checks.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Returning

After each throw, Filigree reappears instantly in your hand in a curl of silver and gold thread. If you have multiple attacks, you can throw Filigree more than once per round.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Seamcutter

When you critically hit with Filigree, you cut through seams or bindings. The target’s worn armor, clothing, or restraints are partially severed, reducing their AC by 1 (to a maximum reduction of 5) until repaired or replaced.

</Trait>
