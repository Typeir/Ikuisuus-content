# Fatespinner’s Spindle

Once wielded by **Yskeia**, the Spear of Resolution, this indestructible staff was molded from **primordial wax** — the same divine substance from which the **White Homunculus** was formed.  
It was created to **subjugate the God of Fate**, and for a brief instant, it succeeded. When Yskeia fell, the staff was lost among the ruins of her final battle.

Uncountable aeons later, it was recovered by the starcallers, who discovered its strange affinity for the wax-born.  
The staff was used by one of them to **bind three existing homunculi** to it: companions of profound emotional importance, each with their own mind and purpose.  
The Spindle does not command them; it simply **anchors them**, preserving their forms and consciousness through the bond.

> “Never will it bind gods again. It shall only protect the memories of our friends.”

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Primordial Wax

The staff is **indestructible**. It cannot melt, crack, warp, or be destroyed by any means short of conceptual erasure. It cannot be affected by antimagic, and appears mundane to detection spells.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Attunement

Attuning to the Spindle requires you to check Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Insight) DC 20 to align your will to the residual homuncular echoes within, or the staff remains inert until dawn. Once attuned, you sense the living presence of your bound companions at all times. When one dies, a faint tremor passes through the staff—cold, brief, and impossibly sad.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Auxiliary Motion

While attuned to the Spindle, you gain one additional Minor Action per turn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Bound Will

If you die, the staff liquefies and cocoons your body in wax. Your body becomes irrecoverable, along with everything you were carrying, as it turns into primordial wax.

</Trait>

#### Bound Companions

You can summon or dismiss one of your bound companions—Cierto, Plinio, or Adastria—as a Minor Action, and command it as a Minor Action on subsequent turns. A summoned companion remains until dismissed or destroyed. If slain, reconstructing one requires 50,000 gp in materials and one month of dedicated work.

Cierto, Poet of Oblivion, immediately performs a strike on summoning, dealing significant damage and potentially stunning the target, then returns to his own adventures. His voice lingers for this round, granting an inspiration die to allies within [= 6 stride =]. Cierto can be summoned once every Repose.

Plinio manifests a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius dome of translucent wax centered on a point within [= 6 stride =]. Creatures inside gain three-quarters cover and resistance to all damage. The dome lasts one minute or until destroyed.

Adastria releases a slow, radiant pulse of warmth: all allies within [= 6 stride =] regain 30 hit points and end one condition ([# kw:condition:blinded #], [# kw:condition:deafened #], [# kw:condition:frightened #], or [# kw:condition:poisoned #]).
