This is the sword Wax left behind when he stepped down from Plato’s Tower. Forged from pure idea, Nullbrand was meant to end wars without being drawn.
But peace died. And the blade, abandoned in brine beneath the station, grew a vaki—not of violence, but of refusal.
Its form is **edgeless**—a monolithic blade of **terracotta** carved from an impossible mold.
It bears **no sharpness**, no runes, no glow. The **pommel is a serene bust of Plato**, face cracked with hairline fractures that never deepen.

> “Oh, to be forgotten.”

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Patronage

Nullbrand, or its Vaki, are a Villein patron. They may only offer patronage to Hexblades or Pact of the Blade Villeins.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Rust aura

Nullbrand quickly rusts any metal it touches, rendering it vulnerable to all damage. Nullbrand is made of primeval terracotta, and is thus immune.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Attunement Condition

Nullbrand cannot be attuned to by any creature with spell slots, except for Villein spell slots. 
Any other attempt to attune saves Charisma [# kw:raw roll;RR #] against DC 25. 
On failure, the creature is banished to a harmless shore of the muck dimension for 7 days. Homunculi are exempt.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Unbreakable

Nullbrand is unbreakable. It cannot be destroyed by any means, and will not rust or corrode.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Death of Inertity

Nullbrand deals double damage to constructs, quadruple to objects.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Impossibility Field

Nullbrand cannot be affected by Identify, Arcane Sense, Wish, Mending, Scrying, Legend Lore, or any effect that targets objects or weapons. It does not register as magical.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Mundanity

Nullbrand is mundane. It does not count as magical for any negative purpose. Counts as magical for the purposes of overcoming resistances.

</Trait>
