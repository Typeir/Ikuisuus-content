# Iridescent Lantern of the Brilliant Will

<Heirloom
  rarity="very rare"
  attunement="required"
  base="hooded lantern"
  focus="spellcasting">

A lantern of crude iron forged by the ancestors of Wil'beraht the Smith. Within it dwells an iridescent flame — practically infinite in colors and forms. This flame blazes with intensity each time its originating Scion channels their primordial magic.

At a material level, this lantern functions as a hooded lantern.

> “I will make this flame my own, so that its primordial radiance answers to my will.”  
> — Wil'beraht the Smith

---

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Unguiding Flames

This lantern interacts with spells that deal fire damage, heal, or belong to the school of abjuration. These are referred to as Brilliant Spells for the lantern's abilities.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Blazing Feedback

The first time the lantern gains a charge from each type of Brilliant Spell, the bearer recovers one sorcery point. This per-type recovery recharges at dawn.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Iridescent Will

The bearer gains a bonus to [# kw:accuracy #] on magical attacks and their spell save DC based on the lantern's current charges:

| Charges | Bonus |
| ------- | ----- |
| 0       | +0    |
| 1–2     | +1    |
| 3       | +2    |

</Trait>

### Features

<Pool max="3">

#### Charges

After the bearer casts a Brilliant Spell of 1st level or higher, the lantern gains one charge, up to a maximum of 3.

</Pool>

<Feature>

#### Color of Creation

When you cast a Brilliant Spell that heals or deals fire damage, you may spend charges to increase the spell's effective level by 1 for each charge spent.

</Feature>

</Heirloom>
