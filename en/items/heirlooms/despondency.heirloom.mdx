# Despondency

<Heirloom
  rarity="rare"
  attunement="required"
  base="Amulet">

This amulet is but a mere iron locket with no markings, pictures or inscriptions. Yet it has been found time and time again in the possesion of those who have ended their own lives.
Those struck by its psychic resonance feel the will to fight drain from their limbs, like leaves dropping from a dying tree.
The despair it inflicts is not an illusion; it is a mirror held to the soul's deepest fractures.

---

### Features

<Feature>

#### Crushing Despair

When you deal psychic damage to a creature, it saves Wisdom or becomes Hopeless [# kw:briefly #]. A creature that succeeds on the save is immune to this effect for the next 24 hours.

A Hopeless creature that targets you with an attack roll must choose a new target or lose the attack or spell. A Hopeless creature automatically fails any check to escape a grapple or restraint.

</Feature>
