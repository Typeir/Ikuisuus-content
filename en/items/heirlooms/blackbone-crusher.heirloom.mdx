# Blackbone Crusher

Forged from the brutalized corpses of void-giants, it's core formed by a **Dreamcatcher shard** whose will has been shattered, and blessed with the still-beating **Heart of the Golden One**, the Blackbone Crusher is the blade meant to end ambition itself.
It is a massive, cathedral-like slab of blackened bone and iron — **too large to be wielded without monumental determination**

When swung, the core of the blade, which is formed by a large, black spine, extends uncanily as if grasping at foes, allowing it to reach further away than any regular weapon.

It lacks an edge, it is a thing made for bludgeoning rather than cutting. Its surface is carved with sculpted figures: humanoids scrambling up the blade, their flesh sloughing away until they are skeletons near the hilt, where they are ground into nothing, and the mortar from their bones forms the singular, crescent edge at the top of the structure, crowned by a hollowed void in which the **Heart Of Wills** sits.
The weapon thwarts sounds around it when swung, and it stills even the loudest, most vibrant of lights.
A dark, sickening throb follows each strike, as if the world itself had been rended with it's passing.

> “Every bone crushed, a brick laid; of blood, sweat and guts mortar that binds. A throne for hope to sit, little one.” — The Golden One, to the white Homunculus.

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Attunement Rite

When a creature attempts to attune to this weapon, it saves Strength against DC 28. Failing creatures are trapped within the heart of will’s event horizon and die instantly, their death marked by an echoing scream that lasts for a minute. Saving creatures gain attunement to it.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Indestructible

This weapon cannot be broken, sundered, or melted by any effect, its blade as unbreakable as the will of the world.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Light Devourer

This blade turns all light within [= 6 stride =] around it into dim light, and dim light into darkness.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### God-Slayer’s Burden

Wielding this weapon halves your base movement speed and imposes disadvantage on all saving throws unless you have Strength 24 or higher.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Anchor of Wills

You cannot be affected by magical fear, charm, or compulsion effects while attuned.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Heart of Motion

You cannot be moved against your will, nor can you be knocked [# kw:condition:prone #] or pushed while attuned.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Weight of Ambition

This weapon deals double damage to Constructs and Aberrations, and quadruple damage to objects and structures.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Judgment Saves

While attuned to this weapon, you no longer make individual ability-based saving throws. Instead, you may use the following saving throws: Fortitude (Strength or Constitution, pick the higher modifier), Reflex (Dexterity or Intelligence, pick the higher modifier), or Will (Wisdom or Charisma, pick the higher modifier).

</Trait>

#### Undefeatable

When you fail a saving throw, you can instead succeed. To recharge this feature, you must feed the blade a +3 or higher weapon during a rest. To do this, throw the desired item at the heart of wills in the gaping hole of the crescent edge of the blade. The item is instantly destroyed, and the blade absorbs its power.

</Feature>
