### Traits

<Trait>

#### Everbark

If the staff is broken or destroyed, it leaves a small sapling in it’s place that can be grafted onto any tree to instantly grow a new Astral Shepherd’s Cane, siphoning the tree’s life force to do so.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Astral Attunement

While you hold this staff, you gain +2 [# kw:accuracy #] on spell attacks and your spell save DC gains a +2 bonus.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### The Roots of the Tree

When you use the Druid’s _Shillelagh_ feature on this staff, the base die becomes [% 2d8 %] instead of [% 1d8 %]. Because this is a large-creature staff, all weapon damage dice are doubled again, making the final Shillelagh die [% 4d8 %]. The staff’s +2 bonus still applies normally.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" trigger="when a spell that targets only you is cast">

#### Spell Absorption

While holding the staff, you have advantage on saving throws against spells. The spell is negated, and the staff regains charges equal to the spell’s level. If this would bring the staff above 50 charges, excess energy dissipates harmlessly.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Major Action" targets="up to 5 willing creatures you can see within [= 12 stride =] of you" charges="50">

#### Astral Chains

You weave luminous chains of starlight between targets. These links last for an hour, or you dismiss them (no action).

Whenever a linked creature makes a saving throw or ability check, that creature may use its reaction to replace its bonus to that roll with the bonus of another linked creature within [= 2 stride =] of it, but must choose to do this before the roll is made. Doing so consumes a charge.

Whenever you cast [_Twilight Bolt_](/en/library/spells/twilight-bolt) or when your Starry Form: Archer makes an attack, the attack may retarget a new creature within [= 6 stride =] of the previous target after each hit, as long as none of the attacks miss. The effect ends immediately on the first missed attack. Every time you select a new target, you consume a charge.

You may only maintain one set of Astral Chains at a time, and you may only link creatures once per [# kw:Repose #] or [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Major Action">

#### Entangling Slam

You slam the staff into the ground, casting [_Entangle_](/en/library/spells/entangle) with a [= 12 stride;ADJ =] square, instead of the usual [= 4 stride;ADJ =] square.

</Feature>
