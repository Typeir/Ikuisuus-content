# Tombsteel Shield

<Heirloom
  rarity="rare"
  base="Shield (Heavy)"
  quality="Mundane"
  damage="[% 1d4 bludgeoning %]"
  mastery="Push"
  burden="[= 5 burden =]">

One of the very first applications of tombsteel was that of crafting rudimentary shields to protect empyrean warriors from the Silent Ones they hunted.
It was during this hunts that the term "God Challenger" was coined, as the warriors eventually ran out of demons to fight, and turned their blades against the intruding gods.

> “In the face of great power, greater cruelty is required.” — An ancient empyrean proverb

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" targets="a willing creature you can see within [= 1 stride =] of you">

#### Entropic Guard

When targets are subjected to a magical effect that requires a saving throw, you interpose the shield so they gain advantage on that saving throw. Declare this reaction after the effect is identified but before the saving throw is rolled.

</Feature>

After you successfully land an attack on a target, the target can cast only cantrips (no leveled spells) [# kw:briefly #].
