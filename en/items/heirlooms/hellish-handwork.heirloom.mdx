# Hellish Handwork

<Heirloom
  rarity="rare"
  attunement="required"
  base="Gloves"
  focus="spellcasting">

A pair of gloves that shimmer with a fiery glow of their own — a fire that leaves no trace. Their delicate lacework is woven with threads singed by the Vaki of fire: animistic, nameless spirits born from splinters of the idea of flame itself.

The fire Vaki are neither kind nor cruel. They are fire, and fire does not choose sides. They gravitate toward those who wield flame regardless of purpose — drawn not by intent but by resonance. To earn a fire Vaki's favour is to perform for it: to sing, to dance, to burn with enough passion that the spirit deems your fire worthy of its attention.

These gloves are a stage for fire Vaki. When the wearer casts flame with enough conviction, the spirits gather — flickering, joyful presences drawn to the warmth.

> “And within the tiny embers and roaring hellfires of the great burning of Emeria, the Vaki danced and laughed, careless and carefree.”

---

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Born of the Flames

Fire Vaki gather around both gloves whenever you cast a spell that deals fire damage or receive fire damage. The number of Vaki drawn equals the spell's level (minimum 1) or the fire damage received divided by 5 (minimum 1). They linger for one minute, flickering around you. Vaki drawn by a spell cannot be used for that same spell.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Consuming Fire

If you have more than 3 Vaki gathered at the end of your turn, you take [% 1d4 fire %] for each Vaki above 3. The spirits do not mean harm — they simply burn, and too many burn too brightly.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" trigger="when you receive frost or fire damage">

#### Like tiny embers

You can release one or more Vaki, reducing the damage taken by [% 1d4 %] per Vaki released.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Reflex" trigger="when you deal fire damage to a creature">

#### Scorching Heat

You can release one Vaki to inflict the [# kw:condition:Burning #] condition on that creature until the end of your next turn.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action">

#### Final Blaze

You can release any number of your Vaki. Your next fire spell damage roll this turn increases by 1d4 for each Vaki released. If you cast a healing spell instead, that healing increases by 2d4 per Vaki released.

If you release more than 3 Vaki, you cast [_Pyroblast_](/en/library/spells/pyroblast) centered on yourself at a spell level equal to the number of Vaki. That casting is affected by your class features as if you cast it normally. You gain no Vaki from Pyroblast's damage, as it is the result of the Vaki's power.

</Feature>

</Heirloom>
