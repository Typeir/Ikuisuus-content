A disgusting, oversized sack of **preserved demon limbs**, grafted and stitched into a loosely humanoid form. Each strike carries the volatile essence of its contents — mangled hands, horns, tendrils, and organs — and channels the **residual elemental energies** from the demons they were torn from.

> “That thing **reeks of incense, bile, and curdled demon sap**. Every time it moves, something stirs inside, unknowable, demonic and disembodied” — Cierto, Poet of Oblivion

On a 6, roll the d6 twice more and repeat this process for each die; you can escalate this chain up to your tier bonus times per attack.
