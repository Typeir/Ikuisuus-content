# Nulled Heart

<Heirloom rarity="Mythic Artifact" base="Bauble">

This is the end of all things, neither predestined nor fated, but utterly inevitable.

> “... And that heart will, in time, hollow thine own.”

---

### Features

<Feature cost="1 Major Action">

#### Nulled Heart

You forego your life, to let the null consume you. You immediately enter the **Nulled Heart** state. This effect cannot be interrupted, suppressed, countered, or reversed by any means.

While in this state, you regain all of your hit points. Whenever you would roll a d20, you treat the result as 20. Any saving throw you impose has its DC increased to 35. All damage you deal is True damage. You are immune to all damage except True and Force damage. You are immune to all conditions. You automatically fail saving throws against effects that would remove you from the battlefield (such as banishment or maze). Your spell slots are immediately refilled. Every spell you cast is considered to be cast at the highest slot possible. You regain all your spell slots every day at midnight. You gain one additional action each round; this action may only be used to make an attack, cast a spell, or activate an effect that deals damage.

This state lasts for [% 1d4 %] + your Charisma modifier rounds. At the end of this duration, the null devours your entire being. This item is destroyed upon use.

Afterwards, from your body emerges a Nascent Earthmover, servant to the New Null. The Earthmover seeks those who knew of you and tries to erase them from existence. When it falls, it reawakens the next midnight at the spot where you let the null consume you, ready to continue its mission. The Earthmover shares your statistics and is permanently affected by the Nulled Heart state. The Earthmover can travel through any kind of planar impediment at the rate of one planar jump per decade. The Earthmover is a Null.

</Feature>
