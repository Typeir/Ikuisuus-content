# Metabolic Furnace

> “It’s awe-inspiring to see a beast able to wield metal-melting heat in absolute cold. It’s a dangerous challenge to face such a creature in combat — and an openly suicidal affair to graft the beast’s organs to oneself.”

This obscene monstrous graft dissolves your innards to fuel never-ending heat. Stoke the flame, consume the world.

## Grafting

Attaching this _major monstrous graft_ to a humanoid requires an **8-hour surgical procedure** using:

- **Alchemist's Supplies**, **Surgical Tools**, and **Magical Healing** (one healing spell per failed check to stabilize the patient)
- A surgeon with **proficiency in Medicine** and the necessary tools.

During the procedure:

- The subject saves Constitution against **DC 10** before **every Medicine check**, [# kw:chained #](8/1). Failing the chain **permanently rejects** this graft, and if self-applied they also **die instantly and violently**.

Each hour of the procedure requires the surgeon to check Wisdom (Medicine) **DC 20** or the subject takes **[% 8d6 fire %]** and the hour yields **no progress**.

- The procedure can be stopped at any moment. Stopping or interrupting it rejects the graft permanently.

- This graft is **permanent** until surgically removed (which destroys it).
- It is **mundane** in nature.

## Features

<Pool max="12" recharge="by consuming fresh corpses">

#### Charges

You restore charges by consuming fresh corpses of wildlife, monstrosities, or humanoids dead no more than 10 minutes or preserved magically or in extreme cold. Consuming a corpse takes 10 minutes per charge and involves a nightmare-inducing digestion process. A Medium creature grants +1 charge, a Large creature +2, a Huge creature +4, and a Gargantuan creature fully restores charges.

</Pool>

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action, 2 charges">

#### Ignite Fuel

You gain resistance to fire and frost damage for 1 minute.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" trigger="when you take damage">

#### Unstable Wrath

While Ignite Fuel is active, you may activate this feature, which lasts until that effect ends. You gain immunity to fire and frost damage. Creatures that touch you or hit you with melee attacks within [= 1 stride =] take 10 fire damage. Once per turn, when you hit with an attack, you deal an extra 15 fire damage. At the start of each turn, you lose 3 charges. If you reach 0 charges, this effect ends immediately. You can choose to end this effect early as a Minor Action. At the end of the effect, you take [% 4d6 true %] per charge spent as your organs dissolve into sludge.

</Feature>
