# Scroll of the Storm Fist Technique

<Heirloom
  rarity="Legendary"
  base="Scroll">

The giants were the first to learn the techniques of the storm bearers, their manuscripts a well-guarded treasure.
This scroll is one of them—an original copy of the Electric Wind God Fist technique.

---

<Sheet>

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Storm Fist Style

Internalizing the forbidden strikes of Ukkonhemmo’s disciples permanently grants you access to the **Electric Wind God Fist** technique.

At the beginning of the round, you can apply **-5 [# kw:accuracy #]** to all **unarmed strikes** you make this round. A strike hit this way deals **+10 damage**, can deal **lightning** damage instead of its normal type, and **ignores resistance to lightning damage**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Thunderous Precision

While using the **Electric Wind God Fist** technique, unarmed strikes critically hit on a roll of **19–20**. When you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike, roll **one additional damage die** of the same type. Each time one of these enhanced strikes lands, you may **push the target [= 1 stride =]** away from you.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Prerequisites

This scroll teaches a martial technique, not a spell.

You must be one of the following:

- A **monk**
- A **College of Dance bard**
- A character with the **Unarmed Fighting Style**
- A creature with **natural weapons** (such as claws, horns, or fangs)

Once read, the scroll leaving only faint ozone.

</Trait>

</Sheet>

</Heirloom>
