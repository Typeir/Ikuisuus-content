# Ruler Of Everything

<Heirloom
  rarity="Legendary"
  attunement="by a paladin"
  base="Any Martial Weapon (chosen on attunement)"
  quality="Superior"
  enchantment="+3 accuracy and damage">

During the early empyrean civil war, the alchemists of the city of Thealas found out the lord of rot grew in strength when the empIre used tombsteel.
As a countermeasure, newly produced weaponry was blessed in the blood-sap of the divine emperor of the clear skies, king Aeridas, heir to nothing.

> “Will and motion; naught but the Mechanical hands of this wicked world.” — Nigredo, lord of rot and flies.

### Features

<Feature cost="1 Minor Action">

#### Form Shifter

You may change this weapon’s type to another Martial weapon type. You must choose these two weapon types on attunement. You retain attunement when swapping. Some restrictions apply, such as not being able to swap between light and heavy weapons, or one handed, versatile and two handed weapons. Both weapons must be of the same category in this regard.
Other properties such as reach and special are generally unaffected by these restrictions (although Game Director discretion is advised)

</Feature>

<Feature cost="Once per [# kw:Repose #], when the wielder uses Paladin's Smite">

#### Engineered Retributuion

This weapon pays the spell slot on your behalf.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="as part of a weapon attack" targets="one creature you hit with a weapon attack">

#### Swap-Strike <span>Masterful Blow</span>

As part of an attack with this weapon, you may trigger its clockwork mechanism, venting white-hot steam. Targets save Constitution (DC = 11 + your tier bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier), halving 25 holy damage. Failing targets are stunned until the start of your next turn.

</Feature>
