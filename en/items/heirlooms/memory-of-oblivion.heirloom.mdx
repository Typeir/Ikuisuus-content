# Memory of Oblivion

<Heirloom rarity="Legendary" base="Bauble">

Beyond the opaline belt, ahead of the all consuming flames of the Everdark, lies the will of world;
ever in struggle against the blackened flames — to peer into it is to forget but the most primal state of the world, oblivion.

> “To forget is to be free.”

---

<Sheet>

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Memory's Price

Permanently take a –1 penalty to Arcana, Nature, History, and Religion checks. Permanently forget a specific memory of your choice; this memory cannot be restored by any means. Starting on the third use of Erase from Existence, every time you use that feature, you lose proficiency in one skill and tool of your choice. If you have no proficiencies left to lose in either skills or tools, you are instantly consumed in the black flames of oblivion and you die. You, along with everything you are carrying, suffer the same fate as those erased by this memory.

</Trait>

## Features

<Feature cost="1 Reflex" trigger="After rolling and confirming damage for an attack or damaging spell you have just made" targets="a creature you hit">

#### Erase from Existence

The damage dealt to targets becomes true damage. Targets gain vulnerability to all damage from that attack. This damage is permanent and cannot be healed or restored by any means. If targets are reduced to 0 hit points by this damage, they are erased from existence. No physical or magical means can restore them, and all memories of them are lost to those who knew them.

</Feature>

</Sheet>
