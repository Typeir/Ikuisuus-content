# Robes of the Grand Starfall

<Heirloom
  rarity="Mythic"
  attunement="by a spellcaster"
  base="Clothing (Robes, Unarmored)"
  armorClass="15 + Dexterity Modifier"

The teardrops that fell from the heavens at the end of the fourth star eventually coalesced into a mundane, silken thread. This thread was then repurposed by a rogue norn into
the robes of the first starcaller, _Väinar the Starry-eyed_, who foretold the end of the fifth star and the return to a new, gentler era of creation, or so the story goes.

> “This age is but a hum, a single teardrop. Gone with the rise of our star, like morning dew”  — Väinar, the Starry-eyed.

#### Song of the Stars

While wearing the robes, you may cast three **Legendary Spells** bound to the fabric of their creation: [_Starpath_](/en/library/spells/starpath), [_True Light_](/en/library/spells/true-light), and [_Earthmove_](/en/library/spells/earthmove). Each may be cast once per [# kw:Recovery #]. These spells cannot be overcast and consume **Hit Dice** instead of spell slots. For each spell level, you lose the corresponding Hit Dice and take an equal amount of **irrecoverable dark damage**. This damage heals by **1 Hit Die** after each [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>
