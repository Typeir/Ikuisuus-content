# Crescent Brimmed Hat

_Rare (requires attunement)_  
_Clothing, Hat_

It is said the Pieni tribes that settled on the Argent Peaks to establish what would become the sediment and foundations of the ancient Vermelka often relied on senses other than
sight for their trips into the corpse-goddess's innards. There, the spiraling and cavernous passages were hosts to the eldritch and the unnatural, and every living being was both predator and prey,
adapted to apex perfection in their environment. The Pieni, however, were simpler creatures with the gift of ingenuity, and with ingenuity they birthed the technique to infuse threads and textiles with the crimson
overflow from the moon itself, granting mere objects the ability to sense that which they could not.

> “It always flows downstream, so you can't get lost!” — Lepakomies the Dweller, before his disappearance.

## Effects

- **Waning Step**:
  While you wear this hat, you have advantage on **Dexterity (Stealth)** checks.

- **Crescent Guile**:
  You may add [% 1d4 %] to **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** checks and to ability checks made with [Thievery](/en/library/items/tools/thievery) tools.

- **Full Sight**:
  While you wear this hat, you have darkvision out to [= 12 stride =].

- **Waxing Grace**:
  While you wear this hat, you gain a +1 bonus to ability checks and saving throws.

- **Moon's Embrace**:
  When you settle down to sleep and ask it to, the hat casts [_Sigil of Ruin_](/en/library/spells/sigil-of-ruin).
  The sigil appears as a Lunar inscriptio, startin on a crescent, and cycling to fullness into waning over the course of eight hours.
  After the sigil is cast, it cannot be cast again until you finish a [# kw:recovery #].
