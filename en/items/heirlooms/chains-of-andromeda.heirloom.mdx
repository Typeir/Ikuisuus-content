An artifact that defied its apparent function — the final creation of a Pieni artisan who rejected inevitability itself. 
The Chains of Andromeda are not tools of binding, but of liberation: forged to protect those surrendered to the world’s cruelty and to remind them that even in despair, there is choice.  

> “Each sunrise is a gift, let us not waste it bound in chains.”  
> — Andromeda, the Ever Standing

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Chains of Sacrifice

The weapon binds itself to your wrists, the metal fusing with your flesh. Once attuned, you cannot doff or be disarmed of the Chains unless you willingly forsake your Oath of Devotion.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Chains of Freedom

While attuned to this weapon, your movement cannot be reduced by any effect, and you ignore difficult terrain.
You are immune to the [# kw:condition:prone #], [# kw:condition:paralyzed #], [# kw:condition:grappled #], [# kw:condition:petrified #], and [# kw:condition:restrained #] conditions.

</Trait>

## Features

<Feature targets="a different creature within reach">

#### Unstoppable Roil

When you hit a creature with this weapon, you may immediately make another melee attack against a different creature within reach.
You can chain up to three total attacks per turn.

</Feature>

<Feature targets="creatures affected by your Aura of Devotion">

#### Savior of the Disempowered

When you score a critical hit, all creatures currently affected by your Aura of Devotion regain hit points equal to [% 1d10 %] + your Paladin level.
You then gain temporary hit points equal to that amount.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Sap

On a hit, the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll until the start of your next turn.

</Feature>

<Feature charges="1/[# kw:Repose #], or 3/[# kw:Repose #] with mastery" mastery="chains" targets="creatures affected by your Aura of Devotion">

#### Vortex Shield <span>Masterful Blow</span>

When a creature affected by your Aura of Devotion makes a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution saving throw, you can use your reaction to weave the chains into a dome of deflective force. That creature takes no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.

</Feature>
