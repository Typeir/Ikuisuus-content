#### Spell Reflection

When you are affected by a spell — whether it targets you directly, includes you in an area of effect, or requires a saving throw — you can use your reaction, before the spell's effects resolve, to expend a number of charges equal to the spell's level. If you do, the spell is reflected back at the caster, who becomes its sole target, resolving against their original attack roll or save DC.

If the spell originally had multiple targets or an area of effect (e.g. [_Pyroblast_](/en/library/spells/pyroblast)), all other targets in the area are affected as normal; the spell is centred on the original caster or redirected appropriately. Spells that do not affect creatures (such as [_Perfect Barrier_](/en/library/spells/perfect-barrier)) cannot be reflected.

If you expend the last charge in this way, roll a d20; on a 1, the shield loses all magical properties and becomes a mundane +1 shield.

</Feature>
