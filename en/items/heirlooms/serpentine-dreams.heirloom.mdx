When Bloodboil Ludwig dreamt of the moonlit sea, he saw a blade of shifting light and shadow, forged from the essence of the lunar godess herself.
In fear of his master, the Red Queen, he kept his vision secret, until the night he was betrayed.  
With his dying breath, he whispered a plea to the moon, and the blade appeared in his hand.

<Feature cost="1 Reaction" trigger="when an attack hits you">

#### Dreamer’s Reversal

When a melee attack hits you, contest the attacker’s roll with an attack roll of your own made using the Serpentine Dreams. If your roll equals or exceeds the attacker’s original result, the attack misses, and you can make one Serpentine Dreams attack against that creature as part of the same reaction. If your roll is lower, the attack resolves normally. For every successful Dreamer’s Reversal, you accrue a -1 penalty to future reversal contests until you miss one.

If you possess or benefit from the following features or spells, you can declare a combined use of a single Dreamer’s Reversal per round, applying only one of these interaction benefits to each contest.

- **Monk – Deflect Missiles / Deflect Attacks:**  
  Add your **Martial Arts die** to your contest roll.

- **Wizard (Bladesinger) – Song of Defense:**  
  When using **Song of Defense**, you may expend a spell slot to bolster your awareness instead of reducing damage.  
  Add **+2 × the slot’s level** to your contest roll for this Dreamer’s Reversal.

- **Rogue – Uncanny Dodge:**  
  Reduce the attacker’s contest roll by **3**.

- **Maneuver – Parry:**  
  Add your **Superiority Die** to your contest roll.

- **Maneuver – Riposte:**  
  If you win the contest, add your **Superiority Die** to the counterattack’s damage.  
  (The die is only expended if you win the contest.)

- **Feat – Defensive Duelist:**  
  If the triggering attack misses you due to **Defensive Duelist**, your Dreamer’s Reversal **automatically hits**, but you do do not get your reaction back.

- **Spell – Shield:**  
  If the triggering attack misses you due to **Shield**, your Dreamer’s Reversal **automatically hits**.

- **Spell – Argent Strands:**  
  You may cast **Argent Strands** before resolving the contest to force the attacker to **reroll** its hit.  
  Regardless of the reroll’s result, you may still take the **Dreamer’s Reversal** contest afterward.

- **Spell – Chromatic Capture:**  
  If the attack deals an elemental damage type that **Chromatic Capture** can affect, reduce the attacker’s contest roll by **5**.

You can combine Dreamer’s Reversal with a feature or spell in this way a number of times equal to your **tier bonus**, regaining all uses after a [# kw:Recovery #].

</Feature>
