# Hand of Heaven

<Heirloom
  rarity="legendary"
  attunement="required"
  base="Gauntlet">

Even when reality diverged into annihilation, a worthless rock remained unchanged.
This unremarkable pebble endured the Interlocking untouched; cursed — or blessed — to exist in the eye of the storm.
Forged into a gauntlet, it became Nekarion’s emblem: a compass of truth, an anchor for impossible certainty.

> “Abandon your wicked doubts; join me and witness the one truth.” — Nekarion, the Dreadlord.

---

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Unstoppable Hand of the Dread Lord

You gain +3 [# kw:accuracy #] with unarmed strikes of that hand. Creatures have disadvantage on checks to escape your grapple. When a creature [# kw:condition:grappled #] by you hits you, that creature takes force damage equal to half the damage you suffered.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Hand of Ambition

While attuned, your tier bonus increases by 1.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Hand of the Demons

You are immune to curses and hexes from creatures of CR 31 or lower.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature charges="Once per [# kw:Repose #]">

#### Hand of the Spawns of Truth

You may cast *Time Stop*. It cannot be counterspelled and ignores antimagic.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="1 Reflex" trigger="During rests">

#### Hand of the Queen

You may spend your hit die to heal others.

</Feature>

<Feature charges="Once per day">

#### Hand of the Archivist

You may fleetingly recall information about a topic you are not knowledgeable in. You may add your proficiency modifier to an Arcana, History, or Religion check.

</Feature>
