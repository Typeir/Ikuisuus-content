# Deep Dredge

<Heirloom
  rarity="Rare"
  attunement="required"
  base="Warhammer (Melee, Versatile)"

  damage="[% 1d8 bludgeoning %]"

This rusty and deteriorated anchor is made out of a mixture of primordial wax and curdled demon sap, many parts have been replaced with traditional metallurgy, giving it a makeshift quality. Still, the core is still in invaluably perfect condition, much like the treasures hidden below the brine.

> “Anchored in time, irrecoverable. All which contacts the null within the brine shall forever carry the void of its hollowed heart...”

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Whispers from the Murk

When attuned, you are proficient with Deep Dredge, even if you are not proficient with Martial weapons or Warhammers.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Bite or Bludgeon

The hammerhead can be smooth (maul) or barbed (harpoon). When you make an attack with Deep Dredge, you decide its damage type (bludgeoning [% 1d8 %] or [% 1d10 %] two-handed, or piercing [% 1d8 %] or [% 1d10 %] two-handed) for that attack.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### War Mage’s Weapon

While attuned, Deep Dredge functions as a Wand of the War Mage, granting +1 accuracy to spell attacks and +1 to your saving throws, and your spell attacks ignore half cover.

</Trait>

### Features

<Feature cost="When you cast catapult" targets="Deep Dredge as the hurled object">

#### Fling Conduit

When you cast _catapult_, treat it as cast one slot level higher. This applies even if Deep Dredge is the object hurled (its [= 5 burden =] weight qualifies for _catapult_). If Deep Dredge is the projectile, it returns to your hand at the end of the spell (if you choose). This benefit does not stack with other effects that increase slot level and does not exceed the spell’s maximum level.

</Feature>

<Feature mastery cost="On a hit" targets="creature no more than one size larger than you">

#### Push

When dealing bludgeoning damage and you hit, you can push the target [= 2 stride =] away from you.

</Feature>

<Feature mastery cost="On a hit" targets="creature hit">

#### Slow

When dealing piercing damage and you hit, the target’s speed is reduced by [= 2 stride =] until the start of your next turn.

</Feature>

<Feature cost="When you hit a creature with this weapon" charges="1/Repose" targets="creature hit">

#### Undertow

When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can call upon the primordial brine pools to summon a grasping hand made out of dredge. The target is immediately targeted by the [_Prasinus’s Heavy Hand_](/en/library/spells/prasinuss-heavy-hand) spell, considering you the caster. This does not use or consume your concentration; the spell persists for one minute unless you are knocked unconscious or the effect is dispelled.

</Feature>
