# Scroll of the Inebriated Warrior

<Heirloom
  rarity="legendary"
  base="Scroll">

---

<Pool max="36">

#### Inebriation Level

Your mastery of inebriation has become an artform. You now track your **Inebriation Level**, which ranges from **0 to 36**.

Drinking alcohol (such as a flask, bottle, or skin) counts as a **Minor Action** and increases your Inebriation Level by **[% 1d6 %]** (maximum 36). At the end of each of your turns, your Inebriation Level decreases by 6, unless you drink again.

</Pool>

<Sheet>

## Traits

<Trait>

#### Hammered Sweet Spot

While your Inebriation Level is **12 or higher**, you can make **one additional attack** as part of your **Attack action** (stacks with Extra Attack). Your **unarmed strikes deal an additional [% 1d4 force %]**, and deal extra damage equal to your **Wisdom modifier**. You have **advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics)** and **Charisma (Performance)** checks. You gain **+1 to AC** and **+1 to all saving throws**.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Overdrunk & [# kw:condition:Exhaustion #]

As your Inebriation Level rises above 18, you begin to suffer the following effects.

| Inebriation Level | Effect                                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 24                | Disadvantage on Wisdom checks and saving throws.  |
| 30                | Gain **1 level of [# kw:condition:exhaustion #].**                   |
| 36                | Gain **2 levels of [# kw:condition:exhaustion #].**                  |

You cannot exceed a level of 36. Your body simply cannot contain more.

</Trait>

## Features

<Feature cost="1 Major Action and 1 ki point">

#### Sober Up

You can forcibly purge your alcohol and reset your Inebriation Level to **0**. When you do, you take **[% 1d10 poison %]**, ignoring resistance and immunity. Alternatively, a [# kw:Repose #] also resets the level and removes any [# kw:condition:exhaustion #] gained from this feature.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Gourd-Borne Techniques

If you are attuned to the **Gourd of the Eternal Drunkard**, you gain access to the following techniques, each can be used a number of times per [# kw:Recovery #] equal to your **tier bonus**.

- **Breath of the Drunkard – Fire Belch**:  
  _Requires Inebriation Level 12+_  
  You may use your Major Action to exhale fire in a **[= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone**.  
  **Dexterity save (DC 10 + proficiency + Wisdom modifier)**  
  - On failure: **[% 5d6 fire %]** and the target is set on fire ([% 1d10 fire %] at the start of each turn).  
  - On success: Half damage, not set on fire.  
  - **Cost**: 6 Inebriation points.

- **Belch of the Deep Still – Toxic Sphere**:  
  _Requires Inebriation Level 24+_  
  You may use your Major Action to unleash a **[= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere** of intoxicating vapor.  
  **Constitution save (DC 10 + proficiency + Wisdom modifier)**  
  - On failure: **[% 5d6 poison %]**, the target is **[# kw:condition:poisoned #] (1 minute)**, and must use its reaction to stumble [= 2 stride =] away from the cloud.  
  - On success: Half damage, no poison.  
  - **Cost**: 6 Inebriation points.  
  - The cloud remains for **1 minute**, dealing its damage as a [# kw:persistent #] effect.  
  - The cloud **heavily obscures** its area and blocks all light.

- **Secret Technique – Drunkard’s Heaven**:  
  Once per [# kw:Repose #], if you use **Breath of the Drunkard** against your **Belch of the Deep Still** cloud, it immediately detonates.  
  Creatures within a **[= 6 stride;ADJ =] sphere** save Dexterity against **DC 10 + proficiency + your Wisdom modifier**, halving [% 20d6 holy %].  
  The explosion dissipates the cloud.

</Feature>
