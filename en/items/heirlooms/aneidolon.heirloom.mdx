The **Aneidolon** series of firearms were forged during the late Empyrean empire, each carefully brought to life by **Master Delphion** within the soot-stained,
petrol-fumed forges of the industrial city of **Orpholea**.
This city once lay beneath the current borders of the **Bladed Crags**, between the what is now southern **Puebla Amapola** and the shrouded **Karvasuola**.

Every discharge from Aneidolon is so potent the weapon turns a glowing white-hot star on the verge of collapse.
The air around it shimmers with intense heat, barely a degree below melting point.
For that reason, the _Delphionic Manual of Radiant Arms_ specifies that, while the weapon lacks a traditional reload mechanism and is thus theoretically semi-automatic, there must be a mandatory three-second vent period before a subsequent shot.

> “May the light burn away the filth that has taken root upon these wretched lands.”
> — Excerpt from “To Forge Hope Itself”, by **Delphion**, Master Gunsmith

### Traits

<Trait>

#### Overheat

Aneidolon can safely fire twice per round. Firing a third time before the start of your next turn overheats the weapon. When this happens, you immediately take 50 fire damage, and Aneidolon’s enchantment is reduced to +1 until it is reforged by a master Gunsmith with knowledge of _To Forge Hope Itself_.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Oversized

Aneidolon is larger than most handguns, doubling it's damage dice but imposing disadvantage on all attacks, unless wielded by a Large or larger creature, or in two hands.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Recoil

When you fire Aneidolon, you can move [= 1 stride =] in the opposite direction to your target, without provoking oportunity attacks.

</Trait>

<Trait>

#### Ammunition

Aneidolon uses special cartridges, nicknamed _”Holy Scriptures”_ or _”Epitaphs”_, each costing about 150 gold-equivalent pieces.

</Trait>

#### Niobean Punishment <span>Masterful Blow</span>

You can fire Aneidolon beyond its safety cycle to invoke a Niobean Punishment—a discharge so precise and violent that even you are struck by it.

The attack deals 40 flat piercing damage (plus your relevant modifiers), doubled on a critical hit. This attack ignores cover and full cover up to [= 2 stride =] thick of all materials except dense tombsteel, and bypasses all resistances and immunities to damage.

Targets save Constitution against DC 11 + your tier bonus + your Dexterity modifier, becoming blinded and stunned until the end of their next turn on failure.

After the attack resolves, you take 15 fire damage. Aneidolon overheats, and you cannot fire it again until the end of your next turn.

</Feature>
