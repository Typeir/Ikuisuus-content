# Burning Hands

Flames erupt from your palms in a broad fan, searing and singeing everything before them.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Self ([= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone)"
  targets="Creatures within the cone">

As you hold your hands with thumbs touching and fingers spread, a thin sheet of flames shoots forth from your outstretched fingertips.

Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 4d6 fire %].

The fire ignites any flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] fire damage.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
