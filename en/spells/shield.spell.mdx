# Shield

A translucent dome of force snaps into being around you, shunting strikes aside and crackling against hostile energies.

---

> **Shield**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when you are hit by an attack or targeted by the magic missile spell  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 1 round  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: you  
>  
> An invisible barrier of magical force appears and protects you.
> Until the start of your next turn, you have a **+3 bonus to AC**, including against the triggering attack, and you take no damage from magic missile.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the bonus to AC gains +1.
