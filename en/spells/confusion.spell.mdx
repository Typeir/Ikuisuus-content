# Confusion

Perception fractures: faces blur, voices contradict, and motion stutters into disjointed jolts.

---

> **Confusion**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (three nut shells)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range  
>  
>  
> This spell assaults and twists creatures’ minds, spawning delusions and provoking uncontrolled action.  
>  
> Creatures in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range save Wisdom or are **staggered** for the spell's duration.  
>  
> Staggered targets roll a d10 at the start of each of their turns to determine their behavior for that turn.  
>  
> | d10 | Behavior |  
> |-----|----------|  
> | 1 | Targets use all their movement to move in a random direction. To determine the direction, roll a d8 and assign a direction to each die face. Targets don’t take a Major Action this turn. |  
> | 2–6 | Targets don’t move or take actions this turn. |  
> | 7–8 | Targets use a Major Action to make a melee attack against a randomly determined creature within their reach. If there is no creature within their reach, targets do nothing this turn. |  
> | 9–10| Targets can act and move normally. |  
>  
> They can resist Wisdom.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the sphere gains [= 1 stride =] of radius.
