# Spider Climb

Fine adhesive hairs sprout across the skin, clinging to stone and timber and allowing movement along walls and ceilings.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature">

Until the spell ends, targets gain the ability to move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and upside down along ceilings, while leaving their hands free.
Targets also gain a climbing speed equal to their walking speed.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** touch one additional willing creature.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
