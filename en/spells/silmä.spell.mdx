# Silmä

An ornate, glassy eye manifests on you. That which was hidden is now bare.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a bit of bat fur)"
  duration="2 hours"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

For the duration, you see invisible creatures and objects as if they were visible, and you perceive the thin Folds of the world laid over the visible — things within them appear ghostly and translucent.  

<Overcast>

<Feature targets="the eye">

#### The Well of Wisdom <span>4th+</span>

The eye detaches and drifts as an invisible, roaming sensor, and the spell becomes **Concentration, up to 1 hour**.
You mentally receive visual information from the eye, which has normal vision and darkvision out to [= 6 stride =] and can look in every direction.
As a Major Action, you can move the eye up to [= 6 stride =] in any direction; there is no limit to how far it can travel, but it cannot leave the Fold of the world you occupy.
A solid barrier blocks its movement, but it can pass through an opening as small as 1 inch in diameter.  

</Feature>
</Overcast>

</Spell>
