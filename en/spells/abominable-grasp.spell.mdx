# Abominable Grasp

A spell that warps the caster’s reach into something unnatural, dragging foes closer with every successful strike.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

For the duration, your melee attacks gain an unnatural extension.  

Your **melee reach increases by [= 4 stride =]**.  

When you **hit** with a melee attack, you may pull that target **up to [= 2 stride =]** straight toward you, and add a bonus **[% 2d6 dark %]** to the damage roll.  

A target pulled by this spell must end the movement in an unoccupied space and cannot be pulled through solid objects or other creatures.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the attacks gain [% 1d6 %] dark damage.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
