# Kultharja's Mantle

A long forgotten oath-spell, now known only to those who are blessed by Kultharja's light.

> “May the sun shine upon these lands”  

---

> **Kultharja's Mantle**  
> _3rd-Level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: 10 minutes.  
> **Range**: Self (Aura)  
> **Targets**: you  
>  
> While this spell is active, the radius of an aura of your choice produced by you increases by **[= 2 stride =]**.  
>  
> The aura **sheds bright light** throughout its area and **dim light** for an additional [= 6 stride =] beyond it. This is considered mundane **sunlight**  
>  
> **Magical darkness** is **suppressed** within the aura’s area for the duration. If an area of magical darkness overlaps the aura, the suppressed portion returns when the spell ends.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the aura gains [= 1 stride =] of radius.
