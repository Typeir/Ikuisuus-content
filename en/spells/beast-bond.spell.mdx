# Beast Bond

A quiet tether snaps to the animal, and for a while you look out through borrowed eyes.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a bit of fur wrapped in a cloth)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing Wildlife (Animal) creature you touch">

You establish a telepathic link with targets. The spell fails if targets' Intelligence is 4 or higher. 
Until the spell ends, you can communicate with targets telepathically while within [= 1 league =] of them.  

As a Major Action, you can perceive through the creature's senses — seeing through its eyes and hearing what it hears — until you use a Major Action to return to your own. 
While doing so, you gain the benefits of any special senses the creature has, but you are [# kw:condition:blinded #] and [# kw:condition:deafened #] to your own surroundings.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** bond with one additional Wildlife (Animal) creature, and shift your perception among any bonded creatures.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
