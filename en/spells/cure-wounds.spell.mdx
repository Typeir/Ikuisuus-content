# Cure Wounds

Warmth pours through your hands, and torn flesh knits toward wholeness.

---

> **Cure Wounds**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one creature you touch  
>  
> Targets regain hit points equal to [% 2d8 %] + your [# kw:damage bonus #]. The dead are beyond it; constructs and the undead are unmoved.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 2d8 %] healing.  
>  
> **Overcast (5th+):** **Mass**: you may instead choose up to six creatures within a [= 6 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point within [= 12 stride =]; each regains the rolled hit points.
