# Hex

A dark, jagged sigil blooms on the target's skin and pulses with cold light.

---

> **Hex**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Until the spell ends, you deal an extra [% 1d6 dark %] to targets when you hit them with an attack. Choose one ability when you cast the spell; targets have disadvantage on ability checks made with the chosen ability.  
>  
> When one of them drops to 0 hit points before the spell ends, you can use a Minor Action on a subsequent turn to curse a new creature.  
>  
> **Overcast (3rd+):** you can maintain your concentration on the spell for up to 8 hours.  
>  
> **Overcast (5th+):** you can maintain your concentration on the spell for up to 24 hours.
