# Orbital Barriers

The seven bodies of the sky are called down and stood on edge, in order, each one as unwilling to be crossed as the last.

---

> **Orbital Barriers**  
> _9th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the wall's area  
>
> A vertical opaque wall of stacked light forms — up to [= 18 stride =] long, [= 6 stride =] high, and 1 inch thick — centered on a point you can see within range. The wall is immobile, blocks line of sight, and remains for the duration. If you position it so that it passes through a creature's space, the spell fails and the slot is wasted. Where its area overlaps a [Perfect Barrier](/en/library/spells/perfect-barrier) or a similar effect, only the stronger applies.
>
> The wall has seven layers, ordered outward from Kultharja. Each layer harms targets that pass through it, and each is undone by one specific kind of damage — dealt to the layer directly, from either side. Only the outermost surviving layer can be destroyed at a time; the rest are shielded behind it.
>
> **Kultharja.** Pure brilliance. Targets save Wisdom, halving [% 10d6 holy %]. Destroyed by 25 **dark** damage.
>
> **Urmela.** Blood. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 10d6 fire %]. Destroyed by 25 **frost** damage.
>
> **Selkara.** A thin sheet of sharpened bone. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 10d6 piercing %]. Destroyed by 25 **bludgeoning** damage.
>
> **Kalmora.** Noxious mist. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 10d6 poison %]. Destroyed by 25 **chemical** damage.
>
> **Damocles.** A mirage inhabited by every soul the world is carrying. Targets save Wisdom, halving [% 10d6 psychic %]. Destroyed by 25 **force** damage.
>
> **Itähenki and Länsihenki.** Wind and lightning. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 10d6 lightning %]. Destroyed by 25 **slashing** damage.
>
> **Mana.** Floating crystals of ice. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 10d6 frost %]. Destroyed by 25 **fire** damage.
>
> **Passing Through.** Targets make the save for each surviving layer in turn, starting from the side they entered. Failing targets are stopped — they do not cross, and do not reach the layers beyond.
>
> **Destroying the Wall.** A destroyed layer has no further effect. When the last layer is destroyed, the wall is gone.
