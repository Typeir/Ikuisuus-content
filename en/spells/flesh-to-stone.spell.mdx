# Flesh to Stone

The target's flesh and muscle harden into cold, brittle stone, joints locking as cracks spider outward.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a pinch of lime, water, and earth)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

You attempt to turn targets to stone.
If target's body is made of flesh, targets save Constitution or are [# kw:condition:restrained #] as their flesh begins to harden.  

They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution.
After three failed saves, targets are turned to stone and subjected to the [# kw:condition:petrified #] condition for the duration; after three successful saves, the spell ends.
The successes and failures don't need to be consecutive; keep track of both until targets collect three of a kind.  

If targets are physically broken while [# kw:condition:petrified #], they suffer from similar deformities if they revert to their original state.  

If you maintain your concentration on the spell for the entire possible duration, targets are [# kw:condition:petrified #] until the effect is removed by [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration) cast as its greater working, or other magic.  

</Spell>
