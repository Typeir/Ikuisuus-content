# Sending

A clear, disembodied voice manifests inside the target's mind, delivering a single spoken message.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="1 round"
  range="Unlimited"
  targets="one creature with which you are familiar">

You send targets a short message of twenty-five words or less.
Targets hear the message in their mind, recognize you as the sender if they know you, and can answer in a like manner immediately.
Targets with an Intelligence score of at least 1 understand the meaning of your message.  

Distance is no object — the message will cross the world to reach them.
If targets are in a different Fold of the world than you, however, there is a 5 percent chance the message simply does not arrive, and no way at all to know whether it did.  

**Overcast:** the message gains 25 words.  

</Spell>
