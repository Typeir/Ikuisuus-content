# Levitate

A column of force lifts the target from the ground, leaving them suspended motionless in midair.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (either a small leather loop or a piece of golden wire bent into a cup shape with a long shank on one end)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature or object you can see within range">

Targets rise vertically, up to [= 4 stride =], and remain suspended for the duration.
The spell can lift targets up to [= 250 burden =].
Unwilling targets save Constitution or are unaffected.  

Targets can move only by pushing or pulling against a fixed object or surface within reach (such as a wall or a ceiling), which allows them to move as if they were climbing.
You can change their altitude by up to [= 4 stride =] in either direction on your turn.
If you are one of the targets, you can move up or down as part of your move.
Otherwise, you can use your Major Action to move targets, which must remain within the spell’s range.  

When the spell ends, targets float gently to the ground if still aloft.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** target one additional creature or object.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
