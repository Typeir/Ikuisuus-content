# Cone of Cold

A frigid blast erupts in a widening cone, freezing the air and coating flesh and stone in brittle rime.

---

> **Cone of Cold**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a small crystal or glass cone)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the cone  
>  
> A blast of cold air erupts from your hands. Creatures in the cone save Constitution, halving [% 8d8 frost %].  
>  
> The cone remains where it fell: for 1 minute, the area is coated in rime and counts as difficult terrain.  
>  
> A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.  
>  
> **Overcast (6th+):** **Hailfall**: you can instead drop the cold from above as a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius, [= 8 stride;ADJ =]-high cylinder centered on a point within [= 60 stride =]. Creatures in the cylinder save Dexterity, halving [% 2d8 bludgeoning %] plus the spell's frost damage, leaving the same rime field.
