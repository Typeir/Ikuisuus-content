# Cone of Cold

A frigid blast erupts in a widening cone, freezing the air and coating flesh and stone in brittle rime.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a small crystal or glass cone)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Self ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] cone)"
  targets="Creatures within the cone">

A blast of cold air erupts from your hands. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 8d8 frost %].  

The cone remains where it fell: for 1 minute, the area is coated in rime and counts as difficult terrain.  

A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.  

**Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.  

**Overcast (6th+):** **Hailfall.**  
**Targets**: creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius, [= 8 stride;ADJ =]-high cylinder centered on a point within [= 60 stride =]  
The cold drops from above. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 2d8 bludgeoning %] plus the spell's frost damage, leaving the same rime field.  

</Spell>
