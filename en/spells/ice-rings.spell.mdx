# Ice Rings

A lattice of rime blooms from your hands in needlelike shards, hardening into jagged rings of ice that clamp around limbs and freeze fast whatever they close on.

---

> **Ice Rings**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M (a vial of meltwater)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 6 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the cone  
>  
> A burst of cold blooms from you in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] cone. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 3d8 frost %]. Failing targets have their speed reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn.  
>  
> The cold freezes mundane liquids in the area that aren't being worn or carried.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] frost damage.
