# Fear

A cold, wavering shadow explodes outward, flooding minds with raw dread as limbs go slack and bodies scramble away.

---

> **Fear**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a white feather or the heart of a hen)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self ([= 6 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures you choose within the cone  
>
> You project a phantasmal image of the most fearsome creature imaginable to targets. Targets save Wisdom or drop whatever they are holding and are [frightened](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions) for the spell's duration.
>
> While frightened by this spell, targets must take the Dash action and move away from you by the safest available route on each of their turns, unless there is nowhere to move. They can resist Wisdom when they end a turn without line of sight to you.
>
> **Overcast:** the cone gains [= 2 stride =] more reach.
