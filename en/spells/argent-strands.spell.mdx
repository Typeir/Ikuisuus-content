# Argent Strands

Silvery filaments whip around the target, then snap outward in a sharp, biting recoil.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Reaction"
  trigger="when a creature you can see within [= 12 stride =] succeeds on an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see within range, other than the triggering creature">

You distract the triggering creature and turn its momentary uncertainty into encouragement for someone else.

**Stranding.**  
**Targets**: one creature you can see  
When targets succeed on an attack, check or save, you can chance the roll. 

**Weaving.**  
**Targets**: a creature other than the one affected by **Stranding**  
Targets gain advantage on their next attack, check or save within a minute.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** grant advantage on one additional roll within the minute.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
