# Scorching Ray

Fiery rays streak from your hand, searing flesh and scorching stone.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="up to three creatures">

You hurl three rays of fire at targets, distributing them as you choose. Make a ranged attack for each ray that deals [% 2d6 fire %].

**Overcast:** you create one additional ray.

</Spell>
