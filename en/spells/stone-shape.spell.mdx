# Stone Shape

Touched stone softens and bends, folding into an imposed form.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Touch"
  targets="a stone object of Medium size or smaller, or a section of stone no more than [= 1 stride =] in any dimension">

You touch targets and form them into any shape that suits your purpose.
So, for example, you could shape a large rock into a weapon, idol, or coffer, or make a small passage through a wall, as long as the wall is less than [= 1 stride =] thick.
You could also shape a stone door or its frame to seal the door shut.
The object you create can have up to two hinges and a latch, but finer mechanical detail isn’t possible.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the section of stone you can work increases by [= 1 stride =] in every dimension.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
