# Sunburst

For one instant Kultharja hangs directly overhead at noon with nothing whatsoever in between. Then it does not, and everything that was hiding has been seen.

---

> **Sunburst**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a lens of polished quartz)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range

> Light floods the sphere.
> Creatures in it save Constitution, halving [% 12d6 holy %].
