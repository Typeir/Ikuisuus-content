# Harm

A jagged pulse of blight rips into the target, flesh withering and blackened veins spreading across their body.

---

> **Harm**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>  
>  
> You unleash a virulent disease upon targets.  
>  
> Targets save Constitution, halving [% 14d6 dark %]. Failing targets have their hit point maximum reduced by the dark damage dealt, for 1 hour. The damage can’t reduce a creature’s hit points below 1. Any effect that removes a disease allows a creature’s hit point maximum to return to normal before that time passes.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
