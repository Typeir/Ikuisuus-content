# Rain of Arrows

Barbed thorns erupt from the caster's hand, skittering outward to shred flesh and tear through armor.

---

> **Rain of Arrows**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: you  
>  
> The next time you hit a creature with a ranged weapon attack before this spell ends, the creature hit and creatures within [= 1 stride =] of it save Dexterity, halving [% 1d10 piercing %] in addition to the attack's own damage.
