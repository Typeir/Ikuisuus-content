# Animal Friendship

A quiet warmth flows into the target animal, dulling aggression and slackening its posture.

---

> **Animal Friendship**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a morsel of food)  
> **Duration**: 24 hours  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one beast  
>
> This spell lets you convince beasts that you mean them no harm.
>
> Targets must see and hear you. If any target's Intelligence is 4 or higher, the spell fails. 
> Otherwise, targets save Wisdom or are charmed by you for the spell's duration. 
> If you or one of your companions harms targets, the spell ends.
>
> **Overcast:** affect one additional beast.
