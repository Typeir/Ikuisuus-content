# Animal Friendship

A quiet warmth flows into the target animal, dulling aggression and slackening its posture.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a morsel of food)"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="one wildlife creature">

This spell lets you convince wildlife that you mean them no harm.

Targets must see and hear you. If any target's Intelligence is 4 or higher, the spell fails. 
Otherwise, targets save Wisdom or are [# kw:condition:charmed #] by you for the spell's duration. 
If you or one of your companions harms targets, the spell ends.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** affect one additional wildlife creature.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
