# Beacon of Hope

A steady pale column of light erupts and envelops the target, bathing them in a calm, clarifying glow that steadies breath.

---

> **Beacon of Hope**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
>  
> This spell bestows hope and vitality. Choose any number of creatures within range. For the duration, each target has advantage on Wisdom saving throws and death saving throws, and regains the maximum number of hit points possible from any healing.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the range increases by [= 6 stride =] for each slot level above 3rd.
