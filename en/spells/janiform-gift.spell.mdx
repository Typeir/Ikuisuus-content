# Janiform Gift

You take on one of the Canvas's two faces — the assertion that makes, or the absence that unmakes.

---

  level="6"
  cost="1 Minor Action"

You invoke the Janiform duality of the Canvas and transform.
Choose one aspect.
In either, you gain: immunity to the [# kw:condition:charmed #], exhaustion, [# kw:condition:frightened #], [# kw:condition:paralyzed #], [# kw:condition:petrified #], and [# kw:condition:poisoned #] conditions; a flying speed of [= 8 stride =] with hovering; the ability to attack twice when you take the Attack action; a weapon attack as a Minor Action, using your accuracy for attack rolls and your damage bonus for damage rolls; advantage on saving throws against spells; and a Major Action to end one spell or magical effect on yourself.

**Will (the Celestial face).** Your creature type is treated as Celestial.
You have **resistance to holy damage** and **resistance to Somatic damage** (poison, chemical, psychic).
You emit bright light in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for a further 30.  

**Null (the Null face).** Your creature type is treated as Null.
You have **resistance to dark damage** and **resistance to Somatic damage**.
Light bends and dims around you; you are lightly obscured to creatures more than [= 6 stride =] away.  

**Overcast (7th+):** the spell gains 1 minute of duration.
