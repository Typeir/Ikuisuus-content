# Burning Barrier

A vertical wall of flame erupts, scorching the air and singeing any matter it touches.

---

> **Burning Barrier**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action, or 1 Reaction, taken as the [barrier](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers) guide dictates  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the wall's area  
>  
> You create a wall of fire on a solid surface within range. 
> You can make the wall up to [= 12 stride =] long, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick, or a ringed wall up to [= 4 stride =] in diameter, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick. 
> The wall is opaque and lasts for the duration.  
>  
> When the wall appears, creatures in its area save Dexterity, halving [% 5d8 fire %]. 
>  
> One side of the wall, selected by you when you cast this spell, deals [% 5d8 fire %] as an [_delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) to each creature within [= 2 stride =] of that side or inside the wall. 
> The wall itself deals the same damage as a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements). 
> The other side of the wall deals no damage. 
>  
> **Energy barrier.** Area effects pass straight through it. 
> See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers). 
>  
> **Overcast:** the wall gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
