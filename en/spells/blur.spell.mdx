# Blur

The subject's outline fractures into shifting, translucent afterimages that blur and refract nearby light.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

Your body becomes blurred, shifting and wavering to all who can see you.  

Any creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against you.
An attacker is immune to this effect if it doesn’t rely on sight, as with blindsight, or can see through illusions, as with truesight.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** blur one additional willing creature you can touch.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
