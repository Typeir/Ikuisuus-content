# Gentle Repose

A cold, waxy stillness settles over the corpse, cooling flesh and halting decay.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="10 days"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one corpse or other remains you touch">

You touch a corpse or other remains.
For the duration targets are protected from decay, and nothing can animate it — no rite, no spell, no accident of the Fold will make a construct of that body while the spell holds.

Days spent under this spell do not count against the time limits of [Revivify](/en/library/spells/revivify) or any other effect that must reach a body before it has been dead too long.
It buys the corpse time, which is often all anyone needed.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** touch one additional corpse.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
