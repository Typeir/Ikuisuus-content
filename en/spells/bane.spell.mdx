# Bane

A choking shadow sinks into the chosen targets, weighing down their limbs and dulling their movements.

---

> **Bane**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a drop of blood)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: up to three creatures you can see  
>
> Targets save Charisma or gain a penalty [% 1d4 %] to attacks and saves for the spell's duration.
>
> **Overcast:** affect one additional creature.
