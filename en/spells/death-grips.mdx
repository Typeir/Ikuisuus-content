# Death Grips

An invocation of forceful unmaking, The Revenant conjures a torrent of pure entropy to pull their enemies to their demise.

---

> **Death Grips**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
>  
> You seize a creature or object with a violent surge of collapsing force.  
> Choose one creature or object you can see within range. You need to be able to effectively grapple or carry said target.  
> The target must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** or be **pulled up to [= 6 stride =]** straight toward you and **knocked prone**.  
>  
> On a **successful** save, the creature is instead **pulled up to [= 2 stride =]** toward you and is **not** knocked prone.  
>  
> This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.**  
> When you cast this spell using a spell slot of **2nd level or higher**, increase the distance the target is pulled on a failed save by **[= 1 stride =]** per slot level above 1st.
