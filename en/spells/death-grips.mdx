# Death Grips

An invocation of forceful unmaking, The Revenant conjures a torrent of pure entropy to pull their enemies to their demise.

---

> **Death Grips**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature or object you can see and effectively grapple or carry  
>  
> You seize a creature or object with a violent surge of collapsing force.  
> Targets save Strength, pulled up to [= 6 stride =] straight toward you and knocked prone. Saving targets are pulled only up to [= 2 stride =] and are not knocked prone.  
>  
> This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the pull on a failed save gains [= 1 stride =].
