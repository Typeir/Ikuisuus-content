# Sleet Storm

Ragged sleet lashes outward, pelting everything with biting ice and turning surfaces slick.

---

> **Sleet Storm**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]  
>
> Until the spell ends, freezing rain and sleet fall in a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-tall cylinder with a [= 8 stride;ADJ =] radius centered on a point you choose within range.
>
> The area is heavily obscured, and exposed flames in the area are doused.
>
> The ground in the area is covered with slick ice, making it difficult terrain. As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures make a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.
>
> If a creature is concentrating in the spell’s area, the creature must make a successful Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or lose concentration.
>
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the radius increases by [= 2 stride =] for each slot level above 3rd.
