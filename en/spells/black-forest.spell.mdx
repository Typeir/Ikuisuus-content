# Black Forest

A sphere of cold darkness forms, swallowing light and sound as writhing tendrils move within.

---

> **Black Forest**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point within range  
>  
> You open a glimpse beyond the Everdark, a region infested with peering, unknown horrors.
> A [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere of blackness and bitter cold appears, centered on a point with range and lasting for the duration. 
> No light, magical or otherwise, can illuminate the area, and creatures fully within the area are blinded.  
>  
> The dark is a thicket, and the thicket is grasping: the area is difficult terrain. 
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures save Dexterity, or take [% 2d6 psychic %]. As something with starlight-reflecting eyes regards them through the undergrowth. 
> As an [_incipient effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures take [% 2d6 frost %].
>  
> **Overcast (per +2):** each kind of damage gains [% 1d6 %].  
>  
> **Overcast (5th):** the undergrowth seizes: failing targets are also restrained until they escape (a Strength or Dexterity check against 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #], made as a Major Action).
