# Selkaran judgement

The image of lonely Selkara rises from the ground, six fossilized ribs bursting upwards from the earth.

---

> **Selkaran Judgement**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within each of six [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-radius, up to [= 6 stride;ADJ =]-high cylinders centred on points you can see within range  
>
> Six pillars of bone burst from the ground in locations you can see within range.
> The ground where a pillar appears must be wide enough for its diameter, and you can target the same area with only one pillar.
>
> When a pillar appears, targets save Dexterity or are lifted by the pillar, or pushed to the nearest unoccupied space if they can't be lifted.
> Targets can choose to fail the save.
>
> If a pillar is prevented from reaching its full height because it comes into contact with a ceiling or other solid barrier, targets on it take [% 10d6 bludgeoning %] and are [# kw:condition:restrained #], pinched between the pillar and the barrier.
> [# kw:condition:Restrained #] targets can use a Major Action to make a Strength or Dexterity check (their choice) against 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #].
> On a success, targets are no longer [# kw:condition:restrained #] and must either move off the pillar or fall off it.
