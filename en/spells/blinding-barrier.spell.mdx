# Blinding Barrier

A vertical wall of blinding light snaps into being, scorching and vaporizing matter where it touches.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action, or 1 Reaction, taken as the [barrier](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers) guide dictates"
  components="V, S, M (a shard of mirror, silvered on both faces)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within the wall">

A wall of brilliant light springs into existence at a point you choose within range.
It appears in any orientation you choose — horizontal, vertical, or angled — and can be free floating or resting on a solid surface.
It must be **straight**: light does not bend for you, and this barrier cannot be shaped into a ring, a curve, or a dome.
You can form it up to [= 12 stride =] long, [= 2 stride =] high, and [= 1 stride =] thick.

The wall sheds bright light out to [= 24 stride =] and dim light for an additional [= 24 stride =].

When the wall appears, creatures in its area save Constitution, halving [% 4d8 holy %].
Failing targets are [# kw:condition:blinded #] for 1 minute, and are knocked [# kw:condition:Prone #] if they were moving.
They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution.

The wall blocks line of sight but not movement.
Creatures and objects can pass through it, and it **cannot be crossed through the Folds**.
Creatures crossing the wall save Constitution or are [# kw:condition:blinded #] [# kw:briefly #].
As a [# kw:delayed #] effect, creatures in the wall take [% 4d8 holy %].

**The Beam.**  
**Targets**: one creature you can see within [= 12 stride =] of the wall  
Once on each of your turns, you can use a **Major Action** to launch a beam of light from the wall at targets.
Make a ranged attack with [# kw:accuracy #], adding your [# kw:damage bonus #] on a hit.
Targets take [% 4d8 holy %] and save Constitution or are [# kw:condition:blinded #] [# kw:briefly #].
A target already [# kw:condition:Restrained #] by the light instead falls [# kw:condition:Unconscious #] [# kw:briefly #].
Hit or miss, the wall is [= 2 stride =] shorter afterward; if its length reaches 0, the spell ends.

**Energy barrier.** Area effects pass straight through it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).

</Spell>
