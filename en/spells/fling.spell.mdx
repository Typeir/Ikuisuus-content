# Fling

An object snaps forward with invisible force, hurtling into the target with brutal impact.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature the object strikes">

Choose one object weighing 1 to [= 3 burden =] within range that isn’t being worn or carried.
The object flies in a straight line up to [= 18 stride =] in a direction you choose before falling to the ground, stopping early if it impacts against a solid surface.
If the object would strike a creature, targets save Dexterity, taking [% 3d8 bludgeoning %].
Failing targets are struck by the object, which stops moving.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
