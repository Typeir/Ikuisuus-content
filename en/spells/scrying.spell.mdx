# Scrying

A pale, glassy aperture opens in the air, revealing a live view of a distant scene.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="10 Minutes"
  components="V, S, M (a basin of still water, or a mirror backed with lead)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 30 minutes"
  range="Self"
  targets="one creature within the same Fold of the world as you">

Targets save Wisdom, modified by how well you know them and what you hold of them.
Targets who know you are casting this spell can fail the save deliberately if they want to be seen.

| Knowledge                               | Save Modifier |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Secondhand (you have heard of targets) | +5            |
| Firsthand (you have met targets)       | +0            |
| Familiar (you know targets well)       | −5            |

| Connection                                        | Save Modifier |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Likeness or picture                               | −2            |
| Possession or garment                             | −4            |
| Body part, lock of hair, bit of nail, or the like | −10           |

Saving targets are unaffected, and you cannot target them with this spell again for 24 hours.

Failing targets are watched by an invisible sensor that forms within [= 2 stride =] of them and moves with them, staying within [= 2 stride =] of them for the duration.
You see and hear through it as though you stood there.
A creature that can see invisible objects sees a luminous orb about the size of your fist.

Instead of a creature, you can choose a location you have seen before. The sensor appears there and does not move.

The sensor is a small aperture held open in the Fold, and the Fold is watched.
Those who scry often, and from the same place, are found eventually by the things that follow such openings home.

</Spell>
