# Glamour

The arcane dazzles and charms those you interact with; you can make yourself more appealing and persuasive, but at a cost.

---

<Spell
  level="cantrip"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="one creature of your choice that isn’t hostile toward you">

For the duration, you have advantage on all Charisma checks directed at targets.
When the spell ends, targets realize that you used magic to influence their mood and move in the reputational track to the hostile range toward you.
A creature [# kw:condition:prone #] to violence might attack you.
Another creature might seek retribution in other ways (at the Game Director’s discretion), depending on the nature of your interaction with it.

</Spell>
