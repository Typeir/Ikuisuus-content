# Blade Barrier

A ragged wall of whirling blades tears through the air, shredding stone and flesh in its path.

---

> **Blade Barrier**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action or 1 Reaction  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the wall created at a point you choose within range  
>
> You create a wall of whirling, razor-sharp blades at a point you choose within range. The wall can be up to [= 20 stride =] long, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1 stride =] thick, or a ringed wall up to [= 12 stride =] in diameter, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1 stride =] thick. The wall provides three-quarters cover to creatures behind it, and its space is difficult terrain.
>
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), targets save Dexterity, halving [% 6d10 physical %].
>
> **The Blade in Your Hand.** When you cast this spell, you may keep one blade out of the wall. It costs nothing to take and lasts for the duration or until you let it go. As a **Major Action** you can strike one creature within [= 1 stride =] of you with it: make a melee attack that deals [% 3d10 physical %].
>
> **Energy barrier.** A wall of moving blades is not a solid thing — area effects pass straight through it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).
>
> **Overcast:** the wall gains [% 1d10 %] damage and [= 2 stride =] of height.
