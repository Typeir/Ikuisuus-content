# Mending

Broken edges draw together as fine filaments stitch and fuse the material into a whole.

---

> **Mending**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one object you touch  
>  
> This spell repairs a single break or tear in targets, as long as the break or tear is no larger than [= 1/5 stride =] in any dimension.  
>  
> This spell can physically repair a magic item or construct, but the spell can't restore magic to such an object.
