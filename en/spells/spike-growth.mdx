# Spike Growth

Jagged spines erupt from the ground, ripping flesh and tearing at anything that crosses them.

---

> **Spike Growth**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 30 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]  
>
> The ground in a [= 4 stride;ADJ =] radius centered on a point within range twists and sprouts hard spikes and thorns. The area becomes difficult terrain for the duration.
>
> When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes [% 2d4 piercing %] for every [= 1 stride =] it travels. The transformation of the ground is camouflaged to look natural. Any creature that can't see the area at the time the spell is cast must make a Wisdom (Perception) check against your spell save DC to recognize the terrain as hazardous before entering it.
>
> **At Higher Levels**: When cast using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the size of the thorny blanket increases by [= 1 stride =] for every level above the 2nd.
