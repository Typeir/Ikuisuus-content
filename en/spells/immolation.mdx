# Immolation

A pillar of fire erupts, incinerating matter into smoking ash.

---

> **Immolation**  
> _5th-level Evocation_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
>  
> Flames wreathe one creature you can see within range. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. It takes [% 8d6 fire %] on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. On a failed save, the target also burns for the spell’s duration. The burning target sheds bright light in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 6 stride =]. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the saving throw. It takes [% 4d6 fire %] on a failed save, and the spell ends on a successful one. These magical flames can’t be extinguished by mundane means. If damage from this spell kills a target, the target is turned to ash.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the initial damage increases by [% 1d6 %] for each slot level above 5th.
