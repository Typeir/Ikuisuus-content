# Barkskin

The target's skin hardens into fissured, bark-like plates.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature you touch">

 Until the spell ends, targets’ skin assumes a bark-like appearance, and targets have an Armor Class of 17 if their AC is lower than that.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the bark gains 1 Armor Class.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
