# Earthquake

The earth violently ruptures, cleaving the ground into jagged fissures and upheaving stone.

---

> **Earthquake**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 100 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures on the ground within a [= 20 stride;ADJ =]-radius circle centered on a point on the ground you can see within range  
>
> You create a seismic disturbance, shaking the ground for the duration. Concentrating targets save Constitution or lose their concentration.
>
> When you cast this spell and at the end of each turn you spend concentrating on it, targets save Dexterity or are knocked prone.
>
> This spell can have additional effects depending on the terrain in the area, as determined by the GM.
>
> **Fissures.**  
> **Targets**: creatures standing on a spot where a fissure opens  
> Fissures open throughout the area at the start of your next turn after you cast the spell. A total of [% 1d6 %] such fissures open in locations chosen by the GM. Each is [= 2 stride =] wide, [= 2 stride =] deep, and [= 4 stride =] long. Targets save Dexterity or fall in. Saving targets move with the fissure’s edge as it opens. A fissure that opens beneath a structure causes it to automatically collapse (see below).
>
> **Structures.** The seismic disturbance deals 50 bludgeoning damage to structures in contact with the ground in the area when you cast the spell and at the start of each of your turns until the spell ends. A structure that drops to 0 hit points collapses and potentially damages nearby creatures.  
> **Targets**: creatures within half the distance of a collapsing structure’s height  
> Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 5d6 bludgeoning %]. Failing targets are knocked prone and buried in the rubble, requiring a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check as a Major Action to escape. The GM can adjust the DC higher or lower, depending on the nature of the rubble.
>
> **Overcast:** one additional fissure opens.
>
> **Overcast (9th+):** the radius increases by [= 5 stride =].
