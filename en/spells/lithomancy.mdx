# Lithomancy

You give an object a life it never asked for. It wakes, senseless, terrified, and flailing.

---

> **Lithomancy**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a drop of your own blood worked into the object's surface)  
> **Duration**: 8 hours
>
> You wake a will inside an object no larger than Medium — a chair, a blade, a shackle, a doorframe. It becomes **The Wretch**, a Construct for the duration: animate and aware, knowing what it is and that it did not choose to be, unable to speak, weep, or die on its own. It obeys you, because obeying you is the only thing its brief, screaming existence permits.
>
> Its statistics derive from you and the slot you spent:
>
> | The Wretch  |                                                                        |
> | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
> | **AC**      | 11 + the spell's level                                                 |
> | **HP**      | 5 × (the spell's level + your spellcasting modifier)                   |
> | **Speed**   | [= 4 stride =], dragging itself (a woken door or window does not move) |
> | **Attacks** | uses your spell attack modifier and save DC                            |
>
> **Flail.** _Melee, reach [= 1 stride =]_ On a hit, [% 1d6 %] bludgeoning, piercing or slashing (by the object's nature).
> A Wretch larger than Medium [multiplies its Flail dice by size category](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapons-and-dice-levels) as normal.
>
> **It Must Scream.** The Wretch screams and moans; the cries of a thing that has just learned it is alive and cannot stop being.
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) creatures within [= 2 stride =] that can hear it save Wisdom or become [Frightened](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions) of the Wretch until the end of their next turn, and take [% 1d4 psychic %].
> The first time each Wretch screams, **you** take [% 1d4 psychic %] as a sliver of what you have made it feel returns to you.
>
> **It Has No Mouth.** Any attempt by any creature other than _you_ to communicate with the wretch results in the creature suffering the effects of **It Must Scream.**
>
> The Wretch acts on your turn, in the space it occupies. You direct **any** number of wretches with **one Minor Action**; left alone, a Wretch repeats its last order, or, given none, **Flails** at whatever stands nearest.
>
> When the spell ends, the will goes out. Whether anything lingers in the object afterward is not known, and those who cast this spell twice upon the same object report that the second waking is worse.
>
> **At Higher Levels.** For each slot level above 4th, choose one:
>
> - Raise the size cap by one category, to a maximum of **Titanic**. Every time its size increases, it gains hit points equal to its **_HP formula_**.
> - Wake one additional Wretch.
>
> The choices may be mixed within a single casting. Every Wretch must be touched over the course of the casting.
>
> **Additionally**, the spell always gains the following effects:
> for every two full slot levels spent, the damage of Flail increases by [% 1d6 %].
> for every two full slot levels spent, the damage of **It Must Scream** increases by [% 1d4 %].
