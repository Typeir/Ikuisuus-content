# Wish

You speak, and the Fold answers with any working ever devised — borrowed whole, formula and all.

---

<Spell
  level="9"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Self">

By speaking aloud, you cast any other spell of **8th level or lower, regardless of spell list**.
You don't need to meet any requirements in that spell, including costly components.
The spell simply takes effect.  

Wish reaches no further than this.
The workings that rewrite reality itself belong to [_True Wish_](/en/library/spells/true-wish) — and to those willing to pay what the Canvas charges for them.  

</Spell>
