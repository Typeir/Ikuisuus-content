# Gust of Wind

A brutal column of wind erupts forward, stripping loose objects and hurling debris in its path.

---

>   
> _2nd-level Evocation_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: Self ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] line)  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
>  
> A line of strong wind [= 12 stride =] long and [= 2 stride =] wide blasts from you in a direction you choose for the spell’s duration. As an [_incipient effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures in the line must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed [= 3 stride =] away from you in a direction following the line.  
>  
> For a creature in the line, moving closer to you **doubles the cost of movement**.  
>  
> The gust disperses gas or vapor, and it extinguishes candles, torches, and similar unprotected flames in the area. It causes protected flames, such as those of lanterns, to dance wildly and has a 50 percent chance to extinguish them.  
>  
> As a Minor Action on each of your turns before the spell ends, you can change the direction in which the line blasts from you.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the length of the line increases by [= 2 stride =] for each slot level above 2nd.
