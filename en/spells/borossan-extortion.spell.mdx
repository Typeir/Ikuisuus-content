# Borossan Extortion

The spell was popular long before there was a King of Borossa. It used to be called the Yellow Whisper, and anyone who has dealt with the Lord Of Sulphur can guess why.

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="until dawn"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="one creature">

You whisper a phrase that only targets can hear. If targets do not share a language with you or cannot hear you, the spell fails.

Otherwise, targets save Wisdom or are [# kw:condition:charmed #] by you for the duration, or until you or your allies attack or damage them.

While [# kw:condition:charmed #] in this way, targets believe you know their most mortifying secret. 
They obey your commands for fear you will reveal the secret.
They won’t risk their lives or fight for you, unless they were already inclined to do so.

When the spell ends, targets have no memory of why they feared you.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** whisper to one additional creature.
Each hears a different secret, each believes you know it, and none of them will discuss it with the others.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
