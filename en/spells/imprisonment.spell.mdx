# Imprisonment

Reality wrenches the target into a motionless pocket of space and seals it away.

---

> **Imprisonment**  
> _9th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Until dispelled  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>  
>  
> You create a magical restraint to hold a creature. Targets save Wisdom or are bound by the spell. Targets that succeed are immune to this spell if you cast it again.  
>  
> While affected by this spell, targets don't need to breathe, eat, or drink, and they don't age. Divination spells can't locate or perceive targets.  
>  
> When you cast the spell, choose one of the following forms of imprisonment.  
>  
> **Burial.** Targets are entombed far beneath the earth in a sphere of magical force just large enough to contain them. Nothing can pass through the sphere, nor can any creature teleport or use planar travel to get into or out of it.  
>  
> **Chaining.** Heavy chains, firmly rooted in the ground, hold targets in place. Targets are restrained until the spell ends, and cannot move or be moved by any means until then.  
>  
> **Hedged Prison.** The spell folds targets into a small held space, warded shut against folding and against any passage between Folds. The space can be a labyrinth, a cage, a tower, or any similar confined structure of your choice.  
>  
> **Minimus Containment.** Targets shrink to a height of 1 inch and are imprisoned inside a gemstone or similar object. Light can pass through the gemstone normally (allowing targets to see out and others to see in), but nothing else can pass through, even by means of teleportation or planar travel.  
>  
> **Slumber.** Targets fall asleep and can't be awoken. The location of their bodies is unchanged.  
>  
> Ending the Spell. During the casting of the spell, in any of its versions, you can specify a condition that will cause the spell to end and release targets. The condition can be as specific or as elaborate as you choose, but the GM must agree that the condition is reasonable and has a likelihood of coming to pass. The conditions might be based on a creature's name, identity, or deity, but should otherwise be based on observable actions or qualities and not on intangibles such as level, class, or hit points.  
>  
> An [_Objection_](/en/library/spells/objection) can end the spell only if it is cast as a 9th-level spell, targeting either the prison or the special material component you used. You can also end the spell if you cast it in the same place where you cast the spell to imprison targets and you use the same material component. Each form of imprisonment requires a different material component, whose cost and form are given below:  
>  
> - **Burial:** a small mithral orb worth at least 500 gp  
> - **Chaining:** a fine chain of precious metal worth at least 1,000 gp  
> - **Hedged Prison:** a tiny silver cage worth at least 500 gp  
> - **Minimus Containment:** a gemstone worth at least 1,000 gp  
> - **Slumber:** a rare flower worth at least 1,000 gp, which must be placed on targets' bodies
