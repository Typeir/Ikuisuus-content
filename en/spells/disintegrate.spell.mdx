# Disintegrate

Whatever the ray touches is not destroyed so much as withdrawn — unwritten from the Canvas, its matter handed back to the Arkhé it was borrowed from. The dust it leaves is a courtesy.

---

> **Disintegrate**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a lodestone and a pinch of dust)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature, object, or creation of magical force you can see  
>
> A thin green ray springs from your pointing finger toward targets within range.
>
> Creature targets save Dexterity or take [% 10d6 +40 force %]. Failing targets reduced to 0 hit points by this damage are disintegrated.
>
> Disintegrated targets and everything they are wearing and carrying, except magic items, are reduced to a pile of fine gray dust. They can be restored to life only by means of a true resurrection or a wish spell.
>
> Targets that are Large or smaller objects or creations of magical force — including a [Perfect Barrier](/en/library/spells/perfect-barrier), which this spell is the only thing that destroys — are automatically disintegrated. Huge or larger ones instead lose a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-cube portion. Magic items are unaffected by this spell.
>
> **Overcast:** the damage gains [% 3d6 %].
