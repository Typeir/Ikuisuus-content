# Disintegrate

Whatever the ray touches is withdrawn; unwritten from the Canvas, its matter handed back to the Arkhé it was borrowed from. The dust it leaves is a courtesy.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a lodestone and a pinch of dust)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature, object, or creation of magical force you can see">

A thin green ray springs from your pointing finger toward targets within range.

Creature targets save Dexterity or take [% 10d6 +40 force %]. Failing targets reduced to 0 hit points by this damage are disintegrated.

Disintegrated targets and everything they are wearing and carrying, except magic items, are reduced to a pile of fine gray dust.
They can be restored to life only by means of a true resurrection or a wish spell.

Targets that are Large or smaller objects or creations of magical force — including a [Perfect Barrier](/en/library/spells/perfect-barrier), which this spell is the only thing that destroys — are automatically disintegrated.
Huge or larger ones instead lose a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-cube portion.
Magic items are unaffected by this spell.

**Overcast:** the damage gains [% 3d6 %].

</Spell>
