# Abominable Grasp

A spell that warps the caster’s reach into something unnatural, dragging foes closer with every successful strike.

---

> **Abominable Grasp**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
>  
> For the duration, your melee attacks gain an unnatural extension.  
>  
> Your **melee reach increases by [= 4 stride =]**.  
>  
> When you **hit** a creature with a melee attack, you may pull that creature **up to [= 2 stride =]** straight toward you.  
>  
> In addition, your melee attacks deal an extra **[% 2d6 dark %]** on a hit.  
>  
> A creature pulled by this spell must end the movement in an unoccupied space and cannot be pulled through solid objects or other creatures.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.**  
> When you cast this spell using a spell slot of **5th level or higher**, the extra dark damage increases by **[% 1d6 %]** per slot level above 4th.
