# Elemental Ward

A translucent membrane of crackling energy envelops the target, forcing incoming elemental forces to arc away from its surface.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature you touch">

For the duration, targets have resistance to **frost, fire, and lightning** damage — the whole Elemental stratum, not a choice from it.

<Overcast>

**(5th+) (per +2):** affect one additional willing creature.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
