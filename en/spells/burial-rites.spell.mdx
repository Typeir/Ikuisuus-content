# Burial Rites

Souls are a contrived topic, but identity is not. Some have learned to thieve the identity of the departed by capturing their shadow at the moment of death.
This spell allows a caster to assume the appearance and some knowledge of a recently deceased humanoid.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Reaction, taken when a humanoid dies within [= 6 stride =] of you"
  components="V, S"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="one dying humanoid">

You bind the departing shadow of targets to yourself.  

Until the spell ends, you can use a **Major Action** to consume the bound shadow. 
When you do, the shadow vanishes and you magically transform into a **disguise** that makes you look like targets as they appeared in life, but healthy and uninjured. 
This disguise lasts for an hour or until you end it early as a **Minor Action**. 

While disguised, you gain access to all information targets would **freely share with a casual acquaintance**. 
This includes general details about their background and personal life but **does not include secrets**. 

Another creature can see through this disguise by succeeding on a **Wisdom (Insight) check** with disadvantage against your passive **Charisma (Deception)**. 
You gain a **+5 bonus** to your Deception checks while you remain disguised by this spell.  

</Spell>
