# Sulfurous Sphere

This spell is not innate to any vocation, and can only be learnt through a pact with the lord of brimstone.

> “A globe of swirling, sulfurous fumes. The very image of the world the lord of the brume envisioned.”

---

> **Sulfurous Sphere**  
> _3rd-Level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a lump of brimstone and a drop of rancid oil)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you can see in range  
>
> The sphere swirls with sulfurous vapor.
> It hovers in place, can be moved up to [= 4 stride =] as a Minor Action on your turn, and heavily obscures its interior.
>
> As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, targets save Constitution, halving [% 1d10 fire %] and [% 1d10 chemical %].
> Failing targets are blinded and their speed is halved [# kw:briefly #].
>
> **Overcast:** the sphere gains [% 1d10 %] fire damage and [% 1d10 %] chemical damage.
