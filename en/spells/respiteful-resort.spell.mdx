# Respiteful Resort

You forcefully tear the idea of a refuge from the unreality within the folds. A shelter emerges from it.

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Minute"
  components="V, S, M (a door-key that opens nothing you still own)"
  duration="16 hours"
  range="Self ([= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius hemisphere)"
  targets="everything within the dome's area">

A [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius immobile dome of force springs into existence around and above you and remains stationary for the duration.
The spell ends if you leave its area.  

The dome holds **nine occupants** besides yourself.
A Medium or smaller creature counts as one occupant; a Large creature counts as four.
The spell fails if what is inside exceeds that.
Targets can leave the dome freely, but once they leave they can't return.
Everything else is barred from passing through it.
Spells and other magical effects can't extend through the dome or be cast through it.
The atmosphere inside the space is comfortable and dry, regardless of the weather outside.  

Until the spell ends, you can command the interior to become dimly lit or dark.
The dome is opaque from the outside, of any color you choose, but it is transparent from the inside.  

</Spell>
