# Synchronicity

A touch snaps the target's movements and sounds into precise, unnerving unison with an external beat.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature">

You touch targets and set their actions running with the beat instead of against it.

For the duration, targets have advantage on initiative rolls, cannot be surprised, and can take **one extra Reaction each round**.
That extra Reaction is a hard cap: it does not stack with any other effect granting additional reactions, and no creature ever has more than one from any source.

**Overcast (per +2):** touch one additional willing creature.

</Spell>
