# Find the Path

A pale seam of light cleaves the surroundings, bending stone and air into a narrow, unerring corridor that points toward the chosen location.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Minute"
  components="V, S, M (a set of divinatory tools—such as bones, ivory sticks, cards, teeth, or carved runes—worth 100 gp and an object from the location you wish to find)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 2 hours"
  range="Self"
  targets="—">

This spell allows you to find the shortest, most direct physical route to a specific fixed location that you are familiar with, within the same Fold of the world.  

For the duration, you know the shortest, most direct physical route to the specified location, as long as you are within the same Fold of the world.
If you are traveling, you will be able to determine when you are moving in the correct direction and when you are moving off course.
The spell does not account for obstacles in the path, such as closed doors, bodies of water, or other hazards, but it does provide the general direction and course to follow.  

</Spell>
