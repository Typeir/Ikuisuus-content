# Tear the Canvas

Reality tears open around the target, folding nearby matter into a jagged void.

---

> **Tear the Canvas**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> You open a seam in the Canvas in front of targets and let them see what is written on the other side: the Clone Worlds, running in parallel, none of them needing it.
>
> Targets save Wisdom, taking [% 5d12 force %].
> As an [# kw:incipient #] effect for the duration, failing targets suffer a random effect from the Reality Break Effects table.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Wisdom.
>
> **Tear The Canvas Effects**
>
> | d10  | Effect                                                                                                                                                |
> | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
> | 1-2  | **Visions of the Clone Worlds.** Targets are **staggered** [# kw:briefly #].                                                          |
> | 3-4  | Rending Rift. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 5d12 force %].              |
> | 5-6  | Wormhole. Targets are teleported to an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within [= 6 stride =] of them.                                       |
> | 7-8  | **Grasp of the Stags.** Targets save Constitution, halving [% 5d12 frost %]. |
> | 9-10 | Mindkilling Truth. Targets save Intelligence, halving [% 5d12 psychic %].   |
