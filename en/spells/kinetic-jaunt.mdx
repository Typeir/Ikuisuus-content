# Kinetic Jaunt

Your body compresses into a streak of motion, snapping forward as air cracks and a trailing afterimage fades.

---

> **Kinetic Jaunt**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: you  
>  
>  
> You magically empower your movement with dancelike steps, giving yourself the ability to move with uncanny speed and agility.
>  
> For the duration, your walking speed increases by [= 2 stride =], you don’t provoke opportunity attacks, you can move through the space of other creatures, and you can move through difficult terrain without expending extra movement. If you end your turn inside another creature’s space, you are immediately shunted to the nearest unoccupied space and take [% 1d8 force %].
