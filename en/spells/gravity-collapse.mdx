# Gravity Collapse

For an instant, "down" points wherever you decide, and everything nearby obeys.

---

> **Gravity Collapse**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a black marble)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the sphere  
>
> A well of crushing force opens in a **[= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere** centered on a point you can see within range. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 5d10 %]. Failing targets are pulled in a straight line toward the center.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
>  
> **Overcast (6th+): Fissure.** the collapse instead opens along a **[= 20 stride;ADJ =] line, [= 1 stride =] wide,** emanating from you. Creatures in the line save Strength or take [% 7d10 bludgeoning %] and are pulled up to [= 6 stride =] toward you.
