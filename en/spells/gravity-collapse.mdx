# Gravity Collapse

For an instant, "down" points wherever you decide, and everything nearby obeys.

---

> **Gravity Collapse**  
> _4th-level Evocation_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a black marble)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous
>
> A well of crushing force opens in a **[= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere** centered on a point you can see within range. Each creature in the area must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes [% 5d10 bludgeoning %] and is pulled in a straight line toward the center, ending in the nearest unoccupied space to it. On a success, half as much damage and no pull.
>
> **At Higher Levels.**
>
> - The damage increases by [% 1d10 %] for each slot level above 4th.
> - **6th level or higher (Fissure):** the collapse instead opens along a **[= 20 stride;ADJ =] line, [= 1 stride =] wide,** emanating from you. Each creature in the line makes a Strength saving throw against [% 7d10 bludgeoning %], pulled up to [= 6 stride =] toward you on a failure.
