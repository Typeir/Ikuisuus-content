# Päivätär's Boon

Something of the harpooned sun still leaks from where it was opened. Stand in it long enough and your flesh remembers a shape it was never given.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

You draw a little of Päivätär's spilled radiance into yourself and let it finish something your bloodline left unfinished.

Choose **5 Boon Points** worth of options from **your own bloodline's Boon table** — your bloodline's, no one else's.
You gain those boons for the duration.

You cannot choose a boon you already have, and you must meet any requirement the option lists.
Unspent points are lost.
A boon that changes your size does not resize what you are wearing or carrying.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** you gain **2 additional Boon Points**.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
