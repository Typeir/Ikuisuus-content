# Purify Food and Drink

A clean, odorless wave washes over food and water, stripping away visible spoilage and organic taint.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 2 stride =]"
  targets="All mundane food and drink within a [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point within range">

</Spell>
