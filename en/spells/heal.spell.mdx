# Heal

The body is told, firmly, what it was — and it complies.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

A surge of vitality washes through targets: they regain **70 hit points** and are cured of blindness, deafness, and any diseases.
Constructs and the undead are unmoved.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains 10 hit points of healing.  

**(8th+):** the surge ends the [# kw:condition:charmed #], [# kw:condition:frightened #], [# kw:condition:paralyzed #], and [# kw:condition:stunned #] conditions on targets.  

<Feature targets="any number of creatures you can see within [= 12 stride =]">

#### Flood <span>9th+</span>

Distribute **700 hit points** of healing among the targets; each creature healed this way also receives the full cleansing.  

</Feature>
</Overcast>

</Spell>
