# Disguise Self

Another face settles over your own, close enough to fool every eye that does not touch it.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="4 hours"
  range="Self"
  targets="yourself">

You make yourself — including your clothing, armor, weapons, and other belongings on your person — look different until the spell ends or you dismiss it as a Major Action.
You can seem [= 1/5 stride =] shorter or taller and appear thin, fat, or in between; you cannot change your basic body shape.
The changes are otherwise up to you.  

The changes fail to hold up to physical inspection: a hand passes through an illusory hat, and a head meets an unseen brow.
A creature that uses a Major Action to study your appearance can discern the illusion with an Intelligence (Investigation) check.  

<Overcast>

**(3rd+):**  
The duration becomes 8 hours, and you decide targets' glamour; they can't change their own.  
**(3rd+) (per +1):** you can also disguise one additional creature within [= 6 stride =].
Unwilling targets save Charisma or are disguised.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
