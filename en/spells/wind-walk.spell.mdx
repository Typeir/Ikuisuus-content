# Wind Walk

Your form unravels into a living wind, flesh dissolving into rushing air that slips through cracks and over obstacles.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 minute"
  components="V, S, M (fire and holy water)"
  duration="8 hours"
  range="[= 6 stride =]">

You and up to ten willing creatures you can see within range assume a gaseous form for the duration, appearing as wisps of cloud.
While in this cloud form, a creature has a flying speed of [= 60 stride =] and has resistance to damage from mundane weapons.
The only actions a creature can take in this form are the Dash action or to revert to its normal form.
Reverting takes 1 minute, during which time a creature is [# kw:condition:incapacitated #] and can't move.
Until the spell ends, a creature can revert to cloud form, which also requires the 1-minute transformation.  

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, you can carry five additional willing creatures for each slot level above 6th.  

</Spell>
