# Dream

Mutable visions flood the sleeper's mind, rewriting memories into vivid, disorienting illusions that feel physically real.

---

> **Dream**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: 8 hours  
> **Range**: Special  
> **Targets**: one creature you know, within the same Fold of the world as you, capable of sleep  
>
> You, or a willing creature you touch, enters a trance state, acting as a messenger. While in the trance, the messenger is aware of its surroundings but can't take actions or move.
>
> If targets are asleep, the messenger appears in their dreams and can converse with them for as long as they remain asleep, through the spell's duration. The messenger can also shape the dream's environment, creating landscapes, objects, and other images. The messenger can emerge from the trance at any time, ending the spell's effect early. Targets recall the dream perfectly upon waking. If targets are awake when you cast this spell, the messenger knows it, and can either end the trance (and the spell) or wait for targets to fall asleep, at which point the messenger appears in their dreams.
>
> You can make the messenger appear monstrous and terrifying. If you do, the messenger delivers a message of no more than ten words. Targets then save Wisdom or endure a nightmare that lasts the duration of their sleep, denying any benefit from that rest, and take [% 3d6 psychic %] upon waking.
>
> If you have a body part, lock of hair, clipping from a nail, or similar portion of targets' bodies, targets make their saving throws with disadvantage.
>
> **Overcast:** you shape the dreams of one additional creature. The messenger visits them one after another over the duration, and delivers the same message to each.
