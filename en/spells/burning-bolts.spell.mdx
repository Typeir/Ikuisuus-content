# Burning Bolts

Touched arrows flare into seething flame that clings to the shaft and burns whatever they pierce.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="4 hours"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one quiver containing arrows or bolts">

You touch a quiver containing arrows or bolts. 
When targets are hit by a ranged weapon attack using a piece of ammunition drawn from the quiver, all damage is dealt as fire damage and targets gain the [# kw:condition:Burning #] condition. 
The spell’s magic ends on the piece of ammunition when it hits or misses, and the spell ends when **four pieces** of ammunition have been drawn from the quiver.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** affect two additional pieces of ammunition.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
