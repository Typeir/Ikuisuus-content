# Puppet

A red thread you did not tie runs from your hand to theirs. You pull, and somewhere, briefly, fate is not consulted.

---

> **Puppet**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one humanoid you can see  
>
> Targets save Wisdom or move up to their speed in a direction you choose. You control the movement entirely, and it does not provoke opportunity attacks. The spell ends when the movement does.
>
> You can walk targets into open air, deep water, or a fire, but not to their own obvious end: the movement stops at the edge of anything that would plainly kill them. Everything short of that is fair.
>
> **Overcast:** affect one additional humanoid.
