# Puppet

A red thread you did not tie runs from your hand to theirs. You pull, and somewhere, briefly, fate is not consulted.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="one humanoid you can see">

Targets save Wisdom or move up to their speed in a direction you choose.
You control the movement entirely, and it does not provoke opportunity attacks.
The spell ends when the movement does.

You can walk targets into open air, deep water, or a fire, but not to their own obvious end: the movement stops at the edge of anything that would plainly kill them.
Everything short of that is fair.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** affect one additional humanoid.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
