# Hellish Rebuke

A jet of searing flame erupts from your hand, lashing the attacker with sudden, blistering heat.

---

> **Hellish Rebuke**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when a creature you can see damages you  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: the creature that damaged you  
>  
> You point your finger, and the creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by hellish flames. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 2d10 fire %].  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
