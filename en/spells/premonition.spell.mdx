# Premonition

Your senses become uniquely attuned to the motions of the world, letting you react faster to events not yet clear in the grooves of causality.

> **Premonition**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 16 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature
>
> For the duration, targets gain a bonus [% 1d8 %] to initiative rolls.
>
> **Overcast (4th+):** affect up to ten willing creatures instead of one.
