# Sanctuary

A pale dome of light blooms around the target, its surface bending and dulling incoming blows.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V, S, M (a small silver mirror)"
  duration="1 minute"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

Targets are warded against attack.
Until the spell ends, creatures who make an attack or cast a harmful spell against targets save Wisdom.
Failing creatures choose a new target or lose the attack or spell.
This spell doesn’t protect targets from area effects, such as the explosion of a [_Pyroblast_](/en/library/spells/pyroblast).  

If targets make an attack or cast a spell that affects an enemy creature, this spell ends.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** ward one additional creature. The spell ends separately for each of them.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
