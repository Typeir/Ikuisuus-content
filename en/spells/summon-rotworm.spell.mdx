# Summon Rotworm

A spell that infests a creature with parasitic necromancy, forcing rot to crawl outward and take form.

---

> **Summon Rotworm**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a fragment of bone or flesh)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> You curse targets, causing necrotic parasites to tear free from their body.
>
> One **rotworm** is summoned in an unoccupied space within [= 1 stride =] of targets.
> The rotworm uses the statistics of a **[_Rotworm_](/en/library/monsters/rotworm)** and acts immediately after you.
> It obeys your verbal commands (no action required).
>
> Targets save Constitution or take [% 2d6 dark %].
>
> The rotworm uses your [# kw:accuracy #] for its attack rolls and adds your [# kw:damage bonus #] to its damage rolls.
>
> When a rotworm summoned by this spell is reduced to 0 hit points, its **Dark Instability** trait triggers as normal, for a creature of your choice.
>
> If uninstructed for a turn, the rotworm attacks targets if within range; otherwise, it moves toward targets by the shortest available route.
>
> **Overcast:** you summon one additional rotworm, and targets take an additional [% 1d6 %] dark damage.
> All rotworms are summoned adjacent to targets, if possible.
