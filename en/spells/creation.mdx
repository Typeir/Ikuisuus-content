# Creation

An object coalesces from shimmering motes into solid matter.

---

> **Creation**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of the material you intend to make, however small)  
> **Duration**: Special  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one object no larger than a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube  
>
> You draw raw **Arkhé** — the stuff everything else was made out of first — and let it settle into a nonliving object of vegetable matter within range: soft goods, rope, wood, or something similar. You can also use this spell to create mineral objects such as stone, crystal, or metal. The object created must be no larger than a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube, and the object must be of a form and material that you have seen before.
>
> The duration depends on the object's material. If the object is composed of multiple materials, use the shortest duration.
>
> | Material         | Duration   |
> | ---------------- | ---------- |
> | Vegetable matter | 1 day      |
> | Stone or crystal | 12 hours   |
> | Precious metals  | 1 hour     |
> | Gems             | 10 minutes |
>
> Using any material created by this spell as another spell's material component causes that spell to fail.
>
> **Overcast:** the cube gains [= 1 stride =] on a side.
