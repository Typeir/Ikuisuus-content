# Judgement

The image of an angel descends upon a creature, delivering justice.

---

> **Judgement**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>
> An angelic figure descends upon a creature within range. Choose its intent:
>
> - **Spare.** Targets become stable
> - **Smite.** Targets save Dexterity or take [% 1d8 holy %]. Targets gain no benefit from cover.
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage increases by [% 1d8 %] at each step.
