# Anaximander's Gift

When Anaximander opened the first stable rift to the Hidden Kingdom, he was rewarded with a boon from the lord of flies. A spell of ruin and rot; may his name remain forever in infamy.

> **Anaximander's Gift**  
> _9th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a fly, dead and dry, carried on your person for at least a year)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> You run a creature's body forward through years it has not lived. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 10d12 dark %]. If the damage reduces targets to 0 hit points, they die and collapse into dust. Failing targets that survive are aged [% 5d20 %] years where they stand.
>
> **The Years.** What that costs depends entirely on what they are; measure it against that lineage's lifespan. For most mortals it is a maiming, and for the old it is simply the end.
>
> **Things That Do Not Age.** Targets outside of time, like a Silent One or an Empyrean, anything genuinely immortal — take the damage and nothing more. The years pale in the face of infinity.
>
> Targets running on borrowed material are the opposite case. A homunculus has perhaps sixty good years in its wax; a construct has however long its body holds. The years are taken out of that material directly, and such creatures age exactly as a mortal would.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d12 %] damage, and the years taken [% 1d20 %].
