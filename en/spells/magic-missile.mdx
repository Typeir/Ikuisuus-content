# Magic Missile

Luminous darts of force streak from your hand, striking with compact, sizzling impact.

---

> **Magic Missile**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one or more creatures you can see in range  
>  
> You create three glowing darts of magical force that strike simultaneously. You can direct them to hit one target or several, each dart dealing [% 1d4 +1 force %].  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains one more dart.
