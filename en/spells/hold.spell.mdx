# Hold

Muscles seize and limbs lock, freezing the target in place.

---

> **Hold**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a small, straight piece of iron)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Targets one creature you can see whose type the slot you cast this spell with can reach (see the table below). Targets save Wisdom or are paralyzed for the duration. They can resist Wisdom.  
>  
> This spell has no effect on the undead. The dead do not answer.  
>  
> | Slot Level | Creature types you can target                |
> | ---------- | -------------------------------------------- |
> | 2nd        | Humanoid                                     |
> | 3rd        | + Wildlife                               |
> | 4th        | + Monstrosity, Construct                     |
> | 5th        | + Väki, Aberration                      |
> | 6th        | + Celestial                                  |
>  
> Each row includes the rows above it. Celestials are the hardest of all things to stop.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional valid creature for each slot level above the minimum required for the target's type. Targets must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other when you target them.
