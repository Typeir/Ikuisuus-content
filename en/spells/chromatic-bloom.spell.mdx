# Chromatic Bloom

The shape light takes while within a prism, a scattered radiance, searching for release.

---

> **Chromatic Bloom**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a shard of refined alchemical precursor worth at least 50 gp)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone  
>
> Choose a damage type from a stratum your slot level has unlocked (see the table below). 
> Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 2d8 %] damage of the chosen type.
>
> If two damage dice show the same number, failing targets are **blinded** until the end of your next turn.
>
> | Slot Level | Strata unlocked                       |
> | ---------- | ------------------------------------- |
> | 1st        | Elemental (frost, fire, lightning)    |
> | 4th        | + Somatic (poison, chemical, psychic) |
> | 8th        | + Akashic (holy, dark, force)         |
> | 12th       | + True                                |
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
