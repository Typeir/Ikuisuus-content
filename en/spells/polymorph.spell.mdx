# Polymorph

Flesh remembers older shapes, and for a while, it wears one.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a caterpillar cocoon)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see within range">

Unwilling targets save Wisdom or are transformed into a wildlife form; willing targets transform without a save.
A shapechanger automatically succeeds.  

The new form can be any wildlife creature whose challenge rating is equal to or less than targets' (or their level, if they lack a challenge rating).
Targets' game statistics are replaced by the statistics of the chosen wildlife creature, but they retain their alignment and personality.  

Targets assume the hit points of their new form.
When they revert, they return to the hit points they had before transforming; if they revert after dropping to 0 hit points, excess damage carries over to their normal form.
Targets are limited to the actions their new form allows, and cannot speak, cast spells, or take other actions that require hands or speech.
Targets' gear melds into the new form and cannot be used.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** affect one additional creature.  

<Feature targets="any number of willing creatures within [= 6 stride =] of a point you choose within range">

#### Herd <span>8th+</span>

Targets take wildlife forms of challenge rating 1 or lower.
The duration becomes 24 hours without concentration, and they can change their wildlife form as a Major Action.  

</Feature>
</Overcast>

</Spell>
