# Pyroblast

A pinpoint of flame streaks from your finger and blooms into a roaring sphere of fire.

---

> **Pyroblast**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you can see in range  
>  
> A bright streak flashes from your pointing finger to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into an explosion of flame. Creatures in the sphere save Dexterity, halving [% 8d6 fire %].  
>  
> The fire spreads around corners. It ignites flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
