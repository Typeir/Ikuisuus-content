# Psychic Crush

A crushing mental pressure rips a target's thoughts into jagged fractures that bloom across the skull and implode.

---

> **Psychic Crush**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Targets save Intelligence, halving [% 12d6 psychic %]. Failing targets are **stunned** for 1 minute. They can resist Intelligence.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 2d6 %] damage.  
>  
> **Overcast (7th+):** target one additional creature for each slot level above 6th (at 9th level, four targets). A creature slain by the damage has its head burst, if it has one.
