# Ice Knife

A jagged shard of ice launches outward and explodes into razor fragments on impact.

---

> **Ice Knife**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M (a drop of water or piece of ice)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>  
> You create a shard of ice and fling it at targets within range. Make a ranged attack that deals [% 1d10 piercing %]. Hit or miss, the shard then explodes, and targets and each creature within [= 1 stride =] of the point save Dexterity or take [% 2d6 frost %].  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] frost damage.
