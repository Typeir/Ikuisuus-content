# Feeblemind

The target's mind collapses into a blank, language stutters and memory frays until their gaze goes hollow.

---

<Spell
  level="8"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a handful of clay, crystal, glass, or mineral spheres)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 30 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

Targets save Intelligence, taking [% 4d6 psychic %].
Failing targets have their Intelligence and Charisma scores reduced to 1, unable to cast spells, activate magic items, understand language, or communicate in any intelligible way, though they can identify their friends, follow them, and protect them.  

At the end of every 30 days, the creature can repeat its saving throw against this spell.
If it succeeds on its saving throw, the spell ends.  

The spell can also be ended by [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration) cast as its greater working, [_Heal_](/en/library/spells/heal), or [_Wish_](/en/library/spells/wish).  

</Spell>
