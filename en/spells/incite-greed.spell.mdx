# Incite Greed

A grasping urge surges through the target, eyes glazing and hands snatching at nearby possessions.

---

> **Incite Greed**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: any number of creatures you can see within [= 6 stride =] of you  
>  
> You present a gem or other precious object and use it to beguile creatures in the area. Targets save Wisdom or are charmed until the spell ends or until you or your allies do anything harmful to them. While charmed, they can do nothing but use their movement to approach you in a safe manner, avoiding opportunity attacks but not area effects, such as a [_Pyroblast_](/en/library/spells/pyroblast). They can resist Wisdom. Saving targets end the effect.  
>  
> **Overcast (4th+):** target one additional creature.
