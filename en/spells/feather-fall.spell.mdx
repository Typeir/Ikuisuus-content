# Feather Fall

Featherlike membranes unfurl around the target, catching and cushioning their fall.

---

> **Feather Fall**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when you or a creature within range falls  
> **Components**: V, M  
> **Duration**: 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: up to five falling creatures within range  
>
> Targets fall at [= 12 stride =] per round until the spell ends. Landing targets take no falling damage, can land on their feet, and the spell ends for them.
>
> **Overcast:** affect five additional falling creatures.
