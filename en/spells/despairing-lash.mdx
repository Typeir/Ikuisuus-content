# Despairing Lash

A stinging lash of mental force snaps outward, battering thoughts and scrambling perception.

---

> **Despairing Lash**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: 1 round  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> Targets save Intelligence, halving [% 3d6 psychic %]. Failing targets are **staggered** [# kw:briefly #] and take only one of a move, a Major Action or a Minor Action on their next turn.
>
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature. Creatures must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other when targeted.
