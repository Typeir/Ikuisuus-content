# Modify Memory

What was lived through can be rearranged. Memory has no spine of its own — with sufficient concentration, the past becomes a workable material.

---

> **Modify Memory**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> You attempt to reshape targets’ memories. Targets save Wisdom, with advantage if you are fighting them, or are charmed by you for the duration. Charmed targets are incapacitated and unaware of their surroundings, though they can still hear you. If targets take damage or are targeted by another spell, this spell ends and none of their memories are modified.  
>  
> While this charm lasts, you can affect targets’ memory of an event they experienced within the last 24 hours and that lasted no more than 10 minutes. You can permanently eliminate all memory of the event, allow targets to recall the event with perfect clarity and exacting detail, change their memory of the details of the event, or create a memory of some other event.  
>  
> You must speak to targets to describe how their memories are affected, and they must be able to understand your language for the modified memories to take root. Their minds fill in any gaps in the details of your description. If the spell ends before you have finished describing the modified memories, their memory isn’t altered.  
>  
> Otherwise, the modified memories take hold when the spell ends.  
>  
> A modified memory does not necessarily change how a creature behaves, particularly where it contradicts that creature’s natural inclinations, [alignment](/en/library/rules/the-measure-of-the-self/alignment), or beliefs. **Memory is not character.** A person can be given a past they did not live and still refuse to become the person who lived it; an illogical memory is dismissed, perhaps as a bad dream, and a sufficiently nonsensical one never takes hold at all.  
>  
> **Overcast:**  
>  
> | **Level** | **Event Age** |
> | --- | --- |
> | 6th | up to 7 days ago |
> | 7th | up to 30 days ago |
> | 8th | up to 1 year ago |
> | 9th | any time in targets’ past |
