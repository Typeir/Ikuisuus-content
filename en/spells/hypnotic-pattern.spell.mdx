# Hypnotic Pattern

A web of writhing colored light unfurls, seizing observers' gazes and warping perception into stuttering, repeating patterns.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S, M (a glowing stick of incense or a crystal vial filled with phosphorescent material)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within the [= 6 stride;ADJ =] cube">

You create a twisting pattern of colors that weaves through the air inside a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] cube within range.
The pattern appears for a moment and vanishes.
Targets save Wisdom or are [# kw:condition:charmed #] for the duration.
Targets are [# kw:condition:incapacitated #] and their speed becomes 0.  

The effect ends on targets when they take any damage or when someone else uses a Major Action to shake them out of their stupor.  

**Overcast:** the cube gains [= 2 stride =] on a side.  

</Spell>
