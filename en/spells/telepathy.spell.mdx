# Telepathy

An invisible lattice of thought snaps into being, linking minds so images, words, and sensations flow between them.

---

<Spell
  level="8"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="Unlimited"
  targets="one willing creature you are familiar with">

You create a telepathic link between yourself and targets.
Targets can be anywhere within the same Fold of the world as you.
If either of you leaves it, the link does not break — it thins, and carries nothing but the knowledge that the other is still alive, until you are both back within one Fold again.  

Until the spell ends, you and targets can instantaneously share words, images, sounds, and other sensory messages with one another through the link, and targets recognize you as the creature they are communicating with.
The spell enables targets with an Intelligence score of at least 1 to understand the meaning of your words and take in the scope of any sensory message you send to them.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** link one additional willing creature. Every linked creature can reach every other, not only you.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
