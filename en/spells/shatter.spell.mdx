# Shatter

A concentrated blast of sound erupts, causing fragile objects to rupture into jagged fragments.

---

> **Shatter**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a chip of mica)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you can see in range
>
> A sudden loud ringing noise, painfully intense, erupts from that point.
>
> Creatures in the sphere save Constitution, halving [% 3d8 bludgeoning %].
> Creatures made of inorganic material such as stone, crystal, or metal save with disadvantage.

> **Overcast (per +2):** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
