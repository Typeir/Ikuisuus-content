# Argent Strands

Silvery filaments whip around the target, then snap outward in a sharp, biting recoil.

---

> **Argent Strands**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when a creature you can see within [= 12 stride =] succeeds on an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within range, other than the triggering creature — yourself included  
>  
>  
> You distract the triggering creature and turn its momentary uncertainty into encouragement for someone else. When targets succeed on an attack, check or save, you can chance the roll. Targets gain advantage on their next attack, check or save within a minute.  
>  
> **Overcast:** grant advantage on one additional roll within the minute.
