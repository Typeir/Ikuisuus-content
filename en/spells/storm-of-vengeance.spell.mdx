# Storm of Vengeance

A vast storm condenses overhead, hammering the ground with spear-like lightning and shredding the terrain with savage winds.

---

> **Storm of Vengeance**  
> _9th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Sight  
> **Targets**: —
>  
> A churning storm cloud forms, centered on a point you can see and spreading to a radius of [= 72 stride =].
> When it appears, and at the start of each of your turns thereafter, the storm advances one **tier**.
> Every tier it has reached applies its effect each round — the tiers **stack**, and the storm only worsens.
> A creature is "under the cloud" if it is beneath the cloud and no more than [= 1000 stride =] below it.  
>  
> **Tier 1 — Thunderhead.**  
> **Targets**: creatures under the cloud  
> Targets save Constitution, halving [% 2d6 bludgeoning %]. Failing targets are [# kw:condition:deafened #] for 5 minutes.
>
> **Tier 2 — Caustic Rain.**  
> **Targets**: everything under the cloud  
> Targets take [% 1d6 chemical %].
>
> **Tier 3 — Spears of the Sky.**  
> **Targets**: six creatures or objects of your choice under the cloud  
> Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 10d6 lightning %].
>
> **Tier 4 — Hailfall.**  
> **Targets**: creatures under the cloud  
> Targets take [% 2d6 bludgeoning %].
>
> **Tier 5 — Killing Gale.**  
> **Targets**: creatures under the cloud  
> The area becomes difficult terrain and is heavily obscured.
> Ranged weapon attacks within it are impossible, and open flames are snuffed.
> Targets take [% 1d6 frost %].
> Once the storm reaches this tier it remains here, applying all five tiers each round for as long as you concentrate.
