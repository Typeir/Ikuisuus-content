# Dancing Lights

Small motes of pale light coalesce into shifting orbs that wheel and drift through the air.

---

> **Dancing Lights**  
> _Evocation cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration
>  
> You create up to four torch-sized lights within range, making them appear as torches, lanterns, or glowing orbs that hover in the air for the duration. You can also combine the four lights into one glowing vaguely humanoid form of Medium size. Whichever form you choose, each light sheds dim light in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius.  
>  
> As a Minor Action on your turn, you can move the lights up to [= 12 stride =] to a new spot within range. A light must be within [= 4 stride =] of another light created by this spell, and a light winks out if it exceeds the spell’s range.
