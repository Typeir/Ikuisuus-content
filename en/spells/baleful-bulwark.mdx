# Baleful Bulwark

Grim darkness envelops you, violently lashing out at any attacker with the crushing finality of oblivion itself.

> **Baleful Bulwark.**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a cup of water)  
> **Duration**: 1 hour  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: you  
>
> A protective magical force surrounds you, manifesting as a spectral mantle that covers you and your gear. You gain 5 temporary hit points for the duration. If a creature hits you with a melee attack while you have these hit points, the creature takes 5 dark damage.
>
> **Overcast:** the mantle gains 5 temporary hit points and 5 dark damage.
