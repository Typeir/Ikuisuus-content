# Conjure Barrage

A thrown weapon multiplies into a wall of identical steel that sweeps forward and vanishes.

---

> **Conjure Barrage**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (one piece of ammunition or a thrown weapon)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the cone  
>  
> You throw a mundane weapon or fire a piece of mundane ammunition into the air to create a barrage of identical weapons that sweep forward and then disappear. Creatures in the cone save Dexterity or take [% 3d8 %] damage of the same type as the weapon or ammunition you used.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.  
>  
> **Overcast (5th+):** **Volley**: you may instead call the barrage down as rain — a [= 8 stride;ADJ =]-radius, [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-high cylinder centered on a point within [= 30 stride =]. Creatures in the cylinder save Dexterity, halving [% 8d8 %] of the weapon's type. Volley gains [% 1d8 %] damage for each slot level above 5th.
