# Cystbolt

A sickly, greenish yellow harpoon of vileness converges from thin air; it skewers and pierces fiercely, leaving a trail of waste in its wake.

---

> **Cystbolt**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (powdered rhubarb leaf and an adder's stomach)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature within range  
>
> A sickly bolt flies toward targets and bursts.
> Make a ranged attack.
> Targets take [% 4d4 chemical %] immediately, and a further [% 2d4 chemical %] as a [# kw:delayed #] effect.
> Missing targets are still sprayed by the burst, taking half the immediate damage and no delayed damage.
>
> **Overcast:** both the immediate and delayed damage gain [% 1d4 %].
