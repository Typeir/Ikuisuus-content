# Psychic Nail

The spell plants a mark behind a creature's attention, too small to find, too exact to shed. As long as it holds, no distance is sufficient.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 4 hours"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see within range">

Targets save Wisdom, halving [% 3d8 psychic %].
Failing targets are tracked: while you share a Fold of the world, you always know their location until the spell ends, they can't become hidden from you, and invisibility grants them no benefit against you.  

**Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] psychic damage.  

</Spell>
