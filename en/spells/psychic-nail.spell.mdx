# Psychic Nail

The spell plants a mark behind a creature's attention, too small to find, too exact to shed. As long as it holds, no distance is sufficient.

---

> **Psychic Nail**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 4 hours  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within range  
>  
> Targets save Wisdom, halving [% 3d8 psychic %]. Failing targets are tracked: while you share a Fold of the world, you always know their location until the spell ends, they can't become hidden from you, and invisibility grants them no benefit against you.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] psychic damage.
