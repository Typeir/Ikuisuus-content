# Locate

A thread only you can feel pulls taut, and somewhere at its far end is the thing you named.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a forked twig)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="Self"
  targets="—">

Name your quarry, and for the duration you sense the direction to it whenever it is within [= 200 stride =] of you.
If it is moving, you know the direction of its movement.

<Choice>

<Feature targets="a specific object familiar to you, seen up close at least once">

#### Miscellany

If an object of that kind is instead merely described, you sense the nearest one.

</Feature>

<Feature targets="the nearest wildlife creature or plant of a kind you name">

#### Menagerie

You sense the direction to it.

</Feature>

</Choice>

Old wardings hold against this thread: the spell cannot find an object behind even a thin sheet of lead, and a named wildlife creature is lost to it across running water.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [= 100 stride =] of range and 15 minutes of duration.  

<Feature targets="a specific creature familiar to you, seen up close at least once">

#### Quarry <span>4th+</span>

You sense the direction to the target.
Running water at least [= 2 stride =] wide breaks the thread.  

</Feature>
</Overcast>

</Spell>
