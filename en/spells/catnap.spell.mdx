# Catnap

A soft, numbing haze envelopes the target, closing their eyes and drawing them into a shallow, dreamless sleep.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S, M"
  duration="10 minutes"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="up to three willing creatures you can see">

You make a calming gesture, and targets fall [# kw:condition:unconscious #] for the spell's duration. 
The spell ends early for targets that take damage or that someone spends a Major Action to shake or slap awake. 
Targets that remain [# kw:condition:unconscious #] for the full duration gain the benefit of a [# kw:Repose #], and can't be affected by this spell again until they finish a [# kw:Recovery #].

**Overcast:** put three additional willing creatures to sleep.

</Spell>
