# Spiritual Weapon

You are not holding it and it is not there, and it will still take a man's arm off at the shoulder.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="1 minute"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="you">

You shape a weapon out of nothing at a point within range.
Choose any melee weapon from the weapon list that **you are proficient with**; the shaped weapon has that weapon's reach and properties, and what you choose is what you get.
It lasts for the duration or until you cast this spell again.

When you cast the spell, and as a **Minor Action** on each of your turns, you can move the weapon up to [= 4 stride =] and make a melee attack against a creature within its reach that deals [% 1d8 force %] plus your [# kw:damage bonus #].

People shape what their god was known to carry.
Followers of the Golden One or his echoes raise Rhompaia of will and null; those of Kalmora, a grave-spade, and they dig with it as often as they swing it.

**Overcast:** the weapon's damage die advances one step along the [Dice Sequence](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapons-and-dice-levels) — [% 1d8 %] to [% 1d10 %] to [% 1d12 %] to [% 2d8 %], and onward.

</Spell>
