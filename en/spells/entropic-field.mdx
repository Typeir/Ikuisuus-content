# Entropic Field

The area becomes blanketed by a shadow of tombsteel, the arcane ceases to be, eternally still.

> **Entropic Field**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a filing of tombsteel, kept in the dark)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 2 hours  
> **Range**: Self ([= 2 stride;ADJ =] aura)  
> **Targets**: creatures and objects within [= 2 stride =] of you  
>
> A [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere of severance forms around you and moves with you. Inside it, **the Fold cannot be reached**. Nothing on the far side answers, and nothing that was already answering continues to.
>
> **What Stops.** Fold-casting stops — spells cannot be cast within the sphere, and a slot spent trying is spent for nothing. Väki bargains go unheard; whatever agreed to the terms is no longer within earshot. Precursor crystals sit inert, the water refusing to react. Any spell or magical effect already running on targets is suppressed while they remain there, and the time they spend suppressed still counts against its duration.
>
> **What Does Not.** **Will does.** Whatever targets hold by will alone — not cast, not bargained for, not bought — keeps working inside the null. The field severs the machinery of the world; it has no purchase whatsoever on somebody simply refusing.
>
> **Areas, Items and Summonings.** No area of magic extends into the sphere; where one overlaps, that part of it is suppressed and a gap opens in it. Heirlooms and magic items are mundane within the sphere, and a magic weapon's properties return the instant it leaves. Targets summoned or created by magic wink out while inside, and return the moment their space is clear of the sphere.
>
> **Travel.** Nothing enters or leaves the sphere by folding space. Passage between Folds of the world, and to the Clone Worlds, fails — whether the sphere is the origin or the destination — and any opening into a held space closes while it lies within.
>
> [_Objection_](/en/library/spells/objection) has no effect on the sphere, and two entropic fields do not cancel one another. Effects of genuinely divine make are unaffected; recognising one takes a DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check.
