# Healing Word

A single spoken word crosses the field and closes a wound as it lands.

---

> **Healing Word**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> Targets regain [% 2d4 %] + [# kw:damage bonus #] hit points. The dead are beyond it; constructs and the undead are unmoved.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 2d4 %] healing.  
>  
> **Overcast (3rd+): Chorus.** you may instead distribute the healing among up to six targets you can see in range.
