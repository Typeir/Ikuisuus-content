# Fold Deduplication

This spell is not innate to any vocation, but rather, granted as a boon or a blessing by a **Heirloom** or a **higher power**.

> “When you stare long enough into the abyss, the abyss casts **Fold Deduplication** back at you” - Empyrean proverb, unknown origin.  

---

> **Fold Deduplication**  
> _5th-Level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when a creature within [= 6 stride =] uses a heirloom of any kind, or casts a spell of 5th level or higher  
> **Components**: S, M (a bag of alchemical precursor worth 50gp)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: the triggering creature  
>  
> The caster launches a dense energy tether at targets, forcing overlapping energies to collapse at the point of impact.
>  
> Make a ranged attack against targets.  
>  
> - Targets take **[% 6d10 %] damage**, divided evenly between **holy, dark, and force**.  
> - The heirloom or heirlooms that triggered this reaction are **forcefully ejected**, landing **[= 1 stride =] away in random adjacent spaces**.  
> If the trigger was a **spell**, targets save **CON**, or lose the spell but **not the slot or spell points**.  
>  
> **On a miss**, the spell fails to connect and no effect occurs.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
