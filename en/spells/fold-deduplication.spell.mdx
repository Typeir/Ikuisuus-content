# Fold Deduplication

This spell is not innate to any vocation, but rather, granted as a boon or a blessing by a **Heirloom** or a **higher power**.

> “When you stare long enough into the abyss, the abyss casts **Fold Deduplication** back at you” - Empyrean proverb, unknown origin.
