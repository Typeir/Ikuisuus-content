# Blinding Barrier

A vertical wall of blinding light snaps into being, scorching and vaporizing matter where it touches.

---

> **Blinding Barrier**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action or 1 Reaction  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a shard of mirror, silvered on both faces)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a wall you create within range  
>
> A wall of brilliant light springs into existence at a point you choose within range. It appears in any orientation you choose — horizontal, vertical, or angled — and can be free floating or resting on a solid surface. It must be **straight**: light does not bend for you, and this barrier cannot be shaped into a ring, a curve, or a dome. You can form it up to [= 12 stride =] long, [= 2 stride =] high, and [= 1 stride =] thick.
>
> The wall sheds bright light out to [= 24 stride =] and dim light for an additional [= 24 stride =].
>
> When the wall appears, creatures in its area save Constitution, halving [% 4d8 holy %]. Failing targets are **blinded** for 1 minute. They can resist Constitution.
>
> The wall blocks line of sight but not movement. Creatures and objects can pass through it, and it **cannot be crossed through the Folds**. Creatures crossing the wall save Constitution or are **blinded** [# kw:briefly #]. Creatures that end their turn in the wall's area take [% 4d8 holy %].
>
> **The Beam.** Once on each of your turns, you can use a **Major Action** to launch a beam of light from the wall at one creature you can see within [= 12 stride =] of it. Make a ranged attack. Targets take [% 4d8 holy %] and save Constitution or are **blinded** [# kw:briefly #]. Hit or miss, the wall is [= 2 stride =] shorter afterward; if its length reaches 0, the spell ends.
>
> **Energy barrier.** Area effects pass straight through it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).
>
> **Overcast:** the wall gains [% 1d8 %] damage and [= 4 stride =] of length.
