# Locust Lunacy

Vermin descends on the target's senses, and every impulse is overshadowed by buzzing, clicking and gnawing.

---

> **Locust Lunacy**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Phantom locusts pour from your shadow and swarm the target's head, filling its senses with false wings and false steps. No attack roll is needed — the swarm simply finds the target.  
> For the duration, the target has disadvantage on **Wisdom (Perception)** checks, **Dexterity (Stealth)** checks, and [# kw:Concentration #] checks, and it cannot benefit from the Invisible condition.  
> They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature.  
>  
> **Overcast (4th+):** **The Swarm.**  
> **Targets**: creatures within a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you choose within range  
> The locusts fill the sphere, spreading around corners, and the spell gains the [( form:swarm )] aspect. As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, targets suffer the spell's effects. As a [# kw:delayed #] effect, they take [% 6d4 slashing %].  
>  
> **Overcast (4th+) (per +1):** the delayed damage gains [% 1d4 %].
