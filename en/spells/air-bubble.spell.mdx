# Air Bubble

A clear globe of air materializes around the target, a small, borrowed sky.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one willing creature you can see">

You create a bubble of breathable air around targets. 
The bubble moves with targets and allows them to breathe normally in an environment where air is otherwise unavailable; underwater, in choking smoke, or in vacuum.

The bubble lasts for the duration or until you dismiss it as a Major Action. 
A creature can only be affected by one air bubble at a time.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** target two additional willing creatures.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
