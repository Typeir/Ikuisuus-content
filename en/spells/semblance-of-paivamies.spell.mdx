# Semblance of Päivämies

The sickening spite of the usurping sun is briefly channeled through you, effusing with death and devastation upon your target.

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="creatures within a [= 6 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range">

Dim, sickly radiant light spreads within the sphere. The light spreads around corners, and it lasts until the spell ends.

As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, targets save Constitution or take [% 4d10 holy %], suffer one level of exhaustion, and emit a dim, golden light in a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] radius.
This light means targets cannot benefit from being invisible.

When the spell ends, the light fades and so does the exhaustion it caused, one level per round — **all but one level of it**.
That last one stays.
Whatever the usurping sun touches, it keeps a piece of.

</Spell>
