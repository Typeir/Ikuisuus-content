# Asphyxiate

A spell that manifests a pitch-black, opaque hand around a creature’s throat, crushing breath and will alike until it breaks free or collapses.

---

> **Asphyxiate**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> You conjure a **featureless, pitch-black hand** that closes around the throat and torso of targets.  
>  
> Targets save Strength or are **restrained** and **silenced** for the duration, the shadowy hand visibly constricting its body. They can resist Strength.  
>  
> While restrained by this spell, targets **cannot breathe**.  
>  
> At the **start of their next turn**, if targets are still restrained by Asphyxiate, they save Constitution or are **stunned** [# kw:briefly #].  
>  
> At the **start of each of their turns** while stunned by this spell, targets take [% 6d10 dark %] as the hand tightens further.  
>  
> If targets are ever more than [= 6 stride =] away from you, the shadowy hand dissipates and the spell immediately ends.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the hand gains [% 1d10 %] dark damage.
