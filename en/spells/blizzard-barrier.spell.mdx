# Blizzard Barrier

A wall of solid ice rockets up from the ground, forming a tall barrier that frosts the air.

---

> **Blizzard Barrier**
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action, or 1 Reaction, taken as the [barrier](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers) guide dictates  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a shard of ice that has never melted)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 30 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the wall's area   
>  
> A wall of ice springs into existence at a point you choose within range.
> You can make it up to **[= 12 stride =] long, [= 2 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick**.
> The wall lasts for the duration.
> When the wall appears, it pushes targets to one side of your choice.
>  
> The wall is an object that can be damaged and thus breached.
> It has AC 12 and 30 hit points per [= 2 stride;ADJ =] section.
> Reducing a section to 0 hit points destroys it and leaves behind a sheet of frigid air in the space the wall occupied.
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures moving through the sheet of frigid air save Constitution, halving [% 5d6 frost %].
> The frigid air lasts for 1 minute, though a strong wind can disperse it.
>  
> Instead of a straight wall, you can shape it into a hemispherical dome or a sphere of up to **[= 6 stride =] radius**, or into a flat surface of ten contiguous [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square panels.
> In any form it is [= 1/5 stride =] thick.
>  
> Creatures breaking through the wall save Strength, halving [% 10d6 frost %] and moving through as difficult terrain.
> Failing targets can't move through.
> 
> If you maintain concentration for the spell's whole duration **and you are still within casting range when it ends**, the wall is left behind as ordinary ice and can no longer be dispelled, melting at a regular pace (24 hours to melt completely in temperate weather).
> Otherwise the wall disappears when the spell ends.
> 
> **Solid barrier.** Area effects break against it.
> See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).
> 
> **Overcast:** the wall gains [= 2 stride =] of length, or its radius [= 2 stride =].
