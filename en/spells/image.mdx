# Image

A picture hangs in the air where nothing is, patient and almost true.

---

> **Image**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a bit of fleece)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: —  
>  
> You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other visible phenomenon no larger than a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] cube. The image is purely visual: sound, smell, and other sensations pass through it unchanged. As a Major Action, you can move the image to any spot within range and animate it naturally as it moves.
>  
> Physical interaction reveals the image for what it is, as things pass through it. A creature that uses a Major Action to examine it can determine it is an illusion with an Intelligence (Investigation) check against 10 plus [# kw:accuracy #]; on a success, the creature sees through the image.  
>  
> **Overcast (3rd+):** the image gains a [= 4 stride;ADJ =] cube and carries sound, smell, and temperature appropriate to what it depicts. It can hold a conversation's worth of noise, though it produces no true speech unless you direct it.  
>  
> **Overcast (6th+):** the image no longer requires concentration and lasts until dispelled.
