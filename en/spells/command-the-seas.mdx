# Command the Seas

You hark upon the life-giving waters, and they briefly listen. In plea, not command, whatever the scholars call the sorcery.

> **Command the Seas**  
> _4th-level Transmutation_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 60 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a mouthful of seawater, swallowed)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
>
> Until the spell ends, you control any freestanding water inside an area you choose that is a cube up to [= 20 stride =] on a side. You can choose from any of the following effects when you cast this spell. As a Major Action on your turn, you can repeat the same effect or choose a different one.
>
> - **Flood.** You cause the water level of all standing water in the area to rise by as much as [= 4 stride =]. If the area includes a shore, the flooding water spills over onto dry land.
>   If you choose an area in a large body of water, you instead create a [= 4 stride;ADJ =] tall wave that travels from one side of the area to the other and then crashes down. Any Huge or smaller vehicles in the wave’s path are carried with it to the other side. Any Huge or smaller vehicles struck by the wave have a 25% chance of capsizing.
>   The water level remains elevated until the spell ends or you choose a different effect. If this effect produced a wave, the wave repeats on your turn for the duration of the spell.
> - **Part Water.** You cause water in the area to move apart and create a trench. The trench extends across the spell’s area, and the separated water forms a wall to either side. The trench remains until the spell ends or you choose a different effect. The water then slowly fills in the trench over the course of the next round until the normal water level is restored.
> - **Redirect Flow.** You cause flowing water in the area to move in a direction you choose, even if the water has to flow over obstacles, up walls, or in other unlikely directions. The water in the area moves as you direct, but once it moves beyond the spell’s area, it resumes its flow based on the terrain conditions.
> - **Whirlpool.** This effect requires a body of water at least [= 10 stride =] square and [= 5 stride =] deep. You cause a whirlpool to form in the center of the area. The whirlpool forms a vortex that is [= 1 stride =] wide at the base, up to [= 10 stride =] wide at the top, and [= 5 stride =] deep. Any creature or object in the water and within [= 5 stride =] of the vortex is pulled [= 2 stride =] toward it. A creature can swim away from the vortex by making a successful Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC.
>   As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes [% 2d8 bludgeoning %] and is caught in the vortex until the spell ends. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage, and isn’t caught in the vortex. A creature caught in the vortex can use a Major Action to try to swim away as described above, but has disadvantage on the Strength (Athletics) check to do so.
>   As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), an object entering the vortex takes [% 2d8 bludgeoning %]; this damage occurs each round it remains in the vortex.
