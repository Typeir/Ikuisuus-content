# Arcane Lock

Runic filaments crawl across the fastening, stiffening metal and sealing its seams beneath a thin, humming film.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Until dispelled"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one closed door, window, gate, chest, or other entryway you touch">

Targets become locked for the duration. 
You and the creatures you designate when you cast this spell can open targets normally. 
You can also set a password that, when spoken within [= 1 stride =] of targets, suppresses this spell for 1 minute. 
Otherwise, they are impassable until they are broken or the spell is dispelled or suppressed. 
Casting knock on targets suppresses arcane lock for 10 minutes. 
While affected by this spell, targets are more difficult to break or force open; the DC to break them or pick any locks on them gains an [# kw:accuracy #] bonus of 10. 

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the lock gains 2 [# kw:accuracy #]. 

</Overcast>

</Spell>
