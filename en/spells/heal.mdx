# Heal

The body is told, firmly, what it was — and it complies.

---

> **Heal**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> A surge of vitality washes through targets: they regain **70 hit points** and are cured of blindness, deafness, and any diseases. Constructs and the undead are unmoved.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains 10 hit points of healing.  
>  
> **Overcast (8th+):** the surge ends the charmed, frightened, paralyzed, and stunned conditions on targets.  
>  
> **Overcast (9th+): Flood.** you may instead distribute **700 hit points** of healing among any number of creatures you can see within [= 12 stride =]; each creature healed this way also receives the full cleansing.
