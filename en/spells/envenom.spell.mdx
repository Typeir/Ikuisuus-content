# Envenom

A jet of acrid, sickly mist lashes from your palm, searing and withering exposed flesh.

---

<Spell
  level="cantrip"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 2 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

You extend your hand and project a puff of noxious gas from your palm. Targets save Constitution or take [% 1d12 poison %].

**Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage increases by [% 1d12 %] at each step.

<Choice>

<Feature targets="one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition">

#### Coat a weapon

You fill a vial with the stuff, enough to coat them.
The next hit made with it deals an extra [% 1d6 poison %].

</Feature>

<Feature targets="a meal or a drinking vessel">

#### Poison food

You taint it.
Creatures that consume it save Constitution or take [% 1d6 poison %] and are **[# kw:condition:poisoned #]** for 1 minute.

</Feature>

</Choice>

**Both decay one minute after you make them**, vial and all.
This is not a trade you can stockpile, you commit to it on the way through the door.

</Spell>
