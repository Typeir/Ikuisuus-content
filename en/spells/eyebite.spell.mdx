# Eyebite

A pair of spectral eyes flare and lock onto a target, flooding their vision with vertigo and nausea.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="one creature you can see within [= 12 stride =]">

For the duration, your eyes become an inky void imbued with dread power.
Until the spell ends, you can use a **Major Action** on each of your turns to fix targets.
Targets save Wisdom or suffer one of the following:  

**Asleep.** Targets fall **[# kw:condition:unconscious #]**.
They wake if they take any damage, or if another creature uses a Major Action to shake them awake.  

**Panicked.** Targets are **[# kw:condition:frightened #]** of you.
On each of their turns they must Dash away from you by the safest available route, unless there is nowhere to go.
The effect ends if targets get at least [= 12 stride =] from you and can no longer see you.  

**Sickened.** Targets are **[# kw:condition:poisoned #]** for the spell's duration. They can [# kw:resist #] Wisdom.  

Saving targets are immune to your gaze for 24 hours.
Constructs, and anything that cannot be [# kw:condition:charmed #], are immune to this spell entirely.  

**Overcast:** each use of your Major Action can fix one additional creature.
They must all be within [= 6 stride =] of one another, and each saves separately against the effect you chose.  

</Spell>
