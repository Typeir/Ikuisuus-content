# Eyebite

A pair of spectral eyes flare and lock onto a target, flooding their vision with vertigo and nausea.

---

> **Eyebite**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within [= 12 stride =]  
>  
> For the duration, your eyes become an inky void imbued with dread power. Until the spell ends, you can use a **Major Action** on each of your turns to fix targets. Targets save Wisdom or suffer one of the following:  
>  
> **Asleep.** Targets fall **unconscious**. They wake if they take any damage, or if another creature uses a Major Action to shake them awake.  
>  
> **Panicked.** Targets are **frightened** of you. On each of their turns they must Dash away from you by the safest available route, unless there is nowhere to go. The effect ends if targets get at least [= 12 stride =] from you and can no longer see you.  
>  
> **Sickened.** Targets are **poisoned** for the spell's duration. They can resist Wisdom.  
>  
> Saving targets are immune to your gaze for 24 hours. Constructs, and anything that cannot be charmed, are immune to this spell entirely.  
>  
> **Overcast:** each use of your Major Action can fix one additional creature. They must all be within [= 6 stride =] of one another, and each saves separately against the effect you chose.
