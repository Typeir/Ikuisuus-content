# Moonbeam

A shaft of pale light descends from whatever Kuutar is willing to lend. What burns is not the light itself but the thing the light reminds every living form that it carries.

---

> **Moonbeam**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a seed of any night-blooming plant)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-radius, [= 8 stride;ADJ =]-high cylinder centered on a point within range  
>
> A silvery beam shines down. Dim light fills the cylinder until the spell ends.
>
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures in the cylinder save Constitution, halving [% 2d10 holy %]. A shapechanger makes this save with disadvantage, and a failing shapechanger also reverts instantly to its true form and cannot take another while it remains in the light.
>
> On each of your turns after you cast this spell, you can move the beam [= 12 stride =] in any direction as a **Minor Action**.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
