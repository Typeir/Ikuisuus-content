# Fated Fist

This spell is not innate to any vocation, but rather, granted as a boon or a blessing by a **Heirloom** or a **higher power**.
The storm of the Everdark coils within the fist, every blow fated since the dawn of time.

> “One strike for every thread that dares bind you.” — The Dreamcatcher, to the white homunculus  

---

> **Fated Fist**  
> _11th-Level Legendary Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self (melee attacks)  
> **Targets**: one creature within your reach  
>  
> You may replace any melee attack you make with a **Fated Fist strike**.  
>  
> **Fated Fist Strike.** Make a melee attack that deals [% 5d12 lightning %]. Targets save Constitution or are stunned until the start of your next turn. A Fated Fist strike critically strikes on a 15–20, dealing [% 10d12 lightning %] on a critical hit.  
>  
> **Chain of Fate.** When you hit a creature with Fated Fist, it becomes marked until the spell ends. You instantly move up to [= 6 stride =] in a straight line toward a marked creature whenever you attempt to strike it with Fated Fist. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks. You may mark multiple creatures, and may dash between them with successive strikes.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the strike gains [% 1d12 lightning %].
