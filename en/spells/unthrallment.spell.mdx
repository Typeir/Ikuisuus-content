# Unthrallment

Invisible filaments slide along the subject's joints, loosening restraint and restoring fluid motion.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a leather strap, bound around the arm or a similar appendage)"
  duration="4 hours"
  range="Touch">

You touch a willing creature.
For the duration, the target’s movement is unaffected by difficult terrain, and spells and other magical effects can neither reduce the target’s speed nor cause the target to be [# kw:condition:paralyzed #] or [# kw:condition:restrained #].
The target can also spend [= 1 stride =] of movement to automatically escape from restraints, such as manacles or a creature that has it [# kw:condition:grappled #].
Finally, being underwater imposes no penalties on the target’s movement or attacks.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell touches one additional willing creature.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
