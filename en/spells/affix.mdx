# Affix

The touched object roots into the ground, its mass locking rigid and refusing to shift.

---

> **Affix**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: 1 hour  
>  
> You touch an object that weighs up to [= 5 burden =] and cause it to become magically fixed in place. Until the spell ends, the object is immobile, and can’t be moved, even by force. If the object is fixed to a surface, such as a wall or a floor, it can support up to [= 1000 burden =] of weight. More weight causes the object to fall. A creature can use a Major Action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC. On a success, the creature can move the object up to [= 2 stride =]. On a failure it gains a level of exhaustion.
