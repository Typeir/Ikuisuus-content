# Jump

Gravity loosens at the target's feet, launching them upward in a sudden, powerful leap.

---

> **Jump**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a grasshopper’s hind leg)  
> **Duration**: 1 minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one creature you touch  
>  
> Targets’ jump distance is tripled until the spell ends.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature.
