# Conjure Barrage

A thrown weapon multiplies into a wall of identical steel that sweeps forward and vanishes.

---

  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"

You throw a mundane weapon or fire a piece of mundane ammunition into the air to create a barrage of identical weapons that sweep forward and then disappear.
