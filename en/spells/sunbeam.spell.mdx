# Sunbeam

Kultharja is very far away and leans closer for nobody. What you are given is a borrowed inch of that light, held in the hand, and an inch of it is more than enough.

---

> **Sunbeam**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a lens of polished quartz)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] line)  
> **Targets**: creatures in a [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-wide, [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-long line originating from you  
>
> A beam of brilliant light flashes from your hand.
> Creatures in the line save Constitution, halving [% 6d8 holy %].
> Failing targets are **blinded** [# kw:briefly #].
>
> As a **Major Action** on any turn until the spell ends, you can loose a new line.
>
> For the duration, a mote of that light burns in your hand, shedding bright light in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 6 stride =].
> **This light is sunlight** — see [Vision, Light, and Environment](/en/library/rules/the-long-road/vision-light-and-environment).
>
> **Overcast:** the beam gains [% 1d8 %] holy damage.
