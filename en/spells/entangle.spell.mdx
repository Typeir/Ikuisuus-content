# Entangle

Knotted roots and thorned vines burst from the earth, wrapping around anything in their path.

---

> **Entangle**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] square starting from a point within range, when you cast this spell  
>
> Grasping weeds and vines sprout from the ground, filling the square. The square is difficult terrain for the duration.
>
> Targets save Strength or are restrained until the spell ends. Failing targets can use a Major Action to make a Strength (Athletics) check, freeing themselves on a success.
> The growth is flammable: any [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube exposed to fire burns away in 1 round, applying the [_Burning_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning) as an [_delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) to creatures in the fire.
> When the spell ends, the conjured plants wilt away.
>
> **Overcast:** the growth gains one additional [= 2 stride;ADJ =] square.
>
> **Overcast (2nd+): Lattice.** The growth thickens into a clinging web of fiber and thorn. The duration becomes Concentration, up to 10 minutes, the square is lightly obscured, and, as a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures also save Strength.
>
> **Overcast (4th+): Crush.** The growth constricts. Restrained targets take [% 3d6 physical %] at the start of each of their turns.
