# Animal Shapes

Bodies convulse as flesh and bone contort into animal anatomies—limbs shorten, jaws lengthen, and fur or scales erupt across the skin.

---

<Spell
  level="8"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 24 hours"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="any number of willing creatures you can see">

Your magic turns others into wildlife.

You transform each target into the form of a Large or smaller wildlife creature with a challenge rating of 4 or lower.
On subsequent turns, you can use your Major Action to transform affected creatures into new forms.
The transformation lasts for the duration for each target, or until it drops to 0 hit points or dies.
You can choose a different form for each target.
A target’s game statistics are replaced by the statistics of the chosen wildlife creature, though it retains its alignment and Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores.
Each target assumes the hit points of its new form, and when it reverts to its normal form, it returns to the number of hit points it had before it transformed.
If it reverts as a result of dropping to 0 hit points, any excess damage carries over to its normal form.
As long as the excess damage doesn’t reduce its normal form to 0 hit points, it isn’t knocked [# kw:condition:unconscious #].

Targets are limited in the actions they can perform by the nature of their new forms, and they can’t speak, cast spells, or take any other action that requires hands or speech.
Each target’s gear melds into the new form.
Each target can’t activate, use, wield, or otherwise benefit from any of its equipment.  

</Spell>
