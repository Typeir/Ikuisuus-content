# True Light

This spell is not innate to any vocation, but granted to those who have collected a tear from the **Twin Eyes of Truth**.
The first burns cold, the other burns bright, locked in eternal conflict.

> “The sun we know is a lie. The moon we long for a fraud.  
> The mad god of creation shunned their children and now we play the part of both their jailor and their plaything”  
>  
> — Aeridas, heir to nothing.  

---

> **True Light**  
> _11th-Level Legendary Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, M (a scale of sunstone and a lunar pearl worth at least 2,500 gp each, both consumed)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
>  
> You conjure **two celestial orbs** — one of radiant sunlight, the other of freezing moonlight — each [= 2 stride =] in diameter.  
> Both appear within range and hover at points you can see.  
> You can move one orb up to **[= 6 stride =]** as a Minor Action on your turn.  
>  
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures within **[= 4 stride =]** of an orb take **10 + 40 ([% 8d10 %])** (holy or fire) damage and must make a **Constitution saving throw**.  
>  
> - **Sun Orb**: On a failed save, a creature takes **holy damage** and is **blinded** until the start of its next turn.  
> - **Moon Orb**: On a failed save, a creature takes **frost damage** and is **slowed**, reducing its movement speed by half and preventing reactions until the start of its next turn.  
>  
> On a success, they take half damage instead, and suffer no other adverse effects.  
> If the two orbs come within **[= 2 stride =]** of each other, they detonate in a pulse of creation.  
> All creatures within **[= 12 stride =]** of the point between them must make a Dexterity Saving throw or take **50 + 100 ([% 20d10 holy %]) and frost damage, split evenly.  
> Creatures take half as much damage on a success.  
> The orbs vanish after this detonation or when the spell ends.  
> The orbs can be pushed by effects from other creatures.  
>  
> You are blinded for the duration of the spell.
