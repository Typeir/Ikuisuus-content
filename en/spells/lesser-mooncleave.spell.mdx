# Lesser Mooncleave

This cantrip is not innate to any vocation, but rather, granted as a boon or a blessing by a **Heirloom** or a **higher power**.
Those who heed the calls of the corpse-god **Küütar** and her master, the **Red Queen**, should find this trick in their repertoire, sooner or later.

---

> **Lesser Mooncleave**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a lunar wyvern scale)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the line  
>
> Unleash a razor-thin crescent of force and frost in a line.  
> Targets take [% 1d6 frost %] + [% 1d6 force %].  
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage doubles at each step.  
> When the wave reduces a target to 0 HP, it continues through them to the next target in line.
