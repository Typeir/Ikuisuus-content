# Fog

You exhale, and the breath does not disperse. The air of a dead world is always most of the way to fog already; it takes very little persuading to finish the job.

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You create a **[= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius** sphere of fog centered on a point within range.
It spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured.

As a **Minor Action** on any later turn, you can plant another fog of the same size at a point within range.
Every fog belongs to this one casting and this one concentration — there is no limit on how many you can lay down, only on how many turns you are prepared to spend laying them.  

Each fog lasts until the spell ends, or until a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least [= 8 league =] per hour) disperses that one.
Wind takes them individually; the rest stay where they are.  

**Overcast:** every fog you plant gains [= 1 stride =] of radius.  

</Spell>
