# Sleet Storm

Ragged sleet lashes outward, pelting everything with biting ice and turning surfaces slick.

---

> **Sleet Storm**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]

> The ground in the cylinder is covered with slick ice, making it difficult terrain.
