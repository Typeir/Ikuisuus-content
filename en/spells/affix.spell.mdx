# Affix

The touched object roots into the ground, its mass locking rigid and refusing to shift.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one object you touch that weighs up to [= 5 burden =]">

You cause targets to become magically fixed in place. 
Until the spell ends, targets are immobile, and can’t be moved, even by force.
If targets are fixed to a surface, such as a wall or a floor, they can support up to [= 1000 burden =].
More weight causes targets to fall. 
A creature can use a Major Action to make a Strength check to move a target up to [= 2 stride =]. 
Failing creatures gain a level of [# kw:condition:exhaustion #].

</Spell>
