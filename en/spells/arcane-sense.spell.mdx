# Arcane Sense

Gain a sixth sense for the duration. It allows you to perceive the arcane in the world.

<Spell
  level="1"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 30 minutes"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

For the duration, you sense the presence of magic within [= 6 stride =] of you. 
If you sense magic in this way, you can use your Major Action to see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bears magic.

The spell can penetrate most barriers, but it is blocked by [= 1/5 stride =] of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of dense metal, or [= 3/5 stride =] of wood or dirt.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [= 1 stride =] of range and 15 minutes of duration.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
