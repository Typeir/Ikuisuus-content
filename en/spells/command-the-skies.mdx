# Command the Skies

You hark upon the sky-väkis, and they briefly listen. They are not obedient so much as agreeable, and only for as long as the arrangement amuses them.

---

> **Command the Skies**  
> _4th-level Transmutation_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 60 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a whistle carved from hollow bone)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour
>
> You take control of the air in a [= 20 stride;ADJ =] cube you can see within range. Choose one of the following effects when you cast the spell. It lasts for the duration unless you use a **Major Action** on a later turn to switch to a different one. Only one effect can be active at a time.
>
> - **Gusts.** A wind picks up within the cube, blowing continuously in a horizontal direction you choose, at an intensity you choose: calm, moderate, or strong. While it is moderate or strong, ranged weapon attacks that pass through the cube or are made against targets inside it have disadvantage. While it is strong, a creature moving against the wind spends [= 1 stride =] extra per [= 1 stride =] moved.
> - **Downdraft.** A sustained blast of wind blows downward from the top of the cube. Ranged weapon attacks that pass through the cube or are made against targets inside it have disadvantage.As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), a flying creature must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be knocked **prone**.
> - **Updraft.** A sustained updraft rises from the cube's bottom edge. A creature that ends a fall within the cube takes only half the falling damage, and a creature making a vertical jump inside it can jump up to [= 2 stride =] higher than normal.
> - **Whirlwind.** A vortex forms in the cube: [= 2 stride =] wide at its base, up to [= 10 stride =] wide at its top, and as tall as the cube. Every unsecured object of Medium size or smaller within it that is not worn or carried is drawn up, carried spiralling for a round, and flung outward to land [% 2d6 %] × [= 2 stride =] from the vortex in a random direction.
>   As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be lifted [= 4 stride =] and dropped, taking falling damage as normal.
