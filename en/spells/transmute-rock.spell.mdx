# Transmute Rock

Stone in the area liquefies into a flowing mass, pouring from supports and causing walls and ground to slump.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Until dispelled"
  range="[= 24 stride =]">

You choose an area of stone or mud in range that fits within a [= 8 stride;ADJ =] cube and that is within sight.
You can transmute rock to mud or mud to rock.  

If you choose an area of stone, mundane rock of any sort in the area becomes an equal volume of thick, flowing mud that remains for the spell’s duration.
The ground in the spell’s area becomes muddy enough that creatures can sink into it.
Each [= 1 stride =] that a creature moves through the mud costs [= 4 stride =] of movement.
As a [# kw:persistent #], [# kw:delayed #] effect, creatures on the ground save Strength.
On a failed save, a creature sinks into the mud and is [# kw:condition:restrained #], though it can use a Major Action to end the [# kw:condition:restrained #] condition by pulling itself free.
If you cast the spell on a ceiling, the mud falls.
Any creature under the mud when it falls saves Dexterity.
A creature takes [% 4d8 bludgeoning %] on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.  

If you choose an area of mud, it is hardened to stone.
If you cast the spell on a ceiling, the mud on it becomes stone and may cause the ceiling to collapse at the Game Director’s discretion.  

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the cube increases by **[= 2 stride =]** on a side for each slot level above 5th.  

</Spell>
