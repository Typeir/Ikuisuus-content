# Compelled Duel

You name a challenge aloud and something older than either of you writes it down. Their blood recognises the terms even where they do not.

---

> **Compelled Duel**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> Targets save Wisdom or are bound to the challenge for the duration: they have **disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but you**, and each time they try to move to a space more than [= 6 stride =] from you they save Wisdom, succeeding to move freely for that turn.
>
> **The spell ends if you attack or target any creature other than them**, or if you end your turn more than [= 6 stride =] away from them. An oath kept badly is not kept.
>
> **Overcast:** the range and leash distance each gain [= 3 stride =]. A larger circle is still a circle.
