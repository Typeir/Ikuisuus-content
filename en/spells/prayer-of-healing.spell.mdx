# Prayer of Healing

A warm, cleansing light washes over the injured, knitting torn flesh and sealing wounds.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="10 Minutes"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="up to six creatures you can see within range">

Targets each regain hit points equal to [% 2d8 %] plus your [# kw:damage bonus #].
Constructs are unaffected.
There is nothing in them for this to knit.  

Targets that spend the full ten minutes with you also gain **the benefit of a [# kw:Repose #]**, and cannot benefit from this part of the spell again until they finish a [# kw:Recovery #].
Ten minutes is a long time to stand still, and it is meant to be.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] healing.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
