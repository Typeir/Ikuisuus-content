# Prayer of Healing

A warm, cleansing light washes over the injured, knitting torn flesh and sealing wounds.

---

> **Prayer of Healing**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 10 Minutes  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: up to six creatures you can see within range  
>  
> Targets each regain hit points equal to [% 2d8 %] plus your [# kw:damage bonus #]. Constructs are unaffected. There is nothing in them for this to knit.  
>  
> Targets that spend the full ten minutes with you also gain **the benefit of a Repose**, and cannot benefit from this part of the spell again until they finish a Recovery. Ten minutes is a long time to stand still, and it is meant to be.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] healing.
