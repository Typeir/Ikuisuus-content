# Dragon Whole

You claim an ephemeral throne besides True Terror and Creation, and your body beckons a vile transmogrification; you become fear incarnate.

---

> **Dragon Whole**  
> _7th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a scale, a fang, and a talon, all taken from one dragon)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: yourself  
>
> You take on a dragon's whole shape and bearing for the duration, gaining all of the following.
>
> **Terrifying Presence.** You gain a +2 bonus to your AC and Saving Throws, and creatures you choose within [= 3 stride =] have disadvantage on saving throws against being frightened.
>
> **Draconic Senses.** You have blindsight out to [= 6 stride =].
>
> **Wings.** You have a flying speed of [= 12 stride =] and do not provoke opportunity attacks when you fly out of a creature's reach.
>
> **Fang and Talon.** When you take the Attack action, or as a **Major Action**, you make one bite and two talon strikes, each a melee spell attack. The bite reaches [= 2 stride =] and deals [% 2d10 physical %]. A hit against a creature no more than one size larger than you leaves it **grappled** in your jaws (escape DC equals 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #]). Each talon reaches [= 1 stride =] and deals [% 3d8 physical %].
>
> **Breath Weapon.** When you cast the spell, choose a damage type: fire, frost, or lightning. As a **Minor Action**, you exhale a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] cone of that type. Creatures in the cone save Dexterity, halving [% 6d6 %] of that type.
>
> **Overcast:** Breath Weapon gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
