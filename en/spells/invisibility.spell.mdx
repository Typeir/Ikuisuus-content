# Invisibility

Light bends around the target until they vanish from sight.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (an eyelash encased in gum arabic)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature you touch">

Targets become invisible until the spell ends.
Anything they are wearing or carrying is invisible as long as it is on their person.
The spell ends for targets that attack or cast a spell.  

<Overcast>

**(3rd+):** target one additional creature.  

**(4th+):** the invisibility no longer ends when targets attack or cast a spell, its duration becomes 1 minute, and the spell gains one additional target.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
