# Shocking Grasp

A blue-white arc of electricity springs from your palm into whatever you touch, muscles convulsing and skin smoking.

---

> **Shocking Grasp**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one creature  
>  
>  
> Lightning springs from your hand to shock targets.  
>  
> Make a melee attack that deals [% 1d8 lightning %] and staggers targets [# kw:briefly #].
> You have advantage on the attack roll if targets are wearing armor made of metal.  
>  
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage increases by [% 1d8 %] at each step.
