# Prasinus's Path

The green knight's road runs through living wood: you step into a tree, and the world's surviving trees are all one door.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

You gain the ability to enter a living tree at least your size and step from inside it to inside another tree of the same kind within [= 100 stride =].
Spending [= 1 stride =] of movement, you step into a tree and know the location of every tree of its kind within [= 100 stride =] large enough to receive you; you may emerge from one of them, appearing in an unoccupied space within [= 1 stride =] of it.
With movement remaining, you may repeat this, tree to tree, for as long as your concentration lasts.  

<Overcast>

<Feature targets="a Large-or-larger plant within [= 2 stride =], and any plant you have seen or touched at least once, at any distance within the same Fold of the world">

#### Greenway <span>6th+</span>

You open a one-round conduit between the two plants.
Any creature can step through, from the one plant to the other, using [= 1 stride =] of movement.
In a dead universe, the great surviving trees are the last reliable roads — and Prasinus knows them all.  

</Feature>
</Overcast>

</Spell>
