# Provisions

Fresh provisions and a basin of clean water materialize, steaming as they settle. Every wizard's is different, and every wizard is oddly proud of theirs.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You create [= 23 burden =] of food and [= 120 volume =] of water on the ground or in containers within range — enough to sustain fifteen humanoids or five steeds for 24 hours.
It keeps for **ten days** before it spoils, which is what makes it worth carrying rather than merely worth eating.
The water is clean and does not go bad at all.  

The food takes whatever form the caster habitually conjures. It is nourishing without exception and delicious without reliability.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** double the food and water.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
