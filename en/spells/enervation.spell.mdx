# Enervation

A shadowy beam lashes the target, siphoning warmth and leaving flesh pale and slack.

---

> **Enervation**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> A tendril of inky darkness reaches out from you, touching a creature within range to drain life from it. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 4d8 dark %]. Saving targets end the spell.  
>  
> For the duration, you can use your Major Action on each of your later turns to deal [% 4d8 dark %] to failing targets automatically. The spell ends if you use your Major Action for anything else, if failing targets are ever outside the spell's range, or if they have total cover from you.  
>  
> Whenever the spell deals damage, you regain hit points equal to half the dark damage dealt.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] dark.
