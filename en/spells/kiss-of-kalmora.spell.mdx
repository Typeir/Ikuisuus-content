# Kiss of Kalmora

A sickly pall blooms on the struck flesh as filaments burrow in, spreading crawling decay.

---

> **Kiss of Kalmora**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 7 days  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one creature you can reach  
>  
> Your touch inflicts disease. Make a melee attack that poisons targets.  
>  
> At the end of each of their turns, targets must make a Constitution saving throw. After failing three of these saving throws, the disease’s effects last for the duration, and they stop making these saves. After succeeding on three of these saving throws, targets are no longer poisoned, and the spell ends.  
>  
> While targets are poisoned in this way, they are subjected to one of the diseases below. The effects of the disease last for the spell’s duration or until the disease is cured.  
>  
> **Blinding Sickness.** Pain grips the creature’s mind, and its eyes turn milky white. Targets have disadvantage on Wisdom checks and Wisdom saving throws and are blinded.  
>  
> **Filth Fever.** A raging fever sweeps through the creature’s body. Targets have disadvantage on Strength checks, Strength saving throws, and attack rolls that use Strength.  
>  
> **Flesh Rot.** The creature’s flesh decays. Targets have disadvantage on Charisma checks and vulnerability to all damage.  
>  
> **Mindfire.** The creature’s mind becomes feverish. Targets have disadvantage on Intelligence checks and Intelligence saving throws and behave as if under the effects of the confusion spell during combat.  
>  
> **Seizure.** The creature is overcome with shaking. Targets have disadvantage on Dexterity checks, Dexterity saving throws, and attack rolls that use Dexterity.  
>  
> **Slimy Doom.** The creature begins to bleed uncontrollably. Targets have disadvantage on Constitution checks and Constitution saving throws. In addition, whenever targets take damage, they are stunned [# kw:briefly #].
