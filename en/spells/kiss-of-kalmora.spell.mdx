# Kiss of Kalmora

A sickly pall blooms on the struck flesh as filaments burrow in, spreading crawling decay.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="7 days"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature you can reach">

Your touch inflicts disease. Make a melee attack that poisons targets.  

They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution, [# kw:chained #](3/3). Failing the chain fixes the disease for the duration.  

While targets are [# kw:condition:poisoned #] in this way, they are subjected to one of the diseases below.
The effects of the disease last for the spell’s duration or until the disease is cured.  

**Blinding Sickness.** Pain grips the creature’s mind, and its eyes turn milky white.
Targets have disadvantage on Wisdom checks and Wisdom saving throws and are [# kw:condition:blinded #].  

**Filth Fever.** A raging fever sweeps through the creature’s body.
Targets have disadvantage on Strength checks, Strength saving throws, and attack rolls that use Strength.  

**Flesh Rot.** The creature’s flesh decays. Targets have disadvantage on Charisma checks and vulnerability to all damage.  

**Mindfire.** The creature’s mind becomes feverish.
Targets have disadvantage on Intelligence checks and Intelligence saving throws and behave as if under the effects of the confusion spell during combat.  

**Seizure.** The creature is overcome with shaking.
Targets have disadvantage on Dexterity checks, Dexterity saving throws, and attack rolls that use Dexterity.  

**Slimy Doom.** The creature begins to bleed uncontrollably.
Targets have disadvantage on Constitution checks and Constitution saving throws.
In addition, whenever targets take damage, they are [# kw:condition:stunned #] [# kw:briefly #].  

</Spell>
