# Telekinesis

An unseen gauntlet of force seizes objects and bodies, lifting and slamming them with crushing pressure.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature or object you can see within range">

You gain the ability to move or manipulate targets at a distance.
When you cast the spell, and as a **Major Action** each round for the duration, you can seize targets, causing the appropriate effect below.
You can affect the same targets round after round, or choose new targets at any time.
Switching targets ends the spell’s effect on the previous ones.

**Creature.**  
**Targets**: a Huge or smaller creature  
You make a check with your accuracy, contested by targets’ Strength check.
If you win the contest, you move targets up to [= 6 stride =] in any direction, including upward, but not beyond the range of this spell.
**Once that movement resolves, you choose** whether to keep hold: if you do, targets are [# kw:condition:restrained #] by your grip until your next turn ends.
Targets can take a Major Action to make a Strength check to break free.

**Object.**  
**Targets**: an object that weighs up to [= 500 burden =]  
If targets aren’t worn or carried, you automatically move them up to [= 6 stride =] in any direction, but not beyond the range of this spell.
If they are worn or carried by a creature, you make a check with your accuracy, contested by that creature’s Strength check.
If you succeed, you pull targets away from that creature and can move them up to [= 6 stride =] in any direction but not beyond the range of this spell.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell’s weight limit gains [= 500 burden =], and its move distance [= 2 stride =].

</Overcast>

</Spell>
