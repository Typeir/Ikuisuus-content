# Null Sphere

Born from nothingness, a ravenous void lurches at reality, undoing all that it touches.

---

> **Null Sphere**  
> _9th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a bead of lead, dropped)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 200 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you can see in range  
>
> A sphere of collapsing gravity forms at the point and pulls at everything not anchored against it. The sphere lasts for the duration, and its area is difficult terrain.
>
> As a [_persistent, delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), targets save Strength, halving [% 5d10 force %]. Failing targets are dragged straight toward the center, ending in the nearest unoccupied space to it. Unattended objects are pulled the same way.
>
> At the start of your turn the sphere deals [% 5d10 force %] to targets and objects inside it, no saving throw. Anything the sphere overlaps is pulled to its center.
>
> Within the area of an [Antimagic Shell](/en/library/spells/antimagic-shell) or an [Entropic Field](/en/library/spells/entropic-field), the sphere is suppressed for as long as that effect lasts.
>
> **Overcast:** the sphere gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
