# Null Sphere

Born from nothingness, a ravenous void lurches at reality, undoing all that it touches.

---

<Spell
  level="9"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a bead of lead, dropped)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 200 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you can see in range">

A sphere of collapsing gravity forms at the point and pulls at everything not anchored against it.
The sphere lasts for the duration, and its area is difficult terrain.

As a [# kw:persistent #], [# kw:delayed #] effect, targets save Strength, halving [% 5d10 force %].
Failing targets are dragged straight toward the center, ending in the nearest unoccupied space to it.
Unattended objects are pulled the same way.

At the start of your turn the sphere deals [% 5d10 force %] to targets and objects inside it, no saving throw.
Anything the sphere overlaps is pulled to its center.

Within the area of an [Antimagic Shell](/en/library/spells/antimagic-shell) or an [Entropic Field](/en/library/spells/entropic-field), the sphere is suppressed for as long as that effect lasts.

**Overcast:** the sphere gains [% 1d10 %] damage.

</Spell>
