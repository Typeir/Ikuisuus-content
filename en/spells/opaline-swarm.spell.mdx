# Opaline Swarm

Airborne glimmer coalesces into minute asteroids resembling Taiva's ever shining belt.

> **Opaline Swarm**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 30 minutes  
> **Range**: Self ([= 24 stride =])  
> **Targets**: you  
>  
> You create six tiny meteors in your space. They float in the air and orbit you for the spell’s duration.  
>  
> When you cast the spell, and as a Minor Action on each of your turns thereafter, you can expend one or two of the meteors, sending them streaking toward a point or points you choose within [= 24 stride =] of you. Once a meteor reaches its destination or impacts against a solid surface, the meteor explodes. Creatures within [= 1 stride =] of the point where the meteor explodes save Dexterity, halving [% 2d6 fire %].  
>  
> **Overcast:** you create two additional meteors.
