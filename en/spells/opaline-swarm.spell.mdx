# Opaline Swarm

Airborne glimmer coalesces into minute asteroids resembling Taiva's ever shining belt.

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 30 minutes"
  range="Self ([= 24 stride =])"
  targets="you">

You create six tiny meteors in your space. They float in the air and orbit you for the spell’s duration.  

When you cast the spell, and as a Minor Action on each of your turns thereafter, you can expend one or two of the meteors, sending them streaking toward a point or points you choose within [= 24 stride =] of you.
Once a meteor reaches its destination or impacts against a solid surface, the meteor explodes.
Creatures within [= 1 stride =] of the point where the meteor explodes save Dexterity, halving [% 2d6 fire %].  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** you create two additional meteors.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
