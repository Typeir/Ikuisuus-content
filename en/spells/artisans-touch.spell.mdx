# Artisan's Touch

A tool becomes pristine and perfected; capable of elevating the work of any artisan.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="10 Minutes"
  components="V, S"
  duration="10 days"
  range="Touch"
  targets="a tool used by a trade">

You touch the target object, giving it the gift of master craftsmanship.

While the caster wields the tool, it gains the following benefits:

For the duration, the tool gains an enchantment, granting a [# kw:accuracy #] bonus of +1.
Any output produced with the tool increases one level in the [# kw:quality #] ladder.

If the tool would be wielded by a creature other than the caster, the benefits do not apply.

If the output produced would already be of the **True** quality, there is no effect.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the duration increases by 10 days, and the tool's bonus increases by 1.
The bonus does not increase the quality of the output further. 

</Overcast>

</Spell>
