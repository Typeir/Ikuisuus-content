# Burning Barrier

A vertical wall of flame erupts, scorching the air and singeing any matter it touches.

---

> **Burning Barrier**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action or 1 Reaction  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
>  
>  
> 
>  
> You create a wall of fire on a solid surface within range. You can make the wall up to [= 12 stride =] long, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick, or a ringed wall up to [= 4 stride =] in diameter, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick. The wall is opaque and lasts for the duration.  
>  
> When the wall appears, each creature within its area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes [% 5d8 fire %], or half as much damage on a successful save.  
>  
> One side of the wall, selected by you when you cast this spell, deals [% 5d8 fire %] as an [_incipient effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) to each creature within [= 2 stride =] of that side or inside the wall. The wall itself deals the same damage as a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements). The other side of the wall deals no damage.  
>  
> **Energy barrier.** Area effects pass straight through it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by [% 1d8 %] for each slot level above 4th.
