# Nailed to the Sky / Bound to the Earth

This spell is not innate to any vocation, but rather, granted as a boon or a blessing by a **Heirloom** or a **higher power**.
This spell can be attained by the scions of ambition, if they ever survive the trial that comes with embracing true potential.

> “Nor heavens above or hells below will take our destiny from us.” - Nekarion, the Dreadlord

---

  level="12"
  rarity="legendary"
  cost="1 Major Action"

You deliver an uppercut with impossible force, launching your foe into the firmament.

**Nailed to the Sky.**
**Targets**: one creature you can effectively grapple
Make a melee attack with your weapon that deals [% 10d12 +75 force %] and causes targets to save Strength or be hurled into the upper atmosphere, vanishing from the battlefield.

While in orbit:

- Targets are banished but continue to suffer [% 4d6 frost %] per round and begin [# kw:condition:suffocating #].
- If no open sky exists, targets instead collide with the ceiling, taking [% 20d12 bludgeoning %], and the spell ends.
- You may choose to follow targets; if you do, you vanish with them and remain linked until impact.
- Targets launched this way fall naturally after 50 rounds (10 minutes), taking frost damage for the first 25 rounds.

</Spell>

<Spell
  cost="1 Minor Action, while concentrating and only if you followed targets"
  targets="the creature you launched">

**Bound to the Earth.**
You end the spell early, dragging targets from orbit in a blazing arc to a point of your choice within [= 24 stride =] of where they were struck.
Targets save Constitution, halving [% 10d12 +75 bludgeoning %] and [% 10d12 force %].
Targets instantly die if brought to 0 HP or less this way.
**Bound to the Earth** immediately triggers **Shattered Into Dust**.

**Shattered Into Dust.**
**Targets**: creatures of your choice within a [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on that point, other than the one affected by **Bound to the Earth**
Targets save Dexterity, halving that damage. Failing targets are knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].

You land harmlessly, standing in an unoccupied space within [= 1 stride =] of the impact zone.

</Spell>
