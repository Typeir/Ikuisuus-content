# Objection

The snapping of fingers, or an open palm against an arcane source; a motion universally declaring a stop, to which the universe answers.

**Objection (Reaction).**  
**Targets**: the triggering effect's origin  
You thrust your refusal between targets and their working.
Make a [# kw:raw roll;RR #] save using [# kw:accuracy #] against a DC equal to 10 + targets' [# kw:accuracy #], increased by the difference between the triggering effect's level and the slot level you cast this spell at (minimum increase of 0).
On a success, the spell or effect fails and has no effect.  

Where targets have no [# kw:accuracy #], the DC is instead 10 + their tier bonus + the difference.
Where no origin can feasibly be determined, the DC is 13 + the difference.  

**Unweaving (Major Action).**  
**Targets**: one creature, object, or magical effect within range  
One spell or magical effect on targets ends if its level is equal to or lower than the slot level you cast this spell at.
Against a higher-level effect, make the same save described above; on a success, it ends.

**(per +1):** the level difference used for the Reaction's DC continues to shrink, and the Unweaving ends one additional spell or magical effect on targets.
