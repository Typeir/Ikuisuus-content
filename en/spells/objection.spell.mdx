# Objection

A jagged seam of muted light lashes outward, and conjured energies shatter into dull embering motes.

---

> **Objection**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when you see a creature within [= 12 stride =] of you casting a spell or when a magical effect activates within range, or 1 Major Action, to unweave standing magic  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: —  
>  
> **Objection (Reaction).** You thrust your refusal between a caster and their working. Make a [RR](/en/library/rules/the-measure-of-the-self/rolling-and-special-rolls) save using [# kw:accuracy #] against a DC equal to 10 + the triggering caster's [# kw:accuracy #], increased by the difference between the triggering effect's level and the slot level you cast this spell at (minimum increase of 0). On a success, the spell or effect fails and has no effect.  
>  
> If the triggering effect is not a traditional spell, the DC is instead 10 + its creator's tier bonus + the difference. If the creator cannot feasibly be determined, the DC is 13 + the difference.  
>  
> **Unweaving (Major Action).**  
> **Targets**: one creature, object, or magical effect within range  
> One spell or magical effect on targets ends if its level is equal to or lower than the slot level you cast this spell at. Against a higher-level effect, make the same save described above; on a success, it ends.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the level difference used for the Reaction's DC continues to shrink, and the Unweaving ends one additional spell or magical effect on targets.
