# Lightning Edge

A spell that condenses electrical energy into a singular cutting vector, striking like a blade drawn at impossible speed.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="one creature within range">

You carve a blade of compressed lightning and drive it forward in a single, decisive cut.  

Make a melee attack that deals [% 4d10 lightning %].  

Targets save Constitution or are [# kw:condition:stunned #] [# kw:briefly #].  

Targets wearing metal armor or made primarily of metal save at disadvantage.  

</Spell>
