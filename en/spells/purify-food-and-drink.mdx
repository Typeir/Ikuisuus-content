# Purify Food and Drink

A clean, odorless wave washes over food and water, stripping away visible spoilage and organic taint.

---

> **Purify Food and Drink**  
> _1st-level Spell (Ritual)_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 2 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
>  
> All mundane food and drink within a [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point of your choice within range is purified and rendered free of poison and disease.
