# Animate Dead

The corpse shudders, joints creaking as sinew tightens and it drags itself upright in jerking, unnatural motion.

---

> **Animate Dead**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a drop of blood, a piece of flesh, and a pinch of bone dust)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 2 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one corpse or pile of bones of a Medium or Small humanoid  
>  
> Cast only at night. 
> You animate targets as **constructs (undead)**. 
> Undead are not spirit or souls, nor a liminal third thing; they are animated corpses, things you have wound up and set moving.
> It has no more of the person it was than a puppet has of the hand.  
>  
> A pile of bones becomes a skeleton; 
> a corpse becomes a ghoul. 
> On each of your turns you can use a Minor Action to command any number of constructs you have animated this way within [= 12 stride =], issuing the same order to any or all of them.
> If you issue no command, a construct only defends itself. Once ordered, it follows the order until the task is complete.  
>  
> The construct obeys you for 24 hours, then stops. The spell can be cast again on the same remains.
>  
> - **Overcast:**: animate one more construct.
> - **Overcast (6th):** whenever you raise a creature, it automatically gains an [affix](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/heroic-awakening) of your choice.
