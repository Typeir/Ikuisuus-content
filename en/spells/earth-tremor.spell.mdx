# Earth Tremor

The ground shudders underfoot, buckling and throwing soil and stone outward.

---

> **Earth Tremor**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 2 stride;ADJ =] aura)  
> **Targets**: Creatures other than you within [= 2 stride;ADJ =] of you  
>
> You cause a tremor in the ground within range.
>
> Targets save Dexterity, taking [% 1d6 physical %]. Failing targets are prone. If the ground in that area is loose earth or stone, it becomes difficult terrain until cleared, with each [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-diameter portion requiring at least 1 minute to clear by hand.
>
> **Overcast:** the tremor gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
