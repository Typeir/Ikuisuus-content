# Dragonmaw

Your jaw unhinges wider than a jaw should, fills with fangs that were never yours, and remembers a hunger from a shape drawn long before you.

---

> **Dragonmaw**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a fang taken from a dragon)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: one creature or object within [= 2 stride =] of you  
>
> Your head and neck take on a draconic cast for the duration, and you gain a bite you know how to use.
>
> As a **Major Action**, you can bite targets. Make a melee attack that deals [% 2d10 piercing %] and grapples targets no more than one size larger than you (escape DC equal to 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #]). While held this way, you can bite them again on later turns, dealing [% 2d10 piercing %] automatically, with no attack roll.
>
> You can hold only one creature in your jaws at a time, and you cannot bite another while you do.
>
> **Overcast:** the bite gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
