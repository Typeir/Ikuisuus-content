# Dragonmaw

Your jaw unhinges wider than a jaw should, fills with fangs that were never yours, and remembers a hunger from a shape drawn long before you.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V, S, M (a fang taken from a dragon)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="one creature or object within [= 2 stride =] of you">

Your head and neck take on a draconic cast for the duration, and you gain a bite you know how to use.

As a **Major Action**, you can bite targets.
Make a melee attack that deals [% 8d10 piercing %] and grapples targets no more than one size larger than you (escape DC equal to 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #]).
While held this way, you can bite them again on later turns as a **Minor Action**, dealing [% 1d10 piercing %] automatically, with no attack roll, and inflicting as many stacks of the [# kw:condition:Bleeding #] condition to targets.
if targets are immune to [# kw:condition:Bleeding #], they instead take an additional [% 1d10 %] damage from both sources.

You can hold only one creature in your jaws at a time, and you cannot bite another while you do.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the bite gains [% 1d10 %] damage.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
