# Skywrite

Cloud-script, written across a dead sky. Whatever you have to say, Kuutar will be behind it, and will not comment.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 day"
  range="Sight"
  targets="—">

You cause up to ten words to form in a part of the sky you can see.
They appear to be made of cloud, remain in place for the duration, and dissipate when the spell ends.
They can be seen — and so read — from up to [= 2 league =] away by anything with eyes to do it.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** The spell gains ten additional words, and they can be read [= 2 league =] further.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
