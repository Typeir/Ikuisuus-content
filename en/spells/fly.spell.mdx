# Fly

The subject's weight slackens and it rises on invisible currents.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature you touch">

You touch a willing creature.
Targets gain a flying speed of [= 12 stride =] for the duration.
When the spell ends, targets fall if still aloft, unless they can stop the fall.  

**Overcast:** target one additional creature.  

</Spell>
