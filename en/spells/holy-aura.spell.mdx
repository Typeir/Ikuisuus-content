# Holy Aura

A crown of searing light flares outward, unmaking shadow and scorching matter caught within its sweep.

---

> **Holy Aura**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a tiny reliquary worth at least 1,000 gp that contains a sacred relic, such as a scrap of cloth from a saint’s robe or a piece of parchment from a religious text)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: Creatures of your choice within [= 6 stride;ADJ =] of you  
>  
> Divine light coalesces in a soft radiance in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius around you. Targets shed dim light in a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] radius and have advantage on all saving throws, and other creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against them until the spell ends. In addition, when a fiend or an undead hits one of them with a melee attack, the aura flashes with brilliant light. The attacker saves Constitution or is blinded until the spell ends.  
>  
> **Overcast (9th+):** the spell gains a duration of concentration, up to 10 minutes.
