# Wrought Watchdog

You mold nothingness into a stony, watchful guarding that will stand their ground fiercely, fighting tooth and nail for their given purpose.

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="[= 6 stride =]">

You conjure a phantom watchdog in an unoccupied space that you can see within range, where it remains for the duration, until you dismiss it as a Major Action, or until you move more than [= 20 stride =] away from it.  

The hound is invisible to all creatures except you and can't be harmed.
When a Small or larger creature comes within [= 6 stride =] of it without first speaking the password that you specify when you cast this spell, the hound starts barking loudly.
The hound sees invisible creatures and sees into the thin Folds laid over the visible.
It ignores illusions.  

At the start of each of your turns, the hound attempts to bite one creature within [= 1 stride =] of it that is hostile to you.
The hound's attack bonus is equal to your casting ability modifier + your tier bonus.
On a hit, it deals 4d8 piercing damage.  

The hound vanishes when it has dealt a total of 50 damage.  

</Spell>
