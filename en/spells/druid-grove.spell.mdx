# Druid Grove

Roots surge from the soil and weave into a ring of gnarled wood, and nature itself wards against intruders.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="10 Minutes"
  components="V, S, M (a handful of seed from the ground you are warding)"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="Touch"
  targets="Creatures within an area outdoors or underground, from a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] cube to an [= 18 stride;ADJ =] cube">

The spell fails if the area includes a building or any other structure.

<Choice pick="up to four">

<Feature targets="creatures in the fog">

#### Fog

Solid fog fills a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-square area within the grove, heavily obscuring it.
As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, targets save Constitution or are **[# kw:condition:poisoned #]** until they leave.
The fog lasts for the duration, or until a wind of at least [= 8 league =] per hour disperses it.

</Feature>

<Feature targets="creatures in the undergrowth">

#### Undergrowth

Grasping undergrowth covers a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-square area within the grove.
The area is difficult terrain.
As a [# kw:delayed #] effect, targets save Strength or are **[# kw:condition:restrained #]** until the spell ends.
A [# kw:condition:restrained #] creature can use a Major Action to make a Strength check, freeing itself on a success.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Guardians

Six awakened trees appear in unoccupied spaces you choose within the grove.
They are **wildlife (plant)** of Lethality 2, they act as guardians, they cannot leave the grove, and they go inanimate when the spell ends.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Spikes

Up to four instances of [Spike Growth](/en/library/spells/spike-growth), each in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square area.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Snares

Up to four instances of [Entangle](/en/library/spells/entangle), each in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square area.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Winds

Up to four instances of [Breeze Barrier](/en/library/spells/breeze-barrier), each in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square area.

</Feature>

</Choice>

When you cast this spell you can specify creatures that are unaffected by one or more of the protections.
You can also set a password that, spoken aloud, makes the speaker and any creatures they designate immune to the protections for 1 minute.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the grove gains one additional protection.

**(7th+):** the grove gains an additional 24 hours of duration.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
