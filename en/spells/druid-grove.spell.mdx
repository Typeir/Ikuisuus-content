# Druid Grove

Roots surge from the soil and weave into a ring of gnarled wood, the bark knotting as the ring hardens into a compact living barricade.

---

> **Druid Grove**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 10 Minutes  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a handful of seed from the ground you are warding)  
> **Duration**: 24 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: Creatures within an area outdoors or underground, from a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] cube to an [= 18 stride;ADJ =] cube  
>
> The spell fails if the area includes a building or any other structure.
>
> You can create up to four protections within the area. Choose from the following when you cast the spell:
>
> - **Fog.** Solid fog fills a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-square area within the grove, heavily obscuring it. As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures in the fog save Constitution or are **poisoned** until they leave. The fog lasts for the duration, or until a wind of at least [= 8 league =] per hour disperses it.
> - **Undergrowth.** Grasping undergrowth covers a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-square area within the grove. The area is difficult terrain. As an [_incipient effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures in the undergrowth save Strength or are **restrained** until the spell ends. A restrained creature can use a Major Action to make a Strength check, freeing itself on a success.
> - **Guardians.** Six awakened trees appear in unoccupied spaces you choose within the grove. They are **wildlife (plant)** of Challenge 2, they act as guardians, they cannot leave the grove, and they go inanimate when the spell ends.
> - **Spikes.** Up to four instances of [Spike Growth](/en/library/spells/spike-growth), each in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square area.
> - **Snares.** Up to four instances of [Entangle](/en/library/spells/entangle), each in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square area.
> - **Winds.** Up to four instances of [Breeze Barrier](/en/library/spells/breeze-barrier), each in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square area.
>
> When you cast this spell you can specify creatures that are unaffected by one or more of the protections. You can also set a password that, spoken aloud, makes the speaker and any creatures they designate immune to the protections for 1 minute.
>
> **Overcast:** the grove gains one additional protection.
>
> **Overcast (7th+):** the grove gains an additional 24 hours of duration.
