# Antimagic Shell

In their study of death and decay, the Revenants have developed a weaving of the arcane that allows them to disperse magical effects around them, a burial of all things arcane.

---

> **Antimagic Shell**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature  
>  
> You envelop targets in a volatile shell of counteractive energy that destabilizes hostile magic.  
> When the spell is cast, you may choose a number of **temporary magical effects** currently affecting targets equal to your [# kw:damage bonus #] (minimum 1).  
> Each chosen effect ends immediately.  
>  
> For each effect ended this way, targets gain **5 temporary hit points**.  
>  
> While protected by [_Antimagic Shell_](/en/library/spells/antimagic-shell), the creature gains **advantage on saving throws** against spells and magical effects.  
>  
> **Unstable Reflection (Once per turn).**  
> Once per turn, when targets are **targeted by a spell or magical effect** (including being included in an area effect), roll **[% 1d6 %]**:  
>  
> - **1–3**: The spell functions normally.  
> - **4–6**: The spell **fails to affect** targets.  
>  
> This spell does not suppress **Heirlooms**.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains 1 minute of duration.
