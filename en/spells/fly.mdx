# Fly

The subject's weight slackens and it rises on invisible currents.

---

> **Fly**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one creature you touch  
>  
> You touch a willing creature. Targets gain a flying speed of [= 12 stride =] for the duration. When the spell ends, targets fall if still aloft, unless they can stop the fall.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature.
