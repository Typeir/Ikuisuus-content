# Fly

The subject's weight slackens and it rises on invisible currents.

---

> **Fly**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
>  
> You touch a willing creature. The target gains a flying speed of [= 12 stride =] for the duration. When the spell ends, the target falls if it is still aloft, unless it can stop the fall.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd.
