# Cause Fear

A cold dread wells in the target, vision narrowing as panic claws at their chest.

---

> **Cause Fear**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> You awaken the sense of mortality. A construct or an undead is immune to this effect.  
>  
> Targets save Wisdom or are frightened of you for the duration. They can resist Wisdom.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature (targets must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other).
