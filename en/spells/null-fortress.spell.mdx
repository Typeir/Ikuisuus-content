# Null Fortress

This spell is not innate to any vocation, but rather, granted as a boon or a blessing by a **Heirloom** or a **higher power**.
This spell can be attained by the scions of ambition, if they ever survive the trial that comes with embracing true potential.

---

> **Null Fortress**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature  
>  
> You envelop targets in a volatile field of counteractive energy that disrupts nearby magic.  
> When cast, all **temporary magical effects** affecting targets end immediately, including beneficial spells such as [_Bless_](/en/library/spells/bless), [_Haste_](/en/library/spells/haste), or [_Invisibility_](/en/library/spells/invisibility).  
> For each effect consumed this way, targets gain temporary hit points equal to 10 + your [# kw:damage bonus #].  
>  
> While protected by the **Null fortress**, targets gain the following benefits:  
>  
> - Immunity to being **put to sleep** by magical means.  
> - Immunity to being **charmed**, **polymorphed**, **frightened**, or **possessed** by spells or magical effects.  
> - Advantage on saving throws against magical effects.  
> - **Resistance to damage** dealt by spells and other magical effects.  
>  
> Whenever targets are targeted by a spell or magical effect (including area effects that include them in their range), roll **[% 1d6 %]**:  
>  
> - **1–2**: The spell functions normally.  
> - **3–5**: The spell **fails to affect** targets.  
> - **6**: The spell is **reflected** back at its caster, as if targets were the point of origin.  
>  
> This spell does not supress **heirlooms**.  
>  
> **Overcast:** The shell gains 10 minutes of duration and 5 base temporary hit points.
