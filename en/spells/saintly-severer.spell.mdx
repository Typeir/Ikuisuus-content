# Saintly Severer

A spell that forms a colossal bone blade, its splintered remnants orbiting the wielder in a grinding storm that strikes and shatters without command.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a two-handed melee weapon)"
  duration="1 hour"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

You wrench a massive bone blade from Selkara and bind it to your weapon.  

**The Severer.** Your weapon becomes _The Severer_ for the duration, counting as **magical**, gaining the **heavy** property if it did not already have it, and extending your **melee reach by [= 2 stride =]**.  
Fragments shear off _The Severer_ and begin to **orbit you**, forming a grinding halo of bone.  

**Bone Charges.**  
- When you **hit a Small or larger creature** with _The Severer_, you gain **[% 1d4 %] Bone Charges**.  
- When you **reduce a Small or larger creature to 0 hit points** with _The Severer_, you gain **[% 4d4 %] Bone Charges** instead.  

You can have a maximum of **20 Bone Charges**.  

**Bone Storm Effects.** The orbiting bone fragments act automatically:  

- **On Your Attacks.**  
When you hit with _The Severer_, that target takes a bonus **[% 1d4 piercing %] for each Bone Charge you currently have**.  
- **When You Are Hit.**  
When you take damage from an attack, the orbiting bones interpose and absorb part of the blow.  
Gain **damage reduction** equal to **[% 1d4 %] for each Bone Charge you currently have**.
Lose as many Bone Charges as you rolled dice this way.  

**Charge Decay.** If you have not gained any Bone Charges in the last **1 minute**, you lose **1 Bone Charge at the end of each minute** until you gain charges again or have none remaining.  

When you have **no Bone Charges**, the orbiting fragments are gone until you gain more.  

If the spell ends or you drop _The Severer_, all remaining Bone Charges are lost and it collapses into inert fragments.  

**Overcast:** the duration increases by 1 hour.  

</Spell>
