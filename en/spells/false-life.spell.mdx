# False Life

A hollow, cold vitality floods your body, firming flesh and slowing the pulse.

> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a small amount of alcohol or distilled spirits)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: you
