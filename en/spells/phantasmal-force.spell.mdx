# Phantasmal Force

A three-dimensional illusion materializes around the target, producing convincing sights and sounds.

---

> **Phantasmal Force**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a bit of fleece)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within range  
>  
> You craft an illusion that takes root in targets' minds. Targets save Intelligence or perceive a phantasmal object, creature, or other visible phenomenon of your choice, no larger than a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] cube, perceivable only to them for the duration. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs.  
>  
> The phantasm includes sound, temperature, and other stimuli, also evident only to targets.  
>  
> Targets can use a Major Action to examine the phantasm with an Intelligence (Investigation) check against 10 plus [# kw:accuracy #]. If the check succeeds, targets realize the phantasm is an illusion, and the spell ends.  
>  
> While targets are affected by the spell, they treat the phantasm as if it were real. Targets rationalize any illogical outcomes from interacting with the phantasm. For example, targets attempting to walk across a phantasmal bridge that spans a chasm fall once they step onto the bridge. If they survive the fall, they still believe the bridge exists and come up with some other explanation for the fall—they were pushed, they slipped, or a strong wind might have knocked them off.  
>  
> Targets so convinced of the phantasm’s reality can even take damage from the illusion. A phantasm created to appear as a creature can attack targets. Similarly, a phantasm created to appear as fire, a pool of chemical, or lava can burn targets. Each round on your turn, the phantasm can deal [% 1d6 psychic %] to targets if they are in the phantasm’s area or within [= 1 stride =] of the phantasm, provided that the illusion is of a creature or hazard that could logically deal damage. Targets perceive the damage as a type appropriate to the illusion.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the phantasm gains [% 1d6 psychic %] damage.
