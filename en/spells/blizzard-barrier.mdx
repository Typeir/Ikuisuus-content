# Blizzard Barrier

A wall of solid ice rockets up from the ground, forming a tall barrier that frosts the air.

---

> **Blizzard Barrier**
> _6th-level Evocation_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action or 1 Reaction  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a shard of ice that has never melted)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 30 minutes
>  
>  
> A wall of ice springs into existence at a point you choose within range. You can make it up to **[= 12 stride =] long, [= 2 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick**. The wall lasts for the duration. If the wall cuts through a creature’s space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (your choice which side).  
>  
> The wall is an object that can be damaged and thus breached. It has AC 12 and 30 hit points per [= 2 stride;ADJ =] section. Reducing a section to 0 hit points destroys it and leaves behind a sheet of frigid air in the space the wall occupied. As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), a creature moving through the sheet of frigid air must make a Constitution saving throw. That creature takes [% 5d6 frost %] on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The frigid air lasts for 1 minute, though a strong wind can disperse it.  
>  
> Instead of a straight wall, you can shape it into a hemispherical dome or a sphere of up to **[= 6 stride =] radius**, or into a flat surface of ten contiguous [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-square panels. In any form it is [= 1/5 stride =] thick.  
>  
> If a creature tries to break through the wall, it must make a Strength saving throw. On a successful save, the creature can move through the wall as if it were difficult terrain, taking [% 5d6 frost %]. On a failed save, the creature can’t move through and takes [% 10d6 frost %].  
> 
> If you maintain concentration for the spell's whole duration **and you are still within casting range when it ends**, the wall is left behind as ordinary ice and can no longer be dispelled, melting at a regular pace (24 hours to melt completely in temperate weather). Otherwise the wall disappears when the spell ends.  
> 
> **Solid barrier.** Area effects break against it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).  
> 
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, increase the length of the wall by [= 2 stride =], or the radius by [= 2 stride =], for each slot level above 6th.
