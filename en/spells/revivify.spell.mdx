# Revivify

A cold, blue-white light stitches flesh and bone together and breath returns to the body.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (diamonds worth 1,000 gp, which the spell consumes)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature that died within the last 10 minutes, its corpse still functional — head, heart, and the greater part of its flesh intact">

This is a jolt to a body that can still hold life, not the making of one that cannot.
You touch targets, who return to life with **1 hit point**.

Revivify cannot restore a creature reduced to pieces, ash, or ruin, nor one that died of old age, and it replaces no missing body parts.
Any Grievous Wounds targets carried in death remain with them in life.

**Overcast:** the window in which targets must have died extends by 1 minute.

</Spell>
