# Zephyr Strike

A blade of compressed wind streaks from you, cutting the air with a sharp, hissing edge.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self">

You move like the wind. Until the spell ends, your movement doesn’t provoke opportunity attacks.  

Once before the spell ends, you can give yourself advantage on one weapon attack roll on your turn.
That attack deals an extra [% 1d8 force %] on a hit.
Whether you hit or miss, your walking speed increases by [= 6 stride =] until the end of that turn.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the strike gains one additional use before the spell ends.  

</Overcast>
