# Despairing Darkness

You pull down a patch of the dark that lies beneath the eternal stairs, from the tunnels under the Brume where the Everdark has been sitting undisturbed for a very long time. The orb it makes is deathly quiet. Nothing comes out of it, sound included, and everyone inside it learns what the tunnels are like.

<Spell
  level="8"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 30 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within a [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you choose within range">

Magical darkness spreads from a point you choose within range to fill a [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere until the spell ends.
The darkness spreads around corners.
A creature with darkvision can't see through this darkness.
Mundane light, as well as light created by spells of 8th level or lower, can't illuminate the area.  

**No sound enters the sphere and none leaves it.** Inside, there is nothing to hear at all — not your own breathing, not the creature beside you, not the thing you are certain is also in here.
As a [# kw:delayed #] effect, creatures in the sphere save Wisdom, halving [% 8d8 psychic %].
The damage is the pressure of the place, not anything it says to you.  

**Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] psychic damage.  

</Spell>
