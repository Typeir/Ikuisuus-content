# Suggestion

The words settle into the target's mind as though they had always been its own idea.

---

> **Suggestion**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, M (a snake's tongue and either a bit of honeycomb or a drop of sweet oil)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 8 hours  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see that can hear and understand you  
>  
> You suggest a course of activity — a sentence or two — to targets.
> The suggestion must be worded to sound reasonable; asking targets to obviously harm themselves ends the spell.  
>  
> Targets save Wisdom or pursue the course of activity you described, as best they can, until it is completed or the spell ends.
> A suggestion works within targets' [# kw:disposition #]: no wording, however reasonable, carries a creature across its will-not line.  
>  
> If you or your companions damage targets, the spell ends.  
>  
> **Overcast:**  
>  
> | **Slot Level** | **Targets** | **Duration** |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 3rd–5th | one additional creature per level above 2nd | Concentration, up to 8 hours |
> | 6th | up to twelve creatures | 24 hours |
> | 7th+ | up to twelve creatures | 10 days |
