# Conjure Aberration

You rend a momentary rift through the fabric of reality, and voice the ambition of a Devil lost to history. It crawls through the gap, in search of a feast.

---

> **Conjure Aberration**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (something you wanted badly and no longer do)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: —  
>  
> You call an **aberration**; a _Hiisi_ or _Silent one_ into an unoccupied space you can see within range.
> Their combined Challenge Rating may not exceed **twice this spell's level**.
> See [Summoning and Conjuration](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/summoning-and-conjuration) for the Challenge Budget, Swarms and Formations, and command.  
>  
> **The row's danger.** An aberration obeys the letter of a command and hunts the gap in it.
> Should your concentration break, it is loosed with the full remaining duration to spend as it likes.
>  
> **Overcast:** the Challenge Budget is always twice the level of the slot spent.
