# Lithomancy

You give an object a life it never asked for. It wakes, senseless, [# kw:condition:terrified #], and flailing.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Minute"
  components="V, S, M (a drop of your own blood worked into the object's surface)"
  duration="8 hours"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one object no larger than Medium">

Targets become **The Wretch**, a Construct for the duration: animate and aware, knowing what it is and that it did not choose to be, unable to speak, weep, or die on its own.
It obeys you, as obeying you is the only thing its brief, screaming existence permits.

**Command.** The Wretch acts immediately after you, in the space it occupies.
You direct any number of Wretches with one Minor Action.
Left alone, a Wretch repeats its last order, or, given none, Flails at whatever stands nearest.

_When the spell ends, the will goes out.
Whether anything lingers in the object afterward is not known, and those who cast this spell twice upon the same object report that the second waking is worse._
