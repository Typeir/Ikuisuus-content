# Bloodlash Rebuke

This spell is not innate to any vocation, but rather, granted as a boon or a blessing by a **Heirloom** or a **higher power**.
Those who heed the calls of the corpse-god **Küütar** and her master, the **Red Queen**, should find this trick in their repertoire, sooner or later.
_This spell replaces [Argent Strands](/en/library/spells/argent-strands) if it is already known._

---

> **Bloodlash Rebuke**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when a creature within [= 12 stride =] of you succeeds on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature in range  
>  
> You snap a blood-curse through the air, searing your foes.  
>
> **The Lash.**
> **Targets**: one creature you can see
> When targets succeed on an attack, check or save, you can chance the roll, and targets save Dexterity or take [% 1d10 chemical %] + [% 1d10 fire %].  
>
> **The Gift.**
> **Targets**: a creature other than the one affected by **The Lash**
> Targets gain advantage on their next attack, check or save within a minute.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d10 chemical %] + [% 1d10 fire %].
