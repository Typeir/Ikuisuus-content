# Storm Sphere

A churning globe of wind and crackling lightning forms, its gusts and forks of electricity tearing at nearby matter.

---

> **Storm Sphere**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range  
>
> A sphere of whirling air springs into existence and remains for the duration.
> Its space is difficult terrain.
> When the sphere appears and as an [_delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) thereafter, targets save Strength, halving [% 2d6 bludgeoning %].  
>
> Until the spell ends, you can use a Minor Action on each of your turns to cause a bolt of lightning to leap from the center of the sphere toward one creature you choose within [= 12 stride =] of the center.
> Make a ranged attack that deals [% 4d6 lightning %].
> You have advantage on the attack roll if that creature is in the sphere.  
>
> Creatures within [= 6 stride =] of the sphere have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to listen.  
>
> **Overcast:** the sphere gains [% 1d6 %] damage, and the bolt [% 1d6 %].
