# Aura of Life

A low, warm halo blooms outward, knitting torn flesh and steadying faltering breath.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="Self ([= 6 stride;ADJ =] aura)"
  targets="Creatures within [= 6 stride;ADJ =] of you">

Life-preserving energy radiates from you in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius. The aura moves with you, centered on you.

As a **Minor Action**, you can cause one creature in the aura to regain [% 2d6 %] hit points.

**Overcast (per +2):** the aura gains [% 1d6 %] healing.

**Overcast (5th+):** the aura wards those inside it; living creatures of your choice gain resistance to dark and their hit point maximum cannot be reduced.

</Spell>
