# Flame Stride

You break into a burning run, and the fire keeps your pace — a searing wake dragged behind each stride, bright in the dark and hot enough to scald whatever you pass too close to.

---

> **Flame Stride**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: Creatures and objects your trail passes within [= 1 stride =] of  
>  
>  
> Flames race across your body, shedding bright light in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 2 stride =] for the duration.  
>  
> Your speed increases by [= 4 stride =] for the duration. Moving doesn’t provoke opportunity attacks. When you move within [= 1 stride =] of targets that aren’t being worn or carried, they take [% 1d6 fire %] from your trail of heat. Targets can take this damage only once during a turn.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the trail gains [% 1d6 %] fire damage.
