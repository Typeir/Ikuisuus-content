# Remote Access

The mechanism does not require your hands, only your intent. Distance collapses between your will and whatever part of the device moves.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="10 minutes"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="one mechanism, lock, or tombsteel contrivance within range that is not being worn or carried">

You manipulate targets at a distance as though with your hands: opening or closing doors, drawers, and containers; throwing levers and pressing catches; retrieving a loose object from an open container.
You cannot pick locks, disarm traps, or activate heirlooms.
The spell ends if you move more than [= 24 stride =] from targets.  

<Overcast>

<Feature targets="one complex tinker-system or ancient Empyrean mechanism you touch">

#### Backdoor <span>4th+ (per +1)</span>

With a 1-minute casting time, you worm a lasting connection into the target, bypassing its safeguards.
For 1 hour (Concentration), you issue commands to it from any distance as though present.
The duration gains 1 hour.

</Feature>  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
