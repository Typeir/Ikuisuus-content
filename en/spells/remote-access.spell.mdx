# Remote Access

The mechanism does not require your hands, only your intent. Distance collapses between your will and whatever part of the device moves.

---

> **Remote Access**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one mechanism, lock, or tombsteel contrivance within range that is not being worn or carried  
>  
> You manipulate targets at a distance as though with your hands: opening or closing doors, drawers, and containers; throwing levers and pressing catches; retrieving a loose object from an open container. You cannot pick locks, disarm traps, or activate heirlooms. The spell ends if you move more than [= 24 stride =] from targets.  
>  
> **Overcast (4th+) (per +1):** **Backdoor**: with a 1-minute casting time, you instead worm a lasting connection into a complex tinker-system or ancient Empyrean mechanism you touch, bypassing its safeguards. For 1 hour (Concentration), you issue commands to it from any distance as though present. The duration gains 1 hour.
