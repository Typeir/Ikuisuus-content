# Fabricate

Raw materials collapse and knit together, reshaping into a finished object.

---

> **Fabricate**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 10 Minutes  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: raw materials you can see  
>  
> You convert targets into products of the same material. For example, you can fabricate a wooden bridge from a clump of trees, a rope from a patch of hemp, and clothes from flax or wool.  
>  
> You can fabricate a Large or smaller object from targets (contained within a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] cube or eight connected [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cubes), given a sufficient quantity of targets. If targets are metal, stone, or another mineral substance, however, the fabricated object can be no larger than Medium (contained within a single [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube). The fabricated object's quality is commensurate with the quality of targets.  
>  
> Creatures or magic items can't be created or transmuted by this spell. You also can't use it to create items that ordinarily require a high degree of craftsmanship, unless you have proficiency with the [Trade](/en/library/items/tools) used to craft such objects.
>
> **Overcast:** the volume of targets you can transform increases by one size category.
