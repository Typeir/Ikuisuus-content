# Haste

Muscles snap taut and motion accelerates into a hard blur, limbs striking with sudden, mechanical quickness.

---

> **Haste**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a shaving of liquorice root)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one willing creature you can see within range  
>
> Until the spell ends, targets' speed is doubled, they gain a **+2 bonus to AC**, and they have advantage on Dexterity saving throws.
>
> Targets also gain a refreshing pool of **1 legendary deed**, regaining it at the start of each of their turns (see [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds)). They may spend this deed only on a **Legendary Deed: Act**, taken at the end of another creature's turn — one weapon attack, or the Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action — and, like all deeds, they may use at most one per turn and it counts against the round's shared deed cap.
>
> When the spell ends, targets are [Stunned](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#stunned) until the end of their next turn.
>
> Targets then quickly pay the cost of the borrowed time; they instantly age as if a tenday had passed.
>
> **Overcast:** the pool gains **1 additional legendary deed**.
