# Gaseous Form

The target's flesh thins and billows into a translucent vapor that flows through cracks and around obstacles.

---

> **Gaseous Form**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature you touch  
>  
>  
> You transform targets, along with everything they're wearing and carrying, into a misty cloud for the duration.  
>  
> Their gear melds into the new form. They have a flying speed of [= 2 stride =] and can hover. They can enter and occupy the space of another creature. Targets have resistance to damage, and advantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws. They can pass through small holes, narrow openings, and even mere cracks, though they treat liquids as though they were solid surfaces. Targets can't fall and remain hovering in the air even when stunned or otherwise incapacitated.  
>  
> While in the form of a misty cloud, targets can't talk or manipulate objects, and any objects they were carrying or holding can't be dropped, used, or otherwise interacted with. Targets can't attack or cast spells.  
>  
> **Overcast (4th+):** target one additional willing creature.
