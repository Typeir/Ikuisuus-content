# Dimension Door

Space folds into a narrow corridor beneath your feet, and you step through to reappear where the gap closes.

---

> **Dimension Door**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 100 stride =]  
> **Targets**: you and up to one willing creature of your size or smaller within [= 1 stride =] of you  
>
> Targets teleport from your location to any other spot within range, arriving at exactly the spot you desire: a place you can see, one you can visualize, or one you can describe by distance and direction, such as "[= 40 stride =] straight downward" or "upward to the northwest at a 45-degree angle, [= 60 stride =]."
>
> You can bring along objects up to what you can carry. The creature can bring gear up to its own capacity.
>
> If targets would arrive in a place already occupied by an object or creature, they each take [% 4d6 force %], and the spell fails to teleport them.
>
> **Overcast:** you bring one additional willing creature, teleporting to a space of your choice within [= 1 stride =] of your arrival point.
