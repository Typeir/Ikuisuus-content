# Crown of Madness

A jagged circlet of iron closes over the brow — a dead king's regalia, from a kingdom that appears on no map — and something wrong gets into the eyes beneath it.

---

> **Crown of Madness**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one humanoid you can see  
>
> Targets save Wisdom or are **charmed** by you for the duration. While charmed this way, the crown sits on its head and a madness glows in its eyes.
>
> Targets cannot tell ally from enemy. Once on each of their turns they must make one melee attack against a creature of your choosing — at whatever point in their turn you direct, not only before they move. They can act normally otherwise, and act entirely normally if you choose no creature or if the one you choose is out of their reach.
>
> Holding the crown in place costs you a **Minor Action** on each of your turns; if you do not spend it, the spell ends. They can resist Wisdom.
>
> **Overcast:** crown one additional humanoid. Each saves separately and breaks free separately, and one Minor Action holds every crown you are maintaining.
