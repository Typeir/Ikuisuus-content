# Brine Barrier

A towering curtain of churning water erupts along a line, roaring outward and battering anything in its path.

---

> **Brine Barrier**  
> _3rd-level Evocation_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action or 1 Reaction  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
>
> You conjure up a wall of water on the ground, or over the water's surface at a point you can see within range. You can make the wall up to [= 6 stride =] long, [= 2 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick, or you can make a ringed wall up to [= 4 stride =] in diameter, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick. The wall vanishes when the spell ends.
>
> The wall’s space is difficult terrain.
>
> Any ranged weapon attack that enters the wall’s space has disadvantage on the attack roll, and fire damage is halved if the fire effect passes through the wall to reach its target. Spells that deal frost damage that pass through the wall cause the area of the wall they pass through to freeze solid (at least a [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-square section is frozen). Each [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-square frozen section has AC 5 and 15 hit points. Reducing a frozen section to 0 hit points destroys it. When a section is destroyed, the water doesn’t fill it.
>
> Any part of the spell cast entirely over water that's at least [= 1 stride =] deep, instead is [= 1 stride =] thick, grants half cover and reduces fire damage to a quarter. Additionally, creatures must swim to cross that space, and if that section of wall is frozen, it has twice as much HP.
>
> **Solid barrier.** Area effects break against it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).
