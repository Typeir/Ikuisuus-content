# Zone of Truth

A low ring of cold light blooms; speech within it emerges blunt and unmistakable.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="10 minutes"
  range="[= 12 stride =]">

You create a magical zone that guards against deception in a [= 3 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point of your choice within range.

As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, creatures save Charisma or are unable speak a deliberate lie while in the radius.
You know whether each creature succeeds or fails on its saving throw.

An affected creature is aware of the spell and can thus avoid answering questions to which it would normally respond with a lie.
Such a creature can be evasive in its answers as long as it remains within the boundaries of the truth.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the radius increases by [= 2 stride =] for each slot level above 2nd.

</Spell>
