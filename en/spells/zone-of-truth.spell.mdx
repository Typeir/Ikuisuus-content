# Zone of Truth

A low ring of cold light blooms; speech within it emerges blunt and unmistakable.

---

> **Zone of Truth**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
>
> You create a magical zone that guards against deception in a [= 3 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point of your choice within range.
>
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures make a Charisma saving throw or be unable speak a deliberate lie while in the radius. You know whether each creature succeeds or fails on its saving throw.
>
> An affected creature is aware of the spell and can thus avoid answering questions to which it would normally respond with a lie. Such a creature can be evasive in its answers as long as it remains within the boundaries of the truth.
>
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the radius increases by [= 2 stride =] for each slot level above 2nd.
