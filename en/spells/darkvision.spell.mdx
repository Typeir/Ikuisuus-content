# Darkvision

A pale, filmy light wells in the recipient's eyes, stripping away shadow and revealing forms in the dark.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="16 hours"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature">

Targets gain darkvision out to a range of [= 12 stride =] for the duration.
Targets that already have darkvision instead have its range increased by [= 12 stride =].  

**Overcast:** touch one additional willing creature.  

</Spell>
