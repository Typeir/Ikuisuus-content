# Lightning Arrow

Electrical energy condenses along the arrow, which streaks forward crackling and flinging jagged arcs at the point of impact.

---

> **Lightning Arrow**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>  
> The next time you make a ranged weapon attack during the spell’s duration, the weapon’s ammunition, or the weapon itself if it’s a thrown weapon, transforms into a bolt of lightning. Make a ranged attack roll as normal that deals [% 4d8 lightning %] to targets instead of the weapon’s normal damage on a hit. Whether you hit or miss, each creature within [= 2 stride =] of targets saves Dexterity, halving [% 2d8 lightning %]. The piece of ammunition or weapon then returns to its normal form.
>  
> If you draw a weapon or piece of ammunition before the spell ends, it transforms into a bolt of lightning as described above.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] on each damage roll.
