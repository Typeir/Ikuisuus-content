# Kultharja's Mantle

A long forgotten oath-spell, now known only to those who are blessed by Kultharja's light.

> “May the sun shine upon these lands”  

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="10 minutes."
  range="Self (Aura)"
  targets="you">

While this spell is active, the radius of an aura of your choice produced by you increases by **[= 2 stride =]**.  

The aura **sheds bright light** throughout its area and **dim light** for an additional [= 6 stride =] beyond it.
This is considered mundane **sunlight**  

**Magical darkness** is **suppressed** within the aura’s area for the duration.
If an area of magical darkness overlaps the aura, the suppressed portion returns when the spell ends.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the aura gains [= 1 stride =] of radius.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
