# Command

A sharp syllable lashes outward as a visible ripple, and the subject's limbs lock into a single involuntary motion.

---

> **Command**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: 1 round  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> You speak a one-word command to targets. The word is not instruction but imposition; it does not ask, and it is not interested in being understood so much as obeyed. Targets save Wisdom or obey on their next turn. The spell fails if targets do not understand your language, or if the command is directly harmful to them.
>
> Some typical commands follow. You may issue others; if targets cannot follow the command you give, the spell ends.
>
> Approach. Targets move toward you by the shortest and most direct route, ending their turn if they move within [= 1 stride =] of you.
>
> Drop. Targets drop whatever they are holding and then end their turn.
>
> Flee. Targets spend their turn moving away from you by the fastest available means.
>
> Grovel. Targets fall prone and then end their turn.
>
> Halt. Targets don’t move and take no actions. A flying creature stays aloft, provided it is able to do so. If it must move to stay aloft, it flies the minimum distance needed to remain in the air.
>
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature (targets must be within [= 12 stride =] of each other).
