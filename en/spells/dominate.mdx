# Dominate

The target's limbs move on strings it cannot see, and its own will watches from the back row.

---

> **Dominate**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see whose type the slot you cast this spell with can reach (see the table below)  
>  
> You seize the body of targets. Targets save Wisdom or are [charmed](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#charmed) by you for the spell's duration. They save with advantage if you or your companions are fighting them.  
>  
> While targets are charmed, you have a telepathic link with them while you are both within the same Fold of the world. You can issue commands through the link (no action required), which they do their best to obey. You can use a Major Action to take total and precise control until the end of your next turn — using their actions, movement, and reactions as your own. They can resist Wisdom. They can resist when they take damage.  
>  
> The [charmed](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#charmed) condition constrains their behavior utterly; their true [disposition](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/disposition-reputation-and-attitude) is unmoved, and they remember everything.  
>  
> | Slot Level | Creature types you can target                | Duration            |
> | ---------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
> | 4th        | Wildlife                                     | 1 minute            |
> | 5th        | + Humanoid                                   | 1 minute            |
> | 6th        | + Monstrosity                         | up to 10 minutes    |
> | 7th        | + Construct, Väki                       | up to 10 minutes    |
> | 8th        | + Aberration                                 | up to 1 hour        |
> | 9th        | + Celestial                                  | up to 8 hours       |
>  
> Each row includes the rows above it. All durations require concentration.
