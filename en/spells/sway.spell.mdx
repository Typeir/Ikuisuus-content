# Sway

Nobody's mind is changed. Their footing is — one step along a slope they were already standing on, and the rest follows on its own.

---

  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a lump of alum soaked in vinegar, or a drop of honey, depending which way you mean it)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"

Targets save Charisma.
Any may choose to fail.
Failing targets move their [# kw:disposition #] toward the named subject **one step** in your chosen direction for the duration.

**Toward neutral** additionally suppresses any effect currently making failing targets [# kw:condition:charmed #] or [# kw:condition:frightened #].

Shifted targets snap back the instant they are attacked or harmed, or see one of their own harmed.
Disposition is cheap to move and expensive to keep.

**Attraction.** Targets spend their movement each turn approaching the anchor, and cannot willingly move away once they arrive.
