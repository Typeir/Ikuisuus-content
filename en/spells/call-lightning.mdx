# Call Lightning

A jagged bolt condenses from the storm and lances the ground in a blinding, ripping strike.

---

> **Call Lightning**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within [= 1 stride =] of a point you can see under the cloud  
>  
> A storm cloud appears in the shape of a cylinder that is [= 2 stride =] tall with a [= 12 stride;ADJ =] radius, centered on a point you can see [= 20 stride =] directly above you.
> The spell fails if you can’t see a point in the air where the storm cloud could appear (for example, if you are in a room that can’t accommodate the cloud). 
>  
> When you cast the spell, a bolt of lightning flashes down from the cloud to that point.
> Creatures within [= 1 stride =] of that point save Dexterity, halving [% 3d10 lightning %].
>  
> On each of your turns until the spell ends, you can use your Major Action to call down lightning in this way again, targeting the same point or a different one.
>  
> If you are outdoors in stormy conditions when you cast this spell, the spell gives you control over the existing storm instead of creating a new one.
> Under such conditions, the spell’s damage increases by [% 1d10 %].
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
