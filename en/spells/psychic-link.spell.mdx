# Psychic Link

A fragment of will is wedged into the gap between intent and action — too small to locate, precise enough to matter.

---

> **Psychic Link**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: 1 round  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within range  
>
> Fix your gaze on targets, then choose one:
> 
> - Whisper a message. Targets, and only targets, hear the message and can reply in a whisper that only you can hear.
>   
> - Drive a disorienting spike of psychic energy into the minds of targets. Targets save Intelligence or take [% 1d6 psychic %] and gain a penalty [% 1d4 %] to their next save before the end of your next turn.  
>  
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage increases by [% 1d6 %] at each step.
