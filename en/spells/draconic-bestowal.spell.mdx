# Draconic Bestowal

The essence of true terror gifts the willing a chance to inflict fear and hurt in equal parts.

---

> **Draconic Bestowal**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a hot pepper)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature  
>
> You touch targets and imbue them with the power to spew magical energy from their mouth, provided they have one.
>
> Choose an **Elemental** type — frost, fire, or lightning.
>
> Until the spell ends, targets can use a Major Action to exhale in a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone. Creatures in the cone save Dexterity, halving [% 3d6 %] of the chosen type.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
