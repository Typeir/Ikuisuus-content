# Everburning Armament

A spell that forces a weapon beyond its material limits, flooding it with relentless, destructive heat until nothing remains but slag.

> “That which burns in the flames of the Everdark is imbued with its power, if only briefly.”  

---

> **Everburning Armament**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a melee weapon you are wielding)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 5 rounds  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: the melee weapon you are wielding  
>  
> You wreathe targets in catastrophic, ever-hungry flame, pushing them far beyond what physical matter was meant to endure.  
>  
> For the duration, targets gain the following benefits:  
>  
> - Targets gain a **+3** [# kw:accuracy #] and [# kw:damage bonus #], in addition to any other bonuses they may have.  
> - Targets gain **[= 5 stride =]** of additional reach.  
> - Attacks made with targets deal an additional **[% 4d6 fire %]** to creatures they hit.  
> - Creatures hit by targets are **[_burning_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning)** and have their **speed reduced by half** [# kw:briefly #].  
> - While wielding targets, you can make **one additional weapon attack as a Minor Action** on each of your turns.  
>  
> When the spell ends, targets are **destroyed**, rendered unusable by the strain of the enchantment.  
>  
> If targets are **unbreakable or indestructible**, they survive the spell, but the additional fire damage that targets deal is reduced to **[% 2d4 %]** instead of [% 4d6 %].
