# Haywire

Jagged arcs of interference snap through the mechanism, and its motions stutter, seize, and fall still.

---

> **Haywire**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one construct or magical device you can see in range  
>  
> Targets save Constitution or are incapacitated with their speed reduced to 0 for the duration. They can resist Constitution.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional construct or magical device within [= 6 stride =] of each other.  
>  
> **Overcast (5th+): Shutdown.** targets are instead **paralyzed** with no repeat saving throw — they are out for the full duration. A construct immune to paralysis is unaffected by this mode.
