# Burning Bolts

Touched arrows flare into seething flame that clings to the shaft and burns whatever they pierce.

---

> **Burning Bolts**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 4 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one quiver containing arrows or bolts  
>  
> You touch a quiver containing arrows or bolts. When targets are hit by a ranged weapon attack using a piece of ammunition drawn from the quiver, all damage is dealt as fire damage and targets gain the [_Burning_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning) condition. The spell’s magic ends on the piece of ammunition when it hits or misses, and the spell ends when **four pieces** of ammunition have been drawn from the quiver.  
>  
> **Overcast:** affect two additional pieces of ammunition.
