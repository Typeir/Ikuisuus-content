# Sigil of Ruin

The ruinous sigil watches the heart of whoever comes to the door, and it has never once been wrong about what it finds waiting there.

---

> **Sigil of Ruin**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minute  
> **Components**: V, S, M (powdered lodestone and a drop of your own blood, worked into the mark)  
> **Duration**: Until dispelled or triggered  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: creatures within [= 6 stride =] of the sigil  
>
> You inscribe a ruinous sigil on a surface no larger than [= 2 stride =] across, or within an object that can be closed to hide it — a book, a chest, a doorframe.
> If that object is moved more than [= 2 stride =] from where you inscribed it, the sigil breaks and the spell ends without triggering.
> The mark is nearly invisible, and finding it takes a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #].
>
> **The sigil reads intent.** Rather than watching for a touch or a footfall, the sigil **reads the true disposition** of targets, as defined in [Disposition, Reputation, and Attitude](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/disposition-reputation-and-attitude).
> It triggers on the first among targets whose disposition toward you — or toward whatever the sigil guards — sits in the **Hostile** range.
> Because it reads intent and not allegiance, a disguise, an invisibility, a false uniform, or a kind word cannot fool it; only a creature that genuinely means no harm passes it by.
> When you inscribe the sigil you may name a password, or a specific creature, that it always lets through.
>
> When you inscribe the sigil, choose one of the following effects.
>
> **Ruin.**  
> **Targets**: creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on the sigil, spreading around corners  
> When triggered, the sigil bursts.
> Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 5d8 %] chemical, frost, fire, lightning, or bludgeoning damage, your choice at inscription.
>
> **Stored Casting.** As part of inscribing the sigil, you cast a prepared spell of a level no higher than this one into it; that spell takes no effect until the sigil triggers, at which point it goes off, targeting or centred on the triggering creature.
> A stored spell that requires concentration lasts its full duration without your attention.
>
> Once triggered, the sigil is spent and the spell ends.
>
> **Overcast:** a **Ruin** sigil gains [% 1d8 %] damage, and a **Stored Casting** sigil can hold a spell of up to the slot's level.
>
> **Overcast (7th+):** you may instead inscribe a **greater sigil**.  
> **Targets**: creatures within a [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on the sigil  
> On triggering, it flares to fill the sphere with dim light for 10 minutes.
> As a [_persistent, delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) before the light fades, choose one of the following effects at inscription:
>
> - **Death.** Targets save Constitution, halving [% 10d10 dark %].
> - **Pain.** Targets save Constitution or are **incapacitated** with agony for a minute. They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution.
> - **Stunning.** Targets save Wisdom or are **stunned** for a minute. They can [# kw:resist #] Wisdom.
> - **Fear.** Targets save Wisdom or are **frightened** for a minute, dropping whatever they hold and moving at least [= 6 stride =] from the sigil on each of their turns if able.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Wisdom.
> - **Sleep.** Targets save Wisdom or fall **unconscious** for 10 minutes, waking early if they take damage or a creature uses a Major Action to rouse them.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Wisdom.
> - **Discord.** Targets save Constitution or are **incapacitated** with uncontrollable laughter for a minute.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution.
>
> **Overcast (7th+) (per +1):** the **Death** effect gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
