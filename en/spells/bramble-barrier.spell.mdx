# Bramble Barrier

A jagged wall of thorns erupts, hooked spines ripping flesh and shredding anything that presses against it.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action, or 1 Reaction, taken as the [barrier](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers) guide dictates"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 30 minutes"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within the wall's area at a point you choose within range">

A wall of tough, pliable, tangled brush bristling with needle-sharp thorns appears at a point you choose within range.  

You choose to make the wall up to [= 12 stride =] long, [= 2 stride =] high, and [= 1 stride =] thick or a circle that has a [= 4 stride;ADJ =] diameter and is up to [= 4 stride =] high and [= 1 stride =] thick.
The wall blocks line of sight.  

When the wall appears, creatures in its area save Dexterity, halving [% 7d8 piercing %].  

A creature can move through the wall, albeit slowly and painfully: doing so **quadruples the cost of movement**. 
Furthermore, as a [# kw:persistent #], [# kw:delayed #] effect, creatures in the wall save Dexterity, halving [% 7d8 slashing %].  

The wall can be damaged and thus removed by normal means, but it is immune to poison and psychic damage.  

If you maintain concentration for the spell's whole duration **and you are still within casting range when it ends**, the thicket is left behind as ordinary bramble and can no longer be dispelled. 
Otherwise it disappears when the spell ends.

**Solid barrier.** Area effects break against it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** both pools gain [% 1d8 %] damage.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
