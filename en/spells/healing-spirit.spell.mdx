# Healing Spirit

A pale wisp settles on the injured, knitting torn flesh and stilling their pain.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You call forth a nature spirit to soothe the wounded.
The intangible spirit appears in a space that is a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube you can see within range.
The spirit looks like a transparent beast no book has ever described.

Until the spell ends, as a [# kw:persistent #] effect, you can cause the spirit to restore [% 1d6 %] hit points to the creature affected.
The spirit can’t heal constructs.
The spirit can heal a number of times equal to 1 + [# kw:accuracy #] (minimum of twice).
After healing that number of times, the spirit disappears.

As a Minor Action on your turn, you can move the spirit up to [= 6 stride =] to a space you can see.

<Overcast>

**(3rd+):** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] healing.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
