# Cloudkill

A roiling, fetid cloud pours out and crawls along the ground, its choking vapors blistering flesh and clogging lungs.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you choose within range">

You create a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere of yellow-green rot-fog centered on a point you choose within range — the same colour as the air over Nigredo, and for the same reason.
The fog spreads around corners.
It lasts for the duration or until strong wind disperses the fog, ending the spell early.

Its area is heavily obscured.
As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, creatures in the fog save Constitution, halving [% 5d8 poison %].
Creatures are affected even if they hold their breath or don’t need to breathe.

The fog moves [= 2 stride =] away from you at the start of each of your turns, rolling along the surface of the ground.
The vapors, being heavier than air, sink to the lowest level of the land, even pouring down openings.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the fog gains 10 minutes of duration.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
