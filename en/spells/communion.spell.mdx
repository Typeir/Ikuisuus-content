# Communion

You open yourself to one domain of the living world and it passes you its impressions — not in words, for it has none, but in urges, images, and the chemistry of intent.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="30 minutes"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

Choose a domain when you cast this spell. For the duration, you can commune with it:  

**Wildlife.** You comprehend the wildlife around you and can press impressions and urges upon them in return.
Beasts do not speak and are not made clever by this — they pass you what they perceive or perceived within the past day: nearby locations, dangers, and creatures, as far as their dim awareness reaches.
You might persuade a wildlife creature to a small favor, at the Game Director's discretion.  

**Flora.** You read the chemical impulses of the growing network around you — root, spore, and sap.
A connected stand of plants tells you what has passed through it, and a clonal grove that runs for kilometers will tell you of everything that has walked its ground.  

**Overcast:** double the duration.  

</Spell>
