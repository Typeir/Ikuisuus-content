# Omen

Conjure a baleful sign, and drag a creature through the inescapable path towards the grave.

---

> **Omen**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within range  
>
> You conjure a small omen: the image and cry of a raven flying by, a small harmless tremor, a sudden breeze that slams or opens windows, the ringing of a bell, cause the flames in the area to flicker...
>
> Then choose one of the following effects
>
> - Gain advantage on Intimidation checks against wounded creatures for a minute.
> - Targets save Wisdom or take [% 1d4 dark %]. Failing targets take a further [% 1d4 dark %] if wounded, again if bloodied, and a final time if doomed.
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage increases by [% 1d4 %] at each step.
