# Detect Thoughts

Stray thoughts unspool into thin, intrusive voices that echo inside your skull.

---

> **Detect Thoughts**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a copper piece)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: one creature within [= 6 stride =] of you that you can see or have detected with this spell  
>  
> For the duration, you can read the thoughts of certain creatures. When you cast the spell, and as a **Minor Action** on each of your turns until it ends, you can focus on a creature. Targets with an Intelligence of 3 or lower or that don’t speak any language are unaffected.  
>  
> You initially learn the surface thoughts of targets — what is most on their mind in that moment. Shifting your attention to another creature costs a **Minor Action**; **probing deeper** into the same mind costs a **Major Action**. Targets save Wisdom. Failing targets reveal their reasoning (if any), their emotional state, and whatever looms large in their mind — something they worry over, love, or hate — to you. Saving targets end the spell. Either way, the target knows that you are probing into its mind, and unless you shift your attention to another creature’s thoughts, the creature can use a Major Action on its turn to make an Intelligence check contested by your Intelligence check; if it succeeds, the spell ends.  
>  
> You can also use this spell to detect the presence of thinking creatures you can’t see. When you cast the spell, or as a **Minor Action** during the duration, you can search for thoughts within [= 6 stride =] of you. The spell can penetrate barriers, but [= 2/5 stride =] of rock, 2 inches of any metal other than lead, or a thin sheet of lead blocks you. You can’t detect a creature with an Intelligence of 3 or lower or one that doesn’t speak any language.  
>  
> Once you detect the presence of a creature in this way, you can read its thoughts for the rest of the duration as described above, even if you can’t see it, but it must still be within range.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell's duration gains 1 minute.
