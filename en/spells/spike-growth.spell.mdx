# Spike Growth

Jagged spines erupt from the ground, ripping flesh and tearing at anything that crosses them.

---

  cost="1 Major Action"

  targets="Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius area centered on a point within range">

Targets take [% 2d4 piercing %] for every [= 1 stride =] they move into or within the area.

Any creature that can't see the area at the time the spell is cast must make a Wisdom (Perception) check to recognize the terrain as hazardous before entering it.
