# Tidal Wave

A towering wall of water surges forward, smashing and sweeping away everything in its path.

---

> **Tidal Wave**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
>  
> You conjure up a wave of water that crashes down on an area within range. The area can be up to [= 6 stride =] long, up to [= 2 stride =] wide, and up to [= 2 stride =] tall. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes [% 4d8 bludgeoning %] and is knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked prone. The water then spreads out across the ground in all directions, extinguishing unprotected flames in that area and within [= 6 stride =] of it, and then it vanishes.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by [% 1d8 %] for each slot level above 3rd.
