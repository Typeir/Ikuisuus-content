# Tidal Wave

A towering wall of water surges forward, smashing and sweeping away everything in its path.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 24 stride =]">

You conjure up a wave of water that crashes down on an area within range.
The area can be up to [= 6 stride =] long, up to [= 2 stride =] wide, and up to [= 2 stride =] tall.
Each creature in that area saves Dexterity.
On a failed save, a creature takes [% 4d8 bludgeoning %] and is knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].
On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked [# kw:condition:prone #].
The water then spreads out across the ground in all directions, extinguishing unprotected flames in that area and within [= 6 stride =] of it, and then it vanishes.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the damage gains [% 1d8 %].  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
