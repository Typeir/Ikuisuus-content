# Grasping Vine

Gnarled vines erupt from the ground, snaking outward to seize and wrench at anything they touch.

---

> **Grasping Vine**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: —  
>  
>  
> You conjure a vine in an unoccupied space you can see within range. As part of casting the spell, you can direct it to lash out at one creature you can see within [= 6 stride =] of it. That creature saves Dexterity or is pulled [= 4 stride =] directly toward the vine.  
>  
> As a Minor Action on your turn, you can direct the vine to lash out at the same creature or another one.  
>  
> **Overcast (5th+):** the spell gains one additional vine. One Minor Action directs all of them, though each must lash a different creature.
