# Blade Barrier

A ragged wall of whirling blades tears through the air, shredding stone and flesh in its path.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action, or 1 Reaction, taken as the [barrier](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers) guide dictates"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 18 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within the wall">

You create a wall of whirling, razor-sharp blades at a point you choose. 
The wall can be up to [= 20 stride =] long, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1 stride =] thick, or a ringed wall up to [= 12 stride =] in diameter, [= 4 stride =] high, and [= 1 stride =] thick. 
The wall provides three-quarters cover to creatures behind it, and its space is difficult terrain.

As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, targets save Dexterity, halving [% 6d10 slashing %].

**Energy barrier.** A wall of moving blades is not a solid thing — area effects pass straight through it. 
See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).

**The Blade in Your Hand.**  
**Targets**: you  
When you cast this spell, you may keep one blade out of the wall. 
It costs nothing to take and lasts for the duration or until you let it go. 
This weapon takes the shape of any weapon of your choice, but its damage is [% 3d10 slashing %], and its range increases by [= 1 stride =].

</Overcast>

</Spell>
