# Slow

Time thickens around the target, making motion stutter and limbs move sluggishly.

---

> **Slow**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
>  
>  
> You alter time around up to six creatures of your choice in a [= 8 stride;ADJ =] cube within range. Each target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be affected by this spell for the duration.  
>  
> An affected target is **slowed**.  
>  
> A creature affected by this spell makes another Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a successful save, the effect ends for it.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can affect two additional creatures for each slot level above 3rd.
