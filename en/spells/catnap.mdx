# Catnap

A soft, numbing haze envelopes the target, closing their eyes and drawing them into a shallow, dreamless sleep.

---

> **Catnap**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M  
> **Duration**: 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: up to three willing creatures you can see  
>
> You make a calming gesture, and targets fall unconscious for the spell's duration. The spell ends early for targets that take damage or that someone spends a Major Action to shake or slap awake. Targets that remain unconscious for the full duration gain the benefit of a Repose, and can't be affected by this spell again until they finish a Recovery.
>
> **Overcast:** put three additional willing creatures to sleep.
