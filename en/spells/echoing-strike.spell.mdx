# Echoing Strike

A thunderous strike. Unobservant targets will be struck by a booming echo of the original trajectory.

<Spell
  level="cantrip"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S, M (A weapon of any kind)"
  duration="1 round"
  range="Self"
  targets="—">

You brandish the weapon used in the spell's casting.

<Choice>

<Feature targets="one creature within reach of the brandished weapon">

#### The Blast

Make a melee attack against targets that deals the attack's normal effects and hangs an echo of the blow over them, waiting to land again through **The Echo**.

<Feature trigger="at the end of targets' turns" targets="those struck with **The Blast**">

##### The Echo

If they are not steady, they take [% 1d8 bludgeoning %] and the spell ends. If they held steady, the echo goes unspent and the spell ends.

</Feature>

</Feature>

<Feature targets="everything within [= 1 stride =] of you">

#### The Wave

You slam the weapon into the ground, sending a pulsing shockwave. Targets save Dexterity or suffer [% 1d8 bludgeoning %] and become [# kw:condition:Unsteady #] 

</Feature>

<Feature targets="you">

#### The Reverberation

You pound the weapon's dulled edge against your chest, amplifying your next five words; they can be heard from anywhere within [= 200 stride =],

</Feature>

</Choice>

**Progression (5th/11th/17th):** **The Blast**, **The Echo** and **The Wave** gain [% 1d8 bludgeoning %].

</Spell>
