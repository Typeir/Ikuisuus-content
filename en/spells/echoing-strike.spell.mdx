# Echoing Strike

A thunderous strike. Unobservant targets will be struck by a booming echo of the original trajectory.

> **Echoing Strike**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M  
> **Duration**: 1 round  
> **Range**: Self ([= 1 stride;ADJ =] aura)  
> **Targets**: one creature within [= 1 stride =] of you  
>  
> You brandish the weapon used in the spell's casting and make a melee attack against targets that deals the attack's normal effects and hangs an echo of the blow over them, waiting to land again.
>
> **At the end of targets' turns, if they are not steady, they take [% 1d8 bludgeoning %] and the spell ends.** If they held steady, the echo goes unspent and the spell ends anyway.
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the melee attack gains [% 1d8 bludgeoning %] additional damage, and the echo's damage increases by [% 1d8 %] at each step.
