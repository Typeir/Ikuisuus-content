# Echoing Strike

A thunderous strike. Unobservant targets will be struck by a booming echo of the original trajectory.

> **Echoing Strike**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M (A weapon of any kind)
> **Duration**: 1 round  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: — 
>  
> You brandish the weapon used in the spell's casting.
>
> **The Blast**  
> **Targets**: one creature within reach of the brandished weapon.  
> Make a melee attack against targets that deals the attack's normal effects and hangs an echo of the blow over them, waiting to land again.
>
> **The Echo**  
> **Targets**: those struck with **The Blast**.  
> **At the end of targets' turns, if they are not steady, they take [% 1d8 bludgeoning %] and the spell ends.** If they held steady, the echo goes unspent and the spell ends.
>
> **The Wave**  
> **Targets**: everything within [= 1 stride =] of you.  
> You slam the weapon into the ground, sending a pulsing shockwave. Targets save Dexterity or suffer [% 1d8 bludgeoning %] and become [Unsteady](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#unsteady) 
>
> **The Reverberation**  
> **Targets**: you.  
> You pound the weapon's dulled edge against your chest, amplifying your next five words; they can be heard from anywhere within [= 200 stride =],
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** **The Blast**, **The Echo** and **The Wave** gain [% 1d8 bludgeoning %].
