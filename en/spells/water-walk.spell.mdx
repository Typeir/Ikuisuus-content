# Water Walk

A translucent crust forms atop water, bearing weight like stone.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a scrap of cork, or a cupful of dried brine)"
  duration="8 hours"
  range="[= 6 stride =]">

Up to ten willing creatures you can see within range gain the ability to move across any liquid surface — water, chemical, mud, snow, quicksand, or lava — as though it were harmless solid ground.
Crossing molten lava still means crossing something that is on fire; the surface holds, the heat does not care.  

If you target a creature already submerged, the spell carries it to the surface at a rate of [= 12 stride =] per round.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell targets five additional willing creatures.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
