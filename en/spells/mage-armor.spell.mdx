# Mage Armor

An invisible shell of force snaps over the target, its surface tight and quietly crackling.

---

> **Mage Armor**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a piece of cured leather)  
> **Duration**: 16 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature you touch  
>  
> Targets who aren’t wearing armor are surrounded by a protective magical force until the spell ends. Their base AC becomes 13 plus their Dexterity modifier. The spell ends if targets don armor or if you dismiss the spell as a Major Action.  
>  
> **Overcast (per +2):** the spell gains 1 base AC.
