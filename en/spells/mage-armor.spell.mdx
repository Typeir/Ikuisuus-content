# Mage Armor

An invisible shell of force snaps over the target, its surface tight and quietly crackling.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a piece of cured leather)"
  duration="16 hours"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature you touch">

Targets who aren’t wearing armor are surrounded by a protective magical force until the spell ends.
Their base AC becomes 13 plus their Dexterity modifier.
The spell ends if targets don armor or if you dismiss the spell as a Major Action.  

<Overcast>

**(per +2):** the spell gains 1 base AC.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
