# Conjure Construct

A made thing assembles out of inert matter, and lumbers onward with mechanical purpose.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a working part of something else — a cog, a hinge, a length of good wire)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="[= 18 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You assemble a **construct** in an unoccupied space you can see within range, drawn together out of the idea of a mechanism rather than any particular one.
Their combined Lethality may not exceed **twice this spell's level**.
See [Summoning and Conjuration](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/summoning-and-conjuration) for the Lethality Budget, Swarms and Formations, command, and loss of control.  

A conjured construct follows orders exactly, including the parts of an order you did not intend to give.
It has no judgement to fall back on and will not improvise a kindness.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the Lethality Budget is always twice the level of the slot spent.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
