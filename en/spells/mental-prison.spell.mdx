# Mental Prison

An invisible cell clamps around the mind, collapsing perception into a sealed, immobile chamber.

---

  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see within range">

You attempt to bind a creature within an illusory cell that only it perceives.
Targets save Intelligence, halving [% 5d10 psychic %].
Failing targets are [# kw:condition:restrained #] in the illusion and can't see or hear anything beyond it for the duration.
If targets are moved out of the illusion, make a melee attack through it, or reach any part of their body through it, they take [% 10d10 psychic %], and the spell ends.  

<Overcast>

**(7th+):** the spell gains [% 1d10 %] damage on failing targets and when they move through the illusion.  

</Overcast>
