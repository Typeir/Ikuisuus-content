# Bolide Bombardment

You reach past the dead heaven and take hold of the Opaline Belt. What comes down is only stone until it meets the air; what the air makes of it is never the same twice.

---

> **Bolide Bombardment**  
> _9th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 1 league =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within each of three [= 8 stride;ADJ =]-radius spheres centered on points you can see within range  
>
> Three stones fall out of the Belt at three different points you can see within range. 
> **Opaline Shift.** Each stone burns a different colour on the way down. 
>
> | d10 | Stratum   | Damage    |
> | --- | --------- | --------- |
> | 1   | Elemental | frost     |
> | 2   | Elemental | fire      |
> | 3   | Elemental | lightning |
> | 4   | Somatic   | poison    |
> | 5   | Somatic   | chemical  |
> | 6   | Somatic   | psychic   |
> | 7   | Akashic   | holy      |
> | 8   | Akashic   | dark      |
> | 9   | Akashic   | force     |
> | 10  | True      | true      |
>
> Roll a d10 separately for each of the three impacts to determine what the stone was carrying. 
> A creature caught in more than one sphere is affected only once, by the impact of your choice. 
> 
> Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 10d12 bludgeoning %] and a further [% 10d12 %] of the type **Opaline Shift** rolled. 
> The spheres spread around corners.
>
> **Overcast:** the damage of both pools increases by 1d12.
> **Overcast (per 2):** one additional stone falls. Roll the Opaline Shift separately for every impact.
