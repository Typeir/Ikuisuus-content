# Waltzing Blades

A spell that pulls the splinters of war into a graceful yet deadly dance, forming a phalanx of domination around the caster.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a simple or martial weapon for each die of damage the spell can deal)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self">

You summon a swarm of ethereal dancing blades that orbit your body in tight, defensive spirals.  

While the spell lasts, when a creature **hits you with an attack**, the blades interpose and grant you **damage reduction** of **[% 1d4 %]** against it.  

When a creature **misses you with an attack**, the blades lash outward, dealing **[% 1d4 slashing %]** to that creature.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the reduction and the retaliatory damage each gain **[% 1d4 %]**.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
