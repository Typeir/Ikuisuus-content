# Thunder Step

A body dissolves into electric crackle and rematerials elsewhere in a thunderclap.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 18 stride =]">

You teleport yourself to an unoccupied space you can see within range.
Immediately after you disappear, a thunderous boom sounds, and each creature within [= 2 stride =] of the space you left saves Constitution, halving [% 3d10 bludgeoning %].
The thunder can be heard from up to [= 60 stride =] away.

You can bring along objects as long as their weight doesn’t exceed what you can carry.
You can also teleport one willing creature of your size or smaller who is carrying gear up to its carrying capacity.
The creature must be within [= 1 stride =] of you when you cast this spell, and there must be an unoccupied space within [= 1 stride =] of your destination space for the creature to appear in; otherwise, the creature is left behind.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the damage gains [% 1d10 %].

</Overcast>

</Spell>
