# Charm

A warmth spreads through the target, their muscles loosening and their gaze fixing with unnatural trust.

---

> **Charm**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 1 hour  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> You attempt to charm targets whose type the slot you cast this spell with can reach (see the table below). 
> Targets save Wisdom, at advantage if you or your companions are fighting them, or are [charmed](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#charmed) by you until the spell ends or until you or your companions do anything harmful to them. 
> While charmed, their [disposition](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/disposition-reputation-and-attitude) toward you is locked in the Friendly range; the charmed condition does not move their true disposition, and when the spell ends targets know they were charmed by you.  
>  
> | Slot Level | Creature types you can target                |
> | ---------- | -------------------------------------------- |
> | 1st        | Humanoid                                     |
> | 2nd        | + Wildlife                                   |
> | 3rd        | + Monstrosity, Construct                     |
> | 4th        | + Väki, Aberration                           |
> | 5th        | + Celestial                                  |
>  
> Each row includes the rows above it.  
>  
> **Overcast:** you can target one additional valid creature for each level above the minimum your chosen type requires. 
> The creatures must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other when you target them.
