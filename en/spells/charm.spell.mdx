# Charm

A warmth spreads through the target, their muscles loosening and their gaze fixing with unnatural trust.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="1 hour"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

You attempt to charm targets whose type the slot you cast this spell with can reach (see the table below). 
Targets save Wisdom, at advantage if you or your companions are fighting them, or are [# kw:condition:charmed #] by you until the spell ends or until you or your companions do anything harmful to them. 
While [# kw:condition:charmed #], their [# kw:disposition #] toward you is locked in the Friendly range; the [# kw:condition:charmed #] condition does not move their true disposition, and when the spell ends targets know they were [# kw:condition:charmed #] by you.  

| Slot Level | Creature types you can target                |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 1st        | Humanoid                                     |
| 2nd        | + Wildlife                                   |
| 3rd        | + Monstrosity, Construct                     |
| 4th        | + Väki, Aberration                           |
| 5th        | + Celestial                                  |

Each row includes the rows above it.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** you can target one additional valid creature for each level above the minimum your chosen type requires. 
The creatures must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other when you target them.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
