# Orrery

You call upon the disembodied astrals of the ancient Golden One, each providing guidance and reckoning with your mere thoughts.

> **Orrery**  
> _7th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 4 hours  
> **Range**: Self  
>  
> Seven star-like motes of light appear and orbit your head until the spell ends. You can use a Minor Action to send one of the motes streaking toward one creature or object within [= 24 stride =] of you. When you do so, make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes [% 4d12 holy %]. Whether you hit or miss, the mote is expended. The spell ends early if you expend the last mote.  
>  
> The motes shed bright light in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 6 stride =]. If you have four or more motes remaining, they shed bright light in a [= 12 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 6 stride =].  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the spell creates one additional mote for each slot level above 7th.
