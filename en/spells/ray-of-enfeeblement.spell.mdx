# Ray of Enfeeblement

A thin beam of shadow crawls over the target, slackening muscles and dulling strength.

---

> **Ray of Enfeeblement**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> Targets save Constitution or are enfeebled for the spell's duration: they subtract [% 1d8 %] from the damage of any attack they make using Strength.
>
> They can resist Constitution when they end a turn **more than [= 6 stride =] from you**.
>
> _Standing close enough to feel the beam is standing too close to shrug it off._
