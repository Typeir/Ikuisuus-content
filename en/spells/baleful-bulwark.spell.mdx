# Baleful Bulwark

Grim darkness envelops you, violently lashing out at any attacker with the crushing finality of oblivion itself.

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a cup of water)"
  duration="1 hour"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

A protective magical force surrounds you, manifesting as a spectral mantle that covers you and your gear. 
You gain 5 temporary hit points for the duration. 
If a creature hits you with a melee attack while you have these hit points, the creature takes 5 dark damage.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the mantle gains 5 temporary hit points and 5 dark damage.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
