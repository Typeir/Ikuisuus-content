# Knock

A sharp, resonant pulse strikes a sealed barrier — and the world, asked politely enough, agrees that this was always a door.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one object you can see in range">

Targets can be a door, a box, a chest, a set of manacles, a padlock, or another object that contains a mundane or magical means that prevents access.

Targets held shut by a mundane lock or that are stuck or barred become unlocked, unstuck, or unbarred.
If targets have multiple locks, only one of them is unlocked.
If targets are held shut with [_arcane lock_](/en/library/spells/arcane-lock), that spell is suppressed for 10 minutes, during which time they can be opened and shut normally.

When you cast the spell, a loud knock, audible from as far away as [= 60 stride =], emanates from targets.

<Overcast>

**(4th+):** any sealed portal-shaped thing — a gate, a grate, a hatch, a mine-seal, a shuttered window — opens as a door, and magical seals of the slot's level or lower are suppressed for 10 minutes.

**(5th+):** you may knock on a blank wooden, plaster, or stone surface, opening a passage [= 1 stride =] wide, [= 2 stride =] tall, and up to [= 4 stride =] deep, lasting **until dawn**.
The passage creates no instability in the structure around it and is covered by a simple illusion of the unbroken wall, which fails to hold up to examination.
When the passage closes, anything still inside is safely ejected to the nearest unoccupied space.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
