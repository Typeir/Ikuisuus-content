# Lost in the Muck

There is a rot-brine microchamber folded under the world, a bad copy of the drowned layer beneath Plato Station. People who come back out of it can draw you a map, and no two maps agree.

---

> **Lost in the Muck**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>
> You fold targets into the **Muck Dimension**. Targets save Wisdom or are banished for the duration or until they find their own way out.
>
> They can resist Intelligence using **Cartographer's Tools** or **Navigator's Tools** to find their own way out.
>
> When the spell ends, targets reappear in the space they left, or in the nearest unoccupied space if that one is taken.
