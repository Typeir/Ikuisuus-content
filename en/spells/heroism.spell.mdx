# Heroism

A steady warmth suffuses the target, steadying their breath and quieting fear.

---

> **Heroism**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature you touch  
>  
> The spell imbues targets with bravery. Until the spell ends, targets are immune to being frightened and gain temporary hit points equal to [# kw:damage bonus #] at the start of each of their turns. When the spell ends, targets lose any remaining temporary hit points from this spell.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature.
