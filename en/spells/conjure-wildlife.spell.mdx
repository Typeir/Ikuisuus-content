# Conjure Wildlife

A call is answered by chitin, claws, tendrils and growth.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a handful of fur, seed, or gristle, offered and not returned)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You strike a bargain, and living things step into unoccupied spaces you can see within range — beast or growth, tooth or thorn; not creatures fetched from elsewhere, but **Väki stuff wearing a living shape** for as long as the bargain holds.
It is a bargain, never a command.  

Their combined Lethality may not exceed **twice this spell's level**.
See [Summoning and Conjuration](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/summoning-and-conjuration) for the Lethality Budget, how a multitude resolves as a Swarm or Formation, command and control, and what becomes of the called when your concentration breaks.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the Lethality Budget is always twice the level of the slot spent — a 5th-level casting calls Lethality 10 of wildlife.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
