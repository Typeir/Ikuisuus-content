# Jaws of Selkara

A spell that manifests a vast pair of ossified jaws, snapping shut in a single, grinding bite before vanishing.

---

> **Jaws of Selkara**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 5 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the cone  
>  
> You call forth a massive pair of bone jaws that erupt outward from you in a **[= 5 stride;ADJ =] cone** and snap shut.
>  
> Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 12d4 piercing %].  
>  
> Creatures that are **Large or larger** have disadvantage on the saving throw.  
>  
> The jaws vanish immediately after closing.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d4 %] damage.
