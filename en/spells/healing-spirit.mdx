# Healing Spirit

A pale wisp settles on the injured, knitting torn flesh and stilling their pain.

---

> **Healing Spirit**  
> _2nd-level Conjuration_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Range**: 60 feet  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute
>
> You call forth a nature spirit to soothe the wounded. The intangible spirit appears in a space that is a 5-foot cube you can see within range. The spirit looks like a transparent beast or fey (your choice).
>
> Until the spell ends, as a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), you can cause the spirit to restore 1d6 hit points to the creature affected, no action required. The spirit can’t heal constructs.
> The spirit can heal a number of times equal to 1 + your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of twice). After healing that number of times, the spirit disappears.
>
> As a Minor Action on your turn, you can move the spirit up to 30 feet to a space you can see.
>
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the healing increases by [% 1d6 %] for each slot level above 2nd.
