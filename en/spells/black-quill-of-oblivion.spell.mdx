# Black Quill Of Oblivion

Ten feet of borrowed edge, hanging in the air with nothing holding it up. 
The God of Ambition's blade is a great deal longer than this. You are permitted the sliver.

---

> **Black Quill Of Oblivion**  
> _9th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute

> **Targets**: Up to two creatures within [= 1 stride =] of a point you can see within range  
>

> The blade has a range of [= 2 stride =]. 
> The blaed deals [% 4d12 true %] damage.
