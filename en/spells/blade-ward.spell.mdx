# Blade Ward

A ragged film of force snaps over your skin, deflecting blades and blows.

---

> **Blade Ward**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: you  
>
> You extend your hand and trace a sigil of warding in the air.
>
> Creatures gain a penalty [% 1d4 %] to attacks against you.
