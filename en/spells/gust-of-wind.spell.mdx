# Gust of Wind

A brutal column of wind erupts forward, stripping loose objects and hurling debris in its path.

---

>   
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] line)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the line  
>  
> A line of strong wind [= 12 stride =] long and [= 2 stride =] wide blasts from you in a direction you choose for the spell’s duration. As an [_delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), targets save Strength or are pushed [= 3 stride =] away from you in a direction following the line.  
>  
> Targets moving closer to you **pay double the cost of movement**.  
>  
> The gust disperses gas or vapor, and it extinguishes candles, torches, and similar unprotected flames in the area. It causes protected flames, such as those of lanterns, to dance wildly and has a 50 percent chance to extinguish them.  
>  
> As a Minor Action on each of your turns before the spell ends, you can change the direction in which the line blasts from you.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the line gains [= 2 stride =] of length.
