# Orbital Cascade

Seven bodies of the sky answer at once. None of them arrive gently, and none of them arrive in the same way twice.

---

> **Orbital Cascade**  
> _7th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 12 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the cone  
>
> You call the orbits down in a cone. Targets save Dexterity, rolling a d8 for each target to see which body answers.
>
> 1. **Kultharja.** Targets take [% 10d6 holy %], halving on the same save.
> 2. **Urmela.** Targets take [% 10d6 fire %], halving on the same save.
> 3. **Selkara.** Targets take [% 10d6 piercing %], halving on the same save.
> 4. **Kalmora.** Targets take [% 10d6 poison %], halving on the same save.
> 5. **Itähenki and Länsihenki.** Targets take [% 10d6 lightning %], halving on the same save.
> 6. **Mana.** Failing targets are **[# kw:condition:restrained #]** as crystal closes over them.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution, [# kw:chained #](3/3). Failing the chain closes the crystal completely, leaving targets **[# kw:condition:petrified #]**.
> 7. **Damocles.** Failing targets are **[# kw:condition:blinded #]** by the mirage of every soul the world is carrying.
> At the start of your next turn, targets save Wisdom or are unmoored from the world entirely: they surface somewhere in the Folds, or adrift toward a Clone World, and where they surface is not yours to choose.
> Saving targets shed the blindness.
> 8. **Conjunction.** Targets are struck by two bodies. Roll twice more, rerolling any 8.
>
> **Overcast:** one additional body strikes each target.
