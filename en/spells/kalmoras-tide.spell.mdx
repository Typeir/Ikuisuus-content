# Kalmora's Tide

The grave-current runs early. It moves through the living the way it moves through the drowned, and it does not much distinguish between the two.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, M (a mouthful of grave-silt)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see in range">

You open Kalmora's pull beneath targets. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 5d12 dark %]. A construct is unaffected.

**The Tide Rises.** At the start of your next turn, corpses within [= 4 stride =] of targets stand up as constructs (undead), each with a Challenge Rating no higher than the creature it was made from.
Together they cannot exceed the **Challenge Budget** for this spell's slot — twice the slot level, as laid out in [Summoning and Conjuration](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/summoning-and-conjuration).
Corpses past the budget stay where they fell.

None of them are yours. Each pursues the closest creature it can see, and shows you no preference.

<Overcast>

**(6th+):** the spell gains [% 1d12 %] damage.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
