# Kalmora's Tide

The grave-current runs early. It moves through the living the way it moves through the drowned, and it does not much distinguish between the two.

---

> **Kalmora's Tide**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, M (a mouthful of grave-silt)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>
> You open Kalmora's pull beneath targets. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 5d12 dark %]. A construct is unaffected.
>
> **The Tide Rises.** At the start of your next turn, corpses within [= 4 stride =] of targets stand up as constructs (undead), each with a Challenge Rating no higher than the creature it was made from. Together they cannot exceed the **Challenge Budget** for this spell's slot — twice the slot level, as laid out in [Summoning and Conjuration](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/summoning-and-conjuration). Corpses past the budget stay where they fell.
>
> None of them are yours. Each pursues the closest creature it can see, and shows you no preference.
>
> **Overcast (6th+):** the spell gains [% 1d12 %] damage.
