# Word of Recall

A vertical seam of compressed light opens at the caster's feet, a hard, silent pull dragging them and nearby matter through before the seam snaps shut.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 1 stride =]">

**Designating a Sanctuary.** You designate a sanctuary by casting this spell in a place consecrated to your god, or bound tightly enough to it that the distinction stops mattering.
Cast this way anywhere else, the spell does nothing.  

**Recall.** You and up to five willing creatures within [= 1 stride =] of you are instantly folded to the sanctuary you designated, arriving in the nearest unoccupied spaces to the spot you marked.
If you cast this spell without having prepared a sanctuary, nothing happens at all.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell takes **five additional creatures** with you.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
