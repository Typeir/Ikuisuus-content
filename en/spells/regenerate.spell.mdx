# Regenerate

Flesh and bone knit and regrow as wounds close, severed limbs reattach and skin seals.

---

<Spell
  level="7"
  cost="1 Minute"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature">

Targets regain [% 4d8 +15 %] Hit Points.
For the duration, targets regain 1 Hit Point at the start of each of their turns, and any severed body parts regrow after 2 minutes.

</Spell>
