# Fling

An object snaps forward with invisible force, hurtling into the target with brutal impact.

---

> **Fling**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
>  
> Choose one object weighing 1 to [= 3 burden =] within range that isn’t being worn or carried. The object flies in a straight line up to [= 18 stride =] in a direction you choose before falling to the ground, stopping early if it impacts against a solid surface. If the object would strike a creature, that creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the object strikes the target and stops moving. When the object strikes something, the object and what it strikes each take [% 3d8 bludgeoning %].  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by [% 1d8 %] for each slot level above 1st.
