# Agathos's Mirth

Convulsive, involuntary laughter explodes from the target, collapsing them into trembling, breathless spasms.

---

> **Agathos's Mirth**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (tiny tarts and a feather that is waved in the air)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Targets perceive everything as hilariously funny and fall into fits of laughter. Targets save Wisdom or fall prone and are incapacitated. Targets with an Intelligence score of 4 or less aren't affected.  
>  
> They can resist Wisdom. They can immediately resist at advantage when they take damage.
