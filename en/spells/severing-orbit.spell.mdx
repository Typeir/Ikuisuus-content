# Severing Orbit

A spell that forges a perfect loop of force into a spinning chakram, endlessly returning to its wielder in a deadly, disciplined arc.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

You conjure a **chakram of condensed force**, its edge humming as it spins in place before snapping forward on command.
While the spell lasts, you can hurl this chakram repeatedly.  

When you cast the spell, and as a **Major Action** on each of your turns for the duration, you can throw the chakram at a creature you can see within **[= 24 stride =]**.  

When you do so, the chakram becomes a line of force **[= 1 stride =] wide** that extends from you to that creature.  

Creatures in the line, other than you and that creature, save Dexterity, halving [% 2d6 force %].  

Make a ranged attack that deals [% 5d6 force %] to that creature.  

After the attack resolves, the chakram curves back through the air and **lands in your hand**, ready to be thrown again.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the attack and the line each gain [% 1d6 %] force damage.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
