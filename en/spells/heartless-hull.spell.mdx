# Heartless Hull

The beat of your heart slows to a crawl, and so does that of those surrounding you, life fleeing from all in terror.

---

> **Heartless Hull**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour  
> **Range**: Self ([= 2 stride;ADJ =] aura)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the [= 2 stride;ADJ =] aura  
>  
> A hull of grave-cold spreads [= 2 stride =] around you and moves with you, remaining centered on you for the duration. Undead and constructs have no heart to still and are unaffected.  
>  
> **The heart falters.** At the start of each of your turns, targets take [% 2d6 dark %].  
>  
> **Conversion.** While targets are inside the hull, any **dark** damage they take — from this spell or any other source — also inflicts an equal amount of [Grievous Wounds](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/grievous-wounds) upon them. Grievous Wounds reduce a creature's maximum and current hit points and can be undone only by spending Hit Dice; no magic mends them.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] dark damage at the start of your turns.
