# Heartless Hull

The beat of your heart slows to a crawl, and so does that of those surrounding you, life fleeing from all in terror.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Self ([= 2 stride;ADJ =] aura)"
  targets="Creatures within the [= 2 stride;ADJ =] aura">

A hull of grave-cold spreads [= 2 stride =] around you and moves with you, remaining centered on you for the duration.
Undead and constructs have no heart to still and are unaffected.  

**The heart falters.** At the start of each of your turns, targets take [% 2d6 dark %].  

**Conversion.** While targets are inside the hull, any **dark** damage they take — from this spell or any other source — also inflicts an equal amount of [Grievous Wounds](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/grievous-wounds) upon them.
Grievous Wounds reduce a creature's maximum and current hit points and can be undone only by spending Hit Dice; no magic mends them.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] dark damage at the start of your turns.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
