# Harm

A jagged pulse of blight rips into the target, flesh withering and blackened veins spreading across their body.

You unleash a virulent disease upon targets.

The damage can’t reduce a creature’s hit points below 1.
Any effect that removes a disease allows a creature’s hit point maximum to return to normal before that time passes.
