# Agathian Guile

Your voice sharpens into a steady, magnetic cadence that fixes listeners on each word.

---

> **Agathian Guile**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Reaction, taken when you have just spoken  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: Creatures that heard the statement  
>  
> When you speak to others, you can subtly alter the words you have just spoken.  
>  
> When you cast this spell, you magically alter your words to be whatever you wish, even if you have already spoken.
> You can change up to 6 seconds (one round) of speech that you have just spoken, replacing it with new words of your choice. 
> Creatures that heard the statement save Wisdom, forgetting it and remembering only your words. Saving targets know the words were altered.
