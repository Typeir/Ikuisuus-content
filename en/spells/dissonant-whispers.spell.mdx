# Dissonant Whispers

A serrated whisper claws into the target's mind, shredding their concentration.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can hear">

Targets save Wisdom, halving [% 3d6 psychic %].
Failing targets immediately use their reaction, if available, to move as far as their speed allows away from you.
They never move into obviously dangerous ground, such as a fire or a pit.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the damage gains [% 1d6 %].  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
