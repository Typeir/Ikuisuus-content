# Dissonant Whispers

A serrated whisper claws into the target's mind, shredding their concentration.

---

> **Dissonant Whispers**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can hear  
>  
> Targets save Wisdom, halving [% 3d6 psychic %]. Failing targets immediately use their reaction, if available, to move as far as their speed allows away from you. They never move into obviously dangerous ground, such as a fire or a pit.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the damage gains [% 1d6 %].
