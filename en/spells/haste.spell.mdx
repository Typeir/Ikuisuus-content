# Haste

Muscles snap taut and motion accelerates into a hard blur, limbs striking with sudden, mechanical quickness.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a shaving of liquorice root)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="one willing creature you can see within range">

Until the spell ends, targets' speed is doubled, they gain a **+2 bonus to AC**, and they have advantage on Dexterity saving throws.

Targets also gain a refreshing pool of **1 legendary deed**, regaining it at the start of each of their turns (see [Legendary Deeds](/en/library/rules/creatures-critters-and-characters/legendary-deeds)).
They may spend this deed only on a **Legendary Deed: Act**, taken at the end of another creature's turn — one weapon attack, or the Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action — and, like all deeds, they may use at most one per turn and it counts against the round's shared deed cap.

When the spell ends, targets are [# kw:condition:Stunned #] [# kw:briefly #].

Targets then quickly pay the cost of the borrowed time; they instantly age as if a tenday had passed.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the pool gains **1 additional legendary deed**.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
