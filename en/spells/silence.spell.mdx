# Silence

Ambient sound collapses into a flat, motionless hush.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range">

For the duration, no sound is created within — and none can pass into or out of — the sphere.
There is no saving throw; the hush simply holds.  

Targets entirely inside the sphere are **[# kw:condition:deafened #]**, cannot cast a spell with a verbal component, and cannot be harmed by anything that works through sound — a thunderous blast, a shattering note, a word of power all die at the sphere's edge (such sonic force deals bludgeoning damage in Damocles, and the sphere negates it).  

**Overcast:** the sphere gains [= 2 stride =] to its radius.  

</Spell>
