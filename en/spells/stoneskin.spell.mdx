# Stoneskin

The target's skin hardens into dull, rock-like plates, flesh taking on the texture of stone.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100 gp, which the spell consumes)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature">

Targets' flesh turns as hard as stone. Until the spell ends, targets have **resistance to physical damage**.

**Overcast (per +2):** target one additional creature.

</Spell>
