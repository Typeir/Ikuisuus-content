# Ward Against Interlopers

A pale, translucent ward clings to the touched creature, pushing external influence away and steadying the target.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (holy water or powdered silver and iron, which the spell consumes)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="Touch">

When you cast this spell, **choose one creature type**: aberration, celestial, construct, monstrosity, null, väki, or wildlife.
You cannot choose humanoid.

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is warded against creatures of the chosen type.
Such creatures have **disadvantage on attack rolls** against the target, and the target cannot be **[# kw:condition:charmed #], [# kw:condition:frightened #], or possessed** by them.
If the target is already suffering one of those effects from such a creature, it has advantage on any new saving throw against it.  

</Spell>
