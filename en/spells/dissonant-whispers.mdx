# Dissonant Whispers

A serrated whisper claws into the target's mind, shredding their concentration.

---

> **Dissonant Whispers**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
>  
> You whisper a discordant melody that only one creature of your choice within range can hear, wracking it with terrible pain. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it takes [% 3d6 psychic %] and must immediately use its reaction, if available, to move as far as its speed allows away from you. The creature doesn’t move into obviously dangerous ground, such as a fire or a pit. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and doesn’t have to move away.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by [% 1d6 %] for each slot level above 1st.
