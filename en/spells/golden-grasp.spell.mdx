# Golden Grasp

A vast, translucent hand of searing gold bursts into being, slamming down and seizing whatever lies beneath its palm.

---

> **Golden Grasp**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (an eggshell and a snakeskin glove)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: —  
>  
>  
> You create a Large hand of shimmering, translucent gold in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The hand lasts for the spell’s duration, and it moves at your command, mimicking the movements of your own hand.  
>  
> The hand is an object that has AC 20 and hit points equal to your hit point maximum. If it drops to 0 hit points, the spell ends. It has a Strength of 26 (+8) and a Dexterity of 10 (+0). The hand doesn’t fill its space.  
>  
> When you cast the spell and as a Minor Action on your subsequent turns, you can move the hand up to [= 12 stride =] and then cause one of the following effects with it.  
>  
> **Clenched Fist.**
> **Targets**: one creature or object within [= 1 stride =] of the hand
> The hand strikes targets. Make a melee attack for the hand using your game statistics, dealing [% 4d8 holy %].  
>  
> **Forceful Hand.**
> **Targets**: one creature within [= 1 stride =] of the hand
> The hand attempts to push targets in a direction you choose. Make a check with the hand’s Strength contested by their Strength (Athletics) check, at advantage if they are Medium or smaller. If you succeed, the hand pushes targets up to [= 1 stride =] plus a number of strides equal to [# kw:accuracy #], and moves with them to remain within [= 1 stride =].  
>  
> **Grasping Hand.**
> **Targets**: one Huge or smaller creature within [= 1 stride =] of the hand
> The hand attempts to grapple targets, using the hand’s Strength score to resolve it, at advantage if they are Medium or smaller. While grappling, you can use a Minor Action to have the hand crush them, dealing physical damage equal to [% 2d6 %] + [# kw:damage bonus #].  
>  
> The hand can restrain only one target at a time.  
>  
> **Interposing Hand.**
> **Targets**: one creature you choose
> The hand interposes itself between you and targets until you give it a different command, moving to stay between you and them, and providing you with half cover against them. Targets can’t move through the hand’s space unless their Strength score is higher than the hand’s, and that space is difficult terrain for them.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the clenched fist gains [% 2d8 %] damage and the grasping hand gains [% 2d6 %] damage.
