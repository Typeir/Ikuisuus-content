# Thieves' Third Hand

A narrow seam opens at your wrist, forming a hollow that swallows and holds small objects out of sight.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  ritual
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="S"
  duration="4 hours, or until you dismiss it"
  range="Self">

You flick your wrist, causing a tiny extradimensional space to open at your fingertips, which you can use to store small objects.
The space can hold up to [= 3 burden =] or an object that fits in your hand.
As part of the action used to cast the spell, or as a Minor Action while the spell persists, you can place one object into the space or retrieve one object from it.
You can only have one object in the space at a time.
You can retrieve the object at any time, and if the spell ends, the object appears in your hand or at your feet (your choice).  

If you die while the object is in the space, it appears at your feet.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell stores one additional object.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
