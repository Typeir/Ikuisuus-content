# Chained Lightning

A tether of lightning leaps to a throat and holds there, humming, yours to feed — and when you pour enough into it, it stops holding to one throat and starts to leap between them.

---

> **Chained Lightning**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a twig from a tree struck by lightning)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> A crackling arc of lightning lances toward a creature within range. Make a ranged attack that deals [% 1d12 lightning %] and links targets to you by an arc of lightning.  
>  
> On each of your later turns, you can use your **Major Action** to feed the tether, dealing [% 1d12 lightning %] to linked targets automatically, with no attack roll. The spell ends if you use your Major Action for anything else, if linked targets leave the spell's range, or if they gain total cover from you.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the initial hit and the sustained damage each gain [% 1d12 %] lightning.  
>  
> **Overcast (3rd+): Chained.** Each time the tether deals damage, the lightning leaps from the linked target to one other creature within [= 6 stride =] of them, dealing the same damage with no attack roll. No creature is struck by more than one leap in a turn.  
>  
> **Overcast (5th+) (per +2):** the arc gains one additional leap.
