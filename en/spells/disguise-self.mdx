# Disguise Self

Another face settles over your own, close enough to fool every eye that does not touch it.

---

> **Disguise Self**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 4 hours  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: yourself  
>  
> You make yourself — including your clothing, armor, weapons, and other belongings on your person — look different until the spell ends or you dismiss it as a Major Action. You can seem [= 1/5 stride =] shorter or taller and appear thin, fat, or in between; you cannot change your basic body shape. The changes are otherwise up to you.  
>  
> The changes fail to hold up to physical inspection: a hand passes through an illusory hat, and a head meets an unseen brow. A creature that uses a Major Action to study your appearance can discern the illusion with an Intelligence (Investigation) check.  
>  
> **Overcast (3rd+):** you can also disguise one additional creature within [= 6 stride =]. Unwilling targets save Charisma or are disguised. The duration becomes 8 hours, and you decide targets' seeming; they can't change their own.
