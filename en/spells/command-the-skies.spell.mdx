# Command the Skies

You hark upon the sky-väkis, and they briefly listen. They are not obedient so much as agreeable, and only for as long as the arrangement amuses them.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a whistle carved from hollow bone)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="[= 60 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within a [= 20 stride;ADJ =] cube you can see within range">

You take control of the air in a [= 20 stride;ADJ =] cube you can see within range.
It lasts for the duration unless you use a **Major Action** on a later turn to switch to a different one.
Only one effect can be active at a time.

<Choice>

<Feature>

#### Gusts

A wind picks up within the cube, blowing continuously in a horizontal direction you choose, at an intensity you choose: calm, moderate, or strong.
While it is moderate or strong, ranged weapon attacks that pass through the cube or are made against targets inside it have disadvantage.
While it is strong, targets moving against the wind spend [= 1 stride =] extra per [= 1 stride =] moved.

</Feature>

<Feature targets="flying targets">

#### Downdraft

A sustained blast of wind blows downward from the top of the cube.
Ranged weapon attacks that pass through the cube or are made against targets inside it have disadvantage.
As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, targets save Strength or are knocked **[# kw:condition:prone #]**.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Updraft

A sustained updraft rises from the cube's bottom edge.
Targets that end a fall within the cube take only half the falling damage, and targets making a vertical jump inside it can jump up to [= 2 stride =] higher than normal.

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Whirlwind

A vortex forms in the cube: [= 2 stride =] wide at its base, up to [= 10 stride =] wide at its top, and as tall as the cube.
Every unsecured object of Medium size or smaller within it that is not worn or carried is drawn up, carried spiralling for a round, and flung outward to land [% 2d6 %] × [= 2 stride =] from the vortex in a random direction.
As a [# kw:persistent #] effect, targets save Strength or are lifted [= 4 stride =] and dropped, taking falling damage as normal.

</Feature>

</Choice>

</Spell>
