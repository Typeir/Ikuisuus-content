# Geas

An invisible injunction lodges in the target's mind, bending their actions toward a single command.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Minute"
  components="V"
  duration="30 days"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

You place a magical command on targets, forcing them to carry out some service or refrain from some action as you decide.
Targets that understand you save Wisdom or are [# kw:condition:charmed #] for the duration.
While [# kw:condition:charmed #], they take [% 5d10 psychic %] each time they act directly counter to your instructions, no more than once per day.
Targets that can't understand you are unaffected.  

You can issue any command you choose, short of an activity that would result in certain death.
Should you issue a suicidal command, the spell ends.  

You can end the spell early by using a Major Action to dismiss it.
A [_Remove Affliction_](/en/library/spells/remove-affliction), [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration), or [_wish_](/en/library/spells/wish) spell also ends it.  

<Overcast>

**(7th+):** the duration becomes 1 year.  

**(9th+):** the spell lasts until ended by one of the spells above.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
