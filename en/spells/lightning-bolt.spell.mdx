# Lightning Bolt

A jagged bolt of lightning rips forward in a straight line, cracking the air and scorching whatever it strikes.

---

> **Lightning Bolt**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a bit of fur and a rod of amber, crystal, or glass)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 20 stride;ADJ =] line)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the line  
>  
> A stroke of lightning forming a line [= 20 stride =] long and [= 1 stride =] wide blasts out from you in a direction you choose. Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 8d6 lightning %]. The lightning ignites flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
