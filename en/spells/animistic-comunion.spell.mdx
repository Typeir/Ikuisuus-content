# Animistic Communion

The splinters of the world dance to your tune, an investiture of the indifferent elements that shape the world around you.

---

> **Animistic Communion**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: you  
>
> When you cast this spell, choose one aspect: **Flame**, **Ice**, **Stone**, or **Wind**.  
> You gain that aspect's benefits for the duration.
>
> **Flame.** Flames race across your body, shedding bright light in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 6 stride =].
>
> - You are immune to fire damage and have resistance to frost damage.
> - As a [_persistent, delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures within [= 1 stride =] of you take [% 1d10 fire %].
> - You can use your Major Action to create a line of fire [= 3 stride =] long and [= 1 stride =] wide extending from you.
> - Creatures in the line save Dexterity, halving [% 4d8 fire %].
> - The line ignites flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried.
>
> **Ice.** Ice rimes your body.
>
> - You are immune to frost damage and have resistance to fire damage.
> - You can move across difficult terrain created by ice or snow without spending extra movement.
> - The ground in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius around you is icy and is difficult terrain for creatures other than you; the area moves with you.
> - You can use your Major Action to create a blast of cold air in a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone.
> - Creatures in that area save Constitution, halving [% 4d6 frost %]. Failing targets have their speed reduced to 0 [# kw:briefly #].
>
> **Stone.** Bits of rock spread across your body.
>
> - You have resistance to physical damage.
> - You can move across difficult terrain made of earth or stone without expending extra movement.
> - You can use your Major Action to create a small earthquake on the ground within [= 3 stride =] of you.
> - Creatures on that ground other than you save Dexterity or are knocked prone.
> - You can also use a Major Action to touch a stone object or surface and merge with it.
> - While merged, you can see and hear through the stone as if in your normal form.
> - You can use your movement to leave the stone at a point you can see within [= 1 stride =] of your entry point.
> - If the spell ends while merged, you are ejected to the nearest unoccupied space and take [% 5d6 force %].
>
> **Wind.** A howling column of wind erupts from you.
>
> - Ranged weapon attacks against you have disadvantage.
> - You gain a flying speed of [= 12 stride =].
> - If you are still flying when the spell ends, you fall unless you can otherwise prevent it.
> - You can use your Major Action to create a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] cube of swirling wind centered on a point you can see within [= 12 stride =] of you.
> - Creatures in that cube save Constitution, halving [% 2d10 bludgeoning %].
> - The wind disperses gas or vapor, and extinguishes candles, torches, and unprotected flames in the area.
