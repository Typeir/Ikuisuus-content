# Flesh to Stone

The target's flesh and muscle harden into cold, brittle stone, joints locking as cracks spider outward.

---

> **Flesh to Stone**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of lime, water, and earth)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
>  
> You attempt to turn one creature you can see within range into stone. If target's body is made of flesh, targets save Constitution or are restrained as their flesh begins to harden.  
>  
> They can resist Constitution. After three failed saves, targets are turned to stone and subjected to the petrified condition for the duration; after three successful saves, the spell ends. The successes and failures don't need to be consecutive; keep track of both until targets collect three of a kind.  
>  
> If targets are physically broken while petrified, they suffer from similar deformities if they revert to their original state.  
>  
> If you maintain your concentration on the spell for the entire possible duration, targets are petrified until the effect is removed by [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration) cast as its greater working, or other magic.
