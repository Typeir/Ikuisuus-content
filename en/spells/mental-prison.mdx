# Mental Prison

An invisible cell clamps around the mind, collapsing perception into a sealed, immobile chamber.

---

> **Mental Prison**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within range  
>  
> You attempt to bind a creature within an illusory cell that only it perceives. Targets save Intelligence, halving [% 5d10 psychic %]. Failing targets are restrained in the illusion and can't see or hear anything beyond it for the duration. If targets are moved out of the illusion, make a melee attack through it, or reach any part of their body through it, they take [% 10d10 psychic %], and the spell ends.  
>  
> **Overcast (7th+):** the spell gains [% 1d10 %] damage on a failed save and when targets move through the illusion.
