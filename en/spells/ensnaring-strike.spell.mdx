# Ensnaring Strike

Barbed bramble bursts out of the wound itself, faster than anything green has a right to move, and closes around whatever is nearest.

---

> **Ensnaring Strike**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: the creature you next hit with a weapon attack before the spell ends  
>  
> A writhing mass of thorny vines appears at the point of impact. Targets save Strength or are restrained until the spell ends. Targets that are Large or larger have advantage on the save.  
>  
> While restrained by this spell, targets take [% 1d6 physical %] at the start of each of their turns. Targets, or a creature that can touch them, can break free with a Major Action and a successful Strength check against 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #].  
>  
> **Overcast:** the vines gain [% 1d6 %] physical damage.
