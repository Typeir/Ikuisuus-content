# Heat Metal

Struck metal flares white‑hot and radiates blistering heat, warping nearby matter and hissing with steam.

---

> **Heat Metal**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a piece of iron and a flame)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one manufactured metal object you can see in range  
>  
> You cause the target to glow red-hot. Any creature in physical contact with it takes [% 2d8 fire %] when you cast the spell. Until the spell ends, you can use a Minor Action on each of your subsequent turns to cause this damage again.
>  
> If a creature is holding or wearing the target and takes damage from it, targets save Constitution or drop the target if they can. If they don't drop the target, they have disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of your next turn.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
