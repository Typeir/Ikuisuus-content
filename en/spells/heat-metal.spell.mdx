# Heat Metal

Struck metal flares white‑hot and radiates blistering heat, warping nearby matter and hissing with steam.

---

> **Heat Metal**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a piece of iron and a flame)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one manufactured metal object you can see in range  
>  
> You cause targets to glow red-hot.
>  
> **The Victim.**  
> **Targets**: any creature in physical contact with the heated metal  
> Targets take [% 2d8 fire %] when you cast the spell, and again whenever you use a Minor Action on a later turn.
> Targets holding or wearing it save Constitution or drop it if they can.
> Targets that don't drop it have disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of your next turn.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
