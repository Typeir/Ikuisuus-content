# Reverse Gravity

A vertical field inverts gravity, wrenching objects and bodies upward in a sudden, violent surge.

---

<Spell
  level="7"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 20 stride =]"
  targets="Everything within a [= 10 stride;ADJ =]-radius, [= 20 stride;ADJ =]-high cylinder centered on a point within range, other than those anchored to the ground">

Targets save Dexterity, grabbing a fixed object they can reach.
Failing targets fall upward, reaching the top of the area when you cast this spell.  

If falling targets encounter a solid object, such as a ceiling, they strike it just as they would during a normal downward fall.
Targets that reach the top of the area without striking anything remain there, oscillating slightly, for the duration.  

When the spell ends, targets fall back down.  

**Overcast:** the radius gains [= 2 stride =], and the height [= 5 stride =].  

</Spell>
