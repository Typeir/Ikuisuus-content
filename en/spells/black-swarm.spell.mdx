# Black Swarm

You call upon the lord of Rot's kin, and they listen. 
A crawling myriad of all-consuming rotlings devours all but time.

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a few grains of sugar, some kernels of grain, and a smear of fat)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 60 stride =]"
  targets="Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range">

Swarming, biting locusts fill the area.
The sphere spreads around corners. 
The sphere remains for the duration, and its area is lightly obscured. 
The sphere’s area is difficult terrain. 

As a [# kw:persistent #], [# kw:delayed #] effect, targets save Constitution, halving [% 4d10 piercing %]. 

**Overcast:** the swarm gains [% 1d10 %] damage.
