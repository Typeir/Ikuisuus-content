# Life Transference

A pale cord of living light forms between bodies, drawing vitality from one and pouring warmth into the other as flesh pales along the link.

---

> **Life Transference**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>  
> You sacrifice some of your health to mend another creature’s injuries. You take [% 4d8 dark %], and targets regain hit points equal to twice the damage you take. This damage can’t be reduced in any way.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
