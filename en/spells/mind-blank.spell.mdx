# Mind Blank

The spell does not hide thought. It erases the surface from which thought can be read, like pulling a page from the book before the reader arrives.

---

<Spell
  level="8"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature you touch">

Until the spell ends, targets are immune to psychic damage, any effect that would sense their emotions or read their thoughts, divination spells, and the [# kw:condition:charmed #] condition.
The spell even foils wish spells and spells or effects of similar power used to affect their mind or to gain information about them.  

</Spell>
