# Mind Blank

The spell does not hide thought. It erases the surface from which thought can be read, like pulling a page from the book before the reader arrives.

---

> **Mind Blank**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 24 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature you touch  
>  
> Until the spell ends, targets are immune to psychic damage, any effect that would sense their emotions or read their thoughts, divination spells, and the charmed condition. The spell even foils wish spells and spells or effects of similar power used to affect their mind or to gain information about them.
