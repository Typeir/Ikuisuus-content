# Mind Blast

A burst of white static rips through the target's mind, splintering sight and sound and seizing muscles in jarring convulsions.

---

> **Mind Blast**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you can see in range  
>  
> Targets save Intelligence, halving [% 6d8 psychic %]. A creature with an Intelligence score of 2 or lower can't be affected by this spell.  
>  
> Failing targets have muddled thoughts for 1 minute. During that time, they roll a d6 and subtract the number rolled from all their attack rolls and ability checks, as well as their Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration. They can resist Intelligence.  
>  
> **Overcast (6th+):** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
