# Entangle

Knotted roots and thorned vines burst from the earth, wrapping around anything in their path.

---

> **Entangle**  
> _1st-level Conjuration_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute
>
> Grasping weeds and vines sprout from the ground in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] square starting from a point within range. For the duration, these plants turn the ground in the area into difficult terrain.
>
> A creature in the area when you cast the spell must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained by the entangling plants until the spell ends. A creature restrained by the plants can use a Major Action to make a Strength(Athletics) check against your spell save DC. On a success, it frees itself.
> The growth is flammable: any [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube exposed to fire burns away in 1 round, applying the [_Burning_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning) as an [_incipient effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) to creatures in the fire.
> When the spell ends, the conjured plants wilt away.
>
> **At Higher Levels.**
>
> - **Each** slot level above 1st adds one additional [= 2 stride;ADJ =] square of growth.
> - **2nd level or higher (Lattice):** the growth thickens into a clinging web of fiber and thorn.
>   **The duration becomes Concentration, up to 10 minutes,** the area is lightly obscured, and as a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures must also make the Strength saving throw.
> - **4th level or higher (Crush):** the growth constricts — a creature restrained by the plants takes [% 3d6 bludgeoning %] at the start of each of its turns.
