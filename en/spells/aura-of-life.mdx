# Aura of Life

A low, warm halo blooms outward, knitting torn flesh and steadying faltering breath.

---

> **Aura of Life**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes  
> **Range**: Self ([= 6 stride;ADJ =] aura)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within [= 6 stride;ADJ =] of you  
>
> Life-preserving energy radiates from you in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius. The aura moves with you, centered on you.
>
> As a **Minor Action**, you can cause one creature in the aura — including yourself — to regain [% 2d6 %] hit points.
>
> **Overcast (per +2):** the aura gains [% 1d6 %] healing.
>
> **Overcast (5th+):** the aura wards those inside it — nonhostile living creatures gain resistance to dark and their hit point maximum cannot be reduced.
