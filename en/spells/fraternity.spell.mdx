# Fraternity

A braided tether of pale light snaps from your hand and fastens to the target, humming with a taut, binding energy.

---

> **Fraternity**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a pair of platinum rings worth at least 50 gp each, which you and the target must wear for the duration)  
> **Duration**: 4 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature you touch  
>  
> 
> This spell creates a mystic connection between you and them until the spell ends.  
>  
> While they are within [= 12 stride =] of you, they gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws, and they have resistance to all damage. Also, each time they take damage, you take the same amount of damage.  
>  
> The spell ends if you drop to 0 hit points or if you and they become separated by more than [= 12 stride =]. It also ends if the spell is cast again on either of the connected creatures. You can also dismiss the spell as a Major Action.
