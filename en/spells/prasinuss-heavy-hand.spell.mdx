# Prasinus's Heavy Hand

A clenched fist of compacted earth erupts from the ground to clamp around the target.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see within [= 1 stride =] of a point within range">

You choose a [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-square unoccupied space on the ground that you can see within range.
A Medium hand made from compacted soil rises there and reaches for targets.
Targets save Strength, taking [% 2d6 bludgeoning %].
Failing targets are [# kw:condition:restrained #] for the spell's duration.

As a Major Action, you can cause the hand to crush targets. Targets save Strength, halving [% 2d6 bludgeoning %].

To break out, targets can use a Major Action to make a Strength check against 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #].
On a success, targets are no longer [# kw:condition:restrained #].

As a Major Action, you can cause the hand to reach for a different creature or move to a different unoccupied space within range.
The hand releases targets if you do either.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [% 2d6 %] bludgeoning damage.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
