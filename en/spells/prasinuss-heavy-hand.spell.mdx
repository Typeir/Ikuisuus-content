# Prasinus's Heavy Hand

A clenched fist of compacted earth erupts from the ground to clamp around the target.

---

> **Prasinus's Heavy Hand**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within [= 1 stride =] of a point within range  
>
> You choose a [= 1 stride;ADJ =]-square unoccupied space on the ground that you can see within range. A Medium hand made from compacted soil rises there and reaches for targets. Targets save Strength, taking [% 2d6 bludgeoning %]. Failing targets are restrained for the spell's duration.
>
> As a Major Action, you can cause the hand to crush targets. Targets save Strength, halving [% 2d6 bludgeoning %].
>
> To break out, targets can use a Major Action to make a Strength check against 10 plus your [# kw:accuracy #]. On a success, targets are no longer restrained.
>
> As a Major Action, you can cause the hand to reach for a different creature or move to a different unoccupied space within range. The hand releases targets if you do either.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 2d6 %] bludgeoning damage.
