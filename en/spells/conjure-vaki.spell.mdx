# Conjure Väki

You invite an idea to partake on your trials and tribulations, on specific terms, undisclosed for all.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a precursor lodgment — a fired clay figure, a knot of sulfur, a stoppered measure of meltwater — which the parlay consumes)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="[= 18 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You open a parlay, and a **Väki** — an Idea-fragment of the world, of flame or brine or hollow places, takes shape in an unoccupied space you can see within range.
Their combined Challenge Rating may not exceed **twice this spell's level**.
See [Summoning and Conjuration](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/summoning-and-conjuration) for the Challenge Budget, Swarms and Formations, and command.

Never command a Väki; you may only ask, and it obeys because the terms hold. Caprice, not allegiance, governs what answers.

**The bargain that slips.** If your concentration breaks, the Väki is not loosed as other summonings are — it is **freed**, and it cannot be dismissed by you or by any effect that ends a spell.
It remains until it is destroyed or until it tires of the world, hostile to whatever stands nearest, and it remembers who broke faith.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the Challenge Budget is always twice the level of the slot spent.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
