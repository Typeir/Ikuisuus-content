# Festering Edge

A spell that wraps a weapon in a malignant hex, ensuring that every wound it carves refuses to close.

---

> **Festering Edge**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one weapon  
>  
> You touch targets, infusing them with a virulent blood curse. For the duration, targets become wreathed festering red hues.  
>  
> When a creature is **hit** by an attack made with targets, it gains **1 stack of [_Bleeding_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#bleeding)**.
