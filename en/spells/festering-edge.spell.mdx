# Festering Edge

A spell that wraps a weapon in a malignant hex, ensuring that every wound it carves refuses to close.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one weapon">

You touch targets, infusing them with a virulent blood curse. For the duration, targets become wreathed festering red hues.  

When a creature is **hit** by an attack made with targets, it gains **1 stack of [# kw:condition:Bleeding #]**.  

</Spell>
