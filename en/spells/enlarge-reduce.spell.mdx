# Enlarge/Reduce

The target either bulks and stretches as its mass swells, skin taut, or collapses inward into a smaller, denser form.

---

> **Enlarge/Reduce**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of powdered iron)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature or object you can see that is not worn or carried  
>
> Willing targets grow larger or smaller for the duration, as you choose. Unwilling targets save Constitution or undergo the same change.
>
> **Enlarge.** Targets’ size doubles in all dimensions, and their weight is multiplied by eight. This growth increases their size by one [size category](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/movement-terrain-and-traversal), up to Large. If there is not enough room for targets to double in size, they attain the maximum possible size in the space available. Until the spell ends, targets also have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws. Targets’ weapons also grow to match their new size, [multiplying their damage dice](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapons-and-dice-levels) accordingly.
>
> **Reduce.** Targets’ size is halved in all dimensions, and their weight is reduced to one-eighth of normal. This reduction decreases their size by one category—from Medium to Small, for example. Until the spell ends, targets also have disadvantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws. Targets’ weapons also shrink to match their new size.
>
> **Overcast:** the maximum size targets can reach rises with the slot used:
>
> | Slot Level | Maximum Size  |
> | ---------- | ------------- |
> | 2nd        | Large         |
> | 4th        | Huge          |
> | 6th        | Gargantuan    |
> | 8th        | Colossal      |
> | 10th       | Titanic       |
> | 12th       | Inconceivable |
