# Animal Messenger

A small animal materializes at your side and darts away, a faint luminous tether marking its destination.

---

> **Animal Messenger**  
> _2nd-level Spell (Ritual)_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: 24 hours  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one Tiny beast you can see  
>
> By means of this spell, you use an animal to deliver a message.
>
> You specify a location, which you must have visited, and a recipient who matches a general description, such as “a man or woman dressed in the uniform of the town guard” or “a red-haired dwarf wearing a pointed hat.” You also speak a message of up to twenty-five words. Targets travel for the duration of the spell toward the specified location, covering about [= 40 league =] per 24 hours for a flying messenger, or [= 20 league =] for other animals.
>
> When targets arrive, they deliver your message to the creature that you described, replicating the sound of your voice. Targets speak only to a creature matching the description you gave. If targets don’t reach their destination before the spell ends, the message is lost, and targets make their way back to where you cast this spell.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains 48 hours of duration.
