# Continuance

Tissue and bone coalesce inside the sealed vessel, knitting into a lifelike duplicate that stirs with faint pulses.

---

<Spell
  level="8"
  cost="1 Hour"
  components="V, S, M (a diamond worth at least 1,000 gp and at least 1 cubic inch of flesh of the creature that is to be cloned, which the spell consumes; a vessel worth at least 2,000 gp that has a sealable lid and is large enough to hold a creature of the correct size, such as a large urn, coffin, mud-filled cyst in the ground, or crystal container filled with salt water)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one object">

This spell grows a **spare vessel** for a living creature's identity, against the day it needs one.
The body forms inside a sealed container and reaches full size and maturity after 120 days.
It remains inert and keeps indefinitely, so long as the container is not disturbed.

At any time after the vessel's occupant matures, if the original creature dies, the new, assembled thing rises. 
Their creature type becomes _construct (undead)_, and they cannot use Continuance again unless they obtain a different vessel.

The original's remains, if any survive, go inert, and cannot be returned to life or motion in any way, shape or form.

</Spell>
