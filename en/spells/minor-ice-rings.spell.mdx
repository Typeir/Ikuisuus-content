# Minor Ice Rings

A spell of icy restraint; death does not need to chase—only to wait, unmoving.

---

> **Minor Ice Rings**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Targets save Strength or are [restrained](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions) for the spell's duration.  
>  
> While restrained by this spell, targets take [% 1d6 frost %] at the start of each of their turns.  
>  
> Targets can use a Major Action to make a Strength (Athletics) check, ending the effect on a success.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] frost damage.
