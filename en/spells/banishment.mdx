# Banishment

A rift opens onto brine and pressure, and the target is dragged down into it.

---

> **Banishment**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (an item distasteful to targets)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> You try to cast targets out of the world. 
> Targets save Charisma or are banished into the **Muck Dimension** — a microchamber folded into reality, a copy of the drowned layer beneath Plato Station. 
> There they are incapacitated, clawing through the grime, until the spell ends, at which point they reappear in the space they left or the nearest unoccupied one.  
>  
> If targets are native to a different Clone World, they are instead flung back there through the Folds. If the spell lasts the full minute, that return is permanent; they do not come back.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature (targets must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other).  
>  
> **Overcast (9th):** **Sealed**: one target that fails the save does not return when the spell ends. The Muck closes over it, and only it can find its own way back, if it ever does.
