# Steel Wind Strike

Razor-steel blades erupt and sweep outward, slicing through armor and flesh.

---

> **Steel Wind Strike**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
>
> You flourish the weapon used in the spell’s casting and then vanish to strike like the wind. Choose up to five creatures you can see within range. Make a melee spell attack against each target. On a hit, a target takes [% 6d10 force %]. You can then teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within [= 1 stride =] of one of the targets you hit or missed.
>
> **At Higher Levels**: When cast using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, make an additional attack for each level above the 5th.
