# Steel Wind Strike

Razor-steel blades erupt and sweep outward, slicing through armor and flesh.

---

> **Steel Wind Strike**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: up to five creatures you can see within range  
>
> You flourish the weapon used in the spell’s casting and then vanish to strike like the wind.
> Make a melee attack that deals [% 6d10 force %].
> You can then teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within [= 1 stride =] of one of the targets you hit or missed.
>
> **Overcast:** make one additional attack.
