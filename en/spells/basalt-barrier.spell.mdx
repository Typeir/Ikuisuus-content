# Basalt Barrier

Massive slabs of rock thrust upward from the earth and fuse into a stark vertical wall.

---

> **Basalt Barrier**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action, or 1 Reaction, taken as the [barrier](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers) guide dictates  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 30 minutes  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures whose space the wall occupies  
>  
> A mundane wall of solid stone springs into existence at a point you choose within range. 
> The wall is 6 inches thick and is composed of up to ten [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-by-[= 2 stride;ADJ =] panels.
> Each panel must be contiguous with at least one other panel. 
> Alternatively, you can create [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-by-[= 4 stride;ADJ =] panels that are only 3 inches thick.  
>  
> When the wall appears, it pushes targets to one side of your choice.
> If enclosed, targets save Dexterity, moving outside it.  
>  
> The wall can have any shape you desire, though it can’t occupy the same space as a creature or object. 
> The wall doesn’t need to be vertical or rest on any firm foundation. 
> It must, however, merge with and be solidly supported by existing stone. 
> Thus, you can use this spell to bridge a chasm or create a ramp.  
>  
> If you create a span greater than [= 4 stride =] in length, you must halve the thickness to 3 inches. 
> If you create a span greater than [= 8 stride =] in length, the spell fails and the wall doesn’t form.  
>  
> The wall is an object that can be damaged and thus breached. 
> Each panel has AC 15 and 30 hit points per inch of thickness. 
> Reducing a panel to 0 hit points destroys it and might cause connected panels to collapse at the GM’s discretion.  
>  
> If you maintain concentration for the spell's whole duration **and you are still within casting range when it ends**, the wall is left behind as ordinary stone and can no longer be dispelled. 
> Otherwise it disappears when the spell ends.
>  
> **Solid barrier.** Area effects break against it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).  
>  
> **Overcast:** the wall gains two additional panels.
