# Twilight Bolt

A bolt with two faces: dawn's light lodges in the target and marks it, or dusk's shadow settles on its hands and drags them down.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="1 round"
  range="[= 24 stride =]">

Make a ranged spell attack against a creature within range.  

<Choice>

<Feature>

#### Dawn

On a hit, the target takes [% 3d8 holy %], and the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn has advantage, guided by the dim light glittering on it until then.  

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Dusk

On a hit, the target takes [% 3d8 dark %], and it has disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of your next turn.  

</Feature>

</Choice>

**Overcast:** the damage gains [% 1d8 %].  

</Spell>
