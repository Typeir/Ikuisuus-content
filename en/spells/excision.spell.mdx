# Excision

You excise life from the shells of your enemies, leaving but husks behind.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (the powder of a crushed black pearl worth at least 500 gp, required for the 6th-level mode and above)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 30 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see within range">

Rot wells up through targets, draining moisture and vitality from them.
Targets save Constitution, halving [% 8d8 dark %].
This spell has no effect on the undead or constructs.

Targets that are plant creatures or magical plants save with disadvantage and take maximum damage.
A mundane plant that isn't a creature simply withers and dies.

**Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.

**Overcast (6th+):** **The Hollow.**  
**Targets**: creatures within a [= 8 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point within range  
Targets save Constitution, halving [% 8d8 dark %].
The corpses of creatures slain in the area are hollowed out, leaving a crumbling husk behind; these corpses cannot be reanimated or healed by any means.

**Overcast (8th+):** **Finality.**  
**Targets**: creatures within a [= 8 stride;ADJ =] cube of momentary, impenetrable darkness centered on a point within range  
Targets save Constitution, halving [% 10d8 dark %].
Aberrations, Monstrosities, and Celestials take maximum damage from it.
Corpses suffer the same fate as in **The Hollow**.

</Spell>
