# Excision

You excise life from the shells of your enemies, leaving but husks behind.

---

> **Excision**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (the powder of a crushed black pearl worth at least 500 gp, required for the 6th-level mode and above)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 30 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see within range  
>
> Rot wells up through targets, draining moisture and vitality from them. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 8d8 dark %]. This spell has no effect on the undead or constructs.
>
> Targets that are plant creatures or magical plants save with disadvantage and take maximum damage. A mundane plant that isn't a creature simply withers and dies.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
>
> **Overcast (6th+) (per +1):** the excision instead fills a **[= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere** centered on a point within range. Creatures in the sphere save Constitution, halving [% 8d6 dark %]. The corpses of creatures slain in the area are scoured — they cannot be raised as undead. The spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
>
> **Overcast (8th+) (per +1):** the excision instead fills a **[= 6 stride;ADJ =] cube** of momentary, impenetrable darkness centered on a point within range. Creatures in the cube save Constitution, halving [% 12d8 dark %]. Aberrations, Monstrosities, and Celestials take maximum damage from it. The spell gains [% 1d8 %] damage.
