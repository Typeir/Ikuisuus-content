# Tsunami

A towering wall of churning water surges outward, shattering structures and sweeping everything in its path.

---

<Spell
  level="8"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 6 rounds"
  range="Sight">

A wall of water springs into existence at a point you choose within range.
You can make the wall up to [= 60 stride =] long, [= 60 stride =] high, and [= 10 stride =] thick.
The wall lasts for the duration.  

When the wall appears, each creature within its area saves Strength.
On a failed save, a creature takes [% 6d10 bludgeoning %], or half as much damage on a successful save.  

At the start of each of your turns after the wall appears, the wall, along with any creatures in it, moves [= 10 stride =] away from you.
Any Huge or smaller creature inside the wall or whose space the wall enters when it moves saves Strength or takes [% 5d10 bludgeoning %].
A creature can take this damage only once per round.
At the end of the turn, the wall’s height is reduced by [= 10 stride =], and the damage creatures take from the spell on subsequent rounds is reduced by [% 1d10 %].
When the wall reaches [= 0 stride =] in height, the spell ends.  

A creature caught in the wall can move by swimming.
Because of the force of the wave, the creature must spend [= 4/5 stride =] of movement for every [= 1/5 stride =] it moves.  

</Spell>
