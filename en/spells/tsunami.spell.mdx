# Tsunami

A towering wall of churning water surges outward, shattering structures and sweeping everything in its path.

---

> **Tsunami**  
> _8th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 6 rounds  
> **Range**: Sight  
>  
>  
> A wall of water springs into existence at a point you choose within range. You can make the wall up to [= 60 stride =] long, [= 60 stride =] high, and [= 10 stride =] thick. The wall lasts for the duration.  
>  
> When the wall appears, each creature within its area must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes [% 6d10 bludgeoning %], or half as much damage on a successful save.  
>  
> At the start of each of your turns after the wall appears, the wall, along with any creatures in it, moves [= 10 stride =] away from you. Any Huge or smaller creature inside the wall or whose space the wall enters when it moves must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take [% 5d10 bludgeoning %]. A creature can take this damage only once per round. At the end of the turn, the wall’s height is reduced by [= 10 stride =], and the damage creatures take from the spell on subsequent rounds is reduced by [% 1d10 %]. When the wall reaches [= 0 stride =] in height, the spell ends.  
>  
> A creature caught in the wall can move by swimming. Because of the force of the wave, the creature must spend [= 4/5 stride =] of movement for every [= 1/5 stride =] it moves.
