# Ashen Annointment

Perform an attack akin to a cremation rite on a creature within range, marking them as about to die or deceased.

---

> **Ashen Annointment**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 1 round  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature  
>
> A spout of ash and ghastly blue flame swirls around targets. 
> Make a ranged attack that deals [% 1d8 force %] and marks targets with ash, so they can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn.
>
> If you leave targets doomed, they also have disadvantage on attacks against you until the end of your next turn.
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage increases by [% 1d8 %] at each step.
