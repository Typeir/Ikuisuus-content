# Invoke Agony

There are words the body was never given the right to refuse. This is one of them; it does not argue with the flesh, it overrules it.

---

> **Invoke Agony**  
> _7th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Choose **Agony**; or, if you are casting this spell with a slot of 8th level or higher, you may instead choose **Rigor**.  
>  
> **Agony.** If targets have **100 hit points or fewer**, they save Wisdom or are **incapacitated** by pain; otherwise the spell has no effect. They can resist Wisdom at the end of each of their turns and whenever they take damage.  
>  
> **Rigor.** If targets have **150 hit points or fewer**, they are **stunned** — the word itself allows no saving throw to resist. Otherwise the spell has no effect. They can resist Constitution at the end of each of their turns.  
>  
> **Overcast: Agony.** the threshold gains [= 20 hit points =].  
>  
> **Overcast (9th+): Rigor.** the threshold gains [= 20 hit points =].
