# Breeze Barrier

A vertical wall of roaring wind materializes, its gusts shredding loose objects and battering flesh.

---

> **Breeze Barrier**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action or 1 Reaction  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a fan of stiffened feathers)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the wall  
>
> A wall of strong wind rises from the ground at a point you choose within range. You can make the wall up to [= 10 stride =] long, [= 3 stride =] high, and [= 1/5 stride =] thick, shaped however you like so long as it makes one continuous path along the ground. The wall lasts for the duration.
>
> When the wall appears, creatures in the wall save Strength, halving [% 3d8 bludgeoning %].
>
> The wind keeps fog, smoke, and other gases at bay. **The wall's space is difficult terrain for every flying creature**, whatever its size, and loose material carried into the wall flies upward.
>
> **Deflection.** A ranged projectile weighing **[= 3 burden =] or less** that is launched at targets behind the wall is thrown upward and automatically misses. Heavier projectiles — boulders from giants, siege ammunition — pass through unaffected, **as do firearm shots**, which are moving far too fast for moving air to argue with.
>
> **Energy barrier.** Area effects pass straight through it. See [Barriers](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/barriers).
>
> **Overcast:** the wall gains [% 1d8 %] damage and [= 1 stride =] of height.
