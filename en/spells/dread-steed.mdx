# Dread Steed

A spell that assembles a temporary mount from a carcass and its own severed bones, forced into motion by binding magic and momentum.
The steed is neither illusion nor resurrection: its flesh is inert, its bones suspended and locked into place only while the spell endures.
When the magic fades, the structure collapses, leaving nothing but scattered remains.

---

> **Dread Steed**  
> _3rd-level Spell (Ritual)_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 minute  
> **Components**: V, S, M (the corpse of a Large quadruped or a similar creature)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one corpse of a Large quadruped or a similar creature  
>
> Targets become a Large skeletal mount, assembled in an unoccupied space you can see within range.  
> The steed’s bones lift free and lock into a running configuration, held together by binding force rather than life.
>
> For the duration, you or a creature you choose can ride the steed.  
> The steed uses the statistics of a **riding horse**, except it has a speed of **[= 14 stride =]** and can travel **[= 6 league =] in an hour**, or **[= 7 league =]** at a fast pace, and has 10 hit points.  
> The steed is immune to poison and psychic damage, as well as the poisoned, and frightened conditions.
>
> Any saddle, reins, or tack formed as part of the steed collapse into loose bone fragments if carried more than **[= 2 stride =]** away from it.
>
> **Trample.**  
> While mounted, you may use your **Major Action** to force the steed to surge forward up to **[= 4 stride =]** in a straight line through a creature.  
> That creature saves Strength or takes **[% 4d8 bludgeoning %]** and is **knocked prone**.
>
> After using Trample, you immediately **dismount**, landing in an unoccupied space within **[= 1 stride =]** of that creature, and the steed **collapses and falls apart**, ending the spell.
>
> When the spell ends normally, the steed loses cohesion over **1 minute**, allowing its rider time to dismount safely before the bones scatter.  
> The spell also ends if you use a Major Action to dismiss it or if the steed dies.
