# Denial

Grief need not come if the first stage becomes realized, you ward against the very end with an invisible field of energy. The very folds are the blanket of your deathbed.

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="24 hours"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature you touch">

Targets gain a measure of protection from death.  

The first time targets would drop to 0 hit points as a result of taking damage, they instead drop to 1 hit point, and the spell ends.  

If the spell is still in effect when targets are subjected to an effect that would kill them instantaneously without dealing damage (such as Thought of Oblivion), that effect is instead negated against them, and the spell ends.  

</Spell>
