# Dawn

A column of light stands where you put it and does not go out. Whether it merely lights the room or scours it depends entirely on how much you were willing to spend.

---

> **Dawn**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a sunburst of beaten gold worth at least 100 gp)  
> **Duration**: 4 hours  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within a [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centred on a point you choose within range  
>
> A [= 12 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere of light spreads from a point you choose within range. The sphere is bright light and sheds dim light for a further [= 12 stride =]. **This light is sunlight** — see [Vision, Light, and Environment](/en/library/rules/the-long-road/vision-light-and-environment).
>
> The light spreads around corners. If you choose a point on an object you are holding, or one not being worn or carried, the light emanates from that object and travels with it. Covering the source completely with something opaque blocks the light.
>
> **Where this light overlaps darkness created by a spell of this spell's level or lower, that darkness is dispelled.**
>
> **Overcast:** the light gains [= 12 stride =] of radius, and dispels magical darkness created by a spell of the slot's level or lower.
>
> **Overcast (5th+):** you may instead call the light down as a burning column: a [= 6 stride;ADJ =]-radius, [= 8 stride;ADJ =]-high cylinder, the spell becoming **Concentration, up to 1 minute**. When the column appears and as an [_incipient effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) thereafter, creatures in the cylinder save Constitution, halving [% 4d10 holy %]. While within [= 12 stride =] of the column you can move it up to [= 12 stride =] as a **Minor Action**. The column gains [% 1d10 %] damage for each slot level above 5th.
