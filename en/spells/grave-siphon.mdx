# Grave Siphon

An incantation that finds itself in the repertoire of most who walk the thin line between life and death.
It seems that to take from the dead is to cling longer to the living.

---

> **Grave Siphon**  
> _1st-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Minor Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self ([= 3 stride;ADJ =] cone)  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the cone  
>  
> You release a current of deathly energy. Targets save Constitution, halving [% 1d8 dark %].  
>  
> You gain **temporary hit points equal to half the total dark damage dealt** by this spell (rounded down).  
>  
> **Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] dark damage.
