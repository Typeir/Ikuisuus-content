# Divination

The offering goes onto the fire and the pyre belches smoke in the shape of prophecy.

---

> **Divination**  
> _4th-level Spell (Ritual)_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (incense and an offering appropriate to your god, together worth at least 25 gp, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: Self
>
> You burn the offering and put a question to your god, patron or chorus.
> Ask one question concerning a specific goal, event, or activity to occur within the next 7 days, and you receive a truthful reply.
> It may come as a short phrase, a cryptic rhyme, or an omen.
>
> The answer accounts for nothing that has not happened yet.
> A spell cast later, a companion gained or lost, a mind changed on the road, what is said is done. 
>
> If you cast this spell two or more times before finishing your next [# kw:Recovery #], the GM will roll a [RR] with your **spellcasting ability modifier**, with an escalating dc of 5 per casting. if it fails, the reading comes back random instead.
> That roll is made in secret.
