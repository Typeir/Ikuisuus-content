# Hallucinatory Terrain

The ground shifts into convincing false cliffs, marshes, and paths that appear real.

---

> **Hallucinatory Terrain**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 10 Minutes  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: 24 hours  
> **Range**: [= 60 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the area  
>  
> You make natural terrain in a [= 30 stride;ADJ =] cube in range look, sound, and smell like some other sort of natural terrain. Thus, open fields or a road can be made to resemble a swamp, hill, crevasse, or some other difficult or impassable terrain. A pond can be made to seem like a grassy meadow, a precipice like a gentle slope, or a rock-strewn gully like a wide and smooth road. Manufactured structures, equipment, and targets aren’t changed in appearance.  
>  
> The tactile characteristics of the terrain are unchanged, so targets entering the area are likely to see through the illusion. If the difference isn’t obvious by touch, targets carefully examining the illusion can attempt an Intelligence (Investigation) check against accuracy to disbelieve it. Targets who discern the illusion see it as a vague image superimposed on the terrain.
