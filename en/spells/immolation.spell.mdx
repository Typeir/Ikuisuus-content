# Immolation

A pillar of fire erupts, incinerating matter into smoking ash.

---

> **Immolation**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see in range  
>  
> Targets save Dexterity, halving [% 8d6 fire %]. Failing targets also burn for the spell’s duration, shedding bright light in a [= 6 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 6 stride =]. They can resist Dexterity at the end of each of their turns, taking [% 4d6 fire %] on a failure and ending the spell on a success. These magical flames can’t be extinguished by mundane means. Fire damage from this spell turns targets it kills to ash.  
>  
> **Overcast (6th+):** the spell gains [% 1d6 %] damage.
