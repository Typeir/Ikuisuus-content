# Forcecage

A transparent cage of humming force snaps into being, sealing and holding everything within.

---

> **Forcecage**  
> _7th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: 1 hour  
> **Range**: [= 20 stride =]  
> **Targets**: Creatures within the cage  
>
> An immobile, invisible, cube-shaped prison composed of magical force springs into existence around an area you choose within range.
>
> You can choose either a cage or a box:
>
> **Cage.** The cage can be up to [= 4 stride =] on a side, made from 1/2-inch bars spaced 1/2 inch apart.
>
> **Box.** The box can be up to [= 2 stride =] on a side, creating a solid barrier.
>
> The prison can be oriented in any way you choose as long as it is anchored on a solid surface. The prison remains for the duration.
>
> When you cast the spell, targets completely inside the cage's area are trapped. Targets only partially within the area, or too large to fit inside, are pushed away from the center until they are completely outside the area.
>
> Targets inside the cage can't leave it by mundane means. If they try to leave by folding space, or by any passage between Folds of the world, they save Charisma or are refused, and the attempt is wasted. The cage is closed across the Folds as well as across the room.
>
> The cage is **immune to all damage except force**. It has **100 hit points** and a **damage threshold of 30**; any single source of force damage that rolls 30 or more before threshold application pushes through. If the cage is reduced to 0 hit points or its threshold is beaten, it shatters and the spell ends.
>
> A [_Objection_](/en/library/spells/objection) spell cast at 7th level or higher ends the cage. A [_Objection_](/en/library/spells/objection) cast at a lower slot level forces the caster to make an accuracy check; on a success, the cage shatters.
