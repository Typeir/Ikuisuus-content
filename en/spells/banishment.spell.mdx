# Banishment

A rift opens onto brine and pressure, and the target is dragged down into it.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (an item distasteful to targets)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="[= 12 stride =]"
  targets="one creature you can see">

You try to cast targets out of the world. 
Targets save Charisma or are banished into the **Muck Dimension** — a microchamber folded into reality, a copy of the drowned layer beneath Plato Station. 
There they are [# kw:condition:incapacitated #], clawing through the grime, until the spell ends, at which point they reappear in the space they left or the nearest unoccupied one.  

If targets are native to a different Clone World, they are instead flung back there through the Folds.
If the spell lasts the full minute, that return is permanent; they do not come back.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** target one additional creature (targets must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other).  

<Feature targets="one banished creature that fails its save">

#### Sealed <span>9th</span>

The target does not return when the spell ends.
The Muck closes over it, and only it can find its own way back, if it ever does.  

</Feature>
</Overcast>

</Spell>
