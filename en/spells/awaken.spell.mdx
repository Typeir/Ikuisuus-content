# Awaken

Luminous filaments crawl over the target, rearranging tissues and sparking sudden, coherent awareness.

---

> **Awaken**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 8 Hours  
> **Components**: V, S, M (an agate worth at least 1,000 gp, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one Huge or smaller creature of the wildlife type  
>
> After spending the casting time tracing pathways into a precious gemstone, you touch targets. Targets must have either no Intelligence score or an Intelligence of 3 or less.
>
> Targets gain an Intelligence of 10 and the ability to speak one language you know. 
> Rooted targets — wildlife (plant) and the like — also gain the ability to move their limbs, roots, vines and creepers, and gain senses comparable to a human's; The GM shall give them statistics appropriate to what they have become.
>
> Awakened targets are charmed by you for 30 days, or until you or your companions do anything harmful to them. When the charmed condition ends, awakened targets choose whether to remain friendly to you, based on how you treated them while they were charmed.
>
> **Overcast:** wake one additional creature, touching each in turn over the casting time. The agate is consumed regardless of how many you wake.
