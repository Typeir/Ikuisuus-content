# Pass Without Trace

The spell does not erase you. It borrows from the world's latent indifference — the quality of being unremarkable, unrecorded, and unmemorable.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (ashes from a burned leaf of mistletoe and a sprig of spruce)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Self"
  targets="creatures you choose within [= 6 stride =] of you, including you">

A veil of shadows and silence radiates from you, masking you and your companions from detection.  

Targets gain a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks and can’t be tracked except by magical means for the duration.
Targets leave behind no tracks or other traces of their passage.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell gains [= 3 stride =] to its radius.  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
