# Chill

A shard of cold energy streaks from your hand, frosting and cracking whatever it strikes.

---

> **Chill**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature  
>  
>  
> A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks toward targets.  
>  
> - If it's targeted against a creature, make a ranged attack that deals [% 1d8 frost %] and reduces their speed by [= 2 stride =] until the start of your next turn. **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the damage increases by [% 1d8 %] at each step.  
> 
> - If it's targeted against water without any creature on it, freeze a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube of it, and it remains frozen for an hour. You can have no more than two of these areas active at a time, and you can dismiss them as a Major Action.
>   
> - If it's targeted against a fire that fits within a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube, it is extinguished.
