# Invoke Agony

To live is to carry the instict to do one thing, and one thing alone. To writhe in agony until life depletes itself.

> **Invoke Agony**  
> _7th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V (The word "Writhe", spoken in a tongue the target can understand), S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Choose **Agony**; or, if you are casting this spell with a slot of 8th level or higher, you may instead choose **Rigor**.  
>  
> **Agony.** If targets have **100 hit points or fewer**, they save Wisdom or are **[# kw:condition:incapacitated #]** by pain; otherwise the spell has no effect.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Wisdom. They can [# kw:resist #] again whenever they take damage.  
>  
> **Rigor.** If targets have **150 hit points or fewer**, they are **[# kw:condition:stunned #]**.
> Otherwise the spell has no effect.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution.  
>  
> **Overcast: Agony.** the threshold gains [= 20 hit points =].  
>  
> **Overcast (9th+): Rigor.** the threshold gains [= 20 hit points =].
