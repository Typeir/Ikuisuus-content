# Beast Bond

A quiet tether snaps to the animal, and for a while you look out through borrowed eyes.

---

> **Beast Bond**  
> _1st-level Spell (Ritual)_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a bit of fur wrapped in a cloth)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing beast you touch  
>  
> You establish a telepathic link with targets. The spell fails if targets' Intelligence is 4 or higher. Until the spell ends, you can communicate with targets telepathically while within [= 1 league =] of them.  
>  
> As a Major Action, you can perceive through the beast's senses — seeing through its eyes and hearing what it hears — until you use a Major Action to return to your own. While doing so, you gain the benefits of any special senses the beast has, but you are blinded and deafened to your own surroundings.  
>  
> **Overcast:** bond with one additional beast, and shift your perception among any bonded beasts.
