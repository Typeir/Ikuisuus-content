# Asphyxiate

A spell that manifests a pitch-black, opaque hand around a creature’s throat, crushing breath and will alike until it breaks free or collapses.

---

> **Asphyxiate**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> You conjure a **featureless, pitch-black hand** that closes around the throat and torso of targets.  
>  
> Targets save Strength or are **[# kw:condition:restrained #]** and **[silenced](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#silenced)** for the duration, the shadowy hand visibly constricting its body.
> They can [# kw:resist #] Strength.  
>  
> While [# kw:condition:restrained #] by this spell, targets **cannot breathe**.  
>  
> As an [# kw:incipient #] effect, targets still [# kw:condition:restrained #] by Asphyxiate save Constitution or are [# kw:condition:stunned #] [# kw:briefly #].  
>  
> As an [# kw:incipient #] effect, targets [# kw:condition:stunned #] by this spell take [% 6d10 dark %] as the hand tightens further.  
>  
> If targets are ever more than [= 6 stride =] away from you, the shadowy hand dissipates and the spell immediately ends.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the hand gains [% 1d10 %] dark damage.
