# Leaping Fire

Flames run along your weapon and lashes outward in a snapping, biting arc.

---

> **Leaping Fire**
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: S, M (a melee weapon worth at least 1 sp)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: one creature within [= 1 stride =] of you  
>
> You harness the power of fire within yourself:
>
> - You brandish a weapon and make a melee attack with it against one creature within [= 1 stride =] of you, dealing the weapon attack’s normal effects. You can then cause fire to leap from that creature to a different creature that you can see within [= 1 stride =] of it.
>   The second creature takes fire damage equal to your [# kw:damage bonus #].
> - You instantaneously expand an already burning flame within [= 6 stride =], [= 1 stride =] in any direction, provided that wood or other fuel is present in the new location.
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** the spell gains [% 1d8 %] on each damage roll at each step.
