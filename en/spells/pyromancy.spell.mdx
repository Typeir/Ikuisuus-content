# Pyromancy

You harness the swirling embers into a speck of pure flame, the thought of incineration.

> **Pyromancy**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 10 minutes  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: —  
>
> A flickering flame appears in your hand. The flame remains there for the duration and harms neither you nor your equipment.
> The flame sheds bright light in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 2 stride =]. The spell ends if you dismiss it as a Major Action or if you cast it again.
>
> As a Major Action while holding the flame, you can set it upon a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] square of ground within [= 4 stride =] to create a bonfire.
> Until the spell ends, the bonfire fills a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube in that space. As a [_persistent, delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures in the bonfire's space save Dexterity or are [burning](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning).
> The bonfire ignites flammable objects in its space that aren't being worn or carried. You can have only one bonfire at a time; creating another extinguishes the first. As a Minor Action, you can recall the flame to your hand.
>
> When you cast this spell, or as a Major Action on a later turn, you can hurl the flame at a creature within [= 4 stride =] of you. Make a ranged attack against that creature.
> That creature saves Dexterity or is [burning](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning).
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** a hurled flame that hits also deals [% 1d8 fire %] regardless of the save, increasing by [% 1d8 %] at each step. The bonfire never deals damage directly.
