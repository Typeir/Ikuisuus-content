# Pyromancy

You harness the swirling embers into a speck of pure flame, the thought of incineration.

> **Pyromancy**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: 10 minutes  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Targets**: —  
>
> A flickering flame appears in your hand.
>
> **The Light.** The flame remains in your hand for the duration and harms neither you nor your equipment.
> It sheds bright light in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 2 stride =].
> The spell ends if you dismiss it as a Major Action or if you cast it again.
>
> **The Bonfire.**
> **Targets**: creatures in the bonfire's space
> As a Major Action while holding the flame, you can set it upon a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] square of ground within [= 4 stride =], where it fills a [= 1 stride;ADJ =] cube until the spell ends.
> As a [_persistent, delayed effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), targets save Dexterity or are [burning](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning).
> You can have only one bonfire at a time; creating another extinguishes the first.
> As a Minor Action, you can recall the flame to your hand.
>
> **The Hurl.**
> **Targets**: one creature within [= 4 stride =] of you
> When you cast this spell, or as a Major Action on a later turn, you can hurl the flame at targets.
> Make a ranged attack that causes targets to save Dexterity or gain the [burning](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#burning) condition.
>
> **Progression (5th/11th/17th):** **The Hurl** gains [% 1d8 fire %] at each step, regardless of the save.
