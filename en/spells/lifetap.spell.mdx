# Lifetap

A brutal exchange of vitality, siphoning strength from a foe and converting it into raw momentum.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Melee"
  targets="one creature within range">

Make a **melee attack** that deals **[% 3d4 dark %]**. You regain **hit points equal to the damage dealt**.  

Until the end of your next turn, you gain an accuracy bonus on **the next attack you make** equal to **half the damage dealt** by this spell, rounded down.  

**Overcast:** the spell gains [% 1d4 %] damage.  

</Spell>
