# Conjure Celestial

You point at a seam in the divine plan, and the gears shift into a shape; It performs the shape of help with the patience of a machine that has done this a great many times.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M (a mirror-polished disc of tombsteel, held up to nothing)"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="[= 18 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You call an **agent of the Canvas** into an unoccupied space you can see within range — an unknowable piece of divine procedure.
Its Challenge Rating, or the combined rating of what answers, may not exceed **twice this spell's level**.
See [Summoning and Conjuration](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/summoning-and-conjuration) for the Challenge Budget, Swarms and Formations, command, and loss of control.  

Celestials are the hardest things in the world to call and the hardest to send away, which is why this spell sits where it does and not lower.  

**The one order it will not take.** A celestial cannot be commanded to act against its alignment.
In practice this seldom binds — most celestials are unaligned servants of the world's uncaring machinery and will do very nearly anything with the same flat courtesy — but the exceptional aligned ones refuse, and are essence-bound enough that any detection worth the name will tell you which you have called.  

**Overcast:** the Challenge Budget is always twice the level of the slot spent.  

</Spell>
