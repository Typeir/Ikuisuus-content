# Focus

Clear the mind of superfluous thought and sharpen the wit and will.

---

> **Focus**  
> _Cantrip_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature you touch  
> 
> Touch a willing creature. If you aren't the creature touched, close your eyes while concentrating.
> 
> Once before the spell ends, targets can roll a [% 1d4 %] and add the number rolled to one check or saving throw. They can roll the die before or after making the ability check. The spell then ends.
