# Focus

Clear the mind of superfluous thought and sharpen the wit and will.

---

<Spell

  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one willing creature you touch">

Touch a willing creature. If you aren't the creature touched, close your eyes while concentrating.

Once before the spell ends, targets can sway a bonus of [% 1d4 %] to one check or saving throw.
They may also cast the die with the roll instead, as a bonus die.
The spell then ends.
