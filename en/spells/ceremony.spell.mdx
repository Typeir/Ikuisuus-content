# Ceremony

A pale sigil blooms at the touch and razor-fine filaments of light snap outward to bind and seal skin.

---

> **Ceremony**  
> _1st-level Spell (Ritual)_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (grave-ash and powdered silver worth 25 gp, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: creatures and objects within [= 2 stride =] of you, throughout the casting  
>
> You perform one of the old rites in whatever form your faith gives it. The words, the tools and the mess involved vary enormously from one god to the next, and the spell does not care which of them you are keeping. Choose one rite when you cast it.
>
> > ### Atonement
> >
> > **Targets**: one willing creature whose [alignment](/en/library/rules/the-measure-of-the-self/alignment) has been shifted by sustained contrary behaviour  
> > You touch targets and make a DC 20 Wisdom (Insight) check. On a success, you reverse that shift by one step. Where targets have also severed a bond to a granted Key, this rite is the one that mends it.
>
> > ### Bless Water
> >
> > **Targets**: one vial of water  
> > You touch targets and make holy water of them.
>
> > ### Coming of Age
> >
> > **Targets**: one humanoid who is a young adult  
> > For the next 24 hours, targets gain a bonus [% 1d4 %] to checks. A creature can benefit from this rite only once.
>
> > ### Dedication
> >
> > **Targets**: one humanoid who wishes to be dedicated to the service of your god  
> > For the next 24 hours, targets gain a bonus [% 1d4 %] to saves. A creature can benefit from this rite only once.
>
> > ### Funeral Rite
> >
> > **Targets**: one corpse  
> > For the next 7 days nothing can animate targets — no rite, no spell, no accident of the Fold will make a construct of targets.
>
> > ### Investiture
> >
> > **Targets**: one willing creature  
> > For the next 24 hours, targets gain a bonus [% 1d4 %] to attacks. A creature can benefit from this rite only once.
>
> > ### Wedding
> >
> > **Targets**: adult humanoids willing to be bound together in marriage  
> > For the next 7 days, each gains a +2 bonus to AC while within [= 6 stride =] of its spouse. A creature can benefit from this rite only once.
>
> **Overcast:** you perform one additional rite, each binding its own targets. The component is consumed once, however long the ceremony runs.
