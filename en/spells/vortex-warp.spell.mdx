# Vortex Warp

You fold the space a creature stands in, and it is standing somewhere else — a corner conducting it out and away.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 18 stride =]">

You fold the space around another creature you can see within range.
The target saves Constitution or is teleported to an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within range.
The chosen space must be on a surface or in a liquid that can support the target without squeezing.  

**At Higher Levels.** The reach and the count grow with the slot:  

| Slot Level | Targets            | Range   |
| ---------- | ------------------ | ------- |
| 2nd        | 1                  | [= 18 stride =]  |
| 3rd        | 2                  | [= 24 stride =] |
| 4th        | 3                  | [= 30 stride =] |
| 5th        | 4                  | [= 40 stride =] |
| 6th        | 5                  | [= 60 stride =] |

All chosen creatures are warped at once; each unwilling creature saves separately.
The destination spaces must all be within the spell's range and visible to you.  

</Spell>
