# Convulsion

Invisible filaments seize the target's nervous system, wrenching limbs into violent, uncontrollable spasms.

---

> **Convulsion**  
> _6th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> Targets save Constitution or are wracked by violent convulsions for the duration: limbs jerking, body seizing, muscles locked in spasm. Something in the flesh has been misread, and the flesh is doing its best to comply.  
>  
> A convulsing creature must use all its movement to thrash without leaving its space, and has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws and attack rolls. Other creatures have advantage on attack rolls against it.  
>  
> As a **Major Action** — its whole turn's worth — a convulsing creature can make a Wisdom saving throw to take itself back. On a success, the spell ends.  
>  
> **Overcast:** affect one additional creature. Each saves separately, and each must buy its own way out.
