# Nondetection

A matte veil settles over the target, dulling features and blurring perception of them.

---

> **Nondetection**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of diamond dust worth 25 gp sprinkled over targets, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration**: 24 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: a willing creature, place, or object no larger than [= 2 stride =] in any dimension, that you touch  
>  
> For the duration, targets can't be targeted by any divination magic or perceived through magical scrying sensors.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature, object, or place.
