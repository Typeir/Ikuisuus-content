# Slow

Time thickens around the target, making motion stutter and limbs move sluggishly.

---

> **Slow**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 24 stride =]  
> **Targets**: up to six creatures in a [= 8 stride;ADJ =] cube within range  
>
> Targets save Wisdom or are **slowed** for the spell's duration. They can [# kw:resist #] Wisdom.
>
> **Overcast:** affect two additional creatures.
