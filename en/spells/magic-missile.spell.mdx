# Magic Missile

Luminous darts of force streak from your hand, striking with compact, sizzling impact.

---

<Spell
  level="1"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="one or more creatures you can see in range">

You create three glowing darts of magical force that strike simultaneously.
You can direct them to hit one target or several, each dart dealing [% 1d4 +1 force %].  

**Overcast:** the spell gains one more dart.  

</Spell>
