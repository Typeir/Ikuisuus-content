# Guardian of Nature

Gnarled roots and tough bark surge up, weaving into a rigid, breathing shell around the subject.

---

<Spell
  level="4"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="you">

A nature spirit answers your call and transforms you into a powerful guardian.  

You call upon the spirits of nature to transform yourself into a powerful guardian.  

<Choice>

<Feature>

#### Primal Beast

Bestial fur covers your body and your facial features become feral.  
- Your walking speed increases by [= 2 stride =].  
- You gain darkvision with a range of [= 24 stride =].  
- You make Strength-based attack rolls with advantage.  
- When you hit with a melee weapon attack, that target takes an extra [% 1d6 force %].  

</Feature>

<Feature>

#### Great Tree

Your skin appears barky and leaves sprout from your hair.  
- You gain 10 temporary hit points.  
- You make Constitution saving throws with advantage.  
- You make Dexterity- and Wisdom-based attack rolls with advantage.  
- While you are on the ground, the ground within [= 3 stride =] of you is difficult terrain for your enemies.  

</Feature>

</Choice>


</Spell>
