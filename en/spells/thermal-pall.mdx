# Thermal Pall

The elements form a buffer between you and the world, both preventing and causing harm as you see fit.

> **Thermal Pall**  
> _4th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: Self  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a bit of phosphorous or a firefly)  
> **Duration**: 1 hour
>
> Thin and wispy flames wreathe your body for the duration, shedding bright light in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius and dim light for an additional [= 2 stride =]. You can end the spell early by using a Major Action to dismiss it.
>
> The flames provide you with a warm shield or a chill shield, as you choose, and you may change it as a Minor Action. The warm shield grants you resistance to frost damage, and the chill shield grants you resistance to fire damage.
>
> In addition, whenever a creature within [= 1 stride =] of you hits you with a melee attack, the shield erupts with flame. The attacker takes [% 2d8 fire %] from a warm shield, or [% 2d8 frost %] from a chill shield.
>
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the retaliation damage increases by [% 1d8 %] for each slot level above 4th.
