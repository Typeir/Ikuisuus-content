# Sulfurous Sphere

This spell is not innate to any vocation, and can only be learnt through a pact with the lord of brimstone.

> “A globe of swirling, sulfurous fumes. The very image of the world the lord of the brume envisioned.”

---

> **Sulfurous Sphere**  
> _3rd-Level Conjuration_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Range**: [= 12 stride =]  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a lump of brimstone and a drop of rancid oil)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute
>
> You conjure a **[= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere** of swirling sulfurous vapor, centered on a point you can see within range. The sphere hovers in place and can be moved up to **[= 4 stride =] as a Minor Action** on your turn.
>
> As a [_persistent effect_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements), creatures make a **Constitution saving throw**. On a failed save:
>
> - They take **[% 1d10 fire %]** and **[% 1d10 chemical %]**.
> - They are **blinded** and their **speed is halved** until the end of its next turn.
>
> On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and suffers no conditions.
>
> The sphere heavily obscures its interior.
>
> **At Higher Levels**: When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, increase both the fire and chemical damage by **[% 1d10 %]** for each slot level above 3rd.
