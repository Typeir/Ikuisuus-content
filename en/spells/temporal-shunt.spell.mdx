# Temporal Shunt

The target vanishes in a stuttering blur and reappears moments later as time collapses around them.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Reaction, taken when a creature you can see makes an attack roll or casts a spell"
  components="V, S"
  duration="1 round"
  range="[= 24 stride =]"
  targets="the triggering creature">

You throw targets a little way into the future — their attack or their spell goes with them, and neither arrives.
Targets save Wisdom or vanish, reappearing in their space at the start of their next turn.
While they are gone, they are [# kw:condition:incapacitated #] and perceive nothing.
On their return they are disoriented and **[# kw:condition:staggered #]** [# kw:briefly #].  

**Overcast:** affect one additional creature involved in the same trigger.  

</Spell>
