# Blindness/Deafness

Blackness floods the target's sight, or a hollow silence swallows their hearing.

---

> **Blindness/Deafness**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature you can see  
>  
> You can blind or deafen a foe. 
> Targets save Constitution or are [blinded](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#blinded) or [deafened](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#deafened) (your choice) for the duration. They can [# kw:resist #] Constitution.  
>  
> **Overcast:** target one additional creature (targets must be within [= 6 stride =] of each other).
> **Overcast (4th):** the effect becomes indefinite until targets [# kw:resist #].
