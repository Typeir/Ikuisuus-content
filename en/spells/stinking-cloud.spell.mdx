# Stinking Cloud

A rancid, choking cloud billows outward, souring the air and clawing at the lungs.

---

> **Stinking Cloud**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a rotten egg or several skunk cabbage leaves)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
>  
>  
> You create a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere of yellow, nauseating gas centered on a point within range. The cloud spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. The cloud lingers in the air for the duration.  
>  
> Each creature completely within the cloud at the start of its turn must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save it spends its Major Action that turn retching and reeling. A creature that does not need to breathe, or that is immune to the **poisoned** condition, succeeds automatically.  
>  
> A moderate wind (at least [= 8 league =] per hour) disperses the cloud after 4 rounds. A strong wind (at least [= 16 league =] per hour) disperses it after 1 round.  
>  
> **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the radius increases by [= 2 stride =] for each slot level above 3rd.
