# Stinking Cloud

A rancid, choking cloud billows outward, souring the air and clawing at the lungs.

---

> **Stinking Cloud**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (a rotten egg or several skunk cabbage leaves)  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: creatures completely within a [= 4 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point within range  
>
> A cloud of yellow, nauseating gas fills the sphere.
> It spreads around corners, heavily obscures its area, and lingers there for the duration.
>
> As an [# kw:incipient #] effect, targets save Constitution or spend their Major Action retching and reeling.
> Creatures that don't need to breathe, or that are immune to the [# kw:condition:poisoned #] condition, succeed automatically.
>
> A moderate wind (at least [= 8 league =] per hour) disperses the cloud after 4 rounds.
> A strong wind (at least [= 16 league =] per hour) disperses it after 1 round.
>
> **Overcast:** the sphere gains [= 2 stride =] radius.
