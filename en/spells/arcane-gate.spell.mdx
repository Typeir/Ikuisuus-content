# Arcane Gate

Two jagged arches of rippling light wrench open, forming a shimmering corridor of warped air between them.

---

<Spell
  level="6"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
  range="[= 100 stride =]"
  targets="—">

You create linked teleportation portals that remain open for the duration.  

You create two linked portals, each [= 2 stride =] in diameter, at locations you can see, one within [= 2 stride =] of you and one within [= 100 stride =] of you. 
A portal is a circular opening, which you can orient in any direction you choose. 
The portal remains open for the duration.  

If the portal would open in the space occupied by a creature, the spell fails, and the casting is lost. 

Any creature or object entering one portal exits from the other as if the two were adjacent to each other, passing through the same orientation. 
For example, if a creature enters the portal facing north, it exits from the other portal facing north. 
Passing through a portal counts as [= 1 stride =] of movement, regardless of the distance between the portals. 

A creature can only pass through a portal once per turn. 
If a creature tries to pass through a portal more than once on a turn, it is blocked by the portal and can't pass through. 

</Spell>
