# Darkvision

A pale, filmy light wells in the recipient's eyes, stripping away shadow and revealing forms in the dark.

---

> **Darkvision**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: 16 hours  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one willing creature  
>  
> Targets gain darkvision out to a range of [= 12 stride =] for the duration. Targets that already have darkvision instead have its range increased by [= 12 stride =].  
>  
> **Overcast:** touch one additional willing creature.
