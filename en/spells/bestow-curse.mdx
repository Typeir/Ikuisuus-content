# Bestow Curse

A living shadow roots itself to the target and begins, quietly, to treat their identity as its own. It bends their motions, dulls their reflexes, and does not consider itself a guest.

---

> **Bestow Curse**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one creature  
>  
>  
> Targets save Wisdom or are cursed for the duration. Choose the shape of the shadow when you cast the spell:  
>  
> | Curse       | Effect                                                                                                                    |
> | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
> | **Hobble**  | Choose one ability score. Targets have disadvantage on ability checks and saving throws made with it.                    |
> | **Avert**   | Targets have disadvantage on attack rolls against you.                                                                   |
> | **Stall**   | At the start of each of their turns, targets save Wisdom or waste their Major Action that turn. |
> | **Mark**    | Your attacks and spells deal an extra [% 1d8 dark %] to targets.                                                        |
>
> Any effect that lifts a curse ends this one. You may devise an alternative curse in the same register, no stronger than those above.  
>  
> **Overcast (4th+):**
>
> | Slot Level | Duration                        |
> | ---------- | ------------------------------- |
> | 4th+       | Concentration, up to 10 minutes |
> | 5th+       | 8 hours, no concentration       |
> | 7th+       | 24 hours                        |
> | 9th        | Until dispelled                 |
