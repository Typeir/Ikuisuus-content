# Cystbolt

A sickly, greenish yellow harpoon of vileness converges from thin air; it skewers and pierces a creature within range, leaving a trail of waste in its wake.

---

> **Cystbolt**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M (powdered rhubarb leaf and an adder's stomach)  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: [= 18 stride =]  
> **Targets**: one creature within range  
>
> A sickly bolt flies toward targets and bursts. Make a ranged attack. Targets take [% 4d4 chemical %] immediately, and a further [% 2d4 chemical %] at the end of their next turn. Missing targets are still sprayed by the burst, taking half the immediate damage, with nothing at the end of their next turn.
>
> **Overcast:** both the immediate and delayed damage gain [% 1d4 %].
