# Magic Weapon

Your touch suffuses a weapon with a pale, hard light that clings to its edge.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 hour"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one weapon you touch">

Until the spell ends, the weapon gains a +1 bonus to accuracy and damage.  

**Overcast (per +2):** the weapon gains a further 1 accuracy and damage.  

</Spell>
