# Invigorate

Heartbeats grow louder and steadier, the body becomes a monument to resilience.

---

> **Invigorate**  
> _2nd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S, M  
> **Duration**: 8 hours  
> **Range**: [= 6 stride =]  
> **Targets**: up to three creatures within range  
>
> Your spell bolsters your allies with toughness and resolve. Targets' hit point maximum and current hit points increase by 5 for the duration.
>
> **Overcast (3rd+):** targets' hit point maximum and current hit points gain 5 more.
