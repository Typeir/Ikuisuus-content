# Invigorate

Heartbeats grow louder and steadier, the body becomes a monument to resilience.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="8 hours"
  range="[= 6 stride =]"
  targets="up to three creatures within range">

Your spell bolsters your allies with toughness and resolve.
Targets' hit point maximum and current hit points increase by 5 for the duration.

**Overcast (3rd+):** targets' hit point maximum and current hit points gain 5 more.

</Spell>
