# Wither and Bloom

Shadowed blossoms erupt at the target, their petals withering living matter as pale flowers unfurl.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S, M"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="[= 12 stride =]">

You invoke both death and life upon a [= 2 stride;ADJ =]-radius sphere centered on a point within range.
Each creature of your choice in that area saves Constitution, halving [% 2d6 dark %].
Mundane vegetation in that area withers.  

In addition, one creature of your choice in that area can spend and roll one of its unspent Hit Dice and regain a number of hit points equal to the roll plus your casting ability modifier.
