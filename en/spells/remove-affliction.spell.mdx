# Remove Affliction

Shadowed bands coil across the target and unweave, crumbling into fine ash.

---

<Spell
  level="3"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature or object">

Choose one [_affliction_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) on targets — a condition, an illness, or a curse.
Make a [# kw:raw roll;RR #] saving throw using your [# kw:accuracy #].

The DC is **10 + the affliction's required casting level + its current stacks**.

- A **condition** requires no particular level, so its DC is **10 + stacks**.
- An **illness** or **curse** states the level this spell must be cast at to touch it.
A slot below that level cannot affect it at all, whatever you roll.

Succeeding ends the affliction. Failing leaves it in place, and this casting is spent against it.

If the object is a cursed magic item, its curse remains, but the spell breaks its owner's attunement to the object so it can be removed or discarded.

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the spell reaches afflictions that require the slot's level, and ends one additional affliction.

</Overcast>

</Spell>
