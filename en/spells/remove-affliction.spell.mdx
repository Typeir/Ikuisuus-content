# Remove Affliction

Shadowed bands coil across the target and unweave, crumbling into fine ash.

---

> **Remove Affliction**  
> _3rd-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V, S  
> **Duration**: Instantaneous  
> **Range**: Touch  
> **Targets**: one creature or object  
>
> Choose one [_affliction_](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/effects-and-enhancements) on targets — a condition, an illness, or a curse. Make a [RR](/en/library/rules/the-measure-of-the-self/rolling-and-special-rolls) saving throw using your [# kw:accuracy #].
>
> The DC is **10 + the affliction's required casting level + its current stacks**.
>
> - A **condition** requires no particular level, so its DC is **10 + stacks**.
> - An **illness** or **curse** states the level this spell must be cast at to touch it. A slot below that level cannot affect it at all, whatever you roll.
>
> Succeeding ends the affliction. Failing leaves it in place, and this casting is spent against it.
>
> If the object is a cursed magic item, its curse remains, but the spell breaks its owner's attunement to the object so it can be removed or discarded.
>
> **Overcast:** the spell reaches afflictions that require the slot's level, and ends one additional affliction.
