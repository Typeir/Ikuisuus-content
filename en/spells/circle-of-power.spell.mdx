# Circle of Power

A ring of shimmering force snaps into being, the air inside stiffening into a tense, humming barrier.

---

> **Circle of Power**  
> _5th-level Spell_  
> **Casting Time**: 1 Major Action  
> **Components**: V  
> **Duration**: Concentration, up to 30 minutes  
> **Range**: Self ([= 6 stride;ADJ =] aura)  
> **Targets**: Friendly creatures within [= 6 stride;ADJ =] of you, including yourself  
>  
>  
> You hold your god's Key open around you, and inside that circle the Fold answers you first. Magic reaching in is distorted and diffused within [= 6 stride =] of you.  
>  
> Until the spell ends, the aura moves with you, centered on you. Targets gain advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. When targets succeed on a saving throw against a spell or magical effect that would deal half damage on a success, they take no damage instead.  
>  
> **Overcast:** the aura gains [= 2 stride =] of radius.
