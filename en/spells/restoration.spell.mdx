# Restoration

Your touch drives out what has claimed the body, and the flesh remembers how to be itself.

---

<Spell
  level="2"
  cost="1 Major Action"
  components="V, S (and, for the greater working, diamond dust worth at least 100 gp, which the spell consumes)"
  duration="Instantaneous"
  range="Touch"
  targets="one creature you touch">

Targets are cured of one disease afflicting them, or one of the following conditions: [# kw:condition:blinded #], [# kw:condition:deafened #], [# kw:condition:paralyzed #], or [# kw:condition:poisoned #].  

<Overcast>

<Feature targets="one creature you touch">

#### The Unravelling <span>5th+</span>

Consuming the diamond dust, the spell cures targets of one of the following:  

- Up to half your **[# kw:damage;damage bonus #]** of levels of exhaustion
- A number of the conditions listed above, up to half your **[# kw:damage;damage bonus #]**.  
- one curse, including their attunement to a cursed object  
- one charm or petrification effect  
- one effect reducing an ability score, to a maximum amount of their **Tier Bonus**.
- one effect reducing their hit point maximum, to a maximum amount of their **Constituion Score**.

</Feature>
</Overcast>

</Spell>
