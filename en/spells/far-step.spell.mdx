# Far Step

A seam in space opens beneath your feet and you reappear a short distance away.

---

<Spell
  level="5"
  cost="1 Minor Action"
  components="V"
  duration="Concentration, up to 1 minute"
  range="Self"
  targets="Self">

You teleport up to [= 12 stride =] to an unoccupied space you can see.
On each of your turns before the spell ends, you can use a Minor Action to teleport in this way again.  

<Overcast>

**(per +1):** the distance increases by [= 6 stride =].  

</Overcast>

</Spell>
