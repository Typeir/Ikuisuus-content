# Stables and Stock

This page is the market and the stable: what animals, tack, vehicles, and travel services cost, and what it costs to keep an animal fed and housed.

How any of it behaves once you are moving — speeds, carrying capacities, pulling limits, barding, saddle effects, vehicle handling, and river current — is in [Mounts and Vehicles](/en/library/rules/the-long-road/mounts-and-vehicles).

## Mechanics

| -------------- | -----: |
| Camel          |  50 gp |

### Tack, Harness, and Drawn Vehicles

| Item           |   Cost |          Weight |
| -------------- | -----: | --------------: |
| Barding        |     ×4 |              ×2 |
| Bit and bridle |   2 gp |  [= 1 burden =] |
| Carriage       | 100 gp | [= 300 burden =] |
| Cart           |  15 gp | [= 100 burden =] |
| Chariot        | 250 gp | [= 50 burden =] |
| Sled           |  20 gp | [= 150 burden =] |
| Wagon          |  35 gp | [= 200 burden =] |

Barding costs four times the equivalent humanoid armour and weighs twice as much. What barding does is in [Mounts and Vehicles](/en/library/rules/the-long-road/mounts-and-vehicles#barding-armour-for-animals).

### Feed and Stabling

- Feed (per day): 5 cp, providing [= 5 burden =] of feed.
- Stabling (per day): 5 sp.

An animal that goes unfed or unstabled does not simply cost less. Missed feed is the Game Director's to price in condition and temper.

### Travel Service Rates

| Service                     |            Cost |
| --------------------------- | --------------: |
| Coach / cab (between towns) | 4 cp per league |
| Messenger                   | 3 cp per league |
| Road or gate toll           |            1 cp |
| Ship's passage              | 1 sp per league |

- Coach, cab, and messenger rates are charged per league of travel between settlements.
- Road and gate tolls are paid per crossing; the listed cost is a typical example.
- Ship's passage is the per-league embarkation cost for sea travel.
- Travel costs may be folded into a hiring contract when a service requires travel.

## Interactions

- Apply local modifiers for rarity, season, or regional scarcity to any price on this page as the Game Director sees fit.
- Feed and stabling are recurring daily costs, and belong in the same ledger as [Lodging and Lifestyle](/en/library/rules/hearth-and-station/lodging-and-lifestyle).
- Vehicle weights on this page feed into [Encumbrance and Carrying Capacity](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/encumbrance-and-carrying-capacity) when working out what a team can pull.
- Distances and pace come from [Travel Pace](/en/library/rules/the-long-road/travel-pace).
