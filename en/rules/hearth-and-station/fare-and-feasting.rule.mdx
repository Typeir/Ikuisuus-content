# Fare and Feasting

This section lists the costs of individual food items, drink, and formal banquets, along with daily meal costs at each lifestyle tier. The two tables answer different questions. **Meals Per Day** is the food share of a lifestyle already paid for — a character maintaining a lifestyle does not pay it a second time. **Food and Drink** is a la carte: provisions bought on the road, outside a lifestyle, for another character, or in quantities a lifestyle does not cover.

## Mechanics

### Food and Drink

| Item                   |  Cost |
| ---------------------- | ----: |
| Ale ([= 4 volume =])           |  2 sp |
| Ale (mug)              |  4 cp |
| Banquet (per person)   | 10 gp |
| Bread, loaf            |  2 cp |
| Cheese, hunk           |  1 sp |
| Meat, chunk            |  3 sp |
| Wine, common (pitcher) |  2 sp |
| Wine, fine (bottle)    | 10 gp |

- Banquet cost reflects a formal, single meal served to guests; it is a separate expenditure from daily meal costs.

### Meals Per Day

Daily meal costs by lifestyle tier. This is the food share of the lifestyle expense, not a charge on top of it. See [Lodging and Lifestyle](/en/library/rules/hearth-and-station/lodging-and-lifestyle) for the lodging share and the full daily rate.

| Lifestyle    | Meals (per day) |
| ------------ | :-------------: |
| Squalid      |      3 cp       |
| Poor         |      6 cp       |
| Modest       |      3 sp       |
| Comfortable  |      5 sp       |
| Wealthy      |      8 sp       |
| Aristocratic |      2 gp       |

## Interactions

- Food and drink item prices are a la carte. They are not charged on top of a maintained lifestyle, whose Meals Per Day share already covers that character's own food.
- Banquet costs apply to formal single meals served to guests and are a separate expenditure from daily meal costs.
- Downtime activities involving hospitality or social maneuvering — hosting dinners, bribing officials with fine wine — use the item prices here as a cost baseline.
- Skipping meals has consequences: see [Resting and Recovery](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/resting-and-recovery#sustenance) for sustenance and [# kw:condition:exhaustion #].
