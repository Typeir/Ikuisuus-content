# Effects and Enhancements

Fire clings to a doorway, the eroding presence of oblivion gnaws at mind and body alike, a swarm gnaws at flesh and keeps gnawing. These rules govern things that stay on the field; how they hurt and when.

---

## Effects

An **effect** is anything that outlives the instant that made it. A spell's lingering area, a monster's aura, a burning path, a curse riding a creature's blood, a boon granted to an ally.

An effect that occupies space on the field triggers on a specific **timing**:

- **Incipient**  — when being **targeted** by it on turn start.
- **Persistent** — when first being **targeted** by it on a round.
- **Delayed** — when a turn ends while **targeted** by it.

Effects will list whether they are **Incipient**, **Persistent**, or **Delayed** before the description of the effect itself is stated.

An area anchored to a creature or object is an **aura**, and travels with its source.

---

## Duration

Effects describe a duration, measured in **minutes**, **seconds**, **hours** or similar time measures, or the **Briefly** keyword. If no duration is stated, the duration is **Briefly**.

### Briefly

The default duration. The effect **ends** at the end of the **sufferer’s** turn, when it is **resisted or ended externally**, or when its **source dies** or **loses concentration**.

### Resist

If an effect would last for longer than **Briefly**, then the **sufferer** may be able to **Resist** the effect by saving against what originally imposed it, or another specified method.
If **no Resist method is specified** and **Resist** is keyed, the method is the **same** by which the effect was first **imposed**.
Targets **Resist** an effect **at the end of their turn**. If they succeed, the effect **ends** immediately.

## Incipient Effects

An **incipient** effect triggers when a sufferer is **targeted** by it **when their turn starts**.
If the effect is an **area** and a sufferer moves into it during their turn, the **Incipient** effect does not trigger until their next **turn start**.

## Persistent Effects

A **persistent** effect triggers when a sufferer is first **targeted** by the effect on a **round**.

- If an effect **targets** something during another creature's turn, it triggers for that **target's** whole round.
- It triggers **once per round per sufferer**. Breaking the overlap and restoring it on the same round does not trigger it again.

For effects that occupy an **area**, intersection is symmetric. It does not matter which of the two moved, or if either did:

- The sufferer moves into the area.
- The area moves onto the sufferer; a drifting cloud, a walking aura, a wall dragged along behind its caster.
- The area is created on top of the sufferer.

If the two overlap and did not overlap a moment ago, the effect triggers.
**Forced movement counts.** A **target** shoved, pulled, or thrown into the area triggers the effect.

---

## Delayed Effects

A **delayed** effect triggers when a sufferer **ends their turn** being targeted by it.

- It triggers **once per turn per sufferer**, at the end of that sufferer's turn.

For effects that occupy an **area**, the resolution is the same but targeting is linked to the **area**:

- A sufferer that is moved out of the area before their turn ends does not trigger it.
- A sufferer whose space intersects the area when their turn ends triggers it, however the overlap came about.

---

## Persistent and Delayed

A few effects are both. The order of resolution stays the same:

1. **When first targeted** — resolve the persistent trigger.
2. **At the end of the sufferer's turn** — resolve the delayed trigger.

Both may strike the **same sufferer on the same turn**; once when an enemy's turn pushes them into the area (or the area into them), once when their own turn ends there.

### Example

A sufferer is shoved into a **Persistent, Delayed** area that deals [% 6d10 slashing %] on a failed Dexterity save.

- Shoved in on the enemy's turn → **persistent** triggers → first save.
- Its own turn: it spends its movement and leaves → turn ends outside → no delayed trigger. **One save.**
- Had it stayed → turn ends inside → **delayed** triggers → **two saves that round.**

## Auras

An **aura** is an area anchored to a creature or an object. It extends a stated distance in every direction from the edge of its source's space, and it goes where its source goes.

- Written as **"a [= 3 stride;ADJ =] aura"**. The distance is how far the area reaches from its source.
- A spell whose area is an aura states it in its range: **Range: Self ([= 3 stride;ADJ =] aura)**.
- While it lasts, an aura moves with its source. This costs no action and is never restated by the effect.
- An aura does not extend through total cover.
- An aura ends when its source dies, is destroyed, or the effect ends.
- An area that must be moved by an action point stays where it was put until moved, and is no aura.

Aura is the only term for an area measured from a creature or an object.

Auras are areas, and carry **persistent** and **delayed** timings like any other. Because an aura travels with its source, a source that walks up to a sufferer intersects them, and a persistent aura triggers on that sufferer exactly as though the sufferer had walked in.

### Example

A monster with a **[= 2 stride;ADJ =] aura** that is **persistent** ends its move adjacent to two characters who have not touched it this round. Both are intersected by the aura and both trigger it. Neither spent movement; the monster did.

## Enhancements

An **enhancement** is an effect attached to a creature or an object that improves it — a boon, a blessing, a conferred resistance, a bonus to a roll.

- An enhancement travels with its bearer. No effect restates this.
- An enhancement granted by an heirloom, item, or feature lasts as long as its bearer keeps it. One granted by a spell lasts for that spell's duration.
- [_Objection_](/en/library/spells/objection) ends an enhancement.

---

## Afflictions

An **affliction** is an effect attached to a creature or an object that harms it. **Conditions**, **illnesses**, and **curses** are afflictions, and what is true of an affliction is true of all three.

| Kind | Arises from | Ends by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Condition](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions) | a blow, a spell, a hazard | its own stated counter or duration |
| [Illness](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/illnesses) | a natural vector | rest, medicine, [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration) |
| [Curse](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/curses) | the attention of an entity | understanding its source; [_Remove Affliction_](/en/library/spells/remove-affliction) |

- An affliction travels with its bearer.
- Multiple symptoms stack. When a sufferer rolls a symptom they already have, they take the next highest result.
- Objection does not end an affliction.
- An effect that reads on "an affliction" reads on conditions, illnesses, and curses alike.

### Curing an Affliction

[_Remove Affliction_](/en/library/spells/remove-affliction) ends afflictions one at a time, chosen by the caster. Its [# kw:raw roll;RR #] saving throw is made against:

**DC = 10 + the affliction's required casting level + its current stacks**

- A **condition** requires no particular level. Its DC is 10 + stacks.
- Every **illness** and **curse** states the level the spell must be cast at to touch it. A lower slot cannot affect it at all.

Being a raw roll, the caster's own afflictions do not apply to it.

**Example.** [# kw:Erosion #] requires a fifth level casting and the patient carries nine stacks: 10 + 5 + 9 = **DC 24**.

Enhancements and afflictions are both subject to the **Single Name Rule** below.

## The Single Name Rule

**Two effects sharing a name apply only once to a single target.**

This holds regardless of source. It does not matter that two different casters cast it, that one came from a spell and one from an heirloom, or that the durations began hours apart. One name, one instance.

- While instances overlap, the **most potent** applies — highest bonus, highest damage, longest reach.
- Ties are resolved in favour of the instance with the **longer remaining duration**.
- When the applied instance ends, any surviving instance of the same name takes over for whatever duration it has left.
- Effects with **different names** stack normally, even when their mechanics are identical.

A sufferer under [_Päivätär's Boon_](/en/library/spells/paivatars-boon) is under one instance of it. A second casting on the same target grants nothing.

### Example

Two casters both cast **Päivätär's Boon** on the same fighter — one from a 2nd-level slot (5 Boon Points), one from a 4th-level slot (9 Boon Points).

- Same name → **one instance applies**.
- Most potent → the **4th-level casting**, for **9 Boon Points**.
- The 2nd-level casting lies dormant. If the 4th-level casting's concentration breaks first, the 2nd-level casting applies for its remaining duration.

---

## Interactions

The Single Name Rule is the general case for **every** named effect in Damocles — spells, monster traits, heirloom properties, vocation features, curses, and boons alike.

[_Combining Magical Effects_](/en/library/rules/arcana-and-the-fold/combining-magical-effects) governs how **differently named spells** interact while their durations overlap.

Incipient, persistent, and delayed are **effect timings**. They do not interact with [# kw:resistance and vulnerability #], and a sufferer immune to an effect's damage simply takes nothing, but still triggers the effect's timing.
