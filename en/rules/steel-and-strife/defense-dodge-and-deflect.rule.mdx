# Defense, Dodge and Deflect

Defence is the number an attack roll must equal or exceed to hit. Defence is the sum of a base of 10, Deflect and Dodge.

---

## Formula

Defence = 10 + Deflect + Dodge.

- 10 is the base. Every creature and object has it.
- Deflect and Dodge are whole numbers. Either can be negative.
- Defence has no minimum. Deflect 0 and Dodge −2 give Defence 8.
- A creature or object has one Defence. Every attack against it uses that number, except where a flag below turns a part off.

## Dodge

Dodge is the part of Defence that makes an attack miss.

Sources:

- The DEX. Every creature has this source.
- A shield: buckler +1, shield +2, greatshield +3. Values on the [armour page](/en/library/items/equipment/armour).
- A feature, spell or item that names Dodge.

Cap:

- Armour lists a Max Dodge. Max Dodge caps the Dexterity source only.
- Light armour: no cap. Medium armour: 2. Heavy armour: 0.
- Dodge from a shield, feature, spell or item is not capped.

Off:

- [# kw:condition:stunned #]: Dodge is 0.
- [# kw:condition:incapacitated #]: Dodge is 0.
- Speed 0 from any source: Dodge is 0. Rules on [Donning, Doffing and Defence](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/donning-doffing-and-defense).
- An unavoidable attack: Dodge is 0 against that attack.

## Deflect

Deflect is the part of Defence that makes a hit fail to harm.

Sources:

- Armour: the Deflect column on the [armour page](/en/library/items/equipment/armour).
- Quality: +1 Deflect per rung above Mundane. Clothing at Mundane gives 0.
- Cover: half +2, three-quarters +5. Rules on [Making an Attack](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/making-an-attack).
- Natural armour: a creature's own body. The value is on the creature's sheet.
- A feature, spell or item that names Deflect.

Cap: none.

Off:

- An unblockable attack: Deflect is 0 against that attack.
- No condition turns Deflect off.

## Modifiers

- A bonus or penalty names the part it changes. Example: +2 Dodge, +1 Deflect.
- A bonus or penalty that names Defence changes the total. It is applied after both parts.
- A part that is off is 0. Every bonus and penalty to that part is off with it.

## Attacks

- Attack roll equal to or above Defence: hit.
- Unavoidable attack: resolved against 10 + Deflect.
- Unblockable attack: resolved against 10 + Dodge.
- Unavoidable and unblockable attack: resolved against 10.
- A natural 20 hits and a natural 1 misses regardless of Defence. Rules on [Making an Attack](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/making-an-attack).

## Objects

- An object has Dodge 0 unless its entry says otherwise.
- An object's Defence is 10 + Deflect. Values on the [Objects page](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/objects).

## Creatures

- A creature's Dodge is its DEX, capped by the armour it wears, plus its shield.
- A creature's Deflect is the rest of its Defence.
- Uncaring: a creature with this trait has Defence 10. No bonus or penalty from any source applies to it.
