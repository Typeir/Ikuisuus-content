# Poise and Stability

In conbat, a quick succession of blows often turns one into a hazy, defenseless bag of meat; unless properly armored, succesive blows will stun even the most dexterous of combatants.

## Poise

Poise is a paralell resource to a creature's HP, and is depleted with [# kw:damage #] exactly like HP is.
Unlike HP, however, a creature's Poise equals **their level (or lethality) plus their Strength Score**, and it is multiplied by the **Stability** of worn armour, or features that grant special stability values. 
Poise may be further enhanced by other features.
Along with the different source, a creature's poise returns to its maximum value at the start of their turns.

## Forced Defense

When poise reaches zero, creatures are forcibly put into a defensive stance, gaining the [# kw:steady #] and [# kw:staggered #] [# kw:briefly #].
Poise, like HP, can have negative values. If a creature's poise would be lower thab their maximum value in the negatives, that creature is instead [# kw:stunned #] [# kw:briefly #].

## Safeguarding the Poise

Certain effects do not diminish the damamge Poise suffers while others do. 
Poise is unnaffected by [# kw:damage reduction #], it is drained before reduction is applied.
Temporary Hit Points, on the contrary, do not transfer damage to Poise.

## Posture Damage

Certain attacks will include **Posture Damage**. This is a special damage kind that only affects Poise, and not HP.
This damage type is usually much larger than normal damage values, read carefully so as not to confuse the two.

## Recovering Poise

Poise is fully recovered at the start of a creature's turn, but certain effects may recover poise in different forms.
