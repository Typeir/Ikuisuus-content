# Measures

Damocles measures distance in **strides**, weight in **burdens**, and volume in **volumes**.

---

## Distance

A **stride** is two metres. It is also exactly one tile on the map.

- A creature's Speed is given in strides. A Speed of **6** crosses six tiles.
- Ranges, radii, reaches, and auras are all given in strides.
- The number an effect states is the number of tiles you count. Nothing is converted at the table.

| Strides | Metres |
| ------- | ------ |
| 1       | 2      |
| 2       | 4      |
| 3       | 6      |
| 5       | 10     |
| 6       | 12     |
| 10      | 20     |
| 12      | 24     |
| 24      | 48     |
| 60      | 120    |

Anything smaller than a stride is described rather than measured. A wall is a hand thick, or an arm thick; it still occupies its tile.

### Leagues

Overland distance is measured in **leagues**. A league is a thousand strides, or two kilometres.

Leagues are used for travel, for senses that reach beyond a battlefield, and for describing the world. They are never used on a map with tiles on it.

---

## Weight

A **burden** is one kilogram.

Carried gear, hauled cargo, and lifting limits are all counted in burdens. See [Encumbrance and Carrying Capacity](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/encumbrance-and-carrying-capacity).

| Burdens | Kilograms |
| ------- | --------- |
| 1       | 1         |
| 10      | 10        |
| 100     | 100       |

### Rounding

Where a weight would land between whole burdens, round to the nearest, and round a half upward.

- **7 and a half burdens → 8.**
- **7 and a quarter burdens → 7.**

---

## Volume

A **volume** is one litre.

Rations, water, oil, alchemical stock, and anything else poured or bottled are counted in volumes. A standard waterskin holds **2 volumes**.

## If You Use Other Metrics

These are the working equivalents. They are rounded so that the numbers land evenly; use them as stated rather than converting precisely.

| Damocles  | Metric       | Imperial |
| --------- | ------------ | -------- |
| 1 stride  | 2 metres     | 5 feet   |
| 4 leagues | 8 kilometres | 5 miles  |
| 1 burden  | 1 kilogram   | 2 pounds |
| 4 volumes | 4 litres     | 1 gallon |

### Distance in Feet

Multiply strides by five.

| Strides | Feet |
| ------- | ---- |
| 1       | 5    |
| 2       | 10   |
| 3       | 15   |
| 4       | 20   |
| 5       | 25   |
| 6       | 30   |
| 8       | 40   |
| 10      | 50   |
| 12      | 60   |
| 18      | 90   |
| 20      | 100  |
| 24      | 120  |
| 30      | 150  |
| 60      | 300  |
| 120     | 600  |

A stride is one five-foot square. Tables written for five-foot squares can be read directly: one square is one stride.

### Weight in Pounds

Double the burdens. A creature carrying **30 burdens** carries about 60 pounds.

### Volume in Gallons

Four volumes make a gallon. A waterskin holding 2 volumes holds about half a gallon.
