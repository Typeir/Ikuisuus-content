# Conditions

Conditions alter a creature’s capabilities in a variety of ways and can arise as a result of a spell, a vocation feature, a monster’s attack, or other effects.  
Most conditions, such as _blinded_, are impairments, but a few, such as _invisible_, can be advantageous.

A condition lasts either until it is countered (the _prone_ condition is countered by standing up, for example) or for a duration specified by the effect that imposed the condition.

---

## Bleeding

Sufferers lose hit points from accumulated open wounds.

- At the start of each of their turns, sufferers take **(Bleeding Counter) × d4** damage.
- After taking this damage, the **Bleeding Counter decreases by 1.**
- The Bleeding Counter **only increases when a weapon, effect, or feature specifically inflicts Bleeding.**
- The Bleeding Counter has no upper limit.
- Sufferers can use a **Major Action** to attempt to **stanch the bleeding**, making a DC 10 + stacks **Wisdom (Medicine)** check.  
  On a success, they reduce the Bleeding Counter by their Medicine modifier + **[% 1d4 %].**
- **Magical healing** reduces the Bleeding Counter by sufferers' spellcasting modifier (minimum 1), regardless of the amount healed.
- The **Bleeding condition ends** when the Bleeding Counter reaches 0.

---

## Blinded

- Sufferers **can’t see** and automatically **fail any ability check that requires sight**.
- **Attack rolls** against sufferers have **advantage**, and their own **attack rolls have disadvantage**.

---

## Burning

Sufferers are on fire and take ongoing heat damage.

- At the start of each of their turns, sufferers take **[% 1d10 fire %].**
- Sufferers can use a **Major Action** to **extinguish the flames** by dropping prone, rolling, or smothering themselves.
  - Doing so **automatically ends the Burning condition**, and they end their turn **prone.**
- Another creature within [= 1 stride =] may also use a **Major Action** to extinguish the flames.
- While burning, sufferers shed **bright light in a [= 2 stride;ADJ =] radius** and **dim light for another [= 2 stride =].**

---

## Charmed

- Sufferers **can’t attack the charmer** or target the charmer with harmful abilities or magical effects.
- The charmer has **advantage on ability checks to interact socially** with sufferers.

---

## Deafened

- Sufferers **can’t hear** and automatically **fail any ability check that requires hearing**.

---

## Dying

- A creature below **1 HP** is **dying**, and is also [# kw:condition:unconscious #].
- A dying creature makes a **death saving throw** at the start of its turn, [# kw:chained #](3/3). On **three successes** it **stabilizes**; on **three failures** it **dies**.
- On a roll of **20**, it regains **1 HP** and becomes conscious immediately.
- Taking damage while dying costs **one failed death saving throw**. A **critical hit** still costs only one.
- A single hit dealing damage equal to or greater than **half the creature's maximum HP** kills it outright.
- Stabilizing ends the dying condition and leaves the creature in a **coma**. See [Healing, Restoration, and Death](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/healing-restoration-and-death) for waking from one.

## Exhaustion

Some special abilities and environmental hazards, such as starvation or extreme environments, can lead to **exhaustion**.  
Exhaustion is measured in six levels.

| Level | Effect                                         |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Disadvantage on ability checks                 |
| 2     | Speed halved                                   |
| 3     | Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws |
| 4     | Hit point maximum halved                       |
| 5     | Speed reduced to 0                             |
| 6     | Death                                          |

- Sufferers suffer the effects of their current level of exhaustion as well as all lower levels.
- finishing a [# kw:Recovery #] **removes all levels of exhaustion**, provided they have consumed food and drink.

---

## Frightened

- Sufferers have **disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls** while the source of their fear is within line of sight.
- When they move **toward the source of their fear**, the **cost of movement is quintupled**.

---

## Grappled

- Sufferers’ **speed becomes 0**, and they **can’t benefit from bonuses to speed**.
- The condition **ends if the grappler is incapacitated**.
- The condition also ends if an effect **removes a sufferer from the reach** of the grappler or grappling effect, such as when an effect would [# kw:displace #] them.

---

## Incapacitated

- Sufferers **can’t take actions or reactions**.

---

## Invisible

- Sufferers are **impossible to see** without the aid of magic or a special sense.  
  For the purpose of hiding, they are **heavily obscured**.
- **Attack rolls** against sufferers have **disadvantage**, and their **attack rolls have advantage**.

---

## Paralyzed

- Sufferers are **incapacitated**, can’t move or speak, and **automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws**.
- **Attack rolls** against them have **advantage**.
- Any attack that hits them is a **critical hit** if the attacker is within [= 1 stride =] of them.

---

## Petrified

- Sufferers are **transformed into an inanimate substance** (usually stone), along with any mundane objects they are wearing or carrying.
- They are considered **restrained** while petrification is being applied.
- They have **resistance to all damage** and are **immune to poison and disease**, although a poison or disease already in their system is suspended.
- They are **incapacitated**, can’t move or speak, and are **unaware of their surroundings**.
- **Attack rolls** against them have **advantage**, and they **automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws**.
- They can [# kw:resist #], [# kw:chained #](3/3), to avoid petrification. 
- If petrification completes, the sufferer **dies instantly** and turns into a mundane object. A _sculpture_.
---

## Poisoned

- Sufferers have **disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks**.

---

## Prone

- Sufferers’ only movement option is to **crawl**, unless they **stand up** and end the condition.
- They have **disadvantage on attack rolls**.
- **Attack rolls** against them have **advantage** if the attacker is within [= 1 stride =]; otherwise, those rolls have **disadvantage**.

---

## Restrained

- Sufferers’ **speed becomes 0**, and they **can’t benefit from any bonus to their speed**.
- **Attack rolls** against them have **advantage**, and their **attack rolls have disadvantage**.
- They have **disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws**.

---

## Slowed

Sufferers’ bodies and perception are weighed down by temporal drag or magical interference.

- Their **speed is halved**.
- They take a **–2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saving throws**.
- They **can’t use reactions**.
- On their turn, they can **use only one action**.
- Regardless of their abilities or items, they **can't make more than one melee or ranged attack** during their turn.
- If a sufferer attempts to cast a spell with a casting time of 1 Major Action, **roll a d20**. On an **10 or lower**, the spell doesn't take effect until their next turn, and they must spend one Major Action on that turn to complete the spell. If they can't, the spell is wasted.

---

## Staggered

- Sufferers **can’t take reactions**.

---

## Steady

- Sufferers have **advantage** against effects that would [# kw:displace #] them.
- The condition ends when they move more than [= 1 stride =].
- They can use any feature that requires them to be steady.
- They cannot become steady while **restrained**, **grappled**, **incapacitated**, or while riding a mount or vehicle.

---

## Stunned

- Sufferers are **incapacitated**, can’t move, and can speak only falteringly.
- They **automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws**.
- **Attack rolls** against them have **advantage**.

---

## Suffocating

Sufferers are **deprived of air** and begin to **die slowly while conscious**.

- They can hold their breath for a number of minutes equal to **1 + their Constitution modifier (minimum 30 seconds)**.
- When that time expires, they begin **suffocating**.
- While suffocating, at the start of each of their turns, they must make a **death saving throw** (as if [# kw:condition:dying #]), even if still conscious.
- The condition **ends immediately** when they can breathe again.
- If they **fail three death saving throws**, they die.

---

## Sundered

Sufferers have their armour or protection compromised by mighty blows, corrosive substances, or other destructive forces.

They lose 1 point of AC for each instance of the sundered condition they have.

---

## Terrified

Sufferers are overwhelmed by panic and survival instinct.

- They **cannot willingly move closer** to the source of their terror.
- They have **disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks** while they can see the source of their terror. If they cannot see the source, they still have disadvantage on ability checks.
- At the start of each of their turns, if the source of their terror is within **[= 12 stride =]** and they have a path away from it, they must take the **Dash action** to move as far from the source as possible.
- They automatically fail any ability check to interact socially with the source of their terror, unless the check involves pleading or submission.

> **Note**: Terrified supersedes Frightened. A sufferer affected by both suffers only the Terrified condition.

---

## Unconscious

- Sufferers are **incapacitated**, can't move, and can only **speak falteringly**. They are **unaware of their surroundings**.
- They **drop whatever they are holding** and **fall prone**.
- They **automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws** and any **skill check that requires a roll**.
- **Attack rolls** against them have **advantage**.- They **lose their Dexterity bonus to AC**.- Any melee attack that hits them is a **critical hit** if the attacker is within [= 1 stride =] of them.
- When a sufferer is restored to positive HP from the unconscious state, they **lose one action** [# kw:briefly #]. They may still move, take Minor Actions, and use Reactions.

## Unsteady

- Sufferers can't become steady.
- They have **disadvantage** against effects that would [# kw:displace #] them.
