# Madness

Madness is an illness of the mind, contracted from hardship, horror, spellwork, or exposure to unbearable truths of the world.

An effect that inflicts madness often names a **Wisdom**, **Intelligence** or **Charisma** saving throw.
On a failed save, roll on the table below.

- **Short-term madness** lasts **1 minute**.
- **Long-term madness** lasts **indefinitely, until treated**.
- Onset is immediate. Madness has no incubation period.
- Where an effect does not name a severity, it is short-term.
- Multiple symptoms can stack indefinitely. A duplicate result is re-rolled.
- Madness is not the [**charmed**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#charmed) or [**frightened**](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/conditions#frightened) condition, and immunity to **psychic** damage grants no immunity to it.

## Breaking Madness

Short-term madness ends on its own when its duration elapses.

Long-term madness ends only when treated:

- A **Respite** spent in company, drink, or song suppresses one long-term madness until the end of your next Repose, whereupon it returns.
- At the end of a **Recovery** spent under the care of a creature that is not itself afflicted, that carer may make a DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check. On a success, one long-term madness ends.
- [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration) ends one long-term madness.
- [_Remove Affliction_](/en/library/spells/remove-affliction), **cast as a third level spell**, ends one long-term madness.
- Madness inflicted by a curse is a symptom of that curse. It ends only when the curse is broken, and it follows that curse's permanence.
