# Illnesses

Not every affliction is a curse laid by a god. The world has mundane sicknesses — fevers, infections, plagues — that spread with ease and respond to natural remedies.

For supernatural afflictions tied to specific entities, see [Curses](/en/library/rules/life-blood-and-ruin/curses).

## How Mundane Illnesses Work

### Transmission

Mundane illnesses spread through ordinary vectors: contaminated water, spoiled food, proximity to the sick, infected wounds, exposure to weather. .

### Course

Mundane illnesses do not use saving throws. Symptoms persist as described until the illness is ended — by rest, medicine, or magic. Severity and duration are set by the Game Director for each illness.

### Recovery

Mundane illnesses respond to rest, mundane medicine, and magical healing. A successful DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine) check during a [# kw:Recovery #] can end a mundane illness. 
Magical _Restoration_ removes most mundane illnesses outright.

| Bog Lung   | Inhaled swamp vapours     | Speed halved; cannot [# kw:bolt #].                                         |
| Red Pox    | Contact with infected     | -2 Constitution; contagious to creatures within [= 1 stride =].            |

The Game Director is encouraged to invent additional mundane illnesses as the story demands, to arm the world with grit that grinds down the unprepared.
