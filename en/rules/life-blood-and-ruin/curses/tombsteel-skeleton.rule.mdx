# Tombsteel Skeleton

_5th level affliction_

The mycelial network of _Stachybotrys ventralis_ spreads through all matter, living or inert, and fouls it into **tombsteel** — indiscriminate as entropy.
When the network reaches bone, Nigredo's curse of ossification begins.

> “They were never the strongest of the Hiisi; they needed not be. They merely needed to be the last.”

This curse cannot be contracted directly. It manifests only as the terminal outcome of [# kw:Black Rot #], on a symptom roll of 100.

Ossification is not survivable by endurance alone, and it does not end in a cure. It ends in one of two ways: the sufferer dies, or the skeleton stabilises and the affliction becomes the [_Tombsteel Skeleton_](/en/library/items/heirlooms/tombsteel-skeleton) monstrous graft, carried for the rest of the sufferer's life.

---

## Affliction Effects

While ossifying, a sufferer endures the following:

- You gain **3 permanent levels of [# kw:condition:exhaustion #]**.
- Your **speed is reduced by [= 2 stride =]** after exhaustion halving.
- **Magical healing you receive is halved.**
- You cannot gain **temporary hit points from spells.**
- You **do not lose [# kw:condition:exhaustion #] from resting**, though magical effects that remove exhaustion still function.

---

## Ossification Trial

At the end of each [# kw:Repose #], or at **dawn** if no rest was taken, you must succeed on a **DC 20 Constitution saving throw [RR](/en/library/rules/the-measure-of-the-self/rolling-and-special-rolls)**.

- **On a failure**: You gain **1 level of [# kw:condition:exhaustion #]**, and the [# kw:Repose #] has no effect on you or any of your features.
- **On a success**: Mark **1 progress**.
- At **3 progress**, the body stabilises. All exhaustion from this curse is removed and the affliction becomes the [_Tombsteel Skeleton_](/en/library/items/heirlooms/tombsteel-skeleton) graft.

Effects that enhance metabolic functions such as [_Catnap_](/en/library/spells/catnap) can turn this roll into a normal roll instead of a Raw Roll, making it qualify for non-persistent modifiers such as [_Bless_](/en/library/spells/bless).

---

## Ending the Curse

Ossification cannot be halted once the trial has begun. The curse leaves only by completing it — three successes stabilise the skeleton and the graft is permanent — or by ending the sufferer.

Magic that lifts [# kw:Black Rot #] before the roll of 100 prevents the curse. It does nothing once ossification has started.
