# Red Ruin

Rubedo, the Red Rebis, is the source of a catastrophic curse known as **Red Ruin**.  
Rubedo is the reddening, the last turn of the Great Work, and it does not know how to stop. What it touches, it finishes. It drags flesh and blood and thought toward a completion nothing living was shaped to survive, and calls the wreck perfection.

There is no fire in it and no wound to bind. The body simply begins to arrive at an end it never agreed to, and keeps arriving.

Rubedo spreads Red Ruin through supercritical emissions, spatial annihilation, and direct proximity to its core output.

When afflicted by **Red Ruin**, a creature must roll on the table below to determine how the Work takes them.  
**Multiple symptoms can stack.**

Additionally, **each time you gain Red Ruin**:

- You gain **1 level of exhaustion**
- You must make **one Permanent Death Saving Throw**
  - On a failure, the save remains failed until all Red Ruin is cured

## Breaking Red Ruin

Red Ruin is an existential curse.

- [_Remove Affliction_](/en/library/spells/remove-affliction): **No effect at any level.**
- [_Restoration_](/en/library/spells/restoration), cast as its greater working: Removes **one** Red Ruin effect and **one** exhaustion level.
- **Wish**, **True Resurrection**, or equivalent magic: Removes all Red Ruin and clears Permanent Death Saving Throws.

A creature Red Ruin kills has been finished. There is nothing left unfinished enough to call back.
