# Curses

Curses in Damocles are the active attention of a specific cosmological entity, manifesting as corruption, decay, madness, or transformation in the afflicted. A curse is a relationship: something has noticed you, and it does not wish you well.

Each curse demands that those who would cure it first understand its source. The cure for Black Rot, if one exists, requires understanding Nigredo.

---

## How Curses Work

### Transmission and Affliction

How a sufferer contracts a curse depends entirely on the entity behind it. Most Damocles curses are not contagious in the mundane sense — they spread through specific vectors tied to the entity or phenomenon responsible. A curse entry specifies:

- **Source**: which entity, creature, or environment usually causes it
- **Transmission**: what action or condition triggers affliction (bite, proximity, spore inhalation, contact, Fold-exposure)

The curse itself carries no numbers. Its save DC and its incubation period belong to whatever inflicts it: a creature uses its own save DC and its own stated incubation, and the Game Director sets both for an environment, a phenomenon, or any other source without a stat block.

When exposure occurs, the sufferer saves against that DC. Failure means the curse takes hold; success means the sufferer has resisted for this exposure. Repeated exposures may require repeated saves at the Game Director's discretion.

### Incubation and Symptom Onset

The incubation period is the time between affliction and the first symptom, and the source sets it. Until that period elapses, the sufferer is cursed but shows no effects. The Game Director is not obliged to disclose whether a character is cursed during incubation.

### Saving Throws and Progression

Many curses worsen over time unless the sufferer actively fights them. Unless a curse entry specifies otherwise:

- At the end of each [# kw:Recovery #], a cursed sufferer makes a Constitution saving throw against the DC of the source that inflicted it.
- **On a failed save**: the sufferer gains a new symptom roll or the curse advances.
- **On a successful save**: the curse does not advance. Some curses specify that successive successes can reduce symptoms — consult the curse entry.

Some curses impose permanent effects that cannot be reversed by rest alone. These entries will specify what, if anything, can remove the condition.

### Recovery and Breaking a Curse

[# kw:Recovery #] conditions vary by curse. Some curses resolve with enough successful saves. Others require:

- A specific reagent or ritual tied to the entity that caused them
- Removal of the sufferer from the afflicting environment
- Magical intervention — where magic applies, the curse entry states it

The Game Director may adjudicate unusual cures. Curses sourced from Fold entities are generally not breakable by generic restoration magic; they require understanding the entity's nature.

### Scale and Outbreaks

A single cursed sufferer is a personal crisis. A spreading curse is a plot event. The mechanical framework above handles the personal case; outbreak scale, contamination spread, and societal response are narrative decisions for the Game Director. If an area is broadly contaminated (such as a Precursor-saturated mine), sufferers may face repeated exposure with every rest taken in that space.
