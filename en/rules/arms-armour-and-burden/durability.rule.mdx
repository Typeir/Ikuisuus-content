# Durability

Gear wears out. A blade turned against a stone wall, a breastplate caught in a corrosive spray, a tool put to work it was never shaped for — each of these costs the object something, and what it costs is a rung of quality.

---

## The Quality Ladder

Every crafted object belongs to one rung of a single ladder. 
The rung is the object's current state rather than its pedigree: a Supreme blade that has been through a siege is no longer Supreme but Worn or Ruined.

|   Rung    | Step from Mundane |
| :-------: | :---------------: |
|  Ruined   |        −4         |
| Degraded  |        −3         |
|   Worn    |        −2         |
|  Shoddy   |        −1         |
|  Mundane  |         0         |
| Enhanced  |        +1         |
| Superior  |        +2         |
|  Supreme  |        +3         |
| Perfected |        +4         |
|   True    |        +5         |

Mundane is the baseline. An object at Mundane performs exactly as its entry in [Equipment](/en/library/items/equipment/weapons) is written, or as the rules in [Weapons and Dice Levels](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapons-and-dice-levels) describe.

---

## What Quality Does

Quality scales the object's basic unit, and moves nothing else.

- **Weapons** — one rung is one step along the dice sequence in [Weapons and Dice Levels](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapons-and-dice-levels#dice-progression-quality). A Mundane weapon rolls the dice from its type; each rung above rolls up the sequence, each rung below rolls down.
- **Armour** — one rung is 1 point of Deflect. A Mundane suit grants the Deflected listed for it.
- **Trinkets** — Trinkets are affected by quality in a way that is specific to each item, often increasing their accuracy and damage along with special effects.
- **Everything else** — the Game Director names the single number the rung moves: a tool's bonus, a rope's hit points, a lantern's radius.

Quality does not affect anything else. A mundane sword is still a sword, and it's accuracy does not differ from that of a shoddy one, nor does it mastery or chasis.
---

## Taking a Blow

The Game Director decides when an object takes a durability blow. The usual causes are a weapon or tool forced against something it cannot cut, an attack that fails to meet a [damage threshold](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/objects#damage-threshold), a corrosive or destructive substance, and any effect that says so in its own text.

Each blow drops the object one rung, and the object's numbers change from that moment on. A Superior longsword that rolls [% 1d12 %] rolls [% 1d10 %] once it is struck down to Enhanced.

Ruined is the last rung. A Ruined object that takes another durability blow is destroyed, and nothing short of remaking it will serve.

Size is unaffected. A Large weapon still multiplies its dice as [Weapons and Dice Levels](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapons-and-dice-levels#weapon-size-dice-multiplication) describes; apply quality first, then size, as it always was.
