# Weapons and Dice Levels

## What Is a Weapon Attack

When you make a weapon attack, you roll your attack die against the target's Armour Class. On a hit, you roll the weapon's damage die and add your relevant ability modifier. Attacks use Strength by default; a weapon with the Finesse attribute uses Dexterity (see [Weapon Attributes](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-properties)).

Specific weapon damage dice and any special rules tied to individual weapons are in [Equipment: Weapons](/en/library/items/equipment/weapons).

## Arts

You add your tier bonus to attack rolls with a weapon your [arts](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-arts) cover. A weapon your arts do not cover is an improvised weapon in your hands.

## Two-Weapon Fighting

Any melee weapon that is not Unwieldy can be used as an off-hand weapon. The rule is in [Making an Attack](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/making-an-attack#two-weapon-fighting).

## Improvised Weapons

A character without a proper weapon can attack with any object they can hold — a broken chair leg, a heavy book, a loose brick. The Game Director adjudicates whether the object is similar enough to a real weapon to use that weapon's statistics. If it bears no useful resemblance, it deals **[% 1d4 %] damage** of a type the Game Director deems appropriate.

Improvised thrown objects have a normal range of [= 4 stride =] and a long range of [= 12 stride =].

## Ointments and Poisons

Certain creatures are susceptible to treated weapons where they would otherwise resist or ignore mundane blows. A weapon can be treated without altering its normal function, though the process requires skilled work and is not trivially inexpensive.

Treatments are trinkets, not weapon attributes. Applying one costs a Major Action, lasts a stated duration or number of hits, and never changes the weapon's damage die, finish, attributes, or masteries. Two treatments cannot be held on the same weapon at once; a new application replaces the old.

Two samples exist, and the full set is still being written:

- [Silver Ointment](/en/library/items/trinkets/silver-ointment) — makes a weapon count as silvered.
- [Basic Poison](/en/library/items/trinkets/basic-poison) — adds a poison save on a hit.

## Masteries and Masterful Blows

A weapon's masteries and its Masterful Blow come from its art. The arts, their masteries and the ladder that opens them are on [Weapon Arts](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-arts); the blows are on [Weapon Attributes](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-properties#masterful-blows).

The full list of weapons, their bases, attributes and dice is in [Equipment: Weapons](/en/library/items/equipment/weapons).

## Dice Levels

Weapons are conduits for violence, and their damage is expressed through **dice levels**.  
The dice level of a weapon represents its **manufacture quality and killing potential**.

### Important Notes

There is **no separate tracker** for weapon dice levels.  
The **Game Director** is expected to decide what quality of weapon is present in the world.  
If a player acquires a superior weapon, they are expected to **replace** the old one rather than "upgrade" it.  
This is a system entirely separate from weapon bonuses (e.g. +1, +2, etc.).

### The Quality Rungs

**Mundane is zero.** A Mundane weapon rolls the die printed for its pattern. Each rung above Mundane moves the die one step up the sequence; each rung below moves it one step down.

| Rung | Step from Mundane |
| --- | --- |
| Ruined | −4 |
| Degraded | −3 |
| Worn | −2 |
| Shoddy | −1 |

## Weapon Size (Dice Multiplication)

All weapons are assumed **Medium** by default.  
Weapons physically made for larger creatures **multiply the number of dice** they roll.

- A creature needs a Strength Score of 18 or higher to wield a weapon one size larger than itself. They do so at disadvantage, even if the threshold is met.
- A creature cannot wield a weapon more than one size larger than itself unless it has a trait that explicitly allows it.
- Homunculi with [Oversized Armament Discipline](/en/library/character-creation/bloodlines/homunculus) ignore that limit for **Large** weapons.

| Weapon Size       | Dice Multiplier |
| ----------------- | --------------- |
| Small and smaller | ×1              |
| Medium            | ×1              |
| Large             | ×2              |
| Huge              | ×3              |
| Gargantuan        | ×4              |
| Colossal          | ×5              |
| Titanic           | ×6              |

Sizes are the canonical ten of [Measures](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/measures#size). A weapon built for a creature smaller than Medium rolls the Medium dice; it is lighter and shorter, not weaker. Nothing scales past Titanic, because nothing wields a weapon past Titanic.

## Game Director Guidelines

- Weapons do **not** improve themselves. A poor weapon must be **replaced** with a better one if higher quality is desired.
- Weapons do fall down the sequence. Each blow to a weapon's durability costs it one step; see [Durability](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/durability).
- Apply **quality first, then size**: determine the weapon's dice on the sequence, then multiply for size.
