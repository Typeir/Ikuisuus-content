# Weapon Attributes

Attributes are the definition of a weapon: what it is beyond its [base](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-arts#chassis), and what it lets you do. No attribute depends on another. An attribute may depend on its chassis, and the ones that do are marked. Some attributes are steppers and move the die; the rest are permissions. An attribute marked repeatable may be taken more than once.

Every attribute on a weapon must belong to an [art](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/weapon-arts) you hold, and your tier in the weapon's own art caps how many it may carry.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Limited to | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Edged | Stepper | — | A second finish: the weapon also deals slashing damage, and you choose which finish it deals with each blow. |
| Pointed | Stepper | — | A second finish: the weapon also deals piercing damage. |
| Blunt | Stepper | — | A second finish: the weapon also deals bludgeoning damage. |
| Unwieldy | Stepper | — | If your Strength is below 15, you have disadvantage on attack rolls with the weapon. Unwieldy says nothing about hands: a one-handed Unwieldy weapon is legal. |
| Two-handed | Stepper, two steps | — | You must use two hands to make an attack with the weapon. |
| Paired | Stepper, −1 main hand, +1 off hand | — | A designated off-hand weapon. It cannot take the grip step. |
| Finesse | Permission | — | The weapon uses Dexterity for attack and damage rolls. A ranged weapon without Finesse uses Strength. |
| Reach | Permission | — | Your reach for attacks and opportunity attacks with the weapon increases by [= 1 stride =]. |
| Bind | Permission | — | When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can attempt to grapple the target as part of the same action. On a Fisted weapon, Bind is the grapple itself. |
| Thrown | Permission | — | You can throw the weapon to make a ranged attack. If it is normally a melee weapon, use the same ability modifier as for its melee attacks. Drawing it is part of the attack. A thrown weapon keeps the base die of its chassis. |
| Cruel | Permission, repeatable | — | The weapon's critical hit threshold is reduced by 1. |
| Vicious | Permission, repeatable | — | The weapon's critical hit multiplier increases by 1. |
| Draw | Stepper | Flexed, Slung | The weapon's range is multiplied by 4. Draw and Finesse cannot sit on the same weapon. |
| Loading (N) | Permission, refunds slots | Flexed | You reload after every Nth shot, and reloading costs a **Minor Action**.  |
| Chamber (N) | Permission, costs slots | Hammered | The gun fires N shots before its chassis reload. |
| Barrel | Permission | Hammered | Range that comes from the weapon, with no body input. |
| Set | Permission | Bracing | When a creature enters your reach, you can make an opportunity attack against it that deals no damage dice. Set needs neither Reach nor being [# kw:condition:steady #], and it shares your reaction with the ordinary opportunity attack. |
| Shod | Permission | Cleaving | As a Minor Action, you can strike with the butt of the weapon for damage equal to 1 plus the number of dice steps the weapon carries. |
| Gracile | Permission | Drawing | The weapon suffers no disadvantage from being mounted or from not being [# kw:condition:steady #]. |
| Concealed | Permission | Vexing | You have advantage on Stealth checks made to conceal the weapon. An Unwieldy weapon fails such checks automatically. |
| Catalyst | Permission | a caster of the named kind | Written Catalyst (Fold), Catalyst (Väki), Catalyst (Key) or Catalyst (Will); a weapon may list several. The weapon serves as a spellcasting focus for the castings named. |

## Exclusions

- Two-handed and Paired cannot sit on the same weapon. The Sword Saint's Colossal Grip is the single exception.
- Draw and Finesse cannot sit on the same weapon.
- A Paired weapon cannot take the grip step.

## Range

A ranged or thrown weapon lists a normal range and a long range. Attacking beyond normal range imposes disadvantage. You can't attack beyond long range. Ammunition is assumed for every ranged weapon: drawing it is part of the attack, and after combat you can spend 1 minute to recover half (rounded down) of what you expended.

## Masterful Blows

Each art carries a Masterful Blow, and an heirloom may carry a blow of its own.

Some blows call for a saving throw, which follows the standard resolution defined in [Checks and Rolls](/en/library/rules/the-measure-of-the-self/checks-and-rolls).

- Save DC = 10 + [# kw:accuracy #]
- Uses: once per [# kw:Repose #] if you are Proficient in the weapon's art, three times per [# kw:Repose #] if you are Expert or Savant.
- When you hit a creature with that weapon, you can expend a use to invoke the Masterful Blow.
- Unless noted otherwise, the effect ends at the start of your next turn.
- The same Masterful Blow on the same target doesn't stack; a new application only refreshes its duration — unless the blow's effect is a condition that inherently accumulates by stacking, such as the [# kw:condition:sundered #] condition.
- A creature can invoke only one Masterful Blow per turn, however many uses or weapons it has. A creature can be affected by any number of Masterful Blows at once, subject to the same-name rule above.

| Name         | Save | Effect                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Concussing   | Wis  | The target can’t take reactions until your next turn ends.                                        |
| Hamstringing | Con  | The target’s speed is halved until your next turn ends.                                           |
| Muddling     | Wis  | On its next turn, the target can’t use Multiattack and can make only one weapon attack.           |
| Confusing    | Wis  | The target has disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes before the end of your next turn.   |
| Tackling     | Str  | The target is pushed [= 4 stride =] straight away from you.                                       |
| Flaying      | Str  | The target is pulled [= 2 stride =] straight toward you (or as far as space allows).              |
| Muzzling     | Int  | The target can cast only cantrips (no leveled spells) until your next turn ends.                  |
| Hemorrhaging | Con  | The target gains [% 1d4 %] stacks of [# kw:condition:Bleeding #].                                 |
| Disarming    | Str  | You choose one item the target is holding; it drops that item in its space.                       |
| Sundering    | Con  | The target gains one instance of the [# kw:condition:Sundered #] condition, reducing its AC by 1. |
| Dazzling     | Con  | The target can't take a Minor Action on its next turn.                                            |
| Redoubling   | Dex  | The target takes another instance of the weapon's damage dice. This damage cannot critically strike. |
