# Heroic Awakening

At the start of combat, when initiative is rolled, each creature also makes a **Fate Roll**.

A **Fate Roll** is a bare [% 1d20 %]. No modifier, bonus, penalty, advantage, or disadvantage applies to it from any source. It measures nothing about the creature; it decides whether the world has singled it out today.

If the result meets or exceeds the creature's **Heroic DC**, that creature becomes **Heroically Awakened**.

A Heroically Awakened creature immediately:

- Maximizes all hit dice.
- Increases its tier bonus, AC, and saving throws by +1.
- Gains a **Heroic Affix**, rolled from the table below.

This effect triggers only once per combat, during initiative.

---

## Heroic DC by Lethality

| Lethality                    | Heroic DC |
| --------------------- | --------- |
| 0 (and 1/8, 1/4, 1/2) | 15        |
| 1–5                   | 15        |
| 6–10                  | 16        |
| 11–15                 | 17        |
| 16–20                 | 18        |
| 21+                   | 19        |

## Heroic Affix Table

When a creature becomes **Heroically Awakened**, roll **[% 1d10 %]** on the table below to determine its Heroic Affix.

If the result is **10**, roll again and take an extra Heroic Affix. The creature gains both resulting affixes and is considered **Legendary**. A Legendary creature's bonus to tier bonus, saving throws, and AC increases to **+2**, and it gains **2 HP per Lethality or level**.

If either of those rerolls results in another 10, the creature gains a third Heroic Affix instead and becomes **Mythic**. A Mythic creature's bonus increases to **+3**, and its extra hit points increase to **3 HP per Lethality or level**.

Each tier replaces the one below it. The Legendary and Mythic values do not stack, and no further increases are made beyond Mythic. A creature cannot gain more than three Heroic Affixes.
