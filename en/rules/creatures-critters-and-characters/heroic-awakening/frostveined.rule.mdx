# Frostveined

A Frostveined Caster channels the stillness and cruelty of winter, shaping ice and cold into weapons of control and endurance.  
Whenever a Heroically Awakened creature gains this Heroic Affix, it gains the following spells,  
along with any other spells it may know.

A Frostveined creature is permanently under the effect of [_Baleful Bulwark_](/en/library/spells/baleful-bulwark). The effect requires no concentration, has no duration, and cannot be dispelled or suppressed. If it ends by any means, it resumes at the start of the creature's next turn.

The spell is cast as though a spell slot of the creature's tier bonus was used.
Creatures of less than 2 Lethality cast it as a **first level spell**
