# Flametongued

A Flametongued caster channels the destructive and purifying power of flame and radiance.
Whenever a Heroically Awakened creature gains this Heroic Affix, it gains the following spells,
along with any other spells it may know.

A Flametongued creature is permanently under the effect of [_Flame Stride_](/en/library/spells/flame-stride). The effect requires no concentration, has no duration, and cannot be dispelled or suppressed. If it ends by any means, it resumes at the start of the creature's next turn.
