# Stormbound

A Stormbound Caster wields the power of storms, wind and sea.
Whenever a Heroically Awakened creature gains this Heroic Affix, it gains the following spells,
along with any other spells it may know.

A Stormbound creature is permanently under the effect of [_Warding Wind_](/en/library/spells/warding-wind). The effect requires no concentration, has no duration, and cannot be dispelled or suppressed. If it ends by any means, it resumes at the start of the creature's next turn.
