# Psionic

A Psionic Creature can hold dominion over the minds of others with thought alone.  
Whenever a Heroically Awakened creature gains this Heroic Affix, it gains the following spells,  
along with any other spells it may know.

A Psionic creature is permanently under the effect of [_Mind Blank_](/en/library/spells/mind-blank). The effect requires no concentration, has no duration, and cannot be dispelled or suppressed. If it ends by any means, it resumes at the start of the creature's next turn.
