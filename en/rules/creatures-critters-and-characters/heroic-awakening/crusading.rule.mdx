# Crusading

A Crusader channels conviction into radiant destruction, embodying relentless zeal and divine endurance.  
Whenever a Heroically Awakened creature gains this Heroic Affix, it gains the following traits in addition to any other abilities it possesses.

The creature's **Charisma score becomes at least 10 + its tier bonus** (if not already higher).

It is a **half caster**. Read its spell slots from the Paladin Features table in the [Paladin vocation](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/paladin), using its **CR as the Paladin level** (CR 20 and above use the level 20 row). It regains all expended slots after a [# kw:Recovery #].

---

- **Divine Smite.**  
  When the creature hits another creature with a melee weapon attack, it can expend one spell slot to deal holy damage in addition to the weapon's damage.  
  The extra damage is [% 2d8 %] for a 1st-level slot, plus [% 1d8 %] for each spell slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of [% 6d8 %].  
  The damage increases by [% 1d8 %] if the target is undead or a fiend.

- **Lay on Hands.**  
  The creature gains a healing pool sized by its CR, as shown in the table below.  
  As a Major Action, it can touch a creature (including itself) to restore hit points from this pool.  
  It can also expend 5 points to cure a disease or neutralize a poison affecting the target.  
  The pool replenishes after a [# kw:Recovery #].

- **Aura of Protection.**  
  The creature and all friendly creatures within the radius shown below gain a bonus to all saving throws equal to the creature's Charisma modifier (minimum +0).  
  A creature can benefit from only one Aura of Protection at a time.
