# Championed

A Championed creature embodies mastery of combat; the apex of martial discipline.  
Whenever a Heroically Awakened creature gains this Heroic Affix, it gains the following traits in addition to any other abilities it possesses.

---

## Features

- **Critical Mastery.** The creature's critical hit range is **increased by 1** (a roll of 19 or 20 scores a critical hit).
- **Extra Attack.** The creature can make one additional attack whenever it takes the Attack action on its turn.
- **Legendary Resistance (1/Day).** If the creature fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
- **Combat Superiority.** The creature gains a pool of **Superiority Dice** as shown in the table below, and knows four [_maneuvers_](/en/library/character-creation/vocations/warrior/maneuvers): **Pushing Attack**, **Trip Attack**, **Disarming Attack**, and **Riposte**. Maneuvers work exactly as written on that page. It regains all expended dice when it finishes a [# kw:Repose #] or [# kw:Recovery #].
  - Where a maneuver calls for a saving throw, the DC is **10 + the creature's tier bonus + its Strength or Dexterity modifier** (the creature's choice).
