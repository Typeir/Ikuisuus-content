# Sulphurous

A Sulphurous Caster channels decay, venom, and corrosive alchemy — the rot that eats through flesh and steel alike.  
Whenever a Heroically Awakened creature gains this Heroic Affix, it gains the following spells,  
along with any other spells it may know.

A Sulphurous creature is permanently under the effect of [_Stinking Cloud_](/en/library/spells/stinking-cloud), centered on itself. The effect requires no concentration, has no duration, and cannot be dispelled or suppressed. If it ends by any means, it resumes at the start of the creature's next turn.

The cloud moves with the creature and does not obscure its own vision.
