# Areas of Effect and the Aspects of Magic

This entry covers the attack and save mechanics for spell effects, and the Aspects they hold.

The geometry of area shapes (cone, cube, cylinder, line, sphere), points of origin, lines of effect, and their interaction with cover are covered in [Movement, Maps, and Shapes](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/maps-grids-and-shapes).

## Spellcasting Mechanics

### Saving Throws

- When a spell allows a saving throw, the spell specifies which ability the target uses for the save and what happens on a success or failure.
- The DC to resist one of your spells equals `10 + your accuracy + any special modifiers imposed by features`, items, or other effects.

### Attack Rolls for Spells

- Some spells require the caster to make an attack roll to determine whether the spell effect hits a target. Most spells that require attack rolls are ranged attacks.
- Spell attack rolls follow the general attack procedure in [Making an Attack](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/making-an-attack).
- Your spell attack bonus equals your spellcasting ability modifier + your tier bonus, plus any other modifiers that apply.
- You have disadvantage on a ranged attack roll if you are within [= 1 stride =] of a hostile creature that can see you and that creature is not incapacitated.

## Interactions

- Spell saving throw DC and spell attack bonus are used by many other rules and features; calculate them as described above and apply any additional modifiers from class features, magic items, or circumstances.
- Area geometry and cover interactions are covered in [Movement, Maps, and Shapes](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/maps-grids-and-shapes).
