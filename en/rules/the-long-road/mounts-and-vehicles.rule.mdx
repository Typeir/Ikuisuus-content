# Mounts and Vehicles

This page covers how mounts, animals, and vehicles behave on the road: speed, carrying capacity, pulling limits, barding, saddles, vehicle handling, and river travel.

What any of it costs to buy, feed, or stable is in [Stables and Stock](/en/library/rules/hearth-and-station/stables-and-stock).

## Mechanics

### Mounts and Other Animals

A good mount can help you move more quickly through the wilderness, but its primary purpose is to carry the gear that would otherwise slow you down.

- An animal pulling a carriage, cart, chariot, sled, or wagon can move weight up to five times its base carrying capacity, including the weight of the vehicle.
- If multiple animals pull the same vehicle, their carrying capacities add together.
- Mounts other than those listed — flying animals such as pegasi, griffons and hippogriffs, or aquatic mounts like giant seahorses — exist, but are rare, and their availability is the Game Director's call.

### Barding (Armour for Animals)

- Barding is armour designed to protect an animal's head, neck, chest, and body. Any armour on the [Equipment: Armour](/en/library/items/equipment/armour) table can be made as barding.
- Barding is the mount's armour for every purpose: it sets the mount's Armour Class and is worn and removed on the same terms as humanoid armour.
- Cost and weight multipliers are in [Stables and Stock](/en/library/rules/hearth-and-station/stables-and-stock#tack-harness-and-drawn-vehicles).

### Saddles

- **Military saddle.** Braces the rider, helping keep a seat on an active mount in battle. It gives advantage on any check made to remain mounted.
- **Exotic saddle.** Required for riding any aquatic or flying mount.

### Vehicle Proficiency

If you have proficiency with a kind of vehicle, land or water, you add your tier bonus to any check you make to control that kind of vehicle in difficult circumstances — rough terrain, stormy seas, or a sudden manoeuvre.

### Rowed Vessels and Boats

- Keelboats and rowboats are used on lakes and rivers.
- Going downstream, add the speed of the current — typically [= 2 league =] per hour — to the speed of the vessel.
- These vessels cannot be rowed against any significant current, but they can be pulled upstream by draft animals on the shore.
- A rowboat weighs [= 50 burden =], in case it has to be carried over land.

## Interactions

- Mount capacities and vehicle-pulling limits feed into [Encumbrance and Carrying Capacity](/en/library/rules/arms-armour-and-burden/encumbrance-and-carrying-capacity): include the vehicle's own weight and every passenger when working out what a team can move.
- Pace, forced marches, and the distance a mounted party covers in a day are in [Travel Pace](/en/library/rules/the-long-road/travel-pace).
- Mounted combat — mounting, dismounting, and controlling a mount under fire — is in [Contextual Conflict](/en/library/rules/steel-and-strife/contextual-conflict).
- Purchase prices, feed, and stabling are in [Stables and Stock](/en/library/rules/hearth-and-station/stables-and-stock).
